### 2. Several metrics are wrong or poorly defined

#### Example:
> “Waiting Time … quantified by the time difference between an order’s start and completion at a specific step”

That is **activity duration**, not waiting time.

Waiting time should typically be estimated as:
- time between previous activity completion and next activity start, or
- time between becoming eligible for a resource and actual resource acquisition

This is a significant analytical mistake in a question that specifically asks to distinguish **within-instance** vs **between-instance** waiting.

#### Another example:
> “Measure how often and for how long resources … are occupied by more than one order”

For a specific station, that is usually impossible if the station handles one order at a time. The relevant metric should be:
- utilization
- queue length
- queue waiting time
- blocking time
- contention frequency across the pool of 5 cold-packing stations
