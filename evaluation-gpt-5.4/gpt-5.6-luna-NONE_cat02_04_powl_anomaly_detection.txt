In pm4py / process-tree-style semantics, `LOOP([A, I])` is not simply “repeat the fragment `A -> I` any number of times.”  
It is closer to:

- execute `A` once,

So the language is more like:

```text
A
A I A
A I A I A
...
```

not simply:

```text
A I
A I A I
...
```
