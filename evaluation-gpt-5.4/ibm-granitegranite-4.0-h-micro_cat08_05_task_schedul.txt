#### Tardiness definition is incorrect
The answer says:
> “Tardiness is calculated as the difference between completion time and due date, with negative values indicating early completion.”

That is **lateness**, not tardiness.  
Tardiness is typically:
\[
T_j = \max(C_j - d_j, 0)
\]
Negative values are not tardiness. This is a notable technical error.

#### Utilization definition is weak/inaccurate
It says utilization is derived by comparing productive time to available time defined as:
> “total time between job arrivals and completions”

That is not a valid general definition of machine availability. Availability should be based on **calendar time**, **shift schedule**, and often net of **planned downtime/unplanned downtime**. The answer confuses horizon time with availability.

#### Makespan not properly handled
The prompt asks for:
- job flow times,
- lead times,
- **makespan distributions**.

Makespan is usually a property of a **schedule/horizon/batch/period**, not of each job. The answer never really addresses this distinctly.
