### 2. Some metric definitions are imprecise or partially conflated
The answer distinguishes “case duration,” “flow time,” and “makespan,” but not rigorously enough:
- **Lead time** and **flow time** are often used differently depending on whether waiting before release is included.
- **Makespan** is usually a schedule-level metric for a set of jobs, not “for each case.”
- The answer says “compute case duration ... and makespan relative to the shop calendar,” which is muddled.

Under strict grading, this is a meaningful technical imprecision.

### 3. “Schedule adherence” is handled too narrowly
The prompt asked for **schedule adherence and tardiness**. The answer mainly evaluates lateness against due date, which is only part of schedule adherence.
