   `ORDER BY case_count DESC, activity_sequence` is used to break ties among equally frequent variants. This is not wrong, but ordering by a list-valued expression is a somewhat brittle choice and is not clearly motivated.

   If multiple variants share the same frequency around rank K, this query arbitrarily picks only some of them due to `LIMIT 10`. That may be acceptable, but the answer does not acknowledge that behavior.

   The solution relies on grouping and joining directly on `LIST` values. This is probably fine in DuckDB, but it is an implicit assumption and not explained.
