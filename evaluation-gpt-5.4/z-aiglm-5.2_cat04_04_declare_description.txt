  - The answer says this means funds are not disbursed “while a raw application is still being processed.”
  - That is incorrect. `noncoexistence` means the two activities cannot both occur in the same trace, not merely not at the same time.

  - `chainprecedence`: `Authorize_Contract_Terms` -> `Preliminary_Credit_Check`
  - Standard meaning: every `Preliminary_Credit_Check` must be immediately preceded by `Authorize_Contract_Terms`.
  - The answer never incorporates this, and its proposed sequence directly conflicts with it.
