1. **Core methodological weakness: uses `MIN(timestamp)` almost everywhere**
   - This is a major simplification.

   - If activities repeat, these queries can compute the wrong intervals and produce misleading anomaly detection.
   - The answer acknowledges this assumption, but it still weakens most verification queries substantially.

     which is effectively all closed claims.
   - That is a genuine logical error, not just an imperfection.
