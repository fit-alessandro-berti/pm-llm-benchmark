- A clear initial and final marking.
- Correct alternating place–transition arcs.

The principal flaw is that **Approval is modeled as mandatory**, although the scenario explicitly states it is required only when the claim amount exceeds a threshold. The comment acknowledges this discrepancy but does not solve it. A silent transition should provide a bypass from `after_CA` to `after_AP` for claims below the threshold, while `AP` handles claims above it. Consequently, the net rejects valid executions in which assessment proceeds directly to payment without approval.

Minor concerns:

- The conditions for selecting RDS, II, or normal continuation are represented only as nondeterministic choices. This is generally acceptable for an ordinary Petri net, but the comments imply condition-aware behavior that the model itself cannot distinguish.
- Modeling “Insurance Claim (C)” as a transition is defensible, but arguably redundant because the token in `start` is already described as representing the arrival of a new claim.

Overall, it is a strong and executable construction, but it does not fully model one explicit routing requirement.
