The visualization is a BPMN-style workflow diagram for a **“Dispatch of goods”** process at a computer hardware shop. It is organized into three horizontal swimlanes:

- **Logistics** (top)
- **Computer Hardware Shop / Secretary** (middle)
- **Warehouse** (bottom)

### Process flow

1. The process starts with **“Ship goods.”**
2. A parallel gateway splits the work:
   - One path goes to the warehouse to **“Package goods.”**
   - The other goes to the secretary to **“Clarify shipment method.”**

3. After shipment clarification, a decision gateway asks **“Special handling?”**
   - **Yes:**  
     - **Get 3 offers from logistic companies**
     - **Select logistic company and place order**
     - This eventually joins the downstream process.
   - **No:**  
     - **Check if insurance is required**, using **customer information** and **order details** as inputs.
     - If insurance is necessary, the process goes to Logistics to **“Insure parcel.”**
     - Whether insurance is required or not, the flow continues to **“Write package label.”**

4. The packaging branch includes a timer:
   - **Package goods** is expected within **48 hours**.
   - If delayed, the workflow triggers **“Notify customer about delay.”**
   - Packaging and any notification are synchronized before proceeding.

5. The completed packaging work and the shipping-method/logistics work join at a parallel gateway in the warehouse.

6. The warehouse then:
   - **Fills out shipment paperwork**
   - **Moves package to pickup area**
   - Reaches the end event, **“Shipment prepared.”**

Overall, the diagram shows how normal shipment preparation, special logistics handling, insurance, labeling, warehouse packing, delay notifications, and final paperwork are coordinated before a parcel is ready for pickup.
