### Data Mapping Assumptions & Notes

1. **Case Identifier**: `order_id` is used as the **Case ID**. In cases with multiple invoices or deliveries (e.g., Order `1002`), all related activities map back to the primary `order_id`.
2. **Activity Derivation**:
   * **Order Created**: Triggered by `order_date` in the `Orders` table.
   * **Item Picked**: Triggered per item by `item_picking_date` in the `Order Lines` table.
   * **Order Dispatched**: Triggered by `delivery_dispatch_date` in the `Delivery` table.
   * **Order Arrived**: Triggered by `delivery_arrival_date` in the `Delivery` table.
   * **Invoice Issued**: Triggered by `invoice_issue_date` in the `Invoice` table.
   * **Payment Received**: Triggered by `payment_date` in the `Payments` table (mapped to `order_id` via `invoice_id`).
3. **Resource & Actor Mapping**:
   * Resource IDs (`SP-xxx`, `WH-xxx`, `CR-xx`, `BL-xxx`) were joined with the optional `Resources` table to pull full names and roles.
   * `Payment Received` events do not specify a personnel ID in the underlying system, so they are attributed to `Automated System` / `Financial System`.
4. **Incomplete Processes**:
   * **Order 1004** currently stops after the `Item Picked` activity. This correctly reflects an active/open case in the ERP that has not yet been dispatched, invoiced, or paid.
