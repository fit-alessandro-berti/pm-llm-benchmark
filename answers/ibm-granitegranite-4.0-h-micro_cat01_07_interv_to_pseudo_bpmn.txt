**Structured BPMN-like Process Model Description**

**Process Overview:**  
The standard invoice approval workflow involves several key stakeholders, including Accounts Payable, Purchasing, Department Managers, and Accounts Payable Supervisors. The process starts with the receipt of an invoice, proceeds through verification, matching, and approval steps, and concludes with payment scheduling. Exception handling is integrated at various stages to manage discrepancies and require additional approvals.

**1. Activities/Tasks:**

- **Invoice Reception:** The invoice is received, typically via email, from the supplier.
- **PO Verification:** The Accounts Payable Clerk (Mary) checks if the invoice is associated with a valid purchase order (PO) number.
- **Logging into Accounting System:** If the invoice has a valid PO, it is logged into the accounting system.
- **Forwarding to Purchasing Department:** The logged invoice is forwarded to the Purchasing department to confirm receipt of goods/services.
- **Confirmation from Purchasing:** The Purchasing Officer (Dan) confirms receipt and verifies invoice details against the PO.
- **Invoice Discrepancy Handling:** If discrepancies are found (e.g., incorrect pricing, missing items), the process triggers a clarification request to the Purchasing team or escalates to the Accounts Payable Supervisor (Rita) if unresolved.
- **Manager Approval Trigger:** Once confirmed, the invoice is sent to the Department Manager (Carlos) for approval once both receipt confirmation and PO matching are verified.
- **Exception Escalation:** Unclear or suspicious invoices are escalated to the Accounts Payable Supervisor (Rita) for further investigation, which may involve contacting suppliers or creating retrospective purchase orders.
- **Manager Approval:** The Department Manager (Carlos) reviews the invoice and approves it if all details are correct.
- **Payment Scheduling:** After manager approval, the invoice is scheduled for payment through the Accounts Payable system.
- **Exception Resolution (if applicable):** In cases where issues are unresolved at the initial stages, the Accounts Payable Supervisor (Rita) works with suppliers and departments to resolve exceptions before re-entering the normal process flow.

**2. Sequence Flow:**

- **Invoice Reception  PO Verification  Logging  Forwarding  Confirmation  Discrepancy Handling  Manager Approval  Payment Scheduling**
- Discrepancy Handling branches off from Confirmation and may lead back to Invoice Reception if unresolved.
- Exception pathways from Discrepancy Handling lead to Accounts Payable Supervisor (Rita) for resolution.

**3. Gateways/Decision Points:**

- **Decision Point after PO Verification:** Determines whether the invoice has a valid PO (goes to Logging) or requires clarification (escalates to Rita).
- **Decision Point after Confirmation:** Determines if discrepancies exist (goes to Discrepancy Handling) or if everything is correct (proceeds to Manager Approval).
- **Manager Approval Decision Point:** Confirms whether the invoice is approved or if it needs to be sent back for further review.

**4. Roles/Participants:**

- **Accounts Payable Clerk (Mary):** Checks PO validity, logs invoices into the system, forwards to Purchasing, handles discrepancies, and escalates unresolved issues.
- **Purchasing Officer (Dan):** Verifies invoice details against PO, confirms receipt, and sends confirmation back to Accounts Payable.
- **Department Manager (Carlos):** Receives invoice approval notifications, reviews and approves invoices, identifies exceptions for Rita.
- **Accounts Payable Supervisor (Rita):** Manages exceptions, resolves discrepancies with suppliers, ensures invoices are cleared for payment.
- **Department Heads (indirectly mentioned):** Involved in creating retrospective purchase orders when informal contracts exist.
- **Supplier Relationship Manager (Karen):** Resolves invoice discrepancies and provides supplier contact information when necessary.

**5. Artifacts/Inputs/Outputs:**

- **Inputs:** Invoice (email), Purchase Order (PO) details, Confirmation of receipt from Purchasing.
- **Outputs:** Confirmation emails to Accounts Payable and Purchasing, Manager approval notifications, Scheduled payment entries.
- **Artifacts:** Purchase Orders, Invoice records, Confirmation emails, Payment schedules.

**6. Exceptions/Variations:**

- **Missing PO Number:** If the PO number is missing, Mary informs the supplier to request it before proceeding.
- **Invoice Discrepancies:** Leads to clarification requests from Dan or escalation to Rita.
- **Suspicious Entries:** May trigger additional review by Carlos before approval.
- **Informal Contracts:** May result in the creation of retrospective POs by Rita and department heads.

**7. Ambiguities and Uncertainties:**

- **Final Payment Scheduling:** The exact trigger for payment scheduling after manager approval is not explicitly defined in all interviews. It is assumed to be part of the system workflow but could vary.
- **Discrepancy Resolution Time:** The process does not specify timeframes for resolving discrepancies, which could affect workflow efficiency.
- **System Notifications:** The exact mechanisms and triggers for system notifications (e.g., automatic emails) are not detailed, which may vary based on the specific software used.
