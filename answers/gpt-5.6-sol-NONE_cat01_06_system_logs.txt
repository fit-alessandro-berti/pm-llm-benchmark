## Transformation Logic

- **Case identification:** Each distinct work item is treated as a case:
  - individual Word documents,
  - the specific Annual Meeting email thread,
  - the PDF,
  - and the Excel workbook.
- **Interrupted work:** Returning to an existing file continues the same case rather than creating a new one. Thus, both Word documents retain their case IDs across application switches.
- **Activity abstraction:** Low-level events were mapped to analyst-friendly activities:
  - `FOCUS`  **Open** or **Resume**
  - `TYPING`  **Edit**, **Update**, or **Compose**, depending on context
  - `SCROLL`  **Read Email** or **Review PDF**
  - `SAVE` and `CLOSE`  artifact-specific save and close activities
  - contextual `CLICK` actions  **Open Email**, **Start Email Reply**, or **Send Email Reply**
  - switching away from an active document  **Suspend Document Work**
- **Switch handling:** `SWITCH` was not retained as a generic activity. It was interpreted through the source and destination work items, creating meaningful suspend, resume, or open activities.
- **Ordering:** The table is grouped by case for trace readability. Process mining tools should sort events within each case by timestamp.
