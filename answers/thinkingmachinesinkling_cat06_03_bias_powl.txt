Consequently, two applicants with the **identical preliminary score (C)** can diverge: one receives an incremental bonus that improves their standing going into manual review (**E**) and the final decision (**F**), while the other proceeds with the raw score.

### 2. Implications of favoring a non-legally protected group

*   **Proxy discrimination:** “Local affiliation” and “known community groups” often correlate strongly with protected classes.
