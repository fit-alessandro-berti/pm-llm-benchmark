**Rationale - How bias is reduced:**

1.  **`existence` ManualReview, BiasMitigationCheck:** Introduces mandatory fairness activities. They must appear in compliant traces.

2.  **`responded_existence` + `coexistence` sensitive -> review:** If `CheckApplicantRace/Gender/Age` or `Approve_Minority/Reject_Minority` occurs, `ManualReview` and `BiasMitigationCheck` must coexist. This guarantees that any decision involving a sensitive demographic cannot exist without an independent human check.

3.  **`response` sensitive -> BiasMitigationCheck:** Any disclosure of a sensitive attribute must eventually be followed by a `BiasMitigationCheck`. Prevents the process from using race/gender/age and proceeding directly to a decision.

4.  **`precedence` BiasMitigationCheck, ManualReview -> FinalDecision/Approve/Reject:** If a final decision occurs, a mitigation check and manual review must have occurred before it. This enforces a four-eyes principle and breaks the direct causal link `sensitive attribute -> reject`.

5.  **`altresponse / altprecedence / chainresponse / chainprecedence / chainsuccession`:** Strengthens the ordering: `BiasMitigationCheck` must be immediately followed by `ManualReview` and `ManualReview` must immediately precede `FinalDecision` in fair traces (`chainprecedence`). This prevents inserting biased reasoning between mitigation and decision.

6.  **`nonchainsuccession` sensitive -> decision:** Explicitly forbids immediate succession `CheckApplicantRace -> Reject/Approve/FinalDecision` and same for Gender/Age. This eliminates the pattern of instant biased rejection after seeing minority status, forcing the intermediate fairness steps.

Together, these constraints implement a fairness-aware workflow: sensitive data may be collected, but it can never directly drive an outcome without a documented bias check and human oversight.
