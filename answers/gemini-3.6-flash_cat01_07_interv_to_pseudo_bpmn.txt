# Process Model: Accounts Payable & Invoice Approval Process

## 1. Process Overview & Execution Scope
* **Process Name:** Vendor Invoice Processing and Approval Flow
* **Process Trigger:** Arrival of a vendor invoice via email.
* **Process End States:**
  * **Success:** Invoice scheduled for payment run.
  * **Failure / Termination:** Invoice rejected and returned to vendor / discarded.

---

## 2. Participants & Pools / Swimlanes
1. **Accounts Payable (AP) Clerk [Mary]:** Frontline logging, initial PO checks, line-item matching, scheduling payment.
2. **Purchasing Officer [Dan]:** Verification of goods/services delivery, quantity, and pricing against POs.
3. **Department Manager [Carlos]:** Final financial sign-off and budget verification.
4. **AP Supervisor [Rita]:** Exception handler for complex discrepancies, informal contracts, and missing POs.
5. **Supplier Relationship Manager [Karen]:** External contact point for vendor communication, contract checking, and dispute resolution.
6. **Accounting System [System]:** Automated email triggers, system matching, and status updates.
7. **Supplier [External]:** Provides invoices, revised documentation, or missing PO numbers.

---

## 3. Data Artifacts
* **Vendor Invoice** (Email attachment / PDF)
* **Purchase Order (PO)** (System record)
* **Goods/Services Receipt Confirmation**
* **Retrospective PO** (Created post-hoc for non-PO invoices)
* **System Approval Notification** (Automated email/task)

---

## 4. Sequential Process Description

### **Phase 1: Ingestion & PO Validation**

* **`[Start Event]`** Invoice received via email by AP Clerk.
* **`[User Task: AP Clerk]`** Check invoice for a valid Purchase Order (PO) number.
* **`[Exclusive Gateway (XOR 1): Valid PO Exists?]`**
  * **Path A: NO**
    * **`[User Task: AP Clerk / Karen]`** Contact Supplier to request PO details or a revised invoice.
    * **`[Sub-Process / Branch: Non-PO Service Exception (Rita)]`**
      * If invoice is for an informal service contract without a PO, escalate to **AP Supervisor (Rita)**.
      * **`[User Task: AP Supervisor & Dept Head]`** Determine whether to generate a *Retrospective PO* or reject the invoice.
      * *If Rejected:* **`[End Event: Invoice Rejected]`**.
      * *If Retrospective PO Created:* Loop back to **`[Exclusive Gateway (XOR 1)]`** as "PO Exists".
  * **Path B: YES**
    * **`[User Task: AP Clerk]`** Log invoice into the Accounting System.
    * Proceed to **Phase 2**.

---

### **Phase 2: Verification & Line Matching**

* **`[User Task: AP Clerk]`** Forward invoice to Purchasing Department.
* **`[User Task: Purchasing Officer (Dan)]`** Open PO and compare against invoice (quantities, pricing, delivery charges, receipt of goods/services).
* **`[Exclusive Gateway (XOR 2): Verification Successful?]`**
  * **Path A: NO (Discrepancy Detected)**
    * **`[User Task: Purchasing Officer / AP Clerk / Karen]`** Contact supplier to request pricing clarification or request AP to hold invoice until a corrected invoice is issued.
    * *If complex or unresolved:* Escalated to **AP Supervisor (Rita)** and/or **Supplier Relationship Manager (Karen)** to verify master contract terms.
    * *Outcome:* Receive corrected/revised invoice from supplier or adjust internal record -> Re-evaluate at **`[Exclusive Gateway (XOR 2)]`**.
  * **Path B: YES (No Discrepancy)**
    * **`[User Task: Purchasing Officer (Dan)]`** Send confirmation email/status update to Accounts Payable.
* **`[User Task: AP Clerk]`** Perform 3-way line-item matching against PO in the Accounting System.

---

### **Phase 3: Managerial Approval & Payment Scheduling**

* **`[Service Task: Accounting System]`** Automatically send email/notification to **Department Manager (Carlos)** requesting sign-off.
* **`[User Task: Department Manager (Carlos)]`** Review invoice details, attached PO, and purchasing confirmation against budget/expectations.
* **`[Exclusive Gateway (XOR 3): Manager Approval?]`**
  * **Path A: REJECT / HOLD**
    * **`[User Task: Dept Manager]`** Flag invoice in system as rejected or request hold for investigation.
    * **`[User Task: AP Clerk / Rita]`** Re-open discrepancy investigation (Loops back to Exception Handling in Phase 2).
  * **Path B: APPROVE**
    * **`[User Task: Dept Manager]`** Submit approval sign-off in the Accounting System.
* **`[User Task: AP Clerk]`** Receive approved invoice notification and schedule invoice for the next payment run.
* **`[End Event]`** Invoice processed and queued for payment.

---

## 5. Exceptions & Loop Summary

| Trigger | Handling Role | Resolution / Path |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Missing PO Number** | AP Clerk / Karen / Rita | Request PO details from supplier or create Retrospective PO via Dept Head. |
| **Price / Quantity Discrepancy** | Dan / Karen | Contact supplier for a corrected invoice; check baseline contract terms with Karen. |
| **Unresolved / Stuck Invoice** | Rita (AP Supervisor) | Manual intervention, direct vendor/department alignment to move or reject invoice. |
| **Budget Variance / Unrecognized Charge** | Carlos (Dept Manager) | Invoice flagged/held; sent back to AP/Rita for investigation. |

---

## 6. Synthesis Notes & Consolidated Ambiguities

During synthesis of the interview excerpts, a few minor overlaps and variations were noted:

1. **Vendor Communication Responsibility:** Mary (AP), Dan (Purchasing), and Karen (SRM) all mention contacting suppliers for missing details or discrepancies. 
   * *Consolidated Logic:* Frontline queries (missing PO, billing corrections) start with **AP/Purchasing**, while high-level contract/relationship issues escalate to **Karen (SRM)**.
2. **Approval Trigger Mechanism:** Dan assumes he sends an email to Mary, who then sends it to the manager. However, Mary and Carlos clarify that **the Accounting System automatically triggers the approval request email** once line items and goods receipt are matched.
3. **Rejection Outcomes:** Rejection can occur at three points: initial PO validation (Rita/AP), verification/contract check (Rita/Karen), or final sign-off (Carlos). All rejections result in either holding for rework (revised invoice) or outright termination/cancellation of the invoice.
