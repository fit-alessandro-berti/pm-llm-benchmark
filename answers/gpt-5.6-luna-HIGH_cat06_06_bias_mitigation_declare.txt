### Rationale

- **`responded_existence`** requires `Approve_Minority` and `Reject_Minority` decisions to have a corresponding `ManualReview` and `SensitiveApplicantDecision`.
- **`coexistence`** ensures that a sensitive-applicant decision is accompanied by a manual review.
- **`precedence`** requires `BiasMitigationCheck` before any approval, rejection, or final decision. It also requires `ManualReview` before a sensitive-applicant decision.
- **`response`** ensures that a bias-mitigation check is not left unresolved and is eventually followed by the final decision.
- **`nonchainsuccession`** prevents `CheckApplicantAge`, `CheckApplicantGender`, or `CheckApplicantRace` from being immediately followed by a decision. This creates room for the required mitigation check or manual review.

These constraints provide procedural safeguards against decisions being made directly from sensitive attributes. They do not by themselves prove statistical fairness, so outcome disparities should still be monitored separately.
