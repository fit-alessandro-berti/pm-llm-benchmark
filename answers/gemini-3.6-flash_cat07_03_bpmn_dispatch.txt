Based on the provided image, here is a detailed description of the **BPMN (Business Process Model and Notation)** diagram:

---

### **Overview**
* **Process Title:** Dispatch of goods
* **Organization:** Computer Hardware Shop
* **Layout:** A swimlane diagram divided into three horizontal lanes representing different departments/roles involved in the process:
  1. **Logistics** (Top Lane)
  2. **Secretary** (Middle Lane)
  3. **Warehouse** (Bottom Lane)

---

### **Step-by-Step Process Flow**

#### **1. Start & Initial Parallel Split**
* **Start Event:** The process begins in the **Secretary** lane with the event **"Ship goods"**.
* **Parallel Gateway (`+`):** Immediately branches the process into two concurrent paths running simultaneously in the **Secretary** and **Warehouse** lanes.

---

#### **2. Secretary Lane Path**
1. **Clarify shipment method:** The Secretary clarifies the shipment method, referencing the **"Order details"** document.
2. **Decision Gateway ("Special handling?"):**
   * **If "yes":**
     * Task: **"Get 3 offers from logistic companies"**.
     * Task: **"Select logistic company and place order"**.
     * Proceeds to a merge gateway.
   * **If "no":**
     * Task: **"Check if insurance is required"** (connects to a **"Customer information"** database).
     * **Insurance Gateway:**
       * **If insurance necessary:** Route moves up to the **Logistics** lane to perform the task **"Insure parcel"**.
       * **Always:** The Secretary performs the task **"Write package label"**.
     * Both sub-paths merge back together before joining the main merge gateway.

Both the "Special handling = yes" and "Special handling = no" branches rejoin at an exclusive gateway before moving to the final synchronization point in the Warehouse lane.

---

#### **3. Warehouse Lane Path**
1. Task: **"Package goods"**.
   * **Timer Event (48 hours):** If packaging takes longer than 48 hours, an boundary timer triggers the task **"Notify customer about delay"**.
   * Both normal completion and delay notification lead into a merge gateway.

---

#### **4. Synchronization & Completion**
* **Parallel Gateway (`+` Join):** The completed tasks from both the **Secretary** path (labeling/ordering logistics) and the **Warehouse** path (packaging) synchronize at a parallel join gateway in the Warehouse lane.
* Task: **"Fill out Shipment paperwork"** (Warehouse).
* Task: **"Move package to pickup area"** (Warehouse).
* **End Event:** The process concludes with the end event **"Shipment prepared"**.
