## 4. Justification of the naming choices

- **Initiate Request:** the resource attribute (WebPortal vs. BranchReception) is the only discriminator; channel determines downstream handling (digital re-upload possible in C001, physical presence required in C003), so it must be visible in the label.
- **Verify Documents – Initial vs. Re-Check:** the first occurrence is the standard control step in the happy path; the later occurrence follows an exception and is explicitly described as "re-checks"/"re-verify". Splitting them turns an invisible self-loop into a measurable **rework** activity (first-pass yield can now be computed).
- **Re-Check After Customer Upload vs. After Internal Adjustment:** the two re-checks have different *causes* — external (missing customer evidence) vs. internal (data quality defect). Lumping them together would hide the fact that C003's rework is bank-caused, not customer-caused, which matters for root-cause analysis and cost allocation.
- **Physical vs. Back Office verification:** BranchOfficer performs an in-person inspection, BackOfficeAgent a documentary/digital check. Different control strength, duration and skill requirement  different activities.
- **Call Customer:** four different purposes are explicitly stated in the context field: (i) chase documents, (ii) inquire about a discrepancy, (iii) confirm a completed change, (iv) get an absent walk-in customer back. Only (i) and (ii) are *exception-triggering* calls; (iii) is a *closing/confirmation* call; (iv) is a *customer-availability* problem, not a data problem. Keeping one label would make C002's two calls look like a redundant repeat, when in fact they bracket the adjustment (inquiry  change  confirmation).
- **Adjust Account:** in C002 the correction is authorised by the customer's clarification (customer-driven); in C003 it is an internally detected omission with no customer input (bank-driven), and it triggers re-verification. The trigger distinction explains why C003 needs another verification step and C002 does not.
- **Submit Additional:** vague label; renaming it to reference the specific requested artefact and its trigger links it unambiguously to the preceding "Request Missing Documents" call, making the document-chase loop explicit.
- **Approve Request:** semantically identical everywhere (single occurrence, Supervisor, final decision). It is kept as one activity but qualified by the evidence path, so approval-basis compliance (re-verified documents vs. telephone confirmation) can be audited without fragmenting the end event.

**Net effect:** the three cases now reveal three distinct exception patterns — a *customer-document loop* (C001), an *account-data clarification/confirmation loop* (C002), and a *combined availability + internal-correction loop* (C003) — instead of appearing as three near-identical traces with unexplained repetitions.
