Based on the interview transcripts, here is a structured, BPMN-like textual process model of the Invoice Approval Process. 

### 1. Pools and Lanes (Roles/Participants)
*   **Accounts Payable (AP):** Mary (Clerk), Rita (Supervisor)
*   **Purchasing:** Dan (Officer)
*   **Department Management:** Carlos (Manager)
*   **Supplier Relations:** Karen (Manager)
*   **External:** Supplier
*   **System:** Accounting System

### 2. Artifacts (Inputs/Outputs/Data Objects)
*   **Invoice:** The primary document moving through the process (received via email).
*   **Purchase Order (PO):** The reference document used for validation.
*   **Confirmation Email:** Sent by Purchasing to AP.
*   **System Notification/Email:** Automatically generated by the system to the Department Manager.
*   **Corrected Invoice:** Sent by the Supplier to resolve discrepancies.

### 3. Process Flow (Textual BPMN Representation)

**Start Event:** Invoice received (usually by email)

**Lane: Accounts Payable (Mary)**
1.  **Task:** Check for valid Purchase Order (PO) number on the invoice.
2.  **Exclusive Gateway:** Valid PO number present?
    *   **No:** 
        *   **Task:** Send invoice back to Supplier requesting more details / PO number.
        *   *(Note: Supplier Relations (Karen) may be looped in here to obtain the PO or revised invoice from the Supplier).*
        *   **Sequence Flow:** Wait for Supplier response -> Return to Step 1.
    *   **Yes:** 
        *   **Task:** Log invoice into the Accounting System.
        *   **Task:** Forward invoice to Purchasing Department to confirm receipt of goods/services.

**Lane: Purchasing (Dan)**
3.  **Task:** Compare invoice details (quantities, prices, delivery charges) against the PO.
4.  **Exclusive Gateway:** Do invoice details match the PO?
    *   **No:** 
        *   **Task:** Contact Supplier directly OR ask AP to hold the invoice until a corrected version is sent.
        *   *(Note: Supplier Relations (Karen) may be looped in here to check contracts and provide correct pricing info to AP/Purchasing).*
        *   **Sequence Flow:** Wait for corrected invoice -> Return to Step 3.
    *   **Yes:** 
        *   **Task:** Send confirmation email back to Accounts Payable.

**Lane: Accounts Payable (Mary)**
5.  **Task:** Match invoice line items against the PO in the system.
6.  **Exclusive Gateway:** Do line items match the PO?
    *   **No:** 
        *   **Task:** Email Purchasing team to clarify discrepancy.
        *   **Exclusive Gateway:** Can the discrepancy be resolved easily?
            *   **No:** 
                *   **Task:** Escalate to AP Supervisor (Rita).
                *   **Lane: Accounts Payable Supervisor (Rita)**
                    *   **Task:** Contact suppliers or coordinate between departments.
                    *   **Exclusive Gateway:** Is there no PO because of an informal contract?
                        *   **Yes:** Work with Department Heads to create a retrospective PO OR decide to reject the invoice.
                        *   **No:** Resolve the exception.
                    *   **Sequence Flow:** Return to normal flow (Step 3 or 5).
            *   **Yes:** Return to Step 5.
    *   **Yes:** 
        *   **Task:** Send invoice to Department Manager for approval.
        *   *(System Action: System sends automatic email requesting manager sign-off).*

**Lane: Department Management (Carlos)**
7.  **Task:** Review invoice details, PO, and Purchasing confirmation via system notification.
8.  **Exclusive Gateway:** Does everything look good (quantities, pricing, budget alignment)?
    *   **No:** 
        *   **Task:** Reject invoice and ask AP to hold it and investigate further.
        *   **Sequence Flow:** Return to AP (Step 5 or 6).
    *   **Yes:** 
        *   **Task:** Approve invoice in the system.

**Lane: Accounts Payable (Mary)**
9.  **Task:** Schedule invoice for payment.

**End Event:** Invoice added to the payment cycle/run.

---

### 4. Exceptions and Variations (Consolidated Accounts)

*   **Missing PO Number:** Mary sends it back to the Supplier. Dan will not proceed without it. Karen may intervene to expedite getting the PO or a revised invoice from the Supplier.
*   **Pricing/Quantity Discrepancies:** Dan contacts the Supplier directly or asks AP to hold the invoice. Karen checks the master contract to provide the correct price to AP/Purchasing. 
*   **Unresolvable Discrepancies:** If Mary and Dan cannot resolve the issue, Mary escalates to Rita. Rita coordinates across departments and contacts Suppliers. In rare cases of informally contracted services, Rita works with Department Heads to create a retrospective PO or reject the invoice entirely.
*   **Budget Anomalies:** Carlos rejects invoices if the total amount deviates significantly from the budget or contains unexpected items, sending it back to Mary for investigation. Carlos noted this is rare (once every couple of months).
*   **System Automation:** The transition from AP to the Department Manager is triggered automatically by the system once the PO match and Purchasing confirmation are complete.
