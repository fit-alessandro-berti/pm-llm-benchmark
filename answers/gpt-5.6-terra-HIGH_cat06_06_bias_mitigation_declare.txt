### Rationale for the bias-mitigating constraints

- **`response` from sensitive checks to `BiasMitigationCheck` and `ManualReview`**  
  If race, gender, or age is inspected, the case must later include both a formal bias check and manual review. This creates an auditable safeguard whenever protected information is accessed.

- **`chainprecedence` from `BiasMitigationCheck` to decisions**  
  `Approve`, `Reject`, and `FinalDecision` must be immediately preceded by a `BiasMitigationCheck`. This makes the mitigation step mandatory directly before a decision is recorded.

- **`nonchainsuccession` from sensitive checks to decisions**  
  Activities such as `CheckApplicantRace` cannot be directly followed by `Reject`, `Approve`, or `FinalDecision`. This explicitly prevents a decision being made immediately after viewing sensitive data.

- **`coexistence` between protected-group decisions and `ManualReview`**  
  Decisions represented as `Approve_Minority`, `Reject_Minority`, or `RequestAdditionalInfo_Minority` require a `ManualReview` in the same trace.

- **`precedence` from `ManualReview` to decisions**  
  The manual review must occur before the final decision, rather than merely somewhere in the trace after it. This prevents an after-the-fact review from satisfying the fairness control.

These constraints provide trace-level procedural fairness controls. They do not by themselves prove statistical fairness, such as equal approval rates across demographic groups, so the process should additionally be monitored using outcome-based fairness metrics.
