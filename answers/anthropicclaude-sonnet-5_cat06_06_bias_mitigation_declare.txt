## Rationale for Each Added Constraint

| Constraint Type | Activities | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **existence** | `BiasMitigationCheck`, `ManualReview` | Guarantees that every trace contains at least one explicit fairness-oriented activity, ensuring these checks are not optional or skipped entirely. |
| **responded_existence** | `CheckApplicantRace/Gender/Age  BiasMitigationCheck` | If a sensitive attribute is ever inspected during the process, a corresponding bias mitigation step must occur somewhere in the same trace — decoupled from strict ordering but still enforced. |
| **coexistence** | `Approve_Minority/Reject_Minority/...  ManualReview` | Any decision outcome tied to a sensitive demographic group must always co-occur with a `ManualReview` activity, ensuring extra scrutiny is applied whenever demographic-linked decisions are made. |
| **response** | `CheckApplicantRace/Gender/Age  BiasMitigationCheck` | Enforces that after any sensitive attribute is examined, a bias mitigation step **must** eventually follow — closing the loophole of checking sensitive data without any fairness safeguard afterward. |
| **precedence** | `BiasMitigationCheck  Approve/Reject`; `ManualReview  Reject_Minority` | Ensures that before any final decision is rendered, a bias mitigation step (and, for minority-flagged rejections, a manual review) has already taken place — decisions cannot be made "blindly." |
| **succession** | `BiasMitigationCheck  Approve/Reject` | Strengthens the precedence/response pairing into a stricter bidirectional guarantee: mitigation checks and decisions are tightly coupled in both directions. |
| **chainprecedence** | `BiasMitigationCheck  Approve/Reject` (immediate) | Directly prevents a decision from being the *immediate next event* unless a `BiasMitigationCheck` was the activity immediately preceding it — closing the specific "immediate succession" bias loophole. |
| **nonchainsuccession** | `CheckApplicantRace/Gender/Age  Reject/Approve` (immediate) | Explicitly forbids a decision activity from being the direct next step after a sensitive-attribute-revealing activity, blocking the most direct form of biased shortcutting (e.g., "see race  reject" with nothing in between). |

### Summary of Bias-Mitigation Strategy

1. **Detection Isolation:** Sensitive attribute checks (`CheckApplicantRace`, etc.) are never allowed to flow directly into decisions (via `nonchainsuccession` and `chainprecedence`), forcing an intermediate fairness step.
2. **Mandatory Safeguards:** `BiasMitigationCheck` and `ManualReview` are required to exist in every trace (`existence`) and are causally linked to both sensitive-attribute checks (`response`) and decisions (`precedence`/`succession`).
3. **Demographic Accountability:** Decisions explicitly tied to sensitive groups (`Approve_Minority`, `Reject_Elderly`, etc.) are forced into `coexistence` with `ManualReview`, ensuring extra human/algorithmic oversight specifically when demographic factors are part of the decision context.
4. **Traceable Fairness Path:** Together, these constraints create an auditable path: *sensitive attribute observed  mitigation/review performed  decision made*, rather than allowing a direct, unchecked route from sensitive data to outcome.

This layered approach — combining existence, ordering (precedence/succession), immediacy (chain constraints), and prohibition (non-succession) constraints — closes multiple potential loopholes through which bias could enter the decision-making sequence, while preserving the original process structure and DECLARE schema.
