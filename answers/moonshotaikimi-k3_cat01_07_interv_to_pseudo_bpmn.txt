## 6. Exception Paths & Rework Loops

**E1 — Missing PO (triggered at G1)**
- AP Clerk returns invoice to supplier requesting PO details.
- Supplier Relationship Manager (Karen) may be engaged to chase the supplier for the PO number or a revised invoice.
- **Loop:** flow re-enters at T1 once information is received.
- **Variant:** If *no PO exists* (e.g., informally contracted services — per Rita), escalate to E4 (Supervisor) instead.

**E2 — Purchasing-stage discrepancy (triggered at G2)**
- Purchasing either (a) contacts the supplier directly, or (b) asks AP to **hold the invoice** until a corrected invoice arrives.
- **Loop:** flow re-enters at T4 upon receipt of corrected invoice. *(Ambiguity: it is unstated whether a corrected invoice must be re-logged at T2 — assume re-verification only, since the original was already logged.)*
- **Variant:** If the PO number is discovered missing at this stage, Purchasing instructs AP to obtain it first  diverts to E1.

**E3 — Match-stage discrepancy (triggered at G3)**
- AP Clerk emails Purchasing to clarify (e.g., incorrect pricing, missing items).
- Karen may be looped in to check the supplier contract and confirm correct pricing.
- Resolved  return to T6. Unresolved  E4.

**E4 — Supervisor escalation (triggered at G3a or E1-variant)**
- AP Supervisor (Rita) contacts suppliers and/or coordinates between departments.
- For non-PO invoices: works with department heads to either **create a retrospective PO** or **decide to reject** the invoice.
- **Outcome A (resolved):** flow re-enters the normal path at **T4** (Purchasing confirmation), per Rita: *"confirmation from Purchasing, approval by the Manager, then scheduling for payment."*
- **Outcome B (rejected):**  **END (b): Invoice rejected.**

**E5 — Manager rejection or hold (triggered at G4; rare — "once every couple of months")**
- Trigger conditions: total amount significantly exceeds budget, or unexpected items appear.
- Manager either (a) **rejects** the invoice  presumed **END (b)** *(endpoint not explicitly described)*, or (b) asks AP to **hold and investigate**  treat as diversion into E4-style investigation.
