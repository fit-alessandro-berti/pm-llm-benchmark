**How the loops work in this net**

| Loop | Trigger | Consumes from | Produces to | Effect |
|------|---------|---------------|-------------|--------|
| **RDS** | `t_rds` fires after verification | `p_after_dv` (token just produced by `t_dv`) | `p_after_cr` (input to `t_dv`) | The claim must be verified again by `DV`. |
| **II** | `t_ii` fires after fraud check | `p_after_fc` (token just produced by `t_fc`) | `p_after_dv` (input to `t_fc`) | The claim undergoes another `FC`; `DV` is **not** repeated. |

Because both loops share places with the forward path, they are *optional* branches: at `p_after_dv` the token can either go straight to `FC` or take the `RDS` detour; at `p_after_fc` it can proceed to `CA` or enter the `II` cycle. Once the token finally reaches `p_final`, the process has reached the accepting final marking `fm`.
