This visualization depicts a **process model** (specifically a **C-net** or **Causal Net**, commonly used in process mining and business process management) that outlines the lifecycle of handling a request, from initial registration to final resolution.

---

### **1. Diagram Legend (Bottom-Right Box)**
The legend defines the control-flow semantics used at the split and join points of activities:
* **XOR-split / XOR-join:** Represented by individual black dots on separate arrows. Exactly one branch is taken/joined.
* **AND-split / AND-join:** Represented by black dots connected by a straight line across arrows. All branches are executed simultaneously or joined together.
* **OR-split / OR-join:** Represented by black dots connected by curved arcs. One or more combinations of branches can be chosen or joined.

---

### **2. Process Flow & Nodes**

The diagram consists of **9 activity nodes** (represented by rounded white boxes labeled with letters and text descriptions):

1. **`a` (register request) — Start Node:**
   * **Behavior:** The process begins here.
   * **Control Flow:** Has an **OR-split**, meaning after registering a request, one or more of the following activities can be triggered:
     * `b` (*examine thoroughly*)
     * `c` (*examine casually*)
     * `d` (*check ticket*)

2. **`b`, `c`, `d` (Parallel / Alternative Examination Tasks):**
   * **`b` (examine thoroughly):** Can receive input from `a` or `f` (XOR-join) and flows into `e`.
   * **`c` (examine casually):** Can receive input from `a` or `f` (XOR-join) and flows into `e`.
   * **`d` (check ticket):** Can receive input from `a` or `f` (XOR-join) and flows into `e`.

3. **`e` (decide) — Central Decision Point:**
   * **Incoming:** Uses an **OR-join** to consolidate completed paths from `b`, `c`, and `d`.
   * **Outgoing:** Leads to either an outcome (`g` or `h`) or a loop back through `f`.

4. **`f` (reinitiate request) — Feedback Loop:**
   * Triggered from `e` if the decision requires re-evaluating the request.
   * Uses an **OR-split** to send the process back to any combination of `b`, `c`, or `d`.

5. **`g`, `h`, `z` (Resolution & Termination):**
   * **`g` (pay compensation):** An outcome node triggered from `e`.
   * **`h` (reject request):** An alternative outcome node triggered from `e`.
   * **`z` (end) — Terminal Node:** An **XOR-join** that completes the process after either paying compensation (`g`) or rejecting the request (`h`).

---

### **3. Key Takeaway**
The visualization models a flexible workflow where a request is registered, evaluated through single or multiple examination steps, evaluated at a decision node, and either resolved (compensated/rejected) or looped back for reinitiation.
