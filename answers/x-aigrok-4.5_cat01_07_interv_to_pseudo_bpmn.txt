**BPMN-like Textual Process Model: Invoice Approval & Payment Scheduling**

**Process Overview**  
This model consolidates the multi-perspective accounts of the invoice approval process. It prioritizes the clearest consensus sequence while explicitly noting points of variation, partial conflict, or incomplete detail from the interviews. The process begins on invoice receipt and ends when the invoice is scheduled for payment. Actual fund transfer (payment run execution) is outside the described scope.

**Participants / Lanes**  
- **Accounts Payable (AP)**: Clerk (Mary), Supervisor (Rita)  
- **Purchasing**: Officer (Dan)  
- **Department Manager**: Carlos  
- **Supplier Relationship Manager (SRM)**: Karen (exception / resolution support only)  
- **External Participant**: Supplier  
- **System**: Accounting system (logging, matching, automated notifications)

**Key Data Objects / Artifacts**  
- Invoice (received typically by email)  
- Purchase Order (PO)  
- Goods/services receipt confirmation  
- Confirmation email (Purchasing  AP)  
- Contract (used by SRM for pricing/term checks)  
- Retrospective PO (exception-only)  
- Manager approval record / system notification  

**Main Process Flow (Happy Path + Gateways)**  

**Start Event**: Invoice received (usually by email)  

1. **Task** (Lane: AP Clerk): Check whether invoice contains a valid PO number.  
   - **Data Object**: Invoice, PO  

2. **Exclusive Gateway (XOR)**: “Valid PO number present?”  
   - **Yes path**  continue to Step 3  
   - **No path**  Exception Path A (Missing/Invalid PO)  

3. **Task** (AP Clerk): Log invoice into accounting system.  

4. **Task** (AP Clerk): Forward invoice to Purchasing Department requesting confirmation that goods/services were received.  

5. **Task** (Purchasing Officer): Open corresponding PO; compare quantities, prices, delivery charges, and confirm actual receipt of goods/services.  
   - **Data Object**: Invoice, PO, (optional) contract  

6. **Exclusive Gateway (XOR)**: “Invoice details match PO and goods/services received?”  
   - **Yes path**  continue to Step 7  
   - **No path**  Exception Path B (Discrepancy)  

7. **Task** (Purchasing Officer): Send confirmation email/notification back to AP Clerk.  

8. **Task** (AP Clerk): Perform final line-item matching of invoice against PO in the accounting system (after Purchasing confirmation).  

9. **Exclusive Gateway (XOR)** (optional second match check; Mary emphasizes this; may overlap with Step 6): “All line items now match and issues resolved?”  
   - **Yes path**  continue to Step 10  
   - **No path**  Exception Path B (Discrepancy) or escalate (see Escalation Path C)  

10. **Task** (Accounting System / AP): Trigger automatic email notification to Department Manager requesting approval.  
    - **Data Object**: Invoice details + PO + Purchasing confirmation  

11. **Task** (Department Manager): Review invoice, PO, confirmation, totals, and any special notes in the system.  

12. **Exclusive Gateway (XOR)**: “Manager approves?”  
    - **Yes path**  continue to Step 13  
    - **No / Suspect path**  Exception Path D (Manager Rejection / Investigation)  

13. **Task** (AP Clerk): Schedule invoice for payment / add to next payment cycle.  

**End Event**: Invoice scheduled for payment  

---

**Exception / Alternate Paths**  

**Exception Path A – Missing or Invalid PO** (triggered at Gateway 2 or mentioned by Dan/Rita)  
- AP Clerk returns invoice to Supplier requesting PO number / revised invoice.  
- Optionally loop in SRM (Karen) to contact supplier and obtain missing information quickly.  
- If invoice still lacks a PO (or relates to an informal/service arrangement with no prior PO):  
  - Escalate to AP Supervisor (Rita).  
  - AP Supervisor coordinates with Department Head(s) to either:  
    - Create a retrospective PO, or  
    - Reject the invoice.  
  - Once resolved (retrospective PO created), invoice re-enters the main flow at Step 3 (logging) or Step 4.  

**Exception Path B – Discrepancy (pricing, quantity, missing items, delivery charges, etc.)** (triggered at Gateways 6 or 9; described by Mary, Dan, Rita, Karen)  
- Purchasing Officer contacts Supplier directly **or** instructs AP Clerk to place invoice on hold while awaiting corrected invoice.  
- Concurrent/alternative: involve SRM (Karen) to clarify terms against contract and/or to negotiate with supplier.  
- AP Clerk may email Purchasing for further clarification.  
- If discrepancy cannot be resolved at clerk/officer level  Escalation Path C.  
- On receipt of corrected invoice or successful clarification: return to appropriate earlier step (usually Step 5 or Step 8) for re-verification (rework loop).  

**Escalation Path C – Unresolved Issues / Stuck Invoices** (Rita’s primary role; also referenced by Mary)  
- AP Clerk escalates to AP Supervisor when matching fails or Purchasing issues cannot be cleared.  
- Supervisor contacts suppliers, coordinates across departments, or involves SRM as needed.  
- After resolution, invoice is re-inserted into the normal flow (typically at Purchasing confirmation  Manager approval  payment scheduling).  
- Goal is prevention of indefinite holds.  

**Exception Path D – Manager Rejection or Investigation Request** (Carlos; low frequency – “once every couple of months”)  
- Department Manager rejects in system **or** requests AP to hold invoice and investigate further (budget variance, unexpected items, etc.).  
- Investigation typically handled by AP Clerk / Supervisor; may pull in Purchasing or SRM.  
- After investigation and any corrections: either re-present to Manager or follow Escalation Path C / rejection.  

---

**Additional Notes on Ambiguities & Variations**  
- **Order of matching**: Mary describes logging  Purchasing confirmation of receipt  then her line-item match  Manager. Dan describes a detailed match performed by Purchasing. The model treats Purchasing’s check as the primary confirmation and AP’s match as a subsequent system step; some overlap/possible reordering exists.  
- **Who first contacts supplier**: Can be Purchasing, AP, or SRM depending on the discrepancy type; SRM is optional support rather than a required lane.  
- **Rework loops**: Corrected invoices re-enter at the verification stage (Steps 5–8). No formal timeout or maximum-loop limit is described.  
- **Informal / non-PO invoices**: Handled exclusively via Supervisor (retrospective PO or reject); not part of the standard happy path.  
- **System automation**: Only the Manager notification is explicitly automatic; other handoffs are described as emails or system logging.  
- Final scheduling for payment is the consistent terminal activity after Manager approval; exact payment-run mechanics are not detailed.
