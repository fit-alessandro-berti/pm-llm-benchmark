### Cases that comply

- **Case 1** complies with the normal fulfillment path:  
  **Receive  Check inventory  Authorize payment  Package items  Ship order  Close order**

- **Case 4** complies with the cancellation path:  
  **Receive  Check inventory  Cancel order  Close order**

### Cases that violate the process

#### Case 2 — Packaging occurs before payment authorization

Sequence:

**Receive  Check inventory  Package items  Authorize payment  Ship  Close**

The violation is that **“Package items” occurs before “Authorize payment.”** The normative process requires payment authorization after inventory confirmation and before packaging:

> “Authorize payment” should occur after “Check inventory” and before packaging.

The case otherwise has the correct starting and ending activities, and shipping occurs after packaging, but the authorization and packaging steps are in the wrong order.

#### Case 3 — Invalid cancellation and shipping after cancellation

Sequence:

**Receive  Check inventory  Authorize payment  Cancel order  Ship order  Close**

This case violates the process in two ways:

1. **Cancellation occurs after payment authorization.**  
   Under the described branching logic, once inventory is checked, the process should either:
   - proceed with authorization and fulfillment if inventory is sufficient, or
   - proceed directly to cancellation if inventory is insufficient.

   Thus, in an inventory-shortage scenario, authorization should not occur before cancellation.

2. **Shipping occurs after cancellation.**  
   This is an explicit violation. The description states that after cancellation, fulfillment activities such as packaging and shipping must not continue, and the next activity should be **“Close order.”**

The shipping-after-cancellation violation is decisive even if one interprets the cancellation as being caused by some exceptional circumstance.

#### Case 5 — Shipping occurs without packaging

Sequence:

**Receive  Check inventory  Authorize payment  Ship order  Close**

The violation is that **“Ship order” occurs without a preceding “Package items” activity.** The normative sequence requires:

**Authorize payment  Package items  Ship order**

The description explicitly states that items must be properly packaged before they can be shipped. Although the case has the correct inventory check and payment authorization order, it omits the required packaging step.

### Summary

| Case | Assessment | Main issue |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compliant | Correct fulfillment sequence |
| 2 | Violation | Packaging before payment authorization |
| 3 | Violation | Cancellation after authorization and shipping after cancellation |
| 4 | Compliant | Correct cancellation sequence |
| 5 | Violation | Shipping without packaging |

Therefore, the violating cases are **Cases 2, 3, and 5**.
