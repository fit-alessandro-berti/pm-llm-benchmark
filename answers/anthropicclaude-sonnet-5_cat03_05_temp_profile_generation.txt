### Notes on Interpretation
- **Short, tightly-controlled intervals** (e.g., `PTPK`, `PKWS`) have small averages and small standard deviations, reflecting predictable internal handling processes.
- **Procurement-related intervals** (e.g., `OPRC`) have large averages and large standard deviations, since supplier lead times are inherently variable (customs delays, production backlogs, etc.).
- **Long-range pairs** (e.g., `SSAS`, `OPAS`) accumulate both the average durations and variances of all intermediate steps, resulting in large average times and large standard deviations — appropriate for detecting major bottlenecks or premature/delayed lifecycle completions.
- A deviation for a given execution would be flagged if the observed time between two activities falls outside `[AVG - ZETA*STDEV, AVG + ZETA*STDEV]`, which — given the wide variances on long-range pairs — allows the model to remain robust to normal process variability while still catching true anomalies (e.g., a shipment taking 90 days instead of the expected ~14).
