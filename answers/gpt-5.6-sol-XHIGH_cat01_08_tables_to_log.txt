### Assumptions

- `order_id` is the case identifier.
- Payment events were linked to orders through `Payments.invoice_id  Invoice.invoice_id  Invoice.order_id`.
- One **Item Picked** event was generated per order-line record rather than aggregating all picked items.
- Each delivery record generated separate **Order Dispatched** and **Order Arrived** events. Therefore, split shipments such as order `1002` produce repeated delivery activities.
- The salesperson was treated as the actor for **Order Created**, and the carrier as the responsible resource for dispatch and arrival.
- Payment actor information and invoice currency were not supplied, so they are marked as unavailable.
- Order `1004` has no delivery, invoice, or payment records. No missing events were synthesized; its trace ends with **Item Picked**.
