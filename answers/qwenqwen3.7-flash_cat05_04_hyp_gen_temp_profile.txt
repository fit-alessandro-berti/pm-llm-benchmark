#### 1. Anomaly: Suspiciously Short Duration Between Assign and Close
**Profile Data:** `('A', 'C'): (7200, 3600)`  
**Observation:** The average time from Assignment to Claim Close is only 2 hours, with a standard deviation of 1 hour. Given the intended process flow (R  A  E  P  N  C), a 2-hour closure suggests that intermediate steps such as Evaluation, Approval, and Notification may be missing or compressed unrealistically.

**Hypothesis:**
*   **Premature Closure:** Claims are being marked as "Close" immediately after assignment due to manual error or a workflow configuration allowing closures without downstream approvals.
*   **Data Quality Issues:** The `activity` column may contain incorrect codes (e.g., logging 'C' instead of 'E' or 'P'), or timestamps are backdated during bulk imports.
*   **Auto-Close Logic:** An automated script closes claims below a certain risk threshold or amount immediately upon assignment, bypassing human review.

**SQL Verification Query:**
This query identifies claims where the duration between Assign and Close is less than 2 hours and checks if required intermediate activities are present. It assumes `claim_events.resource` links to `adjusters.name`.

```sql
WITH claim_timings AS (
    SELECT 
        ce.claim_id,
        MAX(ce.timestamp) FILTER (WHERE ce.activity = 'A') AS t_assign,
        MAX(ce.timestamp) FILTER (WHERE ce.activity = 'C') AS t_close,
        COUNT(DISTINCT ce.activity) AS distinct_activities,
        STRING_AGG(DISTINCT ce.activity, ',' ORDER BY ce.timestamp) AS activity_sequence
    FROM claim_events ce
    GROUP BY ce.claim_id
)
SELECT 
    ct.claim_id,
    c.claim_amount,
    c.claim_type,
    adj.name AS assigned_adjuster,
    adj.region,
    EXTRACT(EPOCH FROM (ct.t_close - ct.t_assign)) AS duration_seconds,
    ct.distinct_activities,
    ct.activity_sequence
FROM claim_timings ct
JOIN claims c ON ct.claim_id = c.claim_id
LEFT JOIN claim_events first_event ON c.claim_id = first_event.claim_id AND first_event.activity = 'A'
LEFT JOIN adjusters adj ON first_event.resource = adj.name
WHERE ct.t_assign IS NOT NULL 
  AND ct.t_close IS NOT NULL
  AND EXTRACT(EPOCH FROM (ct.t_close - ct.t_assign)) < 7200;
```
*Verification Goal:* Inspect results for `distinct_activities` counts significantly lower than expected (e.g., < 6) or sequences missing 'E', 'P', 'N'. Correlate low durations with specific `claim_amount` ranges or `adjuster` regions.

*   **Automated Triggers:** An integration fires notifications automatically upon status change to 'Evaluate', independent of the Approval state.
*   **Process Deviation:** Claims are approved implicitly by the evaluator, and 'P' events are omitted from the logs despite business rules requiring explicit approval.

**SQL Verification Query:**
This query identifies claims where the gap between Evaluation and Notification is unusually short and verifies the presence or absence of the Approval activity.

```sql
SELECT 
    c.claim_id,
    c.customer_id,
    EXTRACT(EPOCH FROM (
        MAX(ce.timestamp) FILTER (WHERE ce.activity = 'N') - 
        MAX(ce.timestamp) FILTER (WHERE ce.activity = 'E')
```
