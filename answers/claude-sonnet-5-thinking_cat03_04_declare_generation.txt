### Explanation of Key Design Choices

| Constraint Type | Rationale |
|---|---|
| **init: IG** | Every process instance must start with Idea Generation. |
| **existence** | Core activities (IG, DD, TFC, CE, PC, LT, AG, MP, FL) must appear at least once — UT is optional since not every product may undergo user testing. |
| **response(IG,DD)  ...  response(MP,FL)** | Encodes the natural end-to-end process flow. |
| **precedence(DD,TFC), (TFC,AG), (AG,MP), (MP,FL)** | Ensures the correct ordering of design  feasibility  approval  marketing  launch. |
| **chainresponse/chainprecedence(AG,MP), (MP,FL)** | Marketing planning and launch tend to occur immediately after approval, reflecting tight coupling near the end of the process. |
| **coexistence(LT,UT)** | Lab and user testing are often correlated — if one is performed, so is the other. |
| **nonsuccession(FL,IG), (FL,DD)** | Once a product is launched, the process should not loop back to idea generation or design. |

This model captures the **sequential**, **existential**, and **negative** relationships that characterize a realistic product design and launch pipeline in a manufacturing context.
