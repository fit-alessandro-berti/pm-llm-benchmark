- “Initial Document Verification” vs. “Document Re-verification” – drawn directly from the timestamp order and the phrases “checks identity documents” / “re-checks \ldots now that ID scan is provided” / “Re-verify docs after account adjustment”.

- Activities that never recur with different meanings (Initiate Request, Submit Additional, Adjust Account, Approve Request) were left unchanged.
