### Accepted but invalid trace

```text
Receive_Order  Check_Inventory  Ship_Order  Package_Items
 Authorize_Payment  Close_Order
```

### Why the model accepts it

- `Receive_Order` and `Check_Inventory` occur first, as required by the root `SEQUENCE`.
- The `XOR` selects the successful-order branch.
- That branch uses `PARALLEL`, which requires payment authorization, packaging, and shipping to occur but places **no ordering constraint** among them. Therefore, `Ship_Order` may occur before `Package_Items` and `Authorize_Payment`.
- The root sequence then adds `Close_Order`.

### Why it is domain-invalid

An order cannot normally be shipped before its items have been packaged. Likewise, payment should be authorized before fulfillment and shipping. The expected successful path is:

```text
Authorize_Payment  Package_Items  Ship_Order
```

Thus, the successful branch should be a `SEQUENCE`, rather than a `PARALLEL` node, if this ordering is mandatory.

The model has an additional underfitting issue on the cancellation path: it accepts

```text
Receive_Order  Check_Inventory  Cancel_Order
 Close_Order  Close_Order
```

because `Close_Order` appears both inside `cancel_subtree` and after the `XOR` in the root sequence. An order should ordinarily be closed only once.
