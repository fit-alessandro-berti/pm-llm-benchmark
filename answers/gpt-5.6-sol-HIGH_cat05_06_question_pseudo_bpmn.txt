17. How should inventory be allocated among regional warehouses, retailers, e-commerce channels, and VIP or pre-order customers during supply shortages?

18. Which performance indicators best measure end-to-end effectiveness, including supplier reliability, cycle time, defect rates, logistics costs, customs delays, inventory turnover, forecast accuracy, and customer satisfaction?

19. How should real-time sales, inventory, shipment, and market data be incorporated into forecasting and supplier-order adjustments?
