**Justification of naming choices**

- **Verify Documents** appears twice in C001 and twice in C003.  
  - First occurrence is always the initial identity/document check (online or physical). Renamed to *Initial Document Verification* (or *Physical Document Verification* when the context explicitly notes in-person/branch verification).  
  - Second occurrence is always a subsequent check performed after new information has arrived or an internal change has been made. Renamed to *Re-verify Documents After Additional Submission* (C001) or *Re-verify Documents After Account Adjustment* (C003) to capture the dependency on the preceding event.

- **Call Customer** appears once in C001, twice in C002, and once in C003, each time with a distinct purpose visible in the “Additional Context” column and the surrounding process steps.  
  - C001: purpose is to obtain missing documents → *Call Customer for Missing Documents*.  
  - C002 first call: purpose is to investigate an account discrepancy → *Call Customer to Clarify Account Discrepancy*.  
  - C002 second call: purpose is confirmation after an adjustment → *Follow-up Call to Confirm Account Adjustments*.  
  - C003: purpose is to bring the customer back to the branch → *Call Customer to Return to Branch*.

These renamings make the different semantic roles of the repeated activity labels explicit while remaining faithful to the timestamps, resources, and free-text context supplied in the original log.
