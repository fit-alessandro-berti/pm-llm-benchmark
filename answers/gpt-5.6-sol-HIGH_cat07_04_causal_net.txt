The visualization is a directed workflow for handling a request:

- **a — register request** starts the process.
- The request can proceed to one or more review activities:
  - **b — examine thoroughly**
  - **c — examine casually**
  - **d — check ticket**
- These paths converge at **e — decide**.
- **f — reinitiate request** forms a feedback/rework loop, returning the request to earlier examination or checking steps.
- From **e**, the process branches to:
  - **g — pay compensation**, or
  - **h — reject request**
- Both outcomes lead to **z — end**.

Rounded rectangles represent activities, arrows indicate flow direction, and black dots with connecting curves encode branching and synchronization rules. A gray legend in the lower-right illustrates the notation for **XOR, AND, and OR splits**, along with their corresponding **joins**.
