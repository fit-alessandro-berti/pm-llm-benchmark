### Standard Hire-to-Retire logic

A typical sequence is:

1. Post the job advertisement  
2. Screen candidates  
3. Conduct interviews  
4. Make the hiring decision  
5. Onboard the selected employee  
6. Add the employee to payroll  
7. Close the case  

Some activities may occur in parallel—for example, screening and interviews may overlap—but a hiring decision should normally follow the evaluation activities, and onboarding and payroll should occur after the decision.

## Model 1

The main ordering constraints are:

- `Post_Job_Ad  Screen_Candidates`
- `Screen_Candidates  Make_Hiring_Decision`
- `Screen_Candidates  Conduct_Interviews`
- `Make_Hiring_Decision  Onboard_Employee`
- `Onboard_Employee  Add_to_Payroll  Close_Case`

### Anomalies

The major anomaly is that `Conduct_Interviews` has no ordering relation to `Make_Hiring_Decision`. Both activities follow screening, but the model does not require the interviews to occur before the hiring decision. Consequently, the decision and interviews may occur in either order, or potentially in parallel.

This is a significant logical weakness because a hiring decision could be made before interviews are completed. The model also does not explicitly require interviews to occur before onboarding, payroll, or case closure. Although the partial-order structure may permit a sensible execution, it does not enforce the normal recruitment logic.

A smaller issue is that the model allows screening and interviewing to proceed in parallel. That is not inherently incorrect, since interviews can sometimes begin while candidate screening continues, but the missing dependency between interviews and the decision is problematic.

## Model 2

The ordering is:

- `Post_Job_Ad  Screen_Candidates`
- `Post_Job_Ad  Conduct_Interviews`
- `Conduct_Interviews  Make_Hiring_Decision`
- `Make_Hiring_Decision  loop_onboarding`
- `loop_onboarding  xor_payroll`
- `xor_payroll  Close_Case`

### Anomalies

The model has several more serious deviations:

1. **Screening is not required before interviewing or deciding.**  
   Screening follows the job posting, but it has no relation to interviews or the hiring decision. Thus, interviews and the decision may occur without completed screening.

2. **Onboarding is modeled as a loop.**  
   `Operator.LOOP` with `Onboard_Employee` and a silent transition allows onboarding to be repeated. Normally, onboarding is a one-time activity, even if it contains multiple steps. Repeated onboarding is therefore unusual and can produce duplicate or inconsistent employee records.

3. **Payroll is optional.**  
   The XOR between `Add_to_Payroll` and a silent transition permits the process to close without adding the employee to payroll. This is a fundamental violation of the Hire-to-Retire process: a hired employee generally must be registered for payroll before the case is closed.

4. **The model does not explicitly require screening to contribute to the decision.**  
   Although screening exists, it can be bypassed in the effective control flow because it is not connected to the interview or decision dependencies.

## Comparison and conclusion

**Model 1 is closer to a normative Hire-to-Retire process.** It contains the expected activities and enforces the important post-decision sequence:

`Decision  Onboarding  Payroll  Closure`.

Its primary defect is an incomplete ordering constraint: interviews are not required to finish before the hiring decision. This is a serious modeling omission, but the model still guarantees onboarding, payroll, and closure after the decision.

Model 2 has more severe integrity problems. It permits the hiring decision without completed screening, allows onboarding to repeat, and—most importantly—allows the process to close without adding the employee to payroll. The optional payroll step undermines the core purpose of the workflow and makes Model 2 substantially less compliant with a normative process.

A better version of Model 1 would add:

```python
model1.order.add_edge(Interview, Decide)
```

This would enforce the usual evaluation sequence:

`Post  Screen  Interview  Decide  Onboard  Payroll  Close`.

Alternatively, if screening and interviews are intentionally parallel, both should still precede the decision.
