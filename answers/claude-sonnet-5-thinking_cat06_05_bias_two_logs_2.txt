# Bias Analysis: Event Log Comparison

## Which Log Exhibits Bias?

**Group B exhibits clear, documented bias** through its `ScoreAdjustment` field, which grants a **+5 CulturalFit boost** to candidates affiliated with the "LocalBusinessLeadersClub."

## How the Bias Manifests

### 1. **Direct Score Manipulation Based on Non-Merit Criteria**
- **U001**: Raw CulturalFit = 60  Adjusted to 65 (+5 boost)
- **U003**: Raw CulturalFit = 58  Adjusted to 63 (+5 boost)
- **U002** (no association): No adjustment applied

The boost is tied to `ProfessionalAssociation = LocalBusinessLeadersClub`, not to any measured competency. This is a proxy variable — membership in this club likely correlates with `LocalResident = TRUE` and pre-existing social/economic networks, potentially serving as an indirect discriminator against outsiders (non-local, non-connected candidates).

### 2. **Outcome Comparison: Similar Candidates, Different Treatment**

| Case | Group | SkillScore | PersonalityScore | Raw CulturalFit | Adjusted CulturalFit | Decision |
|------|-------|-----------|-------------------|------------------|----------------------|----------|
| P003 | A | 82 | 68 | 65 | **65 (no boost)** | Hired |
| U003 | B | 75 | 65 | 58 | **63 (boosted)** | Hired |

U003 has **lower raw scores across every metric** than P003, yet receives an artificial boost that pushes their CulturalFit score close to parity. Without the +5 adjustment, U003's CulturalFit (58) would be notably lower and might have resulted in "Not Hired," consistent with U002's outcome (CulturalFit=60, Not Hired).

This suggests the boost may be **decision-altering** — artificially inflating borderline candidates from the "in-group" into the "Hired" category.

### 3. **Group A as the Counterfactual Baseline**
Group A has **no `ProfessionalAssociation` boosts** — all `ScoreAdjustment = 0` regardless of scores. Hiring decisions correlate cleanly with raw performance (P001: 65 CulturalFit  Hired; P002: 60  Not Hired; P003: 65  Hired). This makes Group A the fair/unbiased baseline for comparison.

## Fairness & Equity Implications

1. **Disparate Impact via Proxy Discrimination**: `ProfessionalAssociation` (LocalBusinessLeadersClub) acts as a proxy for `LocalResident` status, systematically advantaging local candidates and disadvantaging external/non-local applicants — even though `LocalResident` itself isn't directly used in scoring.

2. **Undermines Merit-Based Evaluation**: The SkillScore and PersonalityScore — presumably valid, job-relevant metrics — are diluted by an unrelated social-network variable, violating the principle that hiring should be based on job-relevant qualifications.

3. **Auditability Red Flag**: The explicit `ScoreAdjustment` field with a documented justification ("Association Boost") is a smoking gun for algorithmic/process auditors — it's a traceable, quantifiable form of favoritism embedded directly into the process logs, making it legally and ethically indefensible if challenged (e.g., under anti-discrimination or fair hiring regulations).

4. **Potential Legal Exposure**: If `LocalBusinessLeadersClub` membership correlates with protected characteristics (race, national origin, socioeconomic status) or excludes protected groups disproportionately, this constitutes **indirect discrimination**, even if unintentional.

## Conclusion

Group B's process is biased because it **explicitly and traceably modifies a candidate evaluation score based on club affiliation**, an attribute unrelated to job performance. This creates an uneven playing field where connected/local candidates receive an artificial advantage that can flip hiring outcomes, whereas Group A demonstrates a fair process where identical evaluation criteria are applied uniformly regardless of external affiliations.
