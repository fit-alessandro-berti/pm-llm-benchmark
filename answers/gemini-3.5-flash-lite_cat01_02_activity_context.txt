### 1. Interpretation of Recurring Activities

By analyzing the event log, we can see that several generic activity names are reused across different stages of the support tickets (cases). Depending on the context, resources involved, and their sequence in the workflow, their specific meanings differ:

*   **`Initiate Request`**: Always occurs at the start, but originates via different channels (WebPortal vs. BranchReception). While the functional step is the same, distinguishing the entry channel provides better process clarity.
*   **`Verify Documents`**: Appears multiple times per case. 
    *   *First occurrence:* Initial identity and document check performed right after the request is initiated.
    *   *Later occurrences:* Re-verification or follow-up document checks conducted after missing documents are provided or internal account adjustments are made.
*   **`Call Customer`**: Appears multiple times with very different operational intents (e.g., requesting missing documents, clarifying account discrepancies, or recalling a walk-in customer who stepped away).
*   **`Adjust Account`**: Occurs in cases involving discrepancies or missing information, representing internal system corrections.
*   **`Approve Request`**: Always the final step, representing supervisor sign-off. (Can remain as-is, or be specified as `Final Supervisor Approval`).

---

### 2. Activity Renaming Mapping

To disambiguate the process, the recurring activities are renamed as follows:

*   **Initiate Request** 
    *   *Initial digital submission:* `Initiate Online Request`
    *   *In-person visit:* `Initiate Walk-In Request`
*   **Verify Documents**
    *   *First check:* `Initial Document Verification`
    *   *Subsequent checks:* `Post-Correction Document Re-Verification`
*   **Call Customer**
    *   *For missing docs:* `Call Customer for Missing Documents`
    *   *For discrepancies:* `Call Customer for Discrepancy Clarification`
    *   *For branch recall:* `Call Customer to Return to Branch`
*   **Adjust Account**
    *   `Perform Internal Account Adjustment`
*   **Approve Request**
    *   `Grant Final Supervisor Approval`

### 4. Justification of Naming Choices

*   **Initiance (`Initiate Online Request` vs. `Initiate Walk-In Request`)**: Differentiating based on the resource (`WebPortal` vs. `BranchReception`) clarifies customer acquisition channels, helping operations analyze digital vs. physical branch loads.
*   **Verification (`Initial` vs. `Post-Correction... Re-Verification`)**: The first verification happens immediately post-initiation as a baseline check. Later occurrences happen *after* an external input (document upload) or internal change (`Adjust Account`), serving as a gate before final approval. 
*   **Communication (`Call Customer for...`)**: The generic `Call Customer` masks very different business reasons. Specifying whether the call is for *missing documents*, *discrepancy clarification*, or *branch recall* enables better workload categorization (e.g., separating document chasing from complex exception handling).
*   **Adjustments & Approvals**: `Perform Internal Account Adjustment` highlights that system-level fixes are happening behind the scenes, while `Grant Final Supervisor Approval` marks the distinct concluding administrative milestone.
