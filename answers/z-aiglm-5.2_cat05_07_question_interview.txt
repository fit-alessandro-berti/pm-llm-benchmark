Here is a list of targeted, open-ended questions to ask the interviewee to clarify and deepen your conceptual understanding of their property onboarding process:

**Clarifying Roles and Responsibilities**
*   Who is ultimately responsible for deciding which property manager is assigned, and who has the authority to reassign a manager if there is a personality clash or disagreement with the landlord during the introductory meeting?
*   When the inspector finds issues requiring repairs, who is responsible for sourcing and managing the contractors? Does the property manager have a pre-approved budget for this, or must the landlord authorize all repair expenses?
*   During the final quality checks, do the compliance officer, financial analyst, and IT department review every single property and lease, or are they only triggered for properties flagged as high-risk or unusually complex?
*   Who holds the final authority to "activate" the property in the rental portal once marketing materials are ready?

**Understanding Decision Criteria**
*   When the inspector finds issues, what determines whether the onboarding process is completely paused versus allowing the marketing team to proceed with a conditional listing?
*   Regarding tenant screening, what are the specific "dealbreakers" that would automatically disqualify an applicant (e.g., specific credit score thresholds, eviction history)? 
*   When multiple tenants are interested, how do you weigh factors like a longer lease commitment against a higher offered rental price or short-term promotional discounts?
*   How does the financial analyst evaluate whether a negotiated rental price—perhaps lowered due to promotional discounts—still meets the company's profitability targets?

**Verifying Timing and Sequencing**
*   Does the property manager wait until after the introductory meeting with the landlord to schedule the photographer and inspector, or are these scheduled concurrently to speed up the onboarding timeline?
*   At what exact point do the compliance, financial, and IT audits take place? Do they happen concurrently after the lease is finalized, and does the tenant's actual move-in date depend on their sign-off?
*   What is the typical expected turnaround time from the initial landlord contact to the property being activated in the rental portal? 
*   If the landlord delays in providing missing documentation or completing required repairs, is there a formal timeframe after which the onboarding process is paused or cancelled?

**Exploring Exception Handling**
*   You mentioned properties requiring special insurance or landlords insisting on custom lease clauses. What is the workflow for vetting these custom clauses, and who has the authority to approve or reject them?
*   If a property passes inspection and is activated, but sits on the market for an extended period without receiving suitable tenant applications, what is the process for re-evaluating the pricing strategy or marketing approach?
*   How does the process change, if at all, when onboarding a multi-unit building compared to a single-family home? Are there different stakeholders or sequential steps required for larger portfolios?
*   If a tenant backs out after the lease is finalized but before the move-in date, what is the procedure for reactivating the property and handling the security deposit or lost revenue?
