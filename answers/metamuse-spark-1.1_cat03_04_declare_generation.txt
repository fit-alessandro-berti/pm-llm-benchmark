In `pm4py` a `DECLARE` model is a `dict` with 18 templates. Unary templates use `activity` as key, binary templates use `(antecedent, consequent)` as key.

### Logic behind the constraints:

* **Flow:** `IG -> DD -> {TFC, CE} -> PC -> LT -> UT -> AG -> MP -> FL`
* **Init / Exactly One:** Process always starts with `IG` and ends exactly once with `FL` and `AG`.
* **Response / Precedence / Succession:** Ensures the natural order; e.g., `PC` needs both `TFC` and `CE` before, `AG` needs `LT` and `UT` before.
* **Coexistence:** Engineering and finance checks coexist, lab and user tests coexist.
* **Chain / Alt templates:** Stronger ordering for core handoffs `IG->DD` and `PC->LT`.
* **NonSuccession:** Prevents loops after final launch and after approval gate, which is typical for a linear launch process.
