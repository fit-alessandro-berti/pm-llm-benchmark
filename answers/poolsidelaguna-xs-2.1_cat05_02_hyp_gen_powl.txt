#### a. **Loop Structure Between Evaluation (E) and Approval (P):**  
The loop `(E, P)` implies that after evaluating a claim, the system may approve it, then loop back to evaluate again. This suggests a non-standard process where approvals could be followed by re-evaluations, which is unusual unless there is a specific need to reprocess claims after approval (e.g., corrections). In a typical workflow, evaluation should precede approval once, not in a loop.

#### b. **XOR Between Notification (N) and Skip:**  
The `XOR(N, skip)` allows the process to skip notifying the customer before closing the claim. This could lead to customer dissatisfaction if notifications are omitted, violating standard practices where customers should always be informed of claim closure unless explicitly designed otherwise.

#### c. **Premature Closure via Partial Order:**  
The partial order edges `A  C` (Assign Adjuster  Close Claim) and missing edge `xor  C` create a path where the claim can be closed before the loop (E/P) and notification steps are completed.
