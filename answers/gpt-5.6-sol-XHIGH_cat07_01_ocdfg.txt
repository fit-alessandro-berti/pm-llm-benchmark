The visualization is a multi-object process-flow map showing how five business object types move through procurement, order fulfillment, invoicing, and returns.

### Visual encoding
- **Cross-hatched boxes at the top:** object types and populations.
- **Ovals:** start and end points.
- **Rounded rectangles:** activities, with an **“Overall”** occurrence count.
- **Colored dots and connectors:** the object types participating in each activity.
- **Arrow labels:** transition times in milliseconds.
- Colors are inferred as:
  - **Purple:** PurchaseRequisitionItem
  - **Blue:** PurchaseOrderItem
  - **Orange:** SalesOrderItem
  - **Green:** VendorInvoiceItem
  - **Red:** ReturnOrderItem

### Main flows
- **Purchase requisitions** begin with **Create Requisition** and move to the central **Approve Requisition** activity, then branch toward **Send Order** or **Check Stock**, with some feedback loops.
- **Purchase orders** and **sales orders** converge at **Create Order**. Purchase-order traffic then connects into approval, ordering, payment, and invoicing activities.
- The **sales-order fulfillment path** is the clearest linear sequence:  
  **Create Order  Pack Goods  Ship Goods  Confirm Delivery  Close Order  End**, with transition times of roughly **180–300 ms**.
- **Return orders** enter the approval/return-processing area and can proceed from **Process Return** to **Close Order**.
- **Vendor invoice items** feed into the central approval network and ultimately connect to **Create Invoice**.
