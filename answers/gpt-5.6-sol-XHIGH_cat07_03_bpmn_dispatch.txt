The visualization is a **BPMN swimlane process diagram** titled **“Dispatch of goods – Computer Hardware Shop.”** It shows responsibilities across three lanes: **Logistics**, **Secretary**, and **Warehouse**.

### Process flow

1. **Start: “Ship goods”**
   - A parallel gateway splits the work into two concurrent branches:
     - Clarifying the shipment method.
     - Packaging the goods.

2. **Shipment arrangements — Secretary/Logistics**
   - The secretary performs **“Clarify shipment method.”**
   - An exclusive decision asks **“Special handling?”**
     - **Yes:** Get three offers from logistics companies, then select a company and place the order.
     - **No:** Check whether insurance is required.
       - **Always:** Write the package label.
       - **If necessary:** Logistics performs **“Insure parcel.”**
       - These insurance/label activities are recombined.
   - Both shipment-method routes then merge.

3. **Packaging — Warehouse**
   - The warehouse performs **“Package goods.”**
   - A **48-hour boundary timer** can trigger **“Notify customer about delay.”**
   - The normal packaging and delay-notification paths rejoin.

4. **Completion**
   - A parallel gateway synchronizes the shipment-arrangement branch with the packaging branch.
   - The warehouse then:
     - Fills out shipment paperwork.
     - Moves the package to the pickup area.
   - The process ends at **“Shipment prepared.”**

The diagram also includes an **Order details** document and a **Customer information** data store associated with checking insurance requirements.
