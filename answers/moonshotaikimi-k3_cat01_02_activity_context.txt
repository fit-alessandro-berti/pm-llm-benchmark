## 4. Justification of Naming Choices

- **Verify Documents – Initial Identity Check:** Always occurs immediately after `Initiate Request`, before any new information enters the case. The context ("checks identity documents," "Identity verification," "Physical verification of ID") confirms it is the standard intake verification — same role across all three cases, so it receives one shared name.
- **Verify Documents – Re-Check After Additional Documents (C001):** Directly follows `Submit Additional` (the ID scan upload). The context explicitly says "re-checks documents now that ID scan is provided" — it closes the document-rework loop, which is a different decision point (completeness confirmation) than the initial check.
- **Verify Documents – Re-Verification After Account Adjustment (C003):** Follows `Adjust Account`, not a document submission. The context "Re-verify docs after account adjustment" shows the trigger is the internal correction, so the qualifier references the adjustment rather than new documents.
- **Call Customer – Request Missing Documents (C001):** Triggered by failed verification ("Verification incomplete"); its purpose is to *initiate* the rework loop.
- **Call Customer – Clarify Account Discrepancy (C002, 1st):** Precedes `Adjust Account`; the call is diagnostic — it gathers the information that enables the adjustment.
- **Call Customer – Confirm Adjustments (Follow-Up) (C002, 2nd):** Follows `Adjust Account`; per the context it is confirmatory ("confirm adjustments made"), closing the loop opened by the first call. Naming both calls identically would falsely suggest a simple retry of the same step.
- **Call Customer – Recall Customer to Branch (C003):** Distinct physical-context purpose (customer left the branch), reflected in the `BranchOfficer` resource and the "have them return" context — neither diagnostic nor confirmatory.

**Unchanged activities:** `Initiate Request`, `Submit Additional`, `Adjust Account`, and `Approve Request` occur at most once per case with consistent semantics, so renaming would add noise rather than clarity. *(Optional refinement: if channel matters analytically, `Initiate Request` could be tagged "– Online Portal" vs. "– Branch Walk-In," since the resource and context differ — but this is a cross-case channel distinction, not a within-case recurrence.)*

**Process-mining benefit:** the renamed log now distinguishes *initial* activities from *rework* activities, so a discovered process model will correctly show loops (Initial Check  rework trigger  Re-Check) instead of ambiguous self-loops on a single "Verify Documents" node.
