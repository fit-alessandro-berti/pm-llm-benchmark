### Matching approach

- Used `order_id` as the required business key.
- Treated timestamps within **2 seconds** as candidates for the same event.
- Normalized equivalent event names:
  - `Order Received`  `OrderReceived`
  - `Order Validated`  `OrderValidation`
  - `Item Shipped`  `Shipping`
- Kept `Payment Processed` and `PaymentCheck` separate because, although related and close in time, the names imply different activities and their timestamps differ by **5 seconds**, outside the stated 2-second tolerance.
- Kept `Quality Check` as a System B-only event because no corresponding System A event exists.
- Kept `Item Delivered` as a System A-only event because no corresponding System B event exists.
- Chose Log A’s timestamp as `primary_timestamp` for merged events because System A is the primary timeline; retained both source timestamps for traceability.
