## Modeling logic

- **Case identification:** Cases are artifact-centered:
  - `C-DOCUMENT1` for the editing session on `Document1.docx`
  - `C-ANNUAL-MEETING-EMAIL` for handling the annual meeting email
  - `C-REPORT-DRAFT-PDF` for reviewing `Report_Draft.pdf`
  - `C-BUDGET-2024` for updating `Budget_2024.xlsx`
  - `C-QUARTERLY-REPORT` for editing `Quarterly_Report.docx`
- **Repeated document interaction:** The two editing bursts on `Document1.docx` are kept in the same case because the filename remains unchanged, even though the user temporarily worked on other artifacts.
- **Activity abstraction:** Low-level actions were converted into business-readable activities. For example, `TYPING` became “Edit document,” “Update workbook,” or “Compose email reply,” depending on context.
- **Navigation handling:** `FOCUS` and destination-side `SWITCH` events were represented as “Activate artifact.” This preserves the movement between work items without creating a separate, less useful session case.
- **Incomplete traces:** The PDF and Excel cases do not contain explicit close events because none were present in the source log.
