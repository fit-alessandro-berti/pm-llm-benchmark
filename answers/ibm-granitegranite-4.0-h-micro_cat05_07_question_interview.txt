9. **Quality Checks and Audits:**
   - Describe the role of the compliance officer and financial analyst in the final stages of onboarding. Can you provide examples of common issues that have been identified during these audits and how they were resolved?

10. **Handling Exceptions and Special Cases:**

11. **Communication and Tracking:**
