### 1. Recurring activities and contextual differences

The activities that recur within individual cases are:

- **Verify Documents**
  - First occurrence: initial identity/document validation.
  - Later occurrence: re-verification after missing documents were supplied or account details were corrected.

- **Call Customer**
  - C001: call requesting missing documentation.
  - C002: first call clarifies an account discrepancy; second call confirms the completed adjustment.
  - C003: call asks a walk-in customer to return to the branch.

Other activity labels do not recur within the same case, although some have context-specific variation across cases. The renamed log below also makes these roles more explicit where useful.

### 4. Justification for the naming choices

- **Initial vs. repeat verification**
  - The first `Verify Documents` event in each case is an initial screening activity, so it is named **Perform Initial Document/Identity Verification**.
  - The later verification events are not equivalent repeats:
    - In **C001**, verification follows the upload of a missing ID scan, so it becomes **Re-verify Documents After Customer Submission**.
    - In **C003**, verification follows an internal account correction, so it becomes **Re-verify Documents After Account Correction**.

- **Different purposes of customer calls**
  - In **C001**, the call is a document-remediation step: **Request Missing Documents by Phone**.
  - In **C002**, the first call investigates a problem: **Clarify Account Discrepancy by Phone**; the second call closes the loop after remediation: **Confirm Account Adjustment by Phone**.
  - In **C003**, the call is logistical rather than investigative: **Request Customer Return to Branch by Phone**.

- **Account adjustments**
  - The generic `Adjust Account` label was made more specific:
    - **Correct Account Details** in C002, because the adjustment resolves a discrepancy discussed with the customer.
    - **Correct Account Information** in C003, because the correction addresses missing information before re-verification.

- **Request initiation**
  - Online submissions are labeled **Submit Online Request**, while the branch-originated case is labeled **Initiate Branch Walk-in Request**, distinguishing the intake channel.

These renamed activities preserve the process sequence while making the purpose of repeated steps explicit for analysis, process mining, and reporting.
