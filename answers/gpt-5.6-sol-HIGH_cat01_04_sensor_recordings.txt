## Main pattern indicators

- **Cutting:** sustained material feed, increasing tool position, high vibration, and rising energy.
- **Assembly:** higher pressure with lower vibration and a mostly stationary tool, suggesting clamping or pressing.
- **Welding:** a brief and extreme temperature and energy spike with no material feed.
- **Inspection:** near-static sensor conditions immediately after the joining operation.
- **Packaging:** low-intensity movement and small material flow at a final tool position.
- **Idle:** ambient temperature, zero flow and vibration, home tool position, and baseline energy.

These labels are inferred rather than directly observed. Events **9** and **10** are single-snapshot phases, so “Welding” and especially “Quality Inspection” have less statistical support than the longer cutting or idle segments, but their position and sensor signatures make those labels the most plausible.
