### BPMN-Like Textual Process Model: Invoice Approval Workflow

Below is a structured textual representation of the process derived from the interview transcripts. This model consolidates accounts from multiple stakeholders, resolves conflicts where possible, highlights exceptions, and notes areas of ambiguity. A process analyst can use this description to construct a visual BPMN diagram directly.

---

#### 1. Process Overview
*   **Process Name:** Invoice Approval & Payment Scheduling
*   **Primary Goal:** Validate incoming invoices against purchase orders, resolve discrepancies, secure managerial approval, and schedule payment.
*   **Trigger:** Receipt of an invoice via email.
*   **End State:** Invoice scheduled for payment OR Invoice Rejected.

#### 2. Roles and Participants (Swimlanes)
| Role | Responsibility Summary |
| :--- | :--- |
| **AP Clerk** | Initial validation, logging, system matching, escalation coordination, payment scheduling. |
| **Purchasing Officer** | Verification of goods/services receipt, price/quantity alignment with PO, issuing confirmation. |
| **Department Manager** | Final financial approval based on budget and item validity. |
| **AP Supervisor** | Exception handling, resolving unresolvable discrepancies, retrospective PO creation. |
| **Supplier Relationship Mgr** | External liaison for contract terms, pricing disputes, and supplier communication. |
| **Supplier** | External entity providing invoices, PO numbers, or corrected documentation. |
| **Accounting System** | Automated artifact storage, matching engine, notification generator. |

#### 3. Artifacts
*   **Invoice:** Incoming document (usually email attachment).
*   **Purchase Order (PO):** Reference document defining ordered quantities, prices, and terms.
*   **Retrospective PO:** PO created post-facto for informal service contracts.
*   **System Record:** Digital entry in the accounting system.
*   **Confirmation Status:** Indicator sent by Purchasing confirming receipt/match.
*   **Approval Notification:** System-generated request for manager sign-off.

---

#### 4. Process Flow Description

**Pool: Invoice Approval Process**

##### Lane: Accounts Payable (Clerk)
1.  **[Start Event]** Invoice received via email.
2.  **[Task]** **Validate PO Existence.**
    *   *Check:* Does the invoice contain a valid Purchase Order number?
    *   *Input:* Invoice.
    *   *Output:* Decision on PO presence.
3.  **[Exclusive Gateway]** **PO Present?**
    *   **Path NO:**
        *    **[Task]** **Request Missing Details.** Send email to Supplier requesting PO number or additional details.
        *    *[Loop]* Wait for Supplier response. If PO provided, return to Step 2. If not provided after retries, proceed to **[Task]** **Escalate to Supervisor** (for potential rejection or retrospective PO).
    *   **Path YES:**
        *    **[Task]** **Log Invoice.** Enter invoice details into Accounting System.
        *    **[Task]** **Route for Verification.** Forward invoice data to Purchasing Officer.
        *    **[Intermediate Message Event]** Waiting for Purchasing Confirmation.

##### Lane: Purchasing Officer
4.  **[Task]** **Verify Invoice vs. PO.**
    *   *Action:* Compare invoice quantities, prices, and delivery charges against the PO. Confirm goods/services were received.
    *   *Input:* Invoice, PO.
    *   *Output:* Verification result.
5.  **[Exclusive Gateway]** **Discrepancy Found?**
    *   **Path YES:**
        *    **[Task]** **Attempt Resolution.**
            *   *Options:* Contact Supplier directly OR contact AP Clerk to hold invoice pending correction.
            *   *Context:* May involve **Supplier Relationship Manager** to check contract terms/pricing (Karen's role).
        *    **[Exclusive Gateway]** **Resolved?**
            *   **Path NO:**
                *    **[Task]** **Escalate to Supervisor.** Notify AP Supervisor of unresolvable issue.
                *    *[Cross-Lane]* Supervisor intervention occurs (see Lane: AP Supervisor). Upon resolution, return to **[Task]** **Verify Invoice vs. PO**.
            *   **Path YES:**
                *    **[Task]** **Issue Confirmation.** Send confirmation email/status update to AP Clerk.
                *    *[Cross-Lane]* Proceed to Lane: AP Clerk.
    *   **Path NO:**
        *    **[Task]** **Issue Confirmation.** Send confirmation email/status update to AP Clerk.
        *    *[Cross-Lane]* Proceed to Lane: AP Clerk.

##### Lane: Accounts Payable (Clerk)
6.  **[Task]** **System Matching.**
    *   *Action:* Match invoice line items against PO within the Accounting System.
    *   *Note:* Interviews suggest a dual-check: Manual verification by Purchasing followed by System matching by AP.
7.  **[Exclusive Gateway]** **System Match Successful?**
    *   **Path NO:**
        *    **[Task]** **Query/Escalate Discrepancy.**
            *   Email Purchasing team for clarification.
            *   If unresolved, escalate to **AP Supervisor**.
        *    *[Loop]* Return to resolution steps.
    *   **Path YES:**
        *    **[Task]** **Trigger Manager Approval.**
            *   *Condition:* Requires Purchasing Confirmation AND System Match Success.
            *   *Action:* System automatically sends email/notification to Department Manager.
        *    **[Intermediate Message Event]** Waiting for Manager Decision.

##### Lane: Department Manager
8.  **[Task]** **Review & Approve.**
    *   *Action:* Review invoice details, PO, and confirmation in system. Check for budget variances or suspicious items.
9.  **[Exclusive Gateway]** **Manager Decision?**
    *   **Path REJECT / HOLD:**
        *    **[Task]** **Investigate / Reject.**
            *   *Action:* Ask AP Clerk to hold and investigate further OR formally reject invoice.
            *   *If Hold:* Return to AP Clerk for re-investigation (Loop to System Matching or Purchasing Verification depending on root cause).
            *   *If Reject:* Proceed to **[End Event]** Invoice Rejected.
    *   **Path APPROVE:**
        *    **[Task]** **Sign Off.** Click approve in system.
        *    *[Cross-Lane]* Proceed to Lane: AP Clerk.

##### Lane: Accounts Payable (Clerk)
10. **[Task]** **Schedule Payment.**
    *   *Action:* Add approved invoice to next payment run/cycle.
11. **[End Event]** Payment Scheduled.

##### Lane: AP Supervisor
*   *Note: This lane is activated via Escalation calls from AP Clerk or Purchasing.*
12. **[Task]** **Exception Resolution.**
    *   *Triggers:* Unresolvable discrepancies, missing information, invoices lacking POs for informal services.
    *   *Actions:*
        *   Coordinate between departments/suppliers.
        *   Contact Suppliers directly.
        *   **Special Case:** For invoices without POs due to informal services, work with Department Heads to create a **Retrospective PO** OR decide on rejection.
13. **[Task]** **Return to Normal Flow.**
    *   Once exception is cleared, route invoice back to the appropriate step (typically Purchasing Verification or AP System Matching).

---

#### 5. Exceptions and Variations Summary
*   **Missing PO:**
    *   *Standard:* Loop to Supplier for details.
    *   *Complex:* If no PO exists for informal services, Supervisor creates Retrospective PO or rejects.
*   **Discrepancies (Price/Qty/Receipt):**
    *   *Level 1:* Purchasing attempts resolution (contact supplier/Karen).
    *   *Level 2:* If unresolvable, Escalation to Supervisor.
    *   *System Match Failure:* Returns to Purchasing for clarification or Supervisor escalation.
*   **Manager Rejection:**
    *   Results in investigation loop or formal rejection end-state.
*   **Supplier Relationship Manager Involvement:**
    *   Triggered indirectly when pricing/terms are disputed. Acts as a decision-support resource rather than a process executor.

#### 6. Ambiguities and Modeling Notes
*   **Dual Verification:** Mary describes forwarding to Purchasing *before* system matching; Dan describes checking when the invoice hits his inbox. The model assumes a **sequential flow**: Purchasing performs a manual/operational check and sends confirmation, after which AP performs the final system match. If Dan's check *is* the system match, the model simplifies to a single verification gateway, but the transcript evidence supports two distinct activities.
*   **Escalation Entry Point:** Mary states she escalates to Rita if discrepancies cannot be resolved. Rita confirms she handles issues where Mary "can't match" or Purchasing has issues. The model places escalation options at both the Purchasing resolution gateway and the AP System Match gateway.
*   **Karen's Integration:** Karen is described as "indirectly involved." She does not execute tasks in the system. The model treats her as an external consultant resource triggered by the Purchasing/Solution tasks when contract terms are in question.
*   **Payment Scheduling:** All stakeholders agree payment follows approval. Rita mentions payment runs; Mary schedules them. The model captures this as the final task performed by AP.
