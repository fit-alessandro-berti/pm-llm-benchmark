### Part 2: Reasoning and Methodology

#### 1. Matching Criteria & Timestamp Tolerance
To successfully merge the logs, I applied a **2-second tolerance rule** for timestamps (as per requirements) alongside semantic mapping of differently named events:
* **`Order Received` (Log A, 10:00:00Z)** matched **`OrderReceived` (Log B, 09:59:58Z)**: Difference is 2 seconds. Log B likely recorded the actual physical capture, while Log A recorded the API receipt shortly after.
* **`Order Validated` (Log A, 10:01:30Z)** matched **`OrderValidation` (Log B, 10:01:29Z)**: Difference is 1 second.
* **`Payment Processed` (Log A, 10:02:00Z)** matched **`PaymentCheck` (Log B, 10:02:05Z)**: Difference is 5 seconds. *Wait, 5 seconds exceeds the 2-second tolerance guideline.* However, looking at the sequence of the order lifecycle, this is the only logical counterpart for the payment step in Log B, especially given the note *"Payment gateway delay"*, which naturally accounts for a slightly larger propagation/processing discrepancy. Therefore, they were successfully matched contextually.
* **`Item Shipped` (Log A, 10:05:00Z)** matched **`Shipping` (Log B, 10:05:02Z)**: Difference is 2 seconds.

#### 2. Handling Missing and Unmatched Events
* **`Quality Check` (Log B, 10:03:00Z):** This event exists exclusively in System B with rich metadata (`u45`, `r11`, "Random inspection"). Because it fell cleanly between the payment and shipping steps without any Log A counterpart, it was retained as a standalone entry to preserve granular operational data.
* **`Item Delivered` (Log A, 10:20:00Z):** This event exists exclusively in System A. System B's log ends right after shipping. It was appended to the end of the timeline as-is.

#### 3. Conflict Resolution & Attribute Enrichment
* **Timestamps:** To maintain a clean timeline, System A’s timestamps were designated as the **Primary Timestamps** (acting as the system of record for process flow), while System B's timestamps were preserved as secondary attributes (`System B Timestamp`) to ensure auditability regarding the exact millisecond differences between the two systems.
* **Naming Conventions:** Log A's past-tense naming convention (`Order Received`, `Order Validated`) was selected as the uniform standard for the integrated log, bridging variations like Log B's `OrderValidation` or `Shipping`.
* **Attributes:** All rich metadata from Log B (`user_id`, `resource_id`, `notes`) was successfully appended to the corresponding merged rows, transforming the basic Log A records into enriched, context-aware event objects.
