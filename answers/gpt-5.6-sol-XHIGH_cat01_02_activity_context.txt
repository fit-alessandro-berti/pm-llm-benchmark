## 1. Activities requiring disambiguation

The activities repeated within the same case are:

- **C001 – Verify Documents**
  - First occurrence: initial identity-document review.
  - Second occurrence: re-verification after the customer uploads a missing ID scan.

- **C002 – Call Customer**
  - First occurrence: clarify an account discrepancy before making an adjustment.
  - Second occurrence: confirm the adjustment afterward.

- **C003 – Verify Documents**
  - First occurrence: initial physical ID verification at the branch.
  - Second occurrence: re-verification after an account correction.

For consistency, the other instances of **Verify Documents** and **Call Customer** are also given contextual names, even where they occur only once in a particular case.

## 3. Naming rationale

- **Initial** distinguishes the first verification from later repeated checks.
- **Reverify … After ID Upload** indicates that C001’s second verification was triggered by newly submitted evidence.
- **Reverify … After Account Adjustment** shows that C003’s second verification validates documents after an internal correction.
- The customer calls are named by their purpose:
  - obtaining missing documents,
  - clarifying a discrepancy,
  - confirming a completed adjustment, or
  - asking the customer to return to the branch.
- Activities without meaningful within-case ambiguity, such as **Initiate Request**, **Adjust Account**, and **Approve Request**, remain unchanged.
