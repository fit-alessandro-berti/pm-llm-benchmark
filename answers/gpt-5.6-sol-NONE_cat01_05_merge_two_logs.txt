## Matching Decisions

- **Order Received / OrderReceived:** Same order and semantically equivalent names. The timestamps differ by exactly 2 seconds. This was merged by interpreting the stated tolerance as allowing a difference of up to 2 seconds.
- **Order Validated / OrderValidation:** Same order, equivalent activity, and a 1-second difference; merged.
- **Item Shipped / Shipping:** Same order, equivalent shipping activity, and a 2-second difference; merged.
- **Payment Processed / PaymentCheck:** Although the names are related, their timestamps differ by 5 seconds, exceeding the proposed tolerance. They were therefore kept separate rather than assuming they are the same occurrence. The note “Payment gateway delay” also allows the possibility that `PaymentCheck` is a distinct payment-stage event.
- **Quality Check:** No corresponding event appears in Log A, so it remains a B-only event with all of Log B’s attributes.
- **Item Delivered:** No corresponding event appears in Log B, so it remains an A-only event.

For merged records, naming was normalized to the clearer Log A terminology while preserving each source’s original event name and timestamp.
