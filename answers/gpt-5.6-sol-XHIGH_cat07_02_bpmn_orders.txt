The visualization is a **BPMN-style order-fulfillment workflow** divided into four organizational lanes: **Sales, Production, Procurement, and Warehouse**.

- **Sales:** Every Monday morning, Sales checks an order for completeness and verifies whether the customer exists in the database. A new customer is added if necessary. Sales then sends the order details to Production and Procurement, waits for confirmation that the product was sent, and drafts the invoice.
- **Production:** After receiving the order details, Production plans production while waiting for Procurement to confirm that the materials are not faulty. Once both conditions are met, the product is manufactured and a “Product complete” message is sent to the Warehouse.
- **Procurement:** Procurement receives the order details, orders materials, and checks them. If the materials pass inspection, Production is notified. If the order is canceled or the materials fail the check, a compensation process returns the materials.
- **Warehouse:** The Warehouse receives the completed-product notification, sends the product, and notifies Sales that it has been sent.

Solid arrows show the internal sequence of work, dashed arrows represent messages between departments, diamonds indicate decision or parallel gateways, circles represent events, and the cylinder represents the customer database.
