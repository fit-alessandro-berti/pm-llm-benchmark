**Brief justifications for the naming choices** (based on Additional Context + position/sequence in the case):

- **Verify Documents** (recurs in C001 and C003):  
  The first occurrence in each case is always the primary/identity check performed early in the flow → renamed **Initial Document Verification** (or **Initial Physical Document Verification** for C003, reflecting the explicit “Physical verification of ID” and walk-in/branch context).  
  Subsequent occurrences are always re-checks triggered by intervening updates → renamed **Post-Submission Document Re-verification** (C001, after the customer uploaded the ID scan) and **Post-Adjustment Document Re-verification** (C003, after the internal account correction). These names capture the different triggers and process roles.

- **Call Customer** (recurs in C002; also used with distinct meanings in C001/C003):  
  Each instance has a clearly different purpose stated in the Additional Context column, so they were renamed to surface that purpose:  
  - C001 → **Call Customer for Missing Documents** (“calls to request missing docs”),  
  - C002 first → **Call Customer to Clarify Account Discrepancy** (“Outbound call to clarify account discrepancy”),  
  - C002 second → **Follow-up Call to Confirm Adjustments** (“Follow-up call to confirm adjustments made”),  
  - C003 → **Call Customer to Return to Branch** (“calls to have them return”).

- **Submit Additional** was lightly polished to **Submit Additional Documents** for readability (it does not recur, but the original name was incomplete).

All other activities appear only once per case (or with consistent meaning) and were left unchanged. The new names make the distinct roles of each activity instance immediately visible from the log itself while remaining concise.
