The visualization is a **BPMN-style order fulfillment workflow** divided into four horizontal participants: **Sales, Production, Procurement, and Warehouse**.

### Process flow

- **Sales**
  - A timer event starts the process on **Monday morning**.
  - Sales checks the order for completeness and verifies whether the customer is already in the database.
  - If the customer is new, they are added to the database; otherwise, the process continues directly.
  - Sales then sends the order details to both **Production** and **Procurement**.
  - After receiving confirmation that the product has been sent, Sales drafts the invoice and ends the process.

- **Production**
  - Receives the order details and splits work into parallel paths:
    - Plan production.
    - Wait for Procurement to confirm that the materials are not faulty.
  - Once both conditions are satisfied, the product is manufactured.
  - A **product complete** message is sent to the Warehouse.

- **Procurement**
  - Receives the order details and orders the required materials.
  - Materials are checked.
  - If acceptable, Procurement sends a **material not faulty** confirmation to Production.
  - If there is a problem, an **order cancellation/compensation path** is triggered, including returning the materials.

- **Warehouse**
  - Receives the **product complete** message.
  - Sends the finished product.
  - Sends a **product sent** confirmation back to Sales.

Solid arrows show sequence flow within each participant, dashed arrows show messages between participants, diamonds represent gateways, envelope symbols represent message events, and the cylinder represents the customer database.
