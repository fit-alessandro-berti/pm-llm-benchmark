The visualization is a three-part software/process modeling diagram for a laboratory workflow:

- **(a) Main process model:** Two process classes—**Lab visit** on the left and **Lab test** on the right—are connected through two dashed communication channels:
  - **Order system**, linking the lab visit’s blood-sample order to the lab test process.
  - **HIS** (hospital information system), carrying test results and updated reports between the processes.

  The **Lab visit** workflow includes activities such as *Order entry*, *Take blood sample*, *Receive result*, *Judge results*, *Do tests again*, *Receive updated result*, and *Send report*. It contains branching logic, including an *Everything is fine* outcome or repeating tests.

  The **Lab test** workflow includes *Perform test*, *Make report*, possible repeated testing via *Perform test again* and *Provide updated report*, and *Finish lab test*. Roles such as **doctor**, **lab assistant**, and **chemical analyst** are shown beside the activities.

- **(b) Class diagram:** A **Lab visit** requires one or more **Lab tests**. Each lab test is associated with exactly one lab visit, shown by multiplicities **1..1** and **1..\***.

- **(c) Performative example:** A table records a communication event at **11:00** through the **Order system**. The sender is **Lab visit – John**, the receivers are **Lab test – HGB – John**, the action is **Create**, the content is “Can you perform a HGB test for John?”, the scope is **Private**, and the direction is **OUT**.
