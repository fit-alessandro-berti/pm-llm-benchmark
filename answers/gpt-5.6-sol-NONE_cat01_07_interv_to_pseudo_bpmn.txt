# BPMN-Like Textual Process Model: Invoice Approval

## 1. Participants / Swimlanes

1. **Supplier**
2. **Accounts Payable Clerk (AP Clerk)**
3. **Purchasing Department / Purchasing Officer**
4. **Supplier Relationship Manager**
5. **Accounts Payable Supervisor**
6. **Department Manager**
7. **Accounting / Approval System**

---

## 2. Main Process Flow

### Start Event
**Invoice received**, typically by email from the Supplier.

### Initial validation

1. **AP Clerk — Review invoice for PO reference**
   - Input: Supplier invoice
   - Checks whether the invoice contains or can be associated with a valid purchase order.

2. **Exclusive Gateway G1 — Valid PO reference available?**

   **Yes:**
   - Continue to Task 3.

   **No:**
   - Follow the missing-PO exception flow described in Section 3.1.

3. **AP Clerk — Log invoice in accounting system**
   - Inputs: Invoice and PO reference
   - Output: Recorded invoice

4. **AP Clerk — Forward invoice to Purchasing for receipt/order confirmation**
   - The invoice and associated PO are made available to Purchasing.

### Purchasing verification

5. **Purchasing Officer — Compare invoice with PO and receipt information**
   - Verify:
     - Goods or services were received
     - Quantities
     - Prices
     - Delivery charges
     - Other order terms
   - Inputs:
     - Invoice
     - Purchase order
     - Delivery or receipt information
     - Potentially contract terms

6. **Exclusive Gateway G2 — Goods/services received and invoice details correct?**

   **Yes:**
   - Purchasing sends a confirmation email to AP.
   - Continue to Task 7.

   **No — discrepancy or missing information:**
   - Follow the discrepancy-resolution flow in Section 3.2.

7. **Purchasing Officer — Confirm receipt/order correctness to AP**
   - Output: Purchasing confirmation email or equivalent confirmation record

### AP matching and routing

8. **AP Clerk — Match invoice line items against PO in accounting system**
   - Inputs:
     - Logged invoice
     - PO
     - Purchasing confirmation
   - Checks invoice lines against PO quantities, prices, and related details.

9. **Exclusive Gateway G3 — Invoice successfully matched?**

   **Yes:**
   - Continue to Task 10.

   **No:**
   - Follow the discrepancy-resolution flow in Section 3.2.

10. **Accounting System — Send automatic approval notification to Department Manager**
    - Trigger condition:
      - Receipt of goods/services has been confirmed; and
      - Invoice has been matched successfully to the PO.
    - Notification includes or links to:
      - Invoice details
      - PO
      - Purchasing confirmation

### Manager approval

11. **Department Manager — Review invoice approval package**
    - Reviews:
      - Quantities and prices
      - Total amount
      - Budget expectations
      - Unexpected items
      - Special notes
      - Purchasing confirmation

12. **Exclusive Gateway G4 — Manager approves invoice?**

   **Approved:**
   - Manager records approval in the system.
   - Continue to Task 13.

   **Further investigation required:**
   - Manager asks AP to hold and investigate.
   - Follow the manager-review exception flow in Section 3.3.

   **Rejected:**
   - Follow the rejection flow in Section 3.4.

13. **Accounting System / Department Manager — Return approved invoice to AP**
    - Output: Approved invoice record

14. **AP Clerk — Schedule invoice for payment**
    - Add the approved invoice to the next applicable payment cycle/payment run.

### End Event
**Invoice approved and scheduled for payment.**

> Actual payment execution is outside the process scope described by the interviews.

## 3. Exception and Variation Flows

### 3.1 Missing or Invalid PO

From **G1 — Valid PO reference available?**

1. **AP Clerk — Place invoice on hold**
2. **AP Clerk — Request PO information or revised invoice from Supplier**
   - The Supplier Relationship Manager may assist, especially for key suppliers.
3. **Supplier Relationship Manager — Contact supplier and request missing PO reference or corrected invoice** *(optional path)*
4. **Supplier — Provide PO information or submit revised invoice**
5. **Gateway — Valid PO obtained?**

   **Yes:**
   - Return to **AP Clerk — Log invoice in accounting system**.

   **No, but invoice may represent a legitimate informally contracted service:**
   - Escalate to the **AP Supervisor**.
   - AP Supervisor coordinates with the relevant department head.
   - Proceed to **Gateway — Retrospective PO authorized?**

   **No and invoice cannot be validated:**
   - Reject or return the invoice to the Supplier.
   - End event: **Invoice rejected/returned due to missing valid PO**.

6. **Gateway — Retrospective PO authorized?**

   **Yes:**
   - Relevant department creates or authorizes a retrospective PO.
   - Return to **AP Clerk — Log invoice in accounting system**.

   **No:**
   - AP Supervisor determines that the invoice should be rejected.
   - Notify Supplier and relevant stakeholders.
   - End event: **Invoice rejected**.

**Control rule:** No invoice proceeds to approval without a confirmed PO reference, including a retrospectively created PO where permitted.

---

### 3.2 Invoice, PO, Receipt, or Contract Discrepancy

This flow may be entered from either:

- **G2 — Goods/services received and invoice details correct?**, or
- **G3 — Invoice successfully matched?**

Typical discrepancies include:

- Incorrect prices
- Incorrect quantities or extra units
- Missing items
- Unexpected delivery charges
- Missing information
- Differences from contract terms
- Receipt of goods/services not confirmed

1. **AP Clerk or Purchasing Officer — Place invoice on hold**
2. **AP Clerk / Purchasing Officer — Clarify discrepancy**
   - Purchasing may contact the Supplier directly.
   - Alternatively, Purchasing may ask AP to keep the invoice on hold while awaiting correction.
3. **Optional: Supplier Relationship Manager — Review contract or supplier terms**
   - For pricing discrepancies, determine the contractually correct price.
   - Communicate findings to AP or Purchasing.
4. **Supplier — Provide clarification, missing information, or corrected invoice**
5. **Exclusive Gateway — Discrepancy resolved?**

   **Yes, corrected invoice or clarification received:**
   - Update or replace the invoice record as necessary.
   - Return to **Purchasing Officer — Compare invoice with PO and receipt information**.
   - The invoice must be reconfirmed and rematched before manager approval.

   **No / cannot be resolved easily:**
   - Escalate to the **AP Supervisor**.

6. **AP Supervisor — Coordinate exception resolution**
   - Contact Supplier.
   - Coordinate between AP, Purchasing, the relevant department, and, where useful, the Supplier Relationship Manager.
   - Determine whether corrected documentation, a PO amendment, or rejection is required.

7. **Exclusive Gateway — Supervisor resolves exception?**

   **Yes:**
   - Return to **Purchasing verification** and then the normal matching and approval flow.

   **No:**
   - Reject or return the invoice.
   - End event: **Invoice rejected/unresolved**.

---

### 3.3 Manager Requests Investigation

From **G4 — Manager approves invoice?**

1. **Department Manager — Request hold and investigation**
   - Reasons may include:
     - Large variance from budget
     - Unexpected items
     - Suspicious total amount
     - Questionable special notes or charges

2. **AP Clerk — Place invoice on hold**
3. **AP Clerk — Investigate issue**
   - Coordinate with Purchasing.
   - Where relevant, involve:
     - Supplier Relationship Manager
     - AP Supervisor
     - Supplier

4. **Exclusive Gateway — Manager’s concern resolved?**

   **Yes:**
   - Update supporting information or obtain a corrected invoice.
   - Revalidate Purchasing confirmation and AP match if invoice details changed.
   - Resubmit to the Department Manager for approval.

   **No:**
   - Return to the Manager for a rejection decision or escalate internally according to exception-handling practice.

---

### 3.4 Manager Rejection

1. **Department Manager — Reject invoice in system**
2. **Accounting System — Notify AP of rejection**
3. **AP Clerk — Review rejection reason**
4. **Exclusive Gateway — Rework is allowed?**

   **Yes:**
   - Place invoice on hold.
   - Investigate with Purchasing, the Supplier, AP Supervisor, or Supplier Relationship Manager.
   - If resolved, reconfirm and rematch the invoice before resubmitting it to the Manager.

   **No:**
   - Notify Supplier and relevant internal stakeholders.
   - End event: **Invoice rejected by Department Manager**.

## 4. Compact Flow Representation

```text
Start: Invoice received
  -> [AP Clerk] Check for valid PO
  -> XOR: Valid PO?
      No
        -> Hold invoice
        -> Request PO/revised invoice from Supplier
        -> Optional Supplier Relationship Manager assistance
        -> XOR: Valid PO obtained?
            Yes -> Log invoice
            No -> Escalate to AP Supervisor
                 -> XOR: Retrospective PO authorized?
                     Yes -> Create retrospective PO -> Log invoice
                     No -> Reject invoice -> End
      Yes
        -> Log invoice
  -> Forward invoice and PO to Purchasing
  -> [Purchasing] Verify receipt, quantities, prices, charges, and terms
  -> XOR: Correct and receipt confirmed?
      No
        -> Hold invoice
        -> Contact Supplier / request correction
        -> Optional contract review by Supplier Relationship Manager
        -> XOR: Resolved?
            Yes -> Repeat Purchasing verification
            No -> Escalate to AP Supervisor
                 -> XOR: Resolved?
                     Yes -> Repeat Purchasing verification
                     No -> Reject invoice -> End
      Yes
        -> Purchasing sends confirmation to AP
  -> [AP Clerk] Match invoice lines against PO
  -> XOR: Match successful?
      No -> Enter discrepancy-resolution flow
      Yes
        -> [System] Notify Department Manager
        -> [Manager] Review invoice, PO, confirmation, and budget context
        -> XOR: Decision?
            Approve
              -> Record approval
              -> Return approved invoice to AP
              -> Schedule for payment
              -> End: Scheduled for payment
            Investigate
              -> Hold invoice
              -> Resolve concern
              -> Reconfirm/rematch if data changed
              -> Resubmit to Manager
            Reject
              -> XOR: Rework allowed?
                  Yes -> Resolve, reconfirm, rematch, and resubmit
                  No -> End: Invoice rejected
```

## 5. Key Data Objects / Artifacts

| Artifact | Created or supplied by | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| Supplier invoice | Supplier | AP, Purchasing, Manager |
| Purchase order | Purchasing/accounting system | AP, Purchasing, Manager |
| Retrospective PO | Relevant department, coordinated by AP Supervisor | AP, Purchasing |
| Goods/services receipt confirmation | Purchasing | AP, Manager |
| Purchasing confirmation email/record | Purchasing Officer | AP, Manager |
| Corrected or revised invoice | Supplier | AP, Purchasing |
| Supplier contract / agreed terms | Supplier Relationship Manager | Purchasing, AP |
| Manager approval notification | Accounting system | Department Manager |
| Approval/rejection record | Department Manager/system | AP |
| Payment schedule/payment-run entry | AP | Accounting/payment process |

## 6. Ambiguities and Modeling Assumptions

- Mary describes Purchasing primarily as confirming receipt, while Dan describes a broader comparison of quantities, prices, and delivery charges. The consolidated model treats Purchasing as performing both receipt confirmation and detailed order validation.
- AP also performs a formal line-item match after Purchasing’s confirmation. This may overlap with Purchasing’s comparison but is modeled as a separate system-based control.
- Dan states that Purchasing may contact the Supplier directly, while Mary usually emails Purchasing for clarification. Both interaction patterns are included as valid variations.
- Karen’s involvement is optional and focused on key suppliers, missing PO references, pricing, and contract interpretation.
- The precise system behavior following manager rejection is not specified. The model allows either rework/resubmission or terminal rejection.
- Retrospective PO creation is described only for legitimate exceptional invoices, particularly informally contracted services; the exact authorization role beyond “department heads” is unspecified.
- The process ends when AP schedules the approved invoice for payment. Execution of the payment run is not described in sufficient detail to model further.
