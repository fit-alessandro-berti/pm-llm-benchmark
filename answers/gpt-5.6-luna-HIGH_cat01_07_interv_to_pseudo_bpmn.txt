# BPMN-like Textual Process Model: Invoice Approval and Payment Scheduling

## 1. Participants and lanes

- **Supplier**
  - Sends invoices and, when required, revised invoices or missing PO information.
- **Accounts Payable (AP) Clerk – Mary**
  - Receives, checks, logs, matches, holds, and schedules invoices.
- **Purchasing Officer – Dan**
  - Confirms that goods/services were received and checks invoice details against the PO.
- **Department Manager – Carlos**
  - Performs final business approval.
- **AP Supervisor – Rita**
  - Handles unresolved discrepancies, non-PO invoices, and escalations.
- **Supplier Relationship Manager – Karen**
  - Supports supplier communication and contract/pricing clarification.
- **Department Head(s)**
  - May authorize or create a retrospective PO for informal services.
- **Accounting/Workflow System**
  - Stores invoice and PO information and sends the manager approval notification.

---

## 2. Main process flow

```text
(Message Start)
Invoice received from Supplier, usually by email
        |
        v
[AP Clerk: Check invoice for a valid PO number]
        |
        v
<Gateway G1: Is a valid and confirmed PO reference available?>
   | Yes
   v
[AP Clerk: Log invoice in accounting system]
   |
   v
[AP Clerk: Forward invoice to Purchasing]
   |
   v
[Purchasing: Confirm goods/services were received]
   |
   v
[Purchasing: Compare invoice details with PO
             quantities, prices, delivery charges, and other terms]
   |
   v
<Gateway G2: Are receipt and PO details confirmed?>
   | Yes
   v
[AP Clerk: Match invoice line items against PO in the system]
   |
   v
<Gateway G3: Does the invoice match the PO?>
   | Yes
   v
[Accounting/Workflow System: Send automatic approval notification]
   |
   v
[Department Manager: Review invoice, PO, confirmation,
                    quantities, prices, notes, and budget reasonableness]
   |
   v
<Gateway G4: Does the manager approve?>
   | Yes
   v
[AP Clerk: Schedule invoice for payment / add to next payment cycle]
   |
   v
(End: Invoice scheduled for payment)
```

## 3. Detailed gateway branches and exception flows

### G1 – Valid PO available?

#### G1 = No: PO missing, invalid, or not confirmed

```text
[AP Clerk: Return invoice to Supplier or request missing PO/details]
        |
        v
[Supplier: Provide PO number, additional information,
           or submit a corrected invoice]
        |
        v
(Re-enter at G1: Check for valid PO)
```

Purchasing may also notify AP that the invoice cannot be processed without a confirmed PO reference. The interviews indicate that invoices are normally not approved without a PO.

#### Special non-PO exception

Some invoices may relate to services contracted informally and may not correspond to an existing PO.

```text
<Gateway: Is this a legitimate informal-service invoice requiring exception handling?>
   | No
   v
(End: Invoice rejected or returned to Supplier)

   | Yes
   v
[AP Clerk: Escalate invoice to AP Supervisor]
        |
        v
[AP Supervisor: Coordinate with Department Head(s), Purchasing,
                Supplier, and/or Karen]
        |
        v
<Gateway: Can a retrospective PO or other valid authorization be created?>
   | Yes
   v
[Department Head/AP Supervisor: Create or authorize retrospective PO]
        |
        v
[AP Clerk: Log invoice in accounting system]
        |
        v
Continue at Purchasing confirmation

   | No
   v
(End: Invoice rejected)
```

---

### G2/G3 – Receipt or invoice-to-PO discrepancy

A discrepancy may involve:

- Incorrect pricing
- Incorrect quantities
- Extra units billed
- Missing items
- Incorrect delivery charges
- Invoice terms inconsistent with the contract
- Goods or services not confirmed as received
- Other missing or suspicious information

```text
[AP/Purchasing: Place invoice on hold and investigate discrepancy]
        |
        v
<Gateway: Can the discrepancy be resolved through routine clarification?>
```

#### Routine resolution

```text
   | Yes
   v
[Purchasing and/or AP: Contact Supplier or request clarification]
        |
        +--> [Karen, if needed: Check supplier contract and confirm
              agreed pricing or terms]
        |
        v
<Gateway: Is a corrected invoice or satisfactory explanation received?>
   | Yes
   v
[AP Clerk: Update or replace invoice record]
        |
        v
Re-enter at Purchasing confirmation and validation
```

The corrected invoice may be supplied by the Supplier, while Purchasing may update or clarify the PO-related information.

#### Escalated resolution

```text
   | No
   v
[AP Clerk: Escalate unresolved issue to AP Supervisor]
        |
        v
[AP Supervisor: Coordinate with Purchasing, Department Head(s),
                Supplier, and/or Karen]
        |
        v
<Gateway: Can the issue be resolved or properly authorized?>
   | Yes
   v
[AP Supervisor/Purchasing: Resolve discrepancy, amend documentation,
                            or create retrospective PO where appropriate]
        |
        v
Re-enter at Purchasing confirmation and validation

   | No
   v
(End: Invoice rejected or otherwise removed from the approval flow)
```

---

### G4 – Manager approval decision

#### G4 = Yes: Approved

```text
[Department Manager: Approve invoice in system]
        |
        v
[AP Clerk: Schedule payment / include invoice in payment run]
        |
        v
(End: Invoice scheduled for payment)
```

#### G4 = No: Rejected or returned for investigation

```text
[Department Manager: Reject invoice or request AP investigation]
        |
        v
<Gateway: Is the invoice permanently rejected?>
   | Yes
   v
(End: Invoice rejected)

   | No — investigation required
   v
[AP Clerk: Hold invoice and investigate]
        |
        v
Return to discrepancy-resolution flow
        |
        v
After resolution:
Purchasing confirmation  AP matching  Manager approval
```

Examples of manager concerns include:

- A large difference from the budgeted amount
- Unexpected items
- Suspicious pricing or totals
- Other concerns visible in the invoice, PO, or confirmation

## 5. Key data objects and artifacts

| Artifact | Created or used by | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Supplier invoice** | Supplier, AP | Primary document being approved |
| **PO number / Purchase Order** | Purchasing, AP, system | Establishes authorization and agreed quantities/prices |
| **Goods/services receipt confirmation** | Purchasing | Confirms that the ordered goods or services were received |
| **Invoice-to-PO match result** | AP, Purchasing | Confirms quantities, prices, delivery charges, and line-item consistency |
| **Corrected invoice** | Supplier | Resolves missing information or billing discrepancies |
| **Contract or agreed supplier terms** | Karen, Purchasing | Used to verify disputed pricing or terms |
| **Retrospective PO** | Department Head(s), AP Supervisor | Exception authorization for legitimate non-PO services |
| **Approval notification** | Accounting/Workflow System | Requests the Department Manager’s approval |
| **Manager approval or rejection record** | Department Manager, system | Final business approval decision |
| **Payment schedule/payment-run entry** | AP | Indicates that the invoice is ready for payment |

## 6. Ambiguities and modeling assumptions

1. **Purchasing validation and AP matching overlap in the interviews.**  
   The model treats them as two related but distinct activities:
   - Purchasing confirms receipt and checks the invoice against the PO.
   - AP performs or records the formal invoice-to-PO match in the accounting system.

2. **The standard policy is that invoices require a PO.**  
   Mary and Dan describe missing-PO invoices as being returned or held. Rita identifies an exception for legitimate informal services, where a retrospective PO may be created.

3. **Manager rejection has two possible outcomes.**  
   The manager may permanently reject the invoice or return it to AP for investigation. The latter creates a rework loop through discrepancy resolution and renewed approval.

4. **Payment execution is outside the modeled scope.**  
   The process ends when AP schedules the invoice or places it into the next payment cycle. The actual payment run is not described in detail.

5. **Karen is an optional support participant.**  
   She is involved mainly when supplier communication, contract interpretation, or pricing clarification is needed.
