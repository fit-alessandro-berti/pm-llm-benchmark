This is a classic process mining performance analysis. The delays are not random - they follow a clear pattern.

### 1. Total Cycle Time Analysis

Cycle Time = `Close Ticket - Receive Ticket`

| Case ID | Variant | Cycle Time | Classification |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **103** | Receive > Triage > Assign L1 > Investigate > Resolve > Close | **1h 20m** | Fast - Benchmark |
| **101** | Receive > Triage > Assign L1 > Investigate > Resolve > Close | **2h 15m** | Fast |
| **104** | Receive > Triage > Assign L1 > Investigate > Resolve > Close | **24h 10m** | **Slow - 18x slower than benchmark** |
| **102** | Receive > Triage > Assign L1 > **Escalate L2** > Investigate > Resolve > Close | **25h 10m** | **Slow** |
| **105** | Receive > Triage > Assign L1 > Investigate > **Escalate L2** > Investigate > Resolve > Close | **49h 05m** | **Very Slow - Outlier** |

Average Cycle Time: 20h 24m. Median: 24h 10m.

**Cases 102, 104, and 105 have significantly longer resolution times.** Cases 101 and 103 can be resolved in < 3 hours. The other 3 take 24-49 hours.

Cases 102 and 105 share a variant with escalation, while 104 is the same variant as the fast cases but with performance issues.

### 2. Root Cause Analysis - Where is the time lost?

If we break down waiting time between activities:

**a) The Escalation Bottleneck - The strongest correlation**
*   Non-escalated cases avg: 9h 15m - and 1h 47m if we exclude 104
*   Escalated cases avg: 37h 07m - **~20x slower**

    *   **Case 102:** 2h 30m wait `Assign to Level-1 -> Escalate to Level-2` + 2h 30m wait `Escalate -> Investigate`
    *   **Case 105:** 28h wait `Escalate -> Investigate` - From 2024-03-01 10:00 to 2024-03-02 14:00. This indicates Level-2 queue is severely backlogged or not staffed. Case 105 also has rework: `Investigate > Escalate > Investigate` loop, meaning L1 work was wasted.

Escalation itself is not the root cause, but a symptom of lack of L2 capacity / inefficient handover.

**b) The Overnight Resolution Bottleneck - Investigation -> Resolve**
This is the single biggest delay in ALL slow cases:

*   101: 60 min
*   103: 30 min
*   **102: 19h 00m** - Investigate at 14:00 Day 1 -> Resolve at 09:00 Day 2
*   **104: 19h 00m** - Investigate at 13:00 Day 1 -> Resolve at 08:00 Day 2
*   **105: 19h 00m** - Investigate at 14:00 Day 2 -> Resolve at 09:00 Day 3

Pattern: Any ticket where `Investigate Issue` starts after ~11:30 is not resolved until 08:00-09:00 the next day. This strongly suggests:
1.  No evening / night shift
2.  Resolve activity requires a dependency that is only available in the morning - e.g., approval, deployment window, customer confirmation
3.  Investigators logging off without closing tickets

**c) Initial Waiting Time - Assign to Level-1 -> Investigate**
*   Fast cases: 10-40 min
*   **Case 104: 3h 30m** - This case had no escalation, yet was slow because it sat uninvestigated from 09:30 to 13:00. This points to Level-1 agent overload or poor queue prioritization around 08:00-09:00 peak arrival time. 4 tickets arrived within 25 minutes.

**d) Triage Delay - Also contributes to 104**
*   Fast cases: 5-10 min
*   Case 104: 40 min Receive -> Triage. Triage is slow when intake is high.

### 3. Insights and Recommendations

The delays are not caused by the investigation or resolution work itself being longer, but by **waiting time and handover time**.

**1. Fix the Escalation / L2 Handover Process:**
Define an SLA for L2 pickup. Currently it is 2.5h to 28h. Implement a push notification / on-call rotation for L2. Investigate *why* tickets are escalated - 2 out of 5 required it. If training and knowledge base for L1 agents are improved, escalations can be prevented.

**2. Eliminate the Overnight Blackout:**
This is your biggest ROI. Introduce a second shift, or enforce a rule: tickets investigated after 12:00 must be resolved same day. Check if Resolve is blocked by a customer or a batch job - if so, automate customer nudges or make resolution an async step that doesn't block closing.

**3. Address Level-1 Capacity at Peak Arrival:**
Between 08:00-08:25, 5 tickets arrive. Case 104 suffers. Implement workload balancing, auto-assignment based on agent availability, and prioritize triage. An automated triage for simple issues could bypass L1 entirely.

**4. Prevent Rework - Case 105:**
This is the only case with pattern `Investigate -> Escalate`. L1 investigated *before* escalating, wasting 50 min. Define clear escalation criteria at Triage stage so complex tickets go directly to L2 per Case 102 variant, saving one investigation cycle.

> To confirm these hypotheses, you would need to enrich the log with Resource / Agent ID, Ticket Priority / Category, and Customer response times.
