A standard P2P flow should roughly follow:

**Create Purchase Requisition  Approve Purchase Requisition  Issue Purchase Order  Receive Goods  Vendor Invoice Received  Match Invoice to PO  Release Payment  Close Case**

Case **1001** follows this normal sequence and appears fine.

The anomalous cases are **1002, 1003, and 1004**.

---

### Case 1002 — Multiple severe control violations

**Anomalies:**
- **Issue Purchase Order** happens before **Approve Purchase Requisition**.
- **Release Payment** happens before **Receive Goods** and before **Match Invoice to PO**.
- **Approve Purchase Requisition** happens after the invoice was received and after payment was released.

**Why it is problematic:**
- A purchase order should not be issued without an approved purchase requisition. Doing so bypasses budget and purchasing authorization controls.
- Payment should not be made before goods are received and before the invoice is matched to the purchase order. Without a three-way match — PO, goods receipt, and invoice — the company could pay for goods it never received, pay the wrong amount, or become exposed to duplicate or fraudulent invoices.
- Approving the requisition only after payment is an after-the-fact approval, which defeats the purpose of the approval control.

---

### Case 1003 — Payment before invoice and before goods receipt

**Anomalies:**
- **Release Payment** occurs on 2024-02-08.
- **Vendor Invoice Received** occurs on 2024-02-09.
- **Receive Goods** occurs on 2024-02-10.
- **Match Invoice to PO** occurs on 2024-02-11.

So payment is made **before the invoice exists**, **before goods are received**, and **before any invoice matching is done**.

**Why it is problematic:**
- The payment is not supported by a verified invoice or proof of delivery.
- There is no three-way match before payment, so the organization cannot validate price, quantity, or delivery status.
- This creates a high risk of overpayment, payment for undelivered goods, duplicate payment, or fraud.
- Standard P2P best practice requires payment only after the goods/services are confirmed and the invoice is matched and approved.

---

### Case 1004 — Goods received before purchase order is issued

**Anomalies:**
- **Receive Goods** occurs at 2024-02-06 09:00.
- **Issue Purchase Order** occurs at 2024-02-06 09:15.

So goods are received **before** a formal purchase order exists.

**Why it is problematic:**
- In P2P, goods receipts should be linked to an existing, approved purchase order so receiving staff can verify quantities, prices, and contract terms.
- Receiving goods without a PO means the organization has accepted goods without an authorized purchasing commitment.
- The three-way match is undermined because the goods receipt is not backed by a valid PO at the time of receipt.
- This can lead to unauthorized purchases, invoice disputes, and payment problems.
