## BPMN-Like Textual Process Model: Invoice Approval and Payment

### 1. Participants / Swimlanes

1. **Supplier**
   - Sends original or corrected invoices.
   - Provides missing PO information or clarification on discrepancies.

2. **Accounts Payable Clerk — Mary**
   - Receives, validates, logs, matches, routes, and schedules invoices.
   - Coordinates exception handling.

3. **Purchasing Department — Dan**
   - Confirms the PO, quantities, prices, delivery charges, and receipt of goods or services.
   - Communicates discrepancies to the supplier or AP.

4. **Department Manager — Carlos**
   - Performs final business approval.
   - May reject or request investigation of suspicious invoices.

5. **Accounts Payable Supervisor — Rita**
   - Handles escalated or unresolved exceptions.
   - Coordinates supplier and departmental resolution.
   - May support creation of a retrospective PO.

6. **Supplier Relationship Manager — Karen**
   - Supports supplier communication and contract/pricing verification.
   - Does not perform system approvals.

7. **Accounting / Invoice Management System**
   - Stores invoice and PO records.
   - Supports matching.
   - Sends manager approval notifications.

---

## 2. Process Flow

### Start Event

**Start:** Invoice received, usually by email, by Accounts Payable.

**Input artifact:** Supplier invoice, potentially containing:
- PO number
- Invoice line items
- Quantities
- Prices
- Delivery charges
- Supplier and contract information

---

### A. Initial Invoice Validation

#### A1. Check whether the invoice contains a valid PO number  
**Role:** Accounts Payable Clerk  
**System/artifact:** Supplier invoice and PO reference

#### Gateway G1: Is a valid PO number present?

##### If No — Missing or Invalid PO

1. **Return invoice to supplier requesting the PO number or additional details.**  
   **Role:** AP Clerk  
   **Message flow:** AP  Supplier

2. **Supplier provides the PO number or sends a revised invoice.**  
   **Role:** Supplier

3. **Receive revised invoice or additional information.**  
   **Role:** AP Clerk

4. **Rework loop:** Return to **A1 — Check for a valid PO number**.

Possible variation:

- If the invoice does not correspond to any formal PO because it relates to informally contracted services, the case may be routed to exception handling rather than returned immediately.

##### If Yes — Valid PO Present

Proceed to A2.

---

### B. Invoice Registration

#### A2. Log invoice into the accounting or invoice management system  
**Role:** AP Clerk  
**Output artifact:** Registered invoice record

The invoice record is associated with the relevant PO.

---

### C. Receipt Confirmation

#### A3. Forward invoice to Purchasing for confirmation  
**Role:** AP Clerk  
**Message flow:** AP  Purchasing

#### A4. Verify receipt of goods or services and review PO details  
**Role:** Purchasing Officer  
Purchasing checks:
- Quantities ordered versus invoiced
- Prices
- Delivery charges
- Goods or services received
- Other PO terms

#### Gateway G2: Have the goods/services been received and are the PO details sufficiently confirmed?

##### If No — Receipt or PO Information Not Confirmed

1. **Request clarification or missing information.**  
   **Role:** Purchasing, potentially with AP or Supplier

2. Possible actions:
   - Contact supplier directly.
   - Ask AP to place the invoice on hold.
   - Request the missing PO reference.
   - Investigate whether the goods or services were actually received.

3. **Receive clarification, corrected information, or supplier response.**

4. **Rework loop:** Return to **A4 — Verify receipt and PO details**.

##### If Yes

5. **Send confirmation to Accounts Payable.**  
   **Role:** Purchasing Officer  
   **Output:** Purchasing confirmation email or system notification

Proceed to invoice matching.

---

### D. Invoice-to-PO Matching

#### A5. Match invoice line items against the PO in the system  
**Role:** AP Clerk, supported by the accounting system

The comparison includes:
- Quantities
- Prices
- Line items
- Delivery charges
- Other relevant terms

#### Gateway G3: Does the invoice match the PO?

##### If No — Discrepancy Found

Examples:
- Incorrect pricing
- Extra or missing items
- Incorrect quantities
- Unrecognized delivery charges
- Invoice total inconsistent with the PO
- Terms inconsistent with the supplier contract

1. **Place invoice on hold or suspend further processing.**  
   **Role:** AP Clerk or Purchasing

2. **Investigate discrepancy.**  
   **Roles:** AP, Purchasing, Supplier Relationship Manager, Supplier

3. Possible resolution activities:
   - Purchasing contacts the supplier.
   - Karen checks the supplier contract and confirms the correct price.
   - Supplier provides clarification or a corrected invoice.
   - AP requests missing information.
   - Purchasing confirms whether the invoiced goods or services were received.

4. **Receive corrected invoice, clarification, or contract confirmation.**

5. **Re-match invoice against PO.**  
   **Role:** AP Clerk

#### Gateway G4: Can the discrepancy be resolved through normal clarification?

##### If Yes

Return to **A5 — Match invoice line items against the PO**.

##### If No

Proceed to escalation.

---

### E. Exception Escalation

#### A6. Escalate unresolved invoice exception  
**Role:** AP Clerk  
**Recipient:** AP Supervisor

Escalation triggers include:
- Invoice cannot be matched to the PO
- Missing or unreliable information
- Persistent pricing or quantity discrepancy
- Purchasing cannot resolve the issue
- Invoice appears to relate to an informal service arrangement

#### A7. Investigate and coordinate resolution  
**Role:** AP Supervisor  
Possible actions:
- Contact the supplier
- Coordinate between AP, Purchasing, and the department
- Consult the Department Manager or department head
- Verify contractual terms
- Determine whether the invoice is legitimate
- Arrange creation of a retrospective PO where appropriate

#### Gateway G5: Is the invoice valid and can it be regularized?

##### If No — Invoice Rejected

1. **Reject or cancel invoice.**  
   **Role:** AP Supervisor, potentially with Department Manager or AP

2. **Notify relevant parties, potentially including supplier.**

**End:** Invoice rejected/closed.

##### If Yes — Retrospective PO or Other Resolution Required

1. **Create or arrange a retrospective PO**, where permitted.  
   **Role:** AP Supervisor and relevant department head

2. **Update or correct invoice/PO information.**

3. **Return invoice to normal flow.**

Proceed to **A8 — Obtain Purchasing confirmation**, or, if already confirmed, to **A9 — Manager approval**. The interviews indicate that exception cases generally re-enter the normal flow with Purchasing confirmation before manager approval.

---

### F. Manager Approval

#### A8. Trigger manager approval request  
**Role:** Accounting system / AP process  
**Condition:** Receipt of goods/services has been confirmed and invoice-to-PO matching is complete.

The system sends an automatic email or notification to the Department Manager.

#### A9. Review invoice for final business approval  
**Role:** Department Manager  
The manager reviews:
- Invoice details
- PO
- Purchasing confirmation
- Quantities and prices
- Special notes
- Overall reasonableness against the department budget

#### Gateway G6: Does the manager approve the invoice?

##### If Yes

1. **Approve invoice in the system.**  
   **Role:** Department Manager

2. **Return approved invoice to Accounts Payable.**  
   **System flow:** Manager approval  AP workflow

Proceed to payment scheduling.

##### If No — Reject or Investigate

1. **Reject invoice or request investigation.**  
   **Role:** Department Manager

2. **Place invoice on hold.**  
   **Role:** AP Clerk

3. **Investigate suspected issue**, such as:
   - Large difference from budget
   - Unexpected items
   - Suspicious total
   - Unexplained special charges

4. Possible outcomes:
   - Issue is resolved and invoice is corrected or justified.
   - Invoice is returned to Purchasing, Supplier, Karen, or Rita.
   - Invoice is rejected.

If resolved, return to the appropriate previous activity:
- **A5 — Re-match invoice**, or
- **A8 — Obtain Purchasing confirmation**, followed by manager approval.

If not resolved:

**End:** Invoice rejected/closed.

---

### G. Payment Scheduling

#### A10. Schedule approved invoice for payment  
**Role:** AP Clerk / Accounts Payable team

**Precondition:**  
- Valid PO or approved retrospective PO
- Goods/services confirmed
- Invoice matched or exception resolved
- Manager approval recorded

**Output artifact:** Approved invoice scheduled for payment

#### End Event

**End:** Invoice enters the next payment run/payment cycle.

---

## 4. Key Ambiguities and Modeling Assumptions

- The interviews do not specify whether Purchasing confirms only receipt of goods/services or also performs the full invoice-to-PO comparison. The model represents Purchasing as confirming receipt and reviewing PO details, while AP performs the formal system match.
- The exact payment execution step is not described. The process therefore ends when AP schedules the approved invoice for the next payment run.
- It is unclear whether a missing PO always results in immediate return to the supplier or whether AP Supervisor involvement occurs immediately for informal service arrangements. Both paths are represented.
- The interviews imply that manager approval occurs only after both receipt confirmation and invoice matching are complete.
- Supplier Relationship Management is a support role in exception handling and does not directly approve or modify invoices in the system.
- The precise authority for final invoice rejection is not fully specified. The model assigns operational rejection of unresolved exceptions to the AP Supervisor and managerial rejection of suspicious invoices to the Department Manager.
