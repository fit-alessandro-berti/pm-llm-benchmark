**BPMN-like Textual Process Model: Invoice Approval Process**

**Participants / Lanes (Roles)**  
- Accounts Payable (AP) Clerk (Mary)  
- Purchasing Officer (Dan)  
- Department Manager (Carlos)  
- AP Supervisor (Rita) – exception handling only  
- Supplier Relationship Manager (Karen) – exception support only  
- Supplier (external)  
- Accounting System (automated notifications & matching)

**Primary Data Objects / Artifacts**  
- Invoice (received, usually by email)  
- Purchase Order (PO)  
- Confirmation of receipt / match  
- Approval / rejection decision  
- Corrected invoice or retrospective PO (exceptions)

---

### Main Happy-Path Sequence

1. **Start Event**: Invoice received (email) by AP Clerk.

2. **Task (AP Clerk)**: Check whether invoice has a valid PO number.

3. **Exclusive Gateway (AP Clerk)**: Valid PO present?  
   - **No** → Task (AP Clerk): Return invoice to Supplier requesting missing details / PO.  
     → (Process ends or waits for revised invoice; may re-enter at Start).  
   - **Yes** → Continue.

4. **Task (AP Clerk)**: Log invoice into Accounting System.

5. **Task (AP Clerk)**: Forward invoice to Purchasing for receipt confirmation.

6. **Task (Purchasing Officer)**: Compare invoice details (quantities, prices, delivery charges) against PO and actual receipt of goods/services.

7. **Exclusive Gateway (Purchasing Officer)**: Invoice matches PO / receipt?  
   - **No** → Exception path (see below).  
   - **Yes** → Task (Purchasing Officer): Send confirmation email to AP Clerk.

8. **Task (AP Clerk)**: Match invoice line items against PO in the Accounting System (post-confirmation).

9. **Exclusive Gateway (AP Clerk / System)**: Full match achieved?  
   - **No** → Exception path (see below).  
   - **Yes** → System automatically notifies Department Manager (email + system view showing invoice, PO, and confirmation).

10. **Task (Department Manager)**: Review invoice details, PO, confirmation, and any notes; decide on approval.

11. **Exclusive Gateway (Department Manager)**: Approve?  
    - **No / Suspicious** → Exception path (reject or request hold/investigation).  
    - **Yes** → Continue.

12. **Task (AP Clerk)**: Schedule invoice for payment (add to next payment cycle / run).

13. **End Event**: Invoice approved and scheduled for payment.

---

### Exception / Alternative Paths (and rework loops)

**A. Missing or invalid PO (early detection)**  
- Detected by AP Clerk (or later by Purchasing).  
- AP Clerk returns invoice to Supplier (or, if needed, involves Supplier Relationship Manager).  
- Supplier Relationship Manager may contact Supplier to obtain PO number or revised invoice.  
- Once revised invoice arrives → re-enter at Start / PO-check.  
- Special case (noted by AP Supervisor): Invoice for informally contracted services with no PO → escalate to AP Supervisor, who coordinates with department head to create retrospective PO or reject invoice. After resolution → resume normal flow at logging or matching step.

**B. Discrepancy after Purchasing review or system matching** (incorrect pricing, quantities, missing items, extra units, etc.)  
- Purchasing Officer contacts Supplier directly **or** asks AP Clerk to hold invoice pending corrected version.  
- AP Clerk may email Purchasing for clarification.  
- If not easily resolved → escalate to AP Supervisor.  
- AP Supervisor contacts Supplier, coordinates across departments, and/or involves Supplier Relationship Manager (who checks contracts and communicates correct terms).  
- Possible outcomes: corrected invoice received, retrospective adjustment, or rejection.  
- Once cleared → resume normal flow (confirmation from Purchasing → matching → Manager approval → schedule payment).  
- Note: Process participants indicate these escalations occur infrequently; exact frequency of Supplier Relationship Manager involvement is not uniformly described.

**C. Manager rejection or investigation request**  
- Department Manager rejects or asks AP Clerk to hold and investigate (rare – approximately once every couple of months per Manager account).  
- Returns to AP Clerk / AP Supervisor for further investigation (may involve Purchasing or Supplier Relationship Manager).  
- After resolution → may re-enter at matching or Manager-notification step.

**D. General exception handling note (AP Supervisor)**  
- AP Supervisor intervenes primarily on unresolved matching failures, missing information, or non-PO invoices.  
- After clearing the exception, the invoice re-enters the standard path: Purchasing confirmation → Manager approval → payment scheduling.  
- Supervisor does not perform final approvals.

---

### Notes on Consolidation & Ambiguities
- Sequence after Purchasing confirmation is consistently described as system matching → Manager notification/approval → AP scheduling of payment.  
- Some accounts (Purchasing, Manager) assume the hand-off to Manager occurs automatically once confirmation is given; AP Clerk description adds an explicit matching step in the system.  
- Exact triggers for involving Supplier Relationship Manager versus direct Supplier contact by AP/Purchasing are not fully uniform; both paths are attested.  
- Retrospective PO creation is mentioned only by the Supervisor and applies to informal-service cases.  
- No participant described parallel multi-instance approval or automated three-way matching beyond the manual + system steps above.  
- Payment-run execution itself is outside the scope of the described approval process.

This textual model is sufficiently structured and unambiguous to be converted directly into a standard BPMN diagram (with swimlanes for the listed roles, exclusive gateways for the decision points, and intermediate events or boundary events for exception handling).
