A problematic trace accepted by the model is:

```text
Receive_Order,
Check_Inventory,
Ship_Order,
Package_Items,
Authorize_Payment,
Close_Order
```

Why the model accepts it:

- The root sequence enforces:
  1. `Receive_Order`
  2. `Check_Inventory`
  3. one of the XOR branches
  4. `Close_Order`
- In the successful XOR branch, `Authorize_Payment`, `Package_Items`, and `Ship_Order` are placed under a `PARALLEL` operator.
- A parallel operator allows any interleaving/order of its child activities, as long as each occurs once. Therefore, it permits `Ship_Order` before both `Package_Items` and `Authorize_Payment`.

Why it is invalid in the real Order-to-Cash domain:

- An order should be packaged before it is shipped.
- Payment should normally be authorized before fulfillment/shipping takes place.
- Shipping an item before packaging it is physically and operationally nonsensical; shipping before payment authorization is also generally unacceptable.

Thus, the process tree underfits the intended behavior because its parallel block does not enforce the required dependency:

```text
Authorize_Payment  Package_Items  Ship_Order
```

It also permits other invalid permutations, such as:

```text
Receive_Order, Check_Inventory, Package_Items, Ship_Order, Authorize_Payment, Close_Order
```

where the order is shipped before payment authorization.
