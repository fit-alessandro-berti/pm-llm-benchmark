## Normative Hire-to-Retire sequence

For the hiring portion represented here, a typical successful path is:

`Post_Job_Ad  Screen_Candidates  Conduct_Interviews  Make_Hiring_Decision  Onboard_Employee  Add_to_Payroll  Close_Case`

A complete real-world model would usually also include a rejection or no-hire branch, but neither model represents that explicitly.

## Model 1 analysis

The ordering constraints imply:

- `Post  Screen`
- After screening, both `Interview` and `Decide` become enabled.
- `Decide  Onboard  Payroll  Close`
- There is **no precedence relation between `Interview` and `Decide`**.
- There is also **no relation from `Interview` to the downstream chain**.

Consequently, valid executions can include:

- the hiring decision before interviews;
- onboarding and payroll processing before interviews;
- closing the case before interviews, with the interview occurring only afterward.

Although every activity is mandatory, the interview is effectively an unordered parallel activity once screening has occurred. This is a significant semantic anomaly: the hiring decision should normally depend on completion of the interviews, and case closure should not precede them.

The intended correction would be to add at least:

`Interview  Decide`

That relation would transitively place the interview before onboarding, payroll, and closure.

## Model 2 analysis

Its main flow is:

- `Post  Screen`
- `Post  Interview  Decide`
- `Decide  onboarding loop  optional payroll  Close`

The anomalies are:

1. **Screening is not required before interviewing.**  
   `Screen` and `Interview` are unordered after `Post`, so interviews may begin or even finish before candidate screening.

2. **The hiring decision does not depend on screening.**  
   Since there is no `Screen  Interview` or `Screen  Decide` edge, the process may reach the decision while screening is still incomplete.

3. **Onboarding is repeatable.**  
   `LOOP(Onboard, skip)` executes `Onboard` at least once and permits repetitions, with a silent redo branch. Re-onboarding the same employee without an explicit correction or rework activity is generally abnormal and risks duplicate records or repeated setup actions. It is less fundamental than omitting onboarding because onboarding still occurs at least once.

4. **Payroll can be skipped.**  
   `XOR(Payroll, skip)` makes payroll enrollment optional. For a successfully hired employee, adding the employee to payroll is normally essential. The case can therefore close after onboarding without payroll registration, which is a serious completeness and compliance defect.

Model 2 does correctly enforce:

`Interview  Decide  Onboard  optional Payroll  Close`

Thus, unlike Model 1, it does not permit the decision or closure to occur before the interview.

A technical modeling note is that the same `skip` object is used in both operators. It is generally safer to create separate silent-transition instances for separate control-flow positions, although this is secondary to the business-process anomalies.

## Comparison and conclusion

**Model 1 more closely aligns with the normative process overall, but only narrowly.**

Its central defect is the missing `Interview  Decide` constraint, which allows severe ordering violations. Nevertheless:

- all seven business activities are mandatory;
- onboarding happens once;
- payroll registration is mandatory;
- payroll precedes case closure.

Model 2 preserves the important interview-before-decision order, but introduces several additional integrity problems:

- screening may occur too late;
- onboarding may be duplicated;
- payroll may be omitted entirely.

The optional payroll branch is particularly damaging because it permits a “completed” hiring case for an onboarded employee who was never added to payroll. Therefore, Model 1 is structurally closer to the expected end-to-end successful-hire path, provided that the missing `Interview  Decide` dependency is repaired.

A normative correction would use:

`Post  Screen  Interview  Decide  Onboard  Payroll  Close`

with explicit rejection/no-hire branches added where appropriate.
