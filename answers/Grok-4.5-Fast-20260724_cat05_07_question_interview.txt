Here is a structured list of targeted, open-ended questions designed to deepen conceptual understanding of the property onboarding process. They focus on missing details, decision criteria, roles/responsibilities, timing/sequencing, and exception handling.

### Initial Intake & Documentation
1. What typically triggers the start of the process from the landlord’s side, and how does the regional office first qualify or prioritize incoming requests?
2. Beyond deeds, certificates of compliance, and inspection reports, what other documentation is commonly requested or reviewed, and how do you determine when documentation is “sufficient” to proceed?
3. How are incomplete or outdated documents handled in practice—what is the usual back-and-forth cycle, and who owns the follow-up?

### Property Data Capture & Manager Assignment
4. Once documentation is verified, what additional property attributes or qualitative factors (beyond square footage, units, location, amenities, and price range) are captured in the central database, and why are they considered important?
5. Walk me through how the decision is made when assigning a dedicated property manager—how are the factors of workload, geographic specialization, experience with similar property types, and track record with unique landlord requirements weighted or traded off against one another?
6. Are there situations in which a property manager is reassigned after the initial selection, and what usually drives that change?

### Alignment Meeting & Pre-Activation Steps
7. What does a typical introductory call or meeting with the landlord cover beyond the examples given (pets, maintenance, payment channels), and how are any disagreements or special conditions resolved before moving forward?
8. How is the sequencing and coordination of the photographer and certified inspector managed—do they ever visit together, and what determines the order or timing of their visits relative to other steps?
9. When the inspector identifies issues, how do you decide which repairs are required before activation versus those that can be deferred, and who ultimately decides whether the property is ready to proceed?

### Marketing, Activation & Tenant Placement
10. After the property passes inspection, how does the digital marketing team determine the right pricing strategy and listing approach, and what inputs from the property manager or market data most influence those decisions?
11. What does “activation” in the rental portal and internal systems actually entail from a process perspective, and who confirms that everything is correctly live and synchronized?
12. In cases with multiple interested tenants, how does the property manager or leasing agent decide among competing applications—what criteria tend to carry the most weight, and how are negotiations around lease terms or promotional discounts typically conducted?

### Finalization, Audits & Ongoing Coordination
13. Once a tenant is selected, what are the key hand-off points between the property manager, compliance officer, financial analyst, and IT department during the quality-check and audit phase?
14. How are exceptions—such as properties needing special insurance, landlords insisting on custom lease clauses, or other non-standard situations—identified and routed, and who has authority to approve deviations from the standard process?
15. Throughout the entire onboarding lifecycle, how is communication and status tracked across regional offices, landlords, property managers, and support teams, and what tends to cause the most friction or delays in those information flows?

These questions should surface the nuances, decision logic, and edge cases that the initial description left implicit.
