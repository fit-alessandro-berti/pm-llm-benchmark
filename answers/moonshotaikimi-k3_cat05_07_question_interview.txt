Here are the questions I'd ask, grouped by theme to make the follow-up conversation easier to navigate:

## Process Entry & Scope

1. Walk me through what happens the moment a landlord first contacts the regional office — is there any triage or qualification before a property coordinator gets involved?
2. Does this onboarding process apply uniformly across all cities, or do certain jurisdictions change the steps, documents, or approvals required?
3. How does the process differ, if at all, for a property that already has tenants in place versus a vacant unit?
4. Are there situations where you decline a property at the outset, and what factors drive that decision?

## Documentation & Verification

5. What exactly is on the documentation checklist, and how do the requirements vary by location?
6. How do you define "outdated" for each document type — are there standard validity windows?
7. When documents are missing, what does the follow-up process look like, and at what point do you stop pursuing a landlord who doesn't respond?
8. Does any part of the process continue in parallel while you wait for updated documents, or does everything halt?

## Roles, Responsibilities & Handoffs

9. Can you clarify where the property coordinator's responsibility ends and the property manager's begins? Is there a formal handoff?
10. Who holds ultimate accountability for getting a property fully onboarded — is that one person's job?
11. The compliance officer, financial analyst, and IT checks appear at the end — are those dedicated teams for every onboarding, or shared resources juggling many properties?
12. Under what circumstances can one person fill multiple roles, such as the property manager also acting as the leasing agent?

## Decision Criteria

13. When assigning a property manager, how are the factors you mentioned — workload, geography, experience, track record — weighed against each other? What happens when no manager checks every box?
14. Who actually makes the assignment decision, and can a landlord request or reject a particular manager?
15. How is the expected rental price range determined, and who has final authority if the landlord and your pricing model disagree?
16. When multiple tenants apply, how do you decide which factors take priority — lease length, price, move-in timing, or screening results? Who makes the final call: the property manager, the landlord, or policy?

## Timing & Sequencing

17. How long does a typical end-to-end onboarding take, and where do delays most often occur?
18. Which steps can run in parallel — for example, can photography and inspection happen on the same visit, or does one depend on the other?
19. Are there service-level targets for each phase, and how do you monitor whether a property is stuck?
20. Is there any point where marketing work begins before the inspection is passed, or is that strictly sequential?

## Inspection, Repairs & Issue Resolution

21. What standards does the inspector evaluate against — an internal checklist, local code, or both?
22. When issues are found, how do you and the landlord decide who pays for and arranges repairs?
23. What happens if a landlord refuses to make required repairs or the property repeatedly fails inspection?
24. How are photographers, inspectors, and contractors sourced — employees, approved vendors, or ad hoc — and who pays for their services?

## Marketing, Activation & Listing

25. What approvals are required before a listing goes live, and who signs off?
26. What does "activation" actually entail — what changes in which systems, and is there a final gate before it happens?
27. If the landlord disputes the pricing or marketing approach, how is that resolved?

## Tenant Screening & Lease Finalization

28. What are your standard screening criteria, and how much discretion does the property manager have to deviate from them?
29. How do you ensure tenant selection complies with fair housing regulations, especially when prioritizing certain applicants?
30. When negotiating discounts or custom terms, what authority does the property manager have before needing escalation — and to whom?
31. Who is the legal party to the lease — the landlord, or your company acting as agent?
32. What happens to unsuccessful applicants, and how do you handle a landlord who rejects every candidate you present?

## Quality Checks & Audits

33. Do the compliance, financial, and IT reviews happen before or after the tenant moves in — and what triggers them?
34. Are these checks performed on every property or only a sample?
35. If an audit uncovers a problem after move-in, walk me through how that gets remediated and who owns the fix.

## Exceptions & Edge Cases

36. What triggers a requirement for special insurance, and how does that change the onboarding flow?
37. When a landlord insists on custom lease clauses, who reviews them, and has that ever blocked an onboarding?
38. What happens if the landlord withdraws partway through, or if the assigned property manager leaves mid-process?
39. Can you share an example of a particularly difficult onboarding and how the exceptions were handled?
