The visualization is a process-flow or object-centric process map showing how several business document types interact across purchasing, sales, returns, invoicing, and delivery.

### Main object types at the top
Five source object types feed into the process:

- **VendorInvoiceItem** — 1.2M objects
- **ReturnOrderItem** — 0.8M objects
- **PurchaseRequisitionItem** — 2.0M objects
- **SalesOrderItem** — 3.5M objects
- **PurchaseOrderItem** — 5.0M objects

Each starts through a labeled **Start** event and follows color-coded paths. The colors and colored dots on activities indicate which object types participate in each step.

### Key process activities
The central and right-side workflow includes:

- **Create Requisition** — overall 3.0M
- **Approve Requisition** — overall 5.7M
- **Check Stock** — overall 1.0M
- **Send Order** — overall 5.2M
- **Create Order** — overall 8.5M
- **Pack Goods** — overall 2.0M
- **Ship Goods** — overall 1.8M
- **Confirm Delivery** — overall 1.7M
- **Close Order** — overall 1.7M
- **Process Return** — overall 0.8M
- **Approve Payment** — overall 1.5M
- **Create Invoice** — overall 2.0M

### Overall process structure
There are several interconnected paths:

1. **Purchase requisition flow**  
   Purchase requisition items move from *Create Requisition* to *Approve Requisition*, then may proceed to stock checking, order sending, payment approval, and invoicing.

2. **Sales and purchase order fulfillment flow**  
   Sales order items and purchase order items converge at *Create Order*. The usual downstream sequence is:
   - Create Order  
   - Pack Goods  
   - Ship Goods  
   - Confirm Delivery  
   - Close Order  
   - End

3. **Returns flow**  
   Return order items move through *Process Return*, with connections into later financial/invoice-related steps.

4. **Vendor invoice/payment flow**  
   Vendor invoice items feed into requisition approval and eventually into *Approve Payment* and *Create Invoice*.

### Timing information
The arrows are annotated with elapsed times, mostly in milliseconds—for example:

- 300 ms from requisition item start to *Create Requisition*
- 400 ms from sales order start to *Create Order*
- 350 ms from purchase order start into *Create Order*
- 300 ms from *Create Order* to *Pack Goods*
- 200 ms from *Pack Goods* to *Ship Goods*
- 220 ms from *Ship Goods* to *Confirm Delivery*
- 180 ms from *Confirm Delivery* to *Close Order*

Several paths terminate in separate **End** events, reflecting that different object types or process branches can finish independently.

Overall, it depicts an interconnected order-to-cash / procure-to-pay process rather than a single linear workflow.
