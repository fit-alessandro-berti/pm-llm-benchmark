Here is a list of targeted, open-ended questions I would ask to clarify and deepen my understanding of the property onboarding process:

### Documentation and Verification
- What exactly counts as “complete and verified” documentation? Who is responsible for authenticating documents like deeds and inspection reports?
- If documents are missing or outdated, how long does the landlord have to provide updates? What happens if they repeatedly fail to do so?
- Are there different documentation requirements for different property types, such as multi-family buildings, luxury units, or properties in different cities/regions?

### Property Details and Database Entry
- Which property details are considered mandatory versus optional when entering data into the central database?
- Who is allowed to edit or override property information after it has been recorded?
- Where does the expected rental price range initially come from, and who validates that it is realistic before the property moves forward?

### Property Manager Assignment
- How exactly are the assignment factors—workload, geography, experience, and track record—weighted against each other? Is there a scoring system or is it more subjective?
- Who makes the final decision if multiple property managers could be a good fit? Can the landlord request a different manager?
- What happens if no available property manager perfectly matches the property’s needs? Is there a backup pool or waitlist?

### Inspection and Repairs
- Who is responsible for scheduling the photographer and inspector, and what are the target timeframes for each?
- Who pays for the photographer, inspector, and any necessary repairs? Is this ever negotiable or split with the landlord?
- If the inspector finds serious issues, how many rounds of repair-and-reinspection are allowed? What happens if the landlord refuses to make the repairs?

### Marketing and Pricing
- What input does the landlord have on marketing materials or listing content before it goes live?
- Who ultimately sets the final asking rental price—the property manager, the marketing team, the landlord, or a combination?
- How does the digital marketing team decide which marketing channels to prioritize for a particular property?

### Tenant Screening and Selection
- What are the minimum tenant screening criteria? Are they standardized across the company or adjusted per property/landlord?
- How are the different screening signals—credit score, employment history, landlord references, criminal background—weighted? Is there a point system?
- When multiple tenants are interested, what are the allowed criteria for choosing one? For example, is a longer lease always prioritized over a higher rent offer?
- Who makes the final tenant selection decision? What happens if the landlord rejects the recommended applicant?
- If no suitable tenant applies, what is the fallback: lower rent, wait, or re-list the property?

### Lease, Deposit, and Move-In
- What lease terms are standard, and which terms are allowed to be customized at the landlord’s request?
- How is the security deposit amount determined, and who holds the deposit during the lease?
- Is there a formal move-in inspection with the tenant, and if so, who participates and how is it documented?
- What happens if a tenant wants to move in before the lease is fully executed or before the deposit is collected?

### Compliance, Audits, and Quality Checks
- At what exact stages do the compliance, financial, and IT checks occur? Are they sequential, or can some happen in parallel?
- Who is responsible for correcting issues found in a compliance review, and how long do they have to fix it?
- What happens if the financial analyst determines that the rental price doesn’t meet profitability targets? Can the onboarding be paused mid-way?
- Is there a formal definition of “onboarding complete”? What triggers the final sign-off?

### Exceptions and Communication
- Can you give an example of an unusual exception, such as a special insurance requirement or a custom lease clause, and how it was handled?
- How are communications and handoffs tracked between the regional office, landlord, property manager, and support teams?
- What are the most common bottlenecks or delays you see in this process, and how are they usually resolved?
- Are there any stages where the landlord can change their mind or renegotiate after work has already begun, and what happens then?
