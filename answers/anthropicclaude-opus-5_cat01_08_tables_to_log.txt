## 1. Approach & Assumptions

**Case notion:** `order_id` (ordertocash process instance).

**Event derivation:**

| Source table | Activity name(s) | Timestamp used | Resource used |
|---|---|---|---|
| Orders | `Order Created` | `order_date` | `sales_person_id` |
| Order Lines | `Item Picked` (one event per line) | `item_picking_date` | `picker_id` |
| Delivery | `Order Dispatched`, `Order Arrived` (one pair per delivery record) | `delivery_dispatch_date`, `delivery_arrival_date` | `carrier_id` |
| Invoice | `Invoice Issued` (one per invoice) | `invoice_issue_date` | `billing_person_id` |
| Payments | `Payment Received` (one per payment) | `payment_date` | payer = `customer_id` of the order |

**Assumptions documented:**

1. **Granularity:** One event per order line (`Item Picked`) and one event per delivery leg — this preserves the multiitem / splitshipment behaviour that is analytically interesting (rework, partial shipments). No summarisation was applied.
2. **Payments  Orders:** Payments carry only `invoice_id`; they were joined to the case via `Invoice.invoice_id  Invoice.order_id`.
3. **Payment resource:** No actor is recorded for payments. The paying **customer** is used as the resource (role = *Customer*); alternatively a "Finance/AR system" resource could be used. Activity is named `Payment Received` (orderside view).
4. **Carriers as resources:** Dispatch and arrival are attributed to the carrier organisation (`CRxx`), since no individual driver is recorded. Arrival is in reality confirmed by the carrier/customer; carrier is kept for consistency.
5. **Ordering of simultaneous/nearsimultaneous events:** rows are sorted by `Case ID`, then `Timestamp` ascending. Where a delivery arrival and an invoice fall within minutes (order 1002: arrival of DLV5003 at 10:00 vs. INV1002 at 10:15 on 20240108), the raw timestamps are respected — the log is *not* reordered to fit an idealised model.
6. **Incomplete cases:** Order **1004** has only creation + one picking event (no delivery, invoice, or payment). It is retained as a genuinely open/incomplete case rather than dropped. Order 1004 also has **no delivery record despite being picked** — flagged as a dataquality/process anomaly.
7. **Enrichment:** Item description/category from *Products*; resource name/role from *Resources*. `customer_type` and `order_priority` are caselevel attributes, repeated on the `Order Created` event only (they could be propagated to all rows if the mining tool requires it).
8. All timestamps are UTC (`Z`), already normalised in the source; format `YYYYMMDD HH:MM:SS`.
9. No cancellation, approval, or creditnote data exists, so no such activities were invented.

## 4. Optional Refinements (not applied, but recommended)

- **Milestone events:** add `Picking Completed` (max `item_picking_date` per order) and `Order Fully Delivered` (max arrival) to get a compact, modelfriendly variant set alongside the detailed log.
- **Alternative case notions:** for shipment or billing analysis, use `delivery_id` or `invoice_id` as case ID, or model this as an **objectcentric event log** (objects: Order, Order Line, Delivery, Invoice, Payment) since deliveries/invoices/payments are genuinely manytoone with orders (convergence in the flat log above).
- **Dataquality checks to automate:** orders picked but not shipped (1004), invoice amount vs. sum of payments, arrival before dispatch, invoice before delivery.
