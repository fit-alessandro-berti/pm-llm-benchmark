The visualization is a **BPMN process diagram** for dispatching goods in a computer hardware shop. It uses three swimlanes: **Logistics**, **Secretary**, and **Warehouse**.

### Process flow

1. The process begins with **“Ship goods.”**
2. A parallel gateway starts two activities:
   - The **Secretary** clarifies the shipment method.
   - The **Warehouse** packages the goods.

### Shipment arrangement
After clarifying the shipment method, the secretary checks whether **special handling** is required:

- **Yes:**  
  - Obtain **three offers from logistics companies**.
  - **Select a logistics company and place the order**.

- **No:**  
  - **Check whether insurance is required**, using customer information.
  - If necessary, the **Logistics** team insures the parcel.
  - A **package label is written**.
  - These alternative paths are then merged.

The special-handling and standard-shipping paths converge before shipment preparation continues.

### Packaging and delay handling
The warehouse performs **“Package goods.”** A boundary timer marked **“48 hours”** triggers **“Notify customer about delay”** if packaging takes too long. The normal packaging and delay-notification routes subsequently converge.

### Completion
Once both the shipping arrangement and packaging branches are ready, a parallel gateway synchronizes them. The warehouse then:

1. **Fills out shipment paperwork**
2. **Moves the package to the pickup area**

The process ends at **“Shipment prepared.”**
