### **Process Mining Analysis & Optimization Plan for TechSolve Solutions**

As a Process Improvement Consultant, I propose a structured approach leveraging process mining to transform TechSolve's incident management from a reactive, rule-of-thumb operation into a proactive, optimized workflow. The following analysis utilizes the event log to uncover hidden inefficiencies in resource assignment, quantifies their impact, and prescribes data-driven interventions.

---

### **1. Analyzing Resource Behavior and Assignment Patterns**

To understand the current state, we will apply specific process mining techniques to the event log, moving beyond simple reporting to behavioral analytics.

**Key Metrics & Focus Areas:**
*   **Workload Distribution:** Calculate tickets processed per agent per tier over time. Compute the **Gini Coefficient** of workload to quantify inequality. Identify agents with >90% utilization versus those with <40%.
*   **Performance by Tier/Skill:** Measure **Mean Handling Time** and **First-Call Resolution (FCR)** rates segmented by Agent Tier, Ticket Category, and Required Skill. For example, analyze if L1 agents with `Basic-Troubleshoot` actually resolve `Software-App` tickets without escalation.
*   **Assignment Quality:** Calculate the **Skill Match Ratio**: `(Tickets where Agent_Skills  Required_Skill  ) / Total Tickets`. Track the **Reassignment Frequency** per ticket and per agent.
*   **SLA Performance:** Overlay SLA breach events onto the process. Calculate breach rates by Priority, Category, and Assigned Agent to identify high-risk segments.

**Process Mining Techniques:**
*   **Social Network Analysis (SNA) / Resource Interaction:** Construct a directed graph where nodes are agents/tiers and edges represent handovers (Escalate/Reassign).
    *   *Insight:* This reveals the *actual* flow. We may find "hub" agents who absorb tickets from everyone (potential overload) or "dead ends" where tickets get stuck. It highlights informal networks vs. formal tiers.
    *   *Loop Detection:* Identify cycles in the graph (e.g., `Agent B12  Reassign  Agent B15  Reassign  Agent B12`) which indicate confusion or skill mismatches.
*   **Role Discovery:** Instead of relying on the static `Agent Tier` attribute, use clustering algorithms on activity sequences.
    *   *Insight:* This may reveal "Shadow Roles." For instance, an agent labeled L1 might consistently perform complex `Database-SQL` tasks, indicating a misclassification or that L1 agents are being upskilled informally. Conversely, an L3 agent might be performing L1-level resets.
*   **Utilization vs. Complexity Matrix:** Plot agents based on average ticket complexity (derived from category/skill depth) vs. volume.
    *   *Insight:* Identify quadrants such as "High Skill / Low Complexity" (misuse of specialists) or "Low Skill / High Complexity" (risk of error/reassignment).

**Comparison to Intended Logic:**
If the intended logic is "Round-Robin within Tiers," the SNA and workload metrics should show uniform distribution. Significant deviation indicates the round-robin is failing. If the intention is "Skill-Based," a low Skill Match Ratio confirms the logic is ignored or unknown to the dispatcher/system.

*   **Incorrect Initial Assignments:**
    *   *Analysis:* Analyze cases where `Work Start` occurs but the agent's `Agent_Skills` do not overlap with `Required_Skill`, yet the ticket was resolved quickly (indicating the system failed to enforce skill matching) OR escalated immediately (indicating poor triage).

    *   *Analysis:* Compare workload metrics of agents with high reassignment rates vs. stable agents.

### **3. Root Cause Analysis for Assignment Inefficiencies**

We move from symptoms to causes using advanced process mining diagnostics.

**Root Causes Identified:**
*   **Deficient Assignment Rules:** Variant analysis likely reveals that tickets assigned via "Round-Robin" have significantly higher reassignment rates than those manually assigned by Dispatchers. This suggests round-robin is the primary driver of mismatches.
*   **Static/Inaccurate Skill Profiles:** Role discovery may show agents performing tasks outside their documented skills, or conversely, agents listed with skills never utilized. This indicates skill profiles are not updated or validated.
*   **Poor Categorization/Semantics:** Decision mining on ticket creation attributes may show weak correlation between `Ticket Category` and `Required_Skill`. For example, `Network` tickets frequently require `Firewall` skills, but `Access Management` tickets also require `IAM` skills. The taxonomy may be too broad, causing dispatchers to guess.
*   **Lack of Real-Time Visibility:** If dispatchers rely on manual knowledge of who is free, the uneven workload suggests no centralized view of queue depth per skill exists.
*   **Insufficient L1 Empowerment:** Variant analysis comparing "Smooth Paths" vs. "Reassignment Paths" may show that certain `Software-App` tickets are escalated by L1 despite having resolution patterns identical to L1-resolved tickets. This suggests L1 agents are escalating unnecessarily due to lack of confidence or procedure.

**Diagnostic Techniques:**
*   **Conformance Checking:** Compare the event log against a "Ideal Model" where assignments always match skills and priorities are handled correctly. The deviations highlight exactly where the process breaks down.
*   **Decision Trees / Prediction Trees:** Train a model to predict "Likelihood of Reassignment" based on inputs (Priority, Category, Assigned Agent, Time of Day).
    *   *Result:* The tree may reveal rules like: *"If Category=Software-App AND Agent_Tier=L1 AND Hour=14:00-16:00, then Reassignment Probability > 80%."* This pinpoints specific conditions causing failure.

### **4. Developing Data-Driven Resource Assignment Strategies**

Based on the root causes, I propose three concrete strategies to optimize assignment.

#### **Strategy 1: Skill-Based & Proficiency-Aware Routing Engine**
*   **Issue Addressed:** Skill mismatches, frequent reassignments, misuse of specialists.
*   **PM Insight Leverage:** Uses results from **Role Discovery** to correct agent classifications and **Skill Match Ratio** analysis to weight assignments.
*   **Implementation:**
    *   Replace Round-Robin with a routing engine that assigns tickets based on `Required_Skill` availability.
    *   Incorporate **Proficiency Scores** derived from historical performance (resolution speed + quality score). High-proficiency agents get priority for complex/high-priority tickets; lower-proficiency agents get standard tickets.
    *   Implement **Hard Constraints**: Tickets requiring `Security-IAM` cannot be assigned to agents lacking this skill unless explicitly overridden.
*   **Data Required:** Validated agent skill matrix, proficiency scoring algorithm, real-time queue status.
*   **Expected Benefits:** Reduction in reassignments by ~60%; elimination of specialist waste on low-complexity tasks; improved FCR.

#### **Strategy 2: Workload-Aware Dynamic Balancing with "Smart Queues"**
*   **Issue Addressed:** Uneven workload, SLA breaches, bottlenecks.
*   **PM Insight Leverage:** Uses **Workload Distribution** metrics and **SNA** to identify overloaded hubs and creates queues based on skill clusters rather than just tiers.
*   **Implementation:**
    *   Create dynamic queues grouped by skill domain (e.g., `SQL_Queue`, `CRM_Queue`, `Net_Firewall_Queue`).
    *   Algorithm assigns the next ticket to the agent in the relevant queue with the lowest current load *and* highest predicted capacity (considering estimated handling time per ticket type).
    *   Implement **Threshold Alerts**: If an agent's queue exceeds a threshold, the system automatically pauses new assignments to them and redistributes to peers.
*   **Data Required:** Real-time agent status, estimated handling times per ticket type (from historical logs), capacity limits.
*   **Expected Benefits:** Balanced workload (Gini reduction); P2/P3 SLA breach rate reduction by ~40%; reduced agent burnout.

#### **Strategy 3: Predictive Assignment & Smart Escalation Criteria**
*   **Issue Addressed:** Incorrect initial assignments, unnecessary escalations, slow first response.
*   **PM Insight Leverage:** Uses **Decision Mining** and **Variant Analysis** to build predictive models for assignment and escalation.
*   **Implementation:**
    *   **Predictive Assignment:** Upon ticket creation, use NLP on the description and metadata to predict `Required_Skill` and `Complexity`. The system pre-selects the best agent/tier before the dispatcher even sees it.
    *   **Smart Escalation:** Update L1 guidelines based on variant analysis. If certain patterns (e.g., specific keywords in `Software-App` tickets) correlate with immediate resolution, empower L1 to close them. If patterns correlate with reassignment, force escalation immediately rather than letting L1 struggle.
    *   **Auto-Escalation:** If a ticket remains unassigned or unresolved past a dynamic threshold based on priority and skill scarcity, auto-escalate to the next available specialist tier.
*   **Data Required:** Historical ticket descriptions, labels, resolution outcomes, trained ML models for prediction.
*   **Expected Benefits:** Faster Time-to-Assign; Increase in L1 FCR by ~20%; Reduction in "wasted effort" tickets.
