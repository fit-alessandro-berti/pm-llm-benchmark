### 3. How These Factors Increase Cycle Time & Recommendations

#### How the Bottlenecks Drive Cycle Time
- **Escalation as a multiplier:** Escalation does not just add one step; it shifts the ticket into a separate queue with its own scheduling dynamics. In cases 102 and 105, the escalation event is followed not by immediate action but by a **multi-hour or multi-day idle period**. - **Queue buildup at investigation:** Case 104 demonstrates that even non-escalated tickets suffer when investigation is delayed. A 3.5-hour pre-investigation lag, combined with an overnight stall, turns a same-day fix into a 24-hour cycle.
- **Batch-like resolution patterns:** The consistency of “next-day morning resolution” (around 08:00–09:00) across cases 102, 104, and 105 implies that resolution activity may be concentrated at the start of a work shift, creating a daily bottleneck.

#### Recommendations to Address Performance Bottlenecks
