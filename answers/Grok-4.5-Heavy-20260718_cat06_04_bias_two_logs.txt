**The Event Log for Group B (Unprotected Group) exhibits the bias.**

### Comparison of the logs
Both logs follow the identical process structure and resources:
- Activities: ApplicationReceived → DataValidation → PreliminaryScoring → ManualReview → FinalDecision.
- Resources: Automated Sys, Checker, Scoring Engine, Underwriter, Rules Engine.
- Timestamps are comparable (minor natural variation, no systematic delay pattern favoring one group).

**Group A (Protected Group)** characteristics:
- LocalResident = FALSE and CommunityGroup = None for every case (P001–P003).
- ScoreAdjustment is uniformly 0 (or N/A in ManualReview).
- Final decisions rest solely on the unadjusted PreliminaryScore: 720 Approved, 710 Rejected, 740 Approved.
- Apparent decision threshold on the score used by the Rules Engine is between 710 and 720 (approve if roughly ≥720).

**Group B (Unprotected Group)** characteristics:
- LocalResident = TRUE for every case.
- CommunityGroup is either “Highland Civic Darts Club” or None.
- When CommunityGroup = “Highland Civic Darts Club”, the Scoring Engine applies ScoreAdjustment = +10 (Community Boost) at the PreliminaryScoring step; the adjusted score then propagates through ManualReview and FinalDecision.
  - U001: 720 → 730 Approved  
  - U003: 695 → 705 Approved  
  - U002 (CommunityGroup = None): 710 → 0 adjustment → Rejected.

### How the bias manifests
The bias is **disparate treatment** concentrated in the scoring/adjustment step of Group B’s log:

1. **Selective positive adjustment conditioned on group-linked attributes**  
   The +10 Community Boost is applied only when LocalResident = TRUE *and* CommunityGroup = “Highland Civic Darts Club”. These attributes appear exclusively in the Unprotected Group log; the Protected Group log has no pathway to any positive ScoreAdjustment.

2. **Systematic alteration of decision outcomes**  
   - A low preliminary score that would almost certainly be rejected under Group A’s rules (U003’s 695) becomes approvable after the boost (705 Approved).  
   - Equal or higher scores that receive no boost are rejected (P002’s 710 and U002’s 710).  
   This effectively lowers the approval bar for members of the favored community club while leaving Protected applicants (and non-club Unprotected applicants) subject to the stricter unadjusted threshold.

3. **Influence of LocalResident and CommunityGroup**  
   LocalResident acts as a gate (only TRUE cases ever receive the boost opportunity). CommunityGroup then determines whether the boost is actually granted. Because these attributes define or strongly correlate with membership in the Unprotected Group, the scoring rules embed preferential treatment. The Protected Group is structurally denied any equivalent score inflation, producing unequal process conditions even when preliminary scores are similar or when overall approval rates happen to look balanced in this tiny sample (2/3 in each log).

In short, Group B’s event log explicitly records the preferential ScoreAdjustment mechanism; Group A’s log records the absence of any such advantage. The resulting process systematically advantages certain Unprotected applicants on the basis of LocalResident and CommunityGroup, which is the source of the observed bias.
