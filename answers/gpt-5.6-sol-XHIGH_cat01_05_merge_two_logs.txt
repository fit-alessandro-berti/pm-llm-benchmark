## Matching policy

- Events were matched on `order_id`, normalized event meaning, and timestamp proximity.
- A tolerance of **±2 seconds, inclusive**, was used. This treats events exactly two seconds apart as matches.
- For merged events, the **Log A timestamp is the primary timestamp**, since Log A is the designated primary timeline. Both original timestamps and event names are retained.
- Events outside the tolerance were left separate unless there was unambiguous evidence that they were the same occurrence.

## Match reasoning

- **Order Received / OrderReceived:** Merged because the names are formatting variants, the order IDs match, and the timestamps differ by exactly 2 seconds.
- **Order Validated / OrderValidation:** Merged because the names describe the same activity and the timestamps differ by 1 second.
- **Payment Processed / PaymentCheck:** Left separate. Although both are payment-related, they differ by 5 seconds and may represent different stages: checking payment versus confirming that payment was processed.
- **Quality Check:** Retained as a Log B-only event because no corresponding Log A event exists.
- **Item Shipped / Shipping:** Merged because the names are semantic equivalents and the timestamps differ by exactly 2 seconds.
- **Item Delivered:** Retained as a Log A-only event because Log B has no corresponding delivery event.

If the tolerance were interpreted strictly as **less than** 2 seconds rather than **up to** 2 seconds, the Order Received and Shipping pairs would instead remain separate.
