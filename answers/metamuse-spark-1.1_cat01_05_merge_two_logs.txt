1.  **Primary Timestamp:** For merged events, `timestamp_primary` = Log A timestamp because A is defined as primary timeline. I preserve both original timestamps in `timestamp_A` and `timestamp_B` for auditability.
2.  **Canonical Event Name:** I created a unified name using Log A terminology for consistency: `Order Received`, `Order Validated`, `Payment Processed`, `Quality Check`, `Item Shipped`, `Item Delivered`.
3.  **Attribute Enrichment:** Merged record = All attributes from A + All attributes from B. `user_id`, `resource_id`, `notes` come from B. Missing attributes are `NULL`.
