### Matching policy used

- Matched events only when:
  1. `order_id` was the same,
  2. event names were semantically equivalent, and
  3. timestamps were within an inclusive **±2-second tolerance**.
- Log A is used as the primary timeline timestamp for matched records, because it is designated as the primary log. The original Log B timestamp is retained.
- Events outside the tolerance, even if semantically related, were retained as separate records unless there was enough evidence to confidently treat them as the same occurrence.

### Event-name normalization used

| Log A event type | Log B event name | Unified event |
|---|---|---|
| Order Received | OrderReceived | Order Received |
| Order Validated | OrderValidation | Order Validated |
| Item Shipped | Shipping | Item Shipped |

`Payment Processed` and `PaymentCheck` are related, but their timestamps differ by 5 seconds and they may represent distinct stages (payment completion versus a gateway/check operation), so they were not merged.

## Matching decisions

1. **Order Received**
   - Log A: `2023-10-10T10:00:00Z`, `Order Received`
   - Log B: `2023-10-10T09:59:58Z`, `OrderReceived`
   - Difference: 2 seconds.
   - Decision: merged because the order ID matches, names are equivalent after normalization, and the timestamps are at the inclusive 2-second boundary.

2. **Order Validated**
   - Log A: `2023-10-10T10:01:30Z`, `Order Validated`
   - Log B: `2023-10-10T10:01:29Z`, `OrderValidation`
   - Difference: 1 second.
   - Decision: merged. The names clearly describe the same validation activity and the timestamps are within tolerance.

3. **Payment Processed / PaymentCheck**
   - Log A: `2023-10-10T10:02:00Z`, `Payment Processed`
   - Log B: `2023-10-10T10:02:05Z`, `PaymentCheck`
   - Difference: 5 seconds.
   - Decision: left separate. Although both concern payment, the timing is outside the matching tolerance and the names may describe different operational steps. Log B’s note, “Payment gateway delay,” further supports preserving the payment check as a distinct event.

4. **Quality Check**
   - Appears only in Log B.
   - Decision: retained as a B-only event, including its user, resource, and inspection note.

5. **Item Shipped / Shipping**
   - Log A: `2023-10-10T10:05:00Z`, `Item Shipped`
   - Log B: `2023-10-10T10:05:02Z`, `Shipping`
   - Difference: 2 seconds.
   - Decision: merged because the names are equivalent, the order IDs match, and the timestamps are within the inclusive tolerance.

6. **Item Delivered**
   - Appears only in Log A.
   - Decision: retained as an A-only event because no corresponding Log B event exists.

The resulting log preserves Log A’s primary timestamps for merged records while enriching them with Log B’s user, resource, notes, alternate event name, and original timestamp.
