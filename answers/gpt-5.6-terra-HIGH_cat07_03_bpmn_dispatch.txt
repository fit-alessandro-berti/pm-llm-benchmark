The visualization is a BPMN-style swimlane process diagram for **dispatching goods** at a **Computer Hardware Shop**. It is organized into three responsibility lanes:

- **Logistics** (top)
- **Security** (middle)
- **Warehouse** (bottom)

### Overall process flow
1. The process starts with **“Ship goods.”**
2. A **parallel gateway** splits the process into two simultaneous tracks:
   - **Clarify shipment method** in the Security lane.
   - **Package goods** in the Warehouse lane.

### Shipment-method and logistics track
3. After clarifying the shipment method, an exclusive decision asks **“Special handling?”**
   - One branch proceeds to **“Get 3 offers from logistic companies”** and then **“Select logistic company and place order.”**
   - The other branch goes to **“Check if insurance is required.”**

4. The insurance check uses **Customer information** as a data input.
   - The process always proceeds to **“Write package label.”**
   - If insurance is necessary, it also goes to the Logistics lane to **“Insure parcel.”**
   - These insurance/label activities are merged before rejoining the selected-logistics route.

### Warehouse track
5. In parallel, the warehouse **packages the goods**.
   - A timer boundary event indicates a **48-hour** threshold.
   - If that threshold is reached, the process sends **“Notify customer about delay.”**
   - The packaging and delay-notification paths rejoin.

### Completion
6. The warehouse packaging work and the shipping/logistics preparation are synchronized at a parallel join.
7. The final warehouse tasks are:
   - **Fill out shipment paperwork**
   - **Move package to pickup area**
8. The process ends with the event **“Shipment prepared.”**

The diagram also includes an **“Order details”** document artifact and a **Customer information** data store, showing information used during the process.
