## 1. Normative reference model

A standard HiretoRetire (hiring) flow is essentially a strict sequence, where every step depends on the outcome of the previous one:

```
Post_Job_Ad  Screen_Candidates  Conduct_Interviews  Make_Hiring_Decision
             Onboard_Employee  Add_to_Payroll  Close_Case
```

Key logical invariants:
- Screening precedes interviewing (you interview the candidates you shortlisted).
- Interviews precede the hiring decision (the decision must be evidencebased).
- Onboarding happens exactly once, only after a positive decision.
- Payroll registration is **mandatory** – an onboarded employee who is not in payroll is a compliance/legal defect.
- The case is closed last.

---

## 2. Anomalies in Model 1

Order relation: `Post  Screen`, `Screen  Interview`, `Screen  Decide`, `Decide  Onboard  Payroll  Close`.

| # | Anomaly | Severity |
|---|---------|----------|
| A1 | **`Conduct_Interviews` and `Make_Hiring_Decision` are unordered (concurrent).** Both only depend on `Screen_Candidates`. The decision may therefore be taken *before* or *simultaneously with* the interviews. | Moderate–high (violates evidencebased decision logic) |
| A2 | **`Conduct_Interviews` is a "dangling" node** – it has no outgoing edge. Interviews may legally occur after onboarding, after payroll entry, even after `Close_Case`. | Moderate |

What Model 1 *preserves*: every one of the seven activities is executed **exactly once**; the core backbone `Post  Screen  …  Decide  Onboard  Payroll  Close` is intact; no activity can be skipped or repeated. The defect is purely one of **underspecified ordering** around the interview step.

---

## 3. Anomalies in Model 2

Order relation: `Post  Screen`, `Post  Interview`, `Interview  Decide  LOOP(Onboard, skip)  XOR(Payroll, skip)  Close`.

| # | Anomaly | Severity |
|---|---------|----------|
| B1 | **`XOR(Add_to_Payroll, )` – payroll registration can be skipped entirely.** An employee can be onboarded and the case closed without ever being added to payroll: no salary, no tax/socialsecurity record, broken HR–Finance data integrity. | **Critical – destroys the essence of HiretoRetire** |
| B2 | **`LOOP(Onboard_Employee, )` – onboarding can be repeated arbitrarily many times** (Onboard, exit or  then Onboard again). Repeated onboarding of the same person creates duplicate employee records/contracts. Onboarding is not a naturally iterative step. | High |
| B3 | **`Screen_Candidates` is completely decoupled**: it only follows `Post_Job_Ad` and has no successor. Interviews (and even the decision, onboarding, payroll and case closure) can happen *before* screening, or screening may effectively become meaningless posthoc paperwork. | High |
| B4 | Consequently, **`Conduct_Interviews` may precede `Screen_Candidates`**, inverting the funnel logic (interviewing unscreened applicants). | Moderate |

Model 2 does keep `Interview  Decide`, which Model 1 loses – that is its only relative advantage.

---

## 4. Which model is closer to the normative process?

**Model 1 is clearly closer to the normative HiretoRetire process.**

Reasoning:

1. **Nothing mandatory can disappear in Model 1.** All seven activities are guaranteed to be executed exactly once. Model 2 allows a trace `Post, Interview, Decide, Onboard, Close` (screening late/irrelevant, payroll skipped) – a trace that is not merely badly ordered but **incomplete**: the employment relationship is never made effective in the payroll system. Missing a mandatory compliance step is a fundamentally more severe conformance and control violation than a loose ordering constraint.

2. **Nothing can be duplicated in Model 1.** Model 2's loop over `Onboard_Employee` permits multiple onboardings of the same hire, which corrupts master data and has no plausible business justification (unlike, say, a loop over screening or interview rounds, which would be defensible).

3. **Model 1's anomalies are localized ordering weaknesses.** The parallelism between `Conduct_Interviews` and `Make_Hiring_Decision` (and the dangling interview node) means the decision may not be properly substantiated – a real defect, but one that involves the *same* set of activities in a partially wrong order. Such a model still records a complete, auditable case; the deviation is detectable and correctable by tightening one edge (`Interview  Decide`).

4. **Model 2 breaks two different structural invariants at once** – optionality of a mandatory step (payroll) *and* unbounded repetition of a onceonly step (onboarding) – on top of the same class of ordering problem that Model 1 has (screening/interview decoupling instead of interview/decision decoupling).

**Ranking of severity (worst first):** B1 (payroll skippable) > B2 (onboarding loop) > B3/B4 (screening decoupled/after interviews) > A1 (decision concurrent with interviews) > A2 (interview unordered w.r.t. rest).

**Minimal repairs:**
- *Model 1:* add `order.add_edge(Interview, Decide)` (and thereby make Interview a true predecessor of onboarding)  fully normative.
- *Model 2:* remove the XOR around `Add_to_Payroll` (make it mandatory), replace `LOOP(Onboard, )` with a single `Onboard_Employee`, and add `Screen  Interview`.

Model 2 needs three structural corrections; Model 1 needs one edge. Hence Model 1 is the better approximation of the normative HiretoRetire process.
