### Method Notes (how the labels were derived)
- **Segmentation**: Visual / statistical change-point detection on the multi-variate series (especially jumps in temperature, vibration energy product, material-flow, and tool position). Contiguous windows share a coherent operating regime.
- **Label mapping**: Matched each regime’s sensor fingerprint to physically plausible manufacturing operations:
  - High vibration + continuous material flow + advancing tool  cutting.
  - Elevated constant pressure + held tool position  assembly/clamping.
  - Extreme thermal + power spike with zero flow  welding.
  - Low residual activity after the above  inspection or light finishing.
- Domain priors (the suggested activity vocabulary) were used only after the pure data-driven clusters were formed; the numeric patterns themselves drive the grouping.
- No deep learning or external training data is required—pure unsupervised pattern discovery on the raw numeric log, which is the classic first step in process mining of unlabeled sensor streams.

You can now treat the labeled segments as a discrete event log for further process mining (process discovery, conformance checking, performance analysis, etc.). If more cycles or additional machines become available, the same clustering + regime-labeling approach can be automated (e.g., via change-point detection + clustering on feature windows).
