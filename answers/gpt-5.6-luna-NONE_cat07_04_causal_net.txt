The visualization is a **workflow/Petri-net-style process diagram** for handling a registration request.

- The process begins at **a: “register request.”**
- It can branch into several activities:
  - **b: “examine thoroughly”**
  - **c: “examine casually”**
  - **d: “check ticket”**
- These paths converge at **e: “decide.”**
- From the decision, the request can:
  - proceed to **g: “pay compensation,”** then finish at **z: “end”**
  - proceed to **h: “reject request,”** then finish at **z**
  - be sent to **f: “reinitiate request,”** which loops back into the earlier examination/decision stages.

Black dots and arrows represent control-flow connections and synchronization points. The looping paths indicate that a request may be reconsidered or resubmitted.

The gray inset at the bottom explains the notation for different types of branching and merging:

- **XOR-split / XOR-join:** one alternative path is selected.
- **AND-split / AND-join:** multiple paths are executed in parallel and later synchronized.
- **OR-split / OR-join:** one or more alternative paths may be selected and subsequently merged.
