# BPMN-like Process Model: **Invoice Approval Process**

Below is a structured textual representation of the process derived from the five interviews. It is organized into **Pools/Lanes** (roles), **Activities**, **Gateways**, **Sequence Flows**, **Artifacts**, and **Exception Paths**. Ambiguities across accounts are explicitly flagged.

---

## 1. Participants / Lanes (Pool: "Invoice Approval Process")

| Lane | Role | Description |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | **Accounts Payable Clerk (Mary)** | Initial intake, logging, matching, scheduling payment |
| L2 | **Purchasing Officer (Dan)** | Confirms receipt of goods/services, validates PO match |
| L3 | **Department Manager (Carlos)** | Final approval authority |
| L4 | **AP Supervisor (Rita)** | Handles escalations/exceptions |
| L5 | **Supplier Relationship Manager (Karen)** | Resolves supplier-side discrepancies (missing PO, pricing) |
| L6 | **Supplier** (external participant) | Provides invoices, corrections, missing PO info |
| — | **Accounting/ERP System** | Logs invoices, triggers automatic approval emails |

---

## 2. Artifacts / Data Objects

- **Invoice** (received via email)
- **Purchase Order (PO)**
- **Goods/Service Receipt Confirmation**
- **Match/Discrepancy Notification**
- **Manager Approval Record (system-generated)**
- **Retrospective PO** (created for informally contracted services)
- **Payment Schedule Entry**

---

## 3. Process Flow (Main Path)

**Start Event:** *Invoice received via email* (Lane: AP Clerk)

1. **Task (AP – Mary):** Check invoice for valid PO number  
    **Gateway G1 (Exclusive): "PO number present?"**
   - **[No]**  **Task (AP – Mary):** Send invoice back to supplier requesting PO/details  *(flow returns to Supplier; process pauses until corrected invoice resubmitted  loops back to Start)*
   - **[Yes]**  Continue to Step 2

2. **Task (AP – Mary):** Log invoice into accounting system  
    **Sequence Flow ** Forward invoice to Purchasing

3. **Task (Purchasing – Dan):** Confirm goods/services were received  
    **Task (Purchasing – Dan):** Compare invoice (quantities, prices, delivery charges) against PO  
    **Gateway G2 (Exclusive): "Invoice matches PO?"**
   - **[Discrepancy]**  **Task (Purchasing – Dan):** Contact supplier directly, OR instruct AP to hold invoice pending corrected version  
     - **Sub-path:** If issue involves missing PO or pricing dispute  **Task (Supplier Relationship Manager – Karen):** Contact supplier / verify contract terms  provide correct info back to AP or Purchasing  
     - **Sub-path:** If discrepancy cannot be resolved between AP and Purchasing  **Escalation Task (AP Supervisor – Rita):** Investigate, coordinate between departments, contact supplier if needed  
       - **Special Case:** Invoice does not match any PO (informal service arrangement)  **Task (Rita + Department Head):** Create **Retrospective PO**, OR decide to reject invoice  
     - **Loop back ** Once resolved, flow returns to Step 3 (Purchasing re-confirms match)
   - **[Match Confirmed]**  **Task (Purchasing – Dan):** Send confirmation email to AP

4. **Task (AP – Mary):** Match invoice line items against PO in system  
   *(Note: This step appears related to/possibly overlapping with Dan's match check in Step 3 — interviewees describe this inconsistently; it may represent a secondary system-level verification by AP after Purchasing's confirmation, or the same activity described from two perspectives.)*  
    **Gateway G3 (Exclusive): "Match confirmed & receipt confirmed?"**
   - **[No / Discrepancy]**  **Task (AP – Mary):** Email Purchasing team for clarification  *(loop back to Step 3/4)*
     - If unresolved  **Escalation Task (AP Supervisor – Rita)** *(same exception handling as above)*
   - **[Yes]**  **System Task:** Automatic email sent to Department Manager requesting approval

5. **Task (Manager – Carlos):** Review invoice, PO, and confirmation details in system  
    **Gateway G4 (Exclusive): "Approve invoice?"**
   - **[Suspicious / Rejected]**  **Task (Manager – Carlos):** Reject or instruct AP (Mary) to hold and investigate further  *(loop back to AP/Supervisor exception handling)* *(Carlos notes this occurs infrequently — roughly once every couple of months)*
   - **[Approved]**  System records approval  Flow returns to AP

6. **Task (AP – Mary):** Schedule invoice for next payment cycle

**End Event:** *Invoice scheduled for payment*

---

## 4. Exception / Escalation Sub-Process (AP Supervisor – Rita)

Triggered when:
- AP cannot match invoice to PO, **or**
- Purchasing identifies an unresolved discrepancy, **or**
- Invoice has missing/incomplete information

**Steps:**
1. Rita receives escalation from Mary or Dan.
2. Rita contacts supplier directly or coordinates with Purchasing/Department Heads.
3. **Gateway G5: "Does invoice correspond to a valid (even informal) purchase?"**
   - **[Yes, but no formal PO]**  Create **Retrospective PO** with department head  rejoin main flow at Step 3 (Purchasing confirmation)
   - **[No valid justification]**  Reject invoice  **End Event (Rejected)**
4. Once resolved, process **rejoins main flow**: Purchasing confirmation  Manager approval  Payment scheduling.

*Rita does not perform final approvals; her role is limited to unblocking stalled invoices.*

---

## 5. Supplier Relationship Manager (Karen) – Support Sub-Process

Triggered when AP or Purchasing identifies:
- Missing PO number, **or**
- Pricing discrepancy relative to contract terms

**Steps:**
1. Karen is looped in by AP or Purchasing.
2. **Gateway G6: "Issue type?"**
   - **[Missing PO]**  Karen requests supplier provide PO reference or revised invoice
   - **[Pricing Discrepancy]**  Karen checks contract terms  informs AP/Purchasing of correct price
3. Karen returns resolution to AP/Purchasing  process resumes at Step 3 or 4.

*Karen does not have system access to approve invoices; her role is advisory/resolution support only.*

---

## 6. Noted Ambiguities & Conflicting Accounts

1. **Duplicate Matching Steps:** Mary describes matching invoice line items to PO *after* receiving Purchasing's confirmation, while Dan describes performing essentially the same match himself. It is unclear whether:
   - (a) Both perform independent verification (double-check), or
   - (b) These are the same step described from two role perspectives.  
    **Recommendation:** Clarify with both parties whether AP's "matching" step is redundant or a distinct system-level check.

2. **Sequence After Manager Approval:** Dan "assumes" the invoice goes to the Manager after his confirmation and then back to AP, but does not have direct visibility into this handoff. Carlos confirms this assumption. Mary's account is treated as authoritative for this transition.

3. **Escalation Path Variability:** Rita's escalation process is invoked inconsistently — sometimes triggered by AP (Mary), sometimes by Purchasing (Dan). The exact threshold for escalation (vs. resolving directly between AP and Purchasing) is not clearly defined in the interviews.

4. **Return Path for Missing PO:** Mary states she sends the invoice back to the supplier if no PO is found, but Karen also describes contacting suppliers for missing PO numbers. It is unclear whether Mary always contacts the supplier directly or sometimes delegates this to Karen.

5. **Rejection Frequency:** Carlos notes manager-level rejection is rare, but no interviewee specifies formal criteria for rejection beyond "suspicious" discrepancies — this decision point (G4) may benefit from more precise business rules.

## 7. Summary Diagram Legend (for BPMN Conversion)

- **Rounded rectangles** = Tasks (labeled with lane/role)
- **Diamond shapes** = Exclusive Gateways (G1–G6)
- **Solid arrows** = Sequence flow
- **Dashed arrows** = Message flow (e.g., AP  Supplier, AP  Purchasing)
- **Data object icons** = Invoice, PO, Confirmation, Retrospective PO, Payment Schedule
- **Sub-process boundaries** = Escalation (Rita) and Supplier Resolution (Karen) sub-processes, called from main flow via exception triggers

---

This model captures the **consensus main flow** (Invoice  PO Validation  Logging  Purchasing Confirmation  AP Matching  Manager Approval  Payment Scheduling), while explicitly representing the **exception-handling sub-processes** (Supervisor escalation, Supplier Relationship resolution) and flagging areas where interview accounts diverge or leave sequence/ownership ambiguous.
