The visualization presents a healthcare laboratory workflow in three parts:

1. **Process interaction diagram (a):**  
   - A **Lab visit** process on the left includes entering an order, taking a blood sample, receiving and judging results, and either finishing or requesting tests again.  
   - A **Lab test** process on the right includes performing the test, making a report, potentially repeating the test, providing an updated report, and finishing.  
   - The two processes communicate through two dashed communication channels: the **Order system** and **HIS** (Hospital Information System).  
   - Roles such as **doctor**, **lab assistant**, and **chemical analyst** are shown beside the activities they perform. Arrows, events, and branching points indicate sequence, messages, and decision paths.

2. **Class diagram (b):**  
   A **Lab visit** “requires” **Lab test** instances. The multiplicities indicate that each lab test belongs to one lab visit, while a lab visit can require one or more tests.

3. **Message/performative example (c):**  
   A table records an outgoing private message at **11:00** through the **Order system**, sent from **“Lab visit – John”** to **“Lab test – HGB John.”** Its action is **Create**, with the content: *“Can you perform a HGB test for John?”*
