The intended valid paths are:

- **Fulfillment path:** Receive order  Check inventory  Authorize payment  Package items  Ship order  Close order
- **Insufficient-inventory cancellation path:** Receive order  Check inventory  Cancel order  Close order

### Case-by-case assessment

| Case | Conformant? | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Yes | Follows the fulfillment sequence exactly: receive, inventory check, payment authorization, packaging, shipping, then closure. |
| **2** | **No** | **Package items** occurs before **Authorize payment**. The description explicitly requires payment authorization after inventory checking and **before packaging**. Although shipment occurs after both activities, the order between authorization and packaging is invalid. |
| **3** | **No** | The case contains **Cancel order** followed by **Ship order**. The cancellation branch requires the order to proceed from cancellation to closure, and the text explicitly says shipping must never occur after cancellation. In addition, authorizing payment before a cancellation is inconsistent with the stated insufficient-inventory cancellation path, though the clearest violation is shipping after cancellation. |
| **4** | Yes | Follows the cancellation scenario: receive, check inventory, cancel, close. |
| **5** | **No** | **Ship order** occurs without a preceding **Package items** activity. The normative process requires packaging after authorization and before shipping. |

### Violating cases

The cases that deviate from the normative process are therefore:

- **Case 2:** Packaging before payment authorization.
- **Case 3:** Shipping after cancellation; cancellation should lead to closure rather than fulfillment actions.
- **Case 5:** Shipping without first packaging the items.

Cases **1** and **4** conform to the expected fulfillment and cancellation paths, respectively.
