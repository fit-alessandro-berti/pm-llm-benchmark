## 1. Recurring activities and contextual differences

- **Verify Documents**
  - First occurrence: initial identity/document verification.
  - Later occurrence: re-verification after missing documents or account changes.

- **Call Customer**
  - In C001: call to request missing documentation.
  - In C002: call to clarify an account discrepancy.
  - Second occurrence in C002: follow-up call to confirm an internal adjustment.
  - In C003: call asking the customer to return to the branch.

- **Approve Request**
  - Occurs once per case and does not recur within a case, so it does not require disambiguation.

- **Initiate Request** and **Adjust Account**
  - Also occur only once per case in the supplied log. Their contextual roles can nevertheless be made explicit in the revised labels.

## 4. Justification

- **Initial vs. follow-up document verification:** The position in the case and the preceding events distinguish the first identity check from later checks performed after additional documentation or corrections.
- **Different customer calls:** The call purpose and surrounding activities provide the distinction:
  - missing-document request,
  - account-discrepancy clarification,
  - confirmation of an adjustment,
  - request for a customer to return to the branch.
- **Request initiation:** “Submit Online Request” and “Submit Walk-in Request” distinguish the channel, based on the resource and context.
- **Account adjustment:** “Correct Account Details” makes clear that the activity is an internal correction rather than a customer-facing action.
