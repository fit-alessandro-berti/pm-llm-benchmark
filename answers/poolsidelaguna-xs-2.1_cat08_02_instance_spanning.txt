### **2. Analyzing Constraint Interactions**

**Key Interactions:**  
1. **Express + Cold-Packing:**  
   - An Express order requiring Cold-Packing preempts a Standard order, increasing the latter’s waiting time. This can create a feedback loop where delays cascade.  
2. **Hazardous + Batching:**  
   - If a batch includes hazardous orders, regulatory limits may force delays in batch formation (e.g., waiting to ensure 10 concurrent hazardous orders). This could stall non-hazardous orders in the same region.  
3. **Batching + Priority:**  
   - Express orders may delay batches if their urgency disrupts the grouping of regional orders.  

**Importance of Understanding Interactions:**  
- Interactions amplify delays. For example, an Express order blocking Cold-Packing stations (Constraint 1) may also delay hazardous orders (Constraint 4), which then affects batch formation (Constraint 2).  
- Mitigation requires holistic strategies that balance all constraints simultaneously.
