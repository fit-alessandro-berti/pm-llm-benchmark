The visualization depicts a workflow for handling a registered request.

- The process starts with **a: “register request.”**
- The request can proceed through several review or checking activities:
  - **b: “examine thoroughly”**
  - **c: “examine casually”**
  - **d: “check ticket”**
- These paths converge on **e: “decide.”**
- The decision then branches into three possible outcomes:
  - **g: “pay compensation”**
  - **h: “reject request”**
  - **f: “reinitiate request,”** which creates a feedback loop back to earlier examination/checking steps.
- The payment and rejection paths both lead to **z: “end.”**

Black circles and connecting arrows represent routing points such as splits and joins. The gray panel at the lower right explains the notation:

- **XOR-split/join:** choose or merge one alternative
- **AND-split/join:** activate or synchronize all branches
- **OR-split/join:** activate or merge one or more branches

Thus, the main diagram combines sequential tasks, alternative and possibly parallel routing, joins, and a loop for reprocessing a request.
