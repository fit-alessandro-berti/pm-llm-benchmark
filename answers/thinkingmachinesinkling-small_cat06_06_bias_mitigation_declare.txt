### Rationale for the added constraints

| Constraint(s) Added | Rationale |
|---|---|
| **Coexistence** (`Approve_Minority` / `Reject_Minority`  `ManualReview`) | When a decision is made for an applicant from a sensitive demographic, a `ManualReview` must appear in the same trace. This prevents automated, unexamined rejections or approvals for protected groups. |
| **Response** (`CheckApplicantRace`  `BiasMitigationCheck`) | If an attribute such as race is explicitly checked (`CheckApplicantRace`), the process must respond by performing a `BiasMitigationCheck`. This interrupts a direct path from disclosure to decision. |
| **Precedence** (`BiasMitigationCheck` before `Approve` / `Reject`) | Both final decision activities must be preceded by the fairness audit (`BiasMitigationCheck`). Thus neither `Approve` nor `Reject` can occur in isolation; they require a verified mitigation step first. |
| **Non-chain succession** (`CheckApplicantRace`  `Reject` / `Approve`) | It is prohibited for the sensitive-attribute event to be *immediately* followed by a decision event. This stops “snap judgments” where a race disclosure is instantly followed by Reject/Approve without an intervening fairness step (the mitigation check, which is required by the precedence/response rules above). |

Together these rules enforce that (1) any decision involving sensitive demographics is accompanied by manual oversight, (2) disclosure of sensitive data triggers a mandatory mitigation step, and (3) a decision cannot be made immediately after that disclosure without the mitigating audit, thereby reducing the risk of biased outcomes in the loan-application pipeline.
