## 2. Root Cause Analysis by Attribute

- **Low complexity**  0 document requests  ~1.5h (same-day processing, regardless of region or resource)
- **Medium**  1 request  ~26h
- **High**  2–3 requests  ~48–77h

Each "Request Additional Documents" round adds **~20–30 hours** of elapsed time (mostly overnight waiting — likely customer response latency). High-complexity claims trigger *iterative* requests, and each round compounds the delay multiplicatively rather than additively.

###  Resource — secondary/interaction effects
- **Adjusters (Mike vs. Lisa):** Both handle low-complexity cases in minutes, so neither is inherently slow. However, Mike's document rounds (case 2003) completed within the same business day (1h20m, 6h), while Lisa's rounds in case 2005 took **29.5h and 22h each**. - **Finance_Alan:** Payment on case 2003 took **17h** vs. his 15-minute payment on the low-complexity case — high-complexity (likely high-value) payments seem to require extra verification, at least in Region A.

###  Region — no main effect, but an interaction effect

## 3. Explanations & Mitigation Recommendations

**Why this happens:**
- High-complexity claims inherently need more evidence, but requesting documents **sequentially** (discovering one missing item at a time) means each discovery triggers a full customer wait cycle (~1 day each).
- The waits between request and approval are largely **passive waiting** (customer response + overnight queues), not active work.
- Approvals for complex claims funnel through a single senior manager, creating a capacity constraint.
- Region B may have less digitized customer communication or different customer demographics, lengthening response times.

**Recommendations:**

1. **Front-load document requirements (highest impact).** Deploy complexity-based checklists at submission so *all* required documents for high-complexity claims are requested **once, in parallel**, rather than in 2–3 sequential rounds. This could cut case 2005's lead time from ~77h to ~30h.
2. **Automate document chasing.** Self-service upload portal, automated reminders, and SLA timers on customer responses to compress the ~20–30h passive wait per round.
3. **Triage at intake.** Route high-complexity claims immediately to senior adjusters with a defined document plan, instead of discovering needs iteratively.
4. **Add approval capacity for complex claims.** Manager_Bill is a bottleneck; cross-train a second senior approver or delegate authority for defined claim tiers.
5. **Investigate Region B's document handling.** Standardize Region A's same-day follow-up practices; examine whether communication channels (mail vs. portal) explain the slower rounds.
6. **Pre-process payments.** Begin payment verification for high-complexity claims in parallel with final approval to remove delays like Finance_Alan's 17h.

 **Caveat:** With only 5 cases, these are strong *hypotheses*, not proven causes — complexity explains duration here, but resource and region effects should be validated on a larger log before reassigning staff.
