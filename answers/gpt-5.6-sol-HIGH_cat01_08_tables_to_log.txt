### Assumptions

- Each order line creates a separate **Item Picked** event because it has its own item, timestamp, quantity, and picker.
- Each delivery record creates separate **Order Dispatched** and **Order Arrived** events. Thus, split shipments for order `1002` remain visible.
- Payments are linked to cases through `Payments.invoice_id  Invoice.order_id`.
- The sales person is treated as the associated actor for **Order Created**, although the data does not explicitly state that the sales person entered the order.
- Carriers are treated as the actors for dispatch and arrival milestones.
- No payment-processing resource is supplied, so payment actor fields are left as “Not provided.”
- Order `1004` has no delivery, invoice, or payment records. It is therefore represented as an incomplete/open case rather than having synthetic events added.
- Invoice currency is not present in the source data and has not been inferred.
