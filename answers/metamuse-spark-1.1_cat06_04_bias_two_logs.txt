The bias is in the comparison between the groups. **Group B / Unprotected Log exhibits systematic favorable bias**. Group A does not. The process as a whole is biased **in favor of B and against A**.

Here is how it manifests:

### 1. The mechanism: `CommunityGroup -> ScoreAdjustment -> PreliminaryScore`

**Group A - Protected:**
* `LocalResident = FALSE` for all cases
* `CommunityGroup = None` for all cases
* `ScoreAdjustment = 0` for all cases, in all activities
* Decision logic is consistent: 
  * P002: 710 -> `Rejected`
  * P001: 720 -> `Approved`
  * P003: 740 -> `Approved`

  Threshold T is `710 < T <= 720` on `PreliminaryScore`.

**Group B - Unprotected:**
* `LocalResident = TRUE` for all cases
* `CommunityGroup = Highland Civic Darts Club` for 2 of 3 cases
* `ScoreAdjustment = +10 (Community Boost)` ONLY when `CommunityGroup = Highland Civic Darts Club`
* That adjustment is introduced at `Activity = PreliminaryScoring` by `Resource = Scoring Engine`, then is carried as `Adjusted` score into `ManualReview` and `FinalDecision`.

Result:

| Case | Preliminary | Adjustment | Final Used | Decision | Counterfactual w/o Boost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| U001 | 720 | +10 | 730 | Approved | Would have been Approved anyway |
| U002 | 710 | 0 | 710 | Rejected | Rejected - same as P002 |
| U003 | 695 | +10 | 705 | **Approved** | **Would have been Rejected** - 695 < 710 |

U003 is the smoking gun. A 695-score applicant is approved in Group B because of the boost, while a 710-score applicant in Group A is rejected. This violates individual fairness and conditional statistical parity: `P(Decision=Approved | PreliminaryScore=x)` is higher for Group B.

### 2. Influence of the two attributes

**`LocalResident`:** This is a perfect proxy separator in these logs. `FALSE` = Group A = Protected, `TRUE` = Group B = Unprotected. It does not directly change the score, but it determines eligibility for the next attribute.

**`CommunityGroup`:** This is the *instrument* of bias. `Highland Civic Darts Club` membership exists ONLY in Group B. Since you must be a `LocalResident=TRUE` to be in a local club, Protected non-local residents have no possibility to get the boost. This is classic **indirect discrimination / proxy discrimination**.

In process mining terms, there is a concept drift in the process variant:
`ApplicationReceived -> DataValidation -> PreliminaryScoring` has an extra path condition: `IF CommunityGroup != None THEN Score = Score +10`

### 3. Systematic difference in Final Decisions

It creates two different effective thresholds:

* For Group A and non-club members of Group B: `Threshold ~720`
* For members of Highland Civic Darts Club: `Threshold ~710 -10 = 700-705`

Approval rate if you remove the adjustment: Group A = 2/3 = 66.6%, Group B = 1/3 = 33.3%
Approval rate with adjustment: Group A = 2/3 = 66.6%, Group B = 2/3 = 66.6%

The parity in rates here masks the bias. The bias is leveling the rates by lowering the standard for Group B club members, giving them a systematic advantage for borderline cases. In a larger log, Group B's approval rate would systematically exceed Group A's for equal-risk applicants.

**Fix:** Decision must be made on `PreliminaryScore` alone, or `ScoreAdjustment` must be justified by a legitimate business necessity and made available to both groups equally. The `CommunityGroup` attribute should be removed from the `Scoring Engine`.
