The normative process requires that every case start with **Receive order  Check inventory**, after which there are exactly two allowed branches:

- **Sufficient inventory**: Authorize payment  Package items  Ship order  Close order  
- **Insufficient inventory**: Cancel order  Close order  

In addition, the description explicitly forbids several orderings: packaging before payment authorization, shipping without a preceding package activity, any fulfillment activity after a cancellation, and ending with any activity other than Close order.

**Compliant cases**  
- **Case 1** follows the sufficient-inventory sequence exactly.  
- **Case 4** follows the insufficient-inventory sequence exactly.  

**Non-compliant cases**  
- **Case 2**  
  After Check inventory the observed order is Package items  Authorize payment.  
  This violates the rule that packaging may occur only *after* payment authorization.

- **Case 3**  
  After Check inventory the sequence is Authorize payment  Cancel order  Ship order.  
  Shipping after a cancellation is expressly forbidden; the presence of both authorization and cancellation in the same case is also inconsistent with the two exclusive branches defined by the inventory check.

- **Case 5**  
  After Authorize payment the process proceeds directly to Ship order (no Package items appears).  
  Shipping without a prior packaging activity violates the stated prerequisite “only after items have been properly packaged is it appropriate to proceed with Ship order.”

Thus the cases that deviate from the normative process are **2, 3 and 5**.
