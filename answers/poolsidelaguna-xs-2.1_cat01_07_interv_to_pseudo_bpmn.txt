**BPMN-Like Textual Process Model: Invoice Approval Workflow**  

---

### **Participants (Pools/Lanes)**  
1. **Accounts Payable (AP) Team**  
   - *Mary (AP Clerk)*  
   - *Rita (AP Supervisor)*  
2. **Purchasing Department**  
   - *Dan (Purchasing Officer)*  
3. **Department Management**  
   - *Carlos (Department Manager)*  
4. **Supplier Relationship Management**  
   - *Karen (Supplier Manager)*  
5. **System (Automated Notifications)**  

---

### **Process Flow**  

#### **Start Event**  
- **Start**: Invoice received (via email or other means).  

---

#### **1. Initial Invoice Handling (Mary – AP Clerk)**  
- **Activity**: *Check for Valid Purchase Order (PO) Number*  
  - **Input**: Invoice  
  - **Output**: Valid PO or Invalid PO status  

  **Gateway 1**:  
  - **PO Present?**  
    - **Yes**: Proceed to *Log Invoice in System*.  
    - **No**:  
      - **Activity**: *Send Invoice Back to Supplier*  
      - **Loop**: Return to Start (supplier must provide PO or corrected invoice).  

---

#### **2. Invoice Logging (Mary – AP Clerk)**  
- **Activity**: *Log Invoice in Accounting System*  
  - **Data Object**: Invoice with PO Number  
  - **Output**: Logged Invoice Entry  

  **Sequence Flow**: Forward to *Purchasing Confirmation Request*.  

---

#### **3. Purchasing Confirmation (Dan – Purchasing Officer)**  
- **Activity**: *Verify Receipt of Goods/Services*  
  - **Input**: Logged Invoice  
  - **Data Object**: PO Document  
  - **Output**: Confirmation Email or Discrepancy Report  

  **Gateway 2**:  
  - **Discrepancy Found?**  
    - **Yes**:  
      - **Activity**: *Report Discrepancy to AP Clerk*  
      - **Loop**: Return to Mary for escalation or resolution.  
    - **No**: Proceed to *Match Invoice to PO*.  

---

#### **4. Invoice Matching (Mary – AP Clerk)**  
- **Activity**: *Match Invoice Line Items to PO*  
  - **Input**: Invoice, PO Confirmation  
  - **Output**: Matched Invoice or Mismatch Flag  

  **Gateway 3**:  
  - **Match Valid?**  
    - **Yes**: Proceed to *Manager Approval Request*.  
    - **No**:  
      - **Activity**: *Email Purchasing Team for Clarification*  
      - **Gateway 4**: *Resolution Required?*  
        - **Yes**: Escalate to *Exception Handling (Rita – AP Supervisor)*.  
        - **No**: Loop back to *Match Invoice to PO*.  

---

#### **5. Exception Handling (Rita – AP Supervisor)**  
- **Activity**: *Resolve Discrepancy or Create Retrospective PO*  
  - **Input**: Mismatch Report or Unmatched Invoice  
  - **Data Object**: Supplier Contract (if needed)  
  - **Output**: Resolved Invoice or Rejection Note  

  - **Loop**:  
    - If unresolved, involve *Supplier Relationship Manager (Karen)*.  
    - **Activity**: *Contact Supplier for Clarification* (Karen).  
    - **Output**: Updated Invoice or Rejection.  
  - After resolution, return to *Manager Approval Request*.  

---

#### **6. Manager Approval (Carlos – Department Manager)**  
- **Activity**: *Review and Approve/Reject Invoice*  
  - **Input**: Matched Invoice  
  - **Data Object**: Budget vs. Invoice Amount  
  - **Output**: Approved/Rejected Status  

  **Gateway 5**:  
  - **Approve?**  
    - **Yes**: Proceed to *Payment Scheduling*.  
    - **No**:  
      - **Activity**: *Reject Invoice*  
      - **Loop**: Return to AP Team for follow-up (e.g., supplier correction).  

---

#### **7. Payment Scheduling (Mary – AP Clerk)**  
- **Activity**: *Schedule for Payment Run*  
  - **Input**: Approved Invoice  
  - **Output**: Scheduled Payment  

  **End Event**: *Payment Processed*.  

---

### **Alternate Paths and Exceptions**  
- **Key Exception Loop**:  
  - Discrepancy  Escalation to Rita  Karen’s involvement  Resolution  Return to AP Flow.  
- **Non-PO Invoices**:  
  - Handled by Rita: Create retrospective PO or reject.  
- **Manager Rejection**:  
  - Triggers supplier follow-up or rejection loop.  

---

### **Artifacts and Data Objects**  
- **Invoice**: Moves through all activities.  
- **PO Number**: Required for progression; missing PO triggers return to supplier.  
- **Confirmation Email**: From Purchasing to AP.  
- **Mismatch Report**: Generated during matching discrepancies.  
- **Budget vs. Invoice Amount**: Used by Manager for approval.  

---

### **Key Notes**  
- **Uncertainty**:  
  - "In some cases, after Task B, participants move directly to Task D" (e.g., if discrepancies are minor, Mary may skip escalation).  
  - Karen’s role is indirect; she resolves issues but does not act in the system.  
- **System Triggers**:  
  - Automatic notifications to Carlos upon invoice readiness.

This model reflects the consensus in interviews while explicitly noting ambiguities (e.g., minor discrepancies bypassing escalation).
