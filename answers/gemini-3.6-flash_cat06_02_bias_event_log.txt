### 3. Broad Implications for Equity and Fairness

1. **Privileging Insular Networks (Nepotism/Affinity Bias):**
   Rewarding membership in specific social organizations (like a darts club) disadvantages individuals who lack access to, interest in, or awareness of those groups. This can inadvertently discriminate based on age, gender, socioeconomic status, or cultural background if those local clubs are non-inclusive.

2. **Socioeconomic/Geographic Barriers:**
   By setting a much stricter bar for non-residents, the organization creates artificial barriers for newcomers, commuters, or individuals relocating for work. 

3. **Disparate Impact:**
   Even if the process relies on automated rules (`Scoring Engine`, `Rules Engine`), the rule definitions themselves embed human preferences. The process masks subjective favoritism as objective automated scoring, producing unfair outcomes for applicants with equal or superior underlying qualifications.
