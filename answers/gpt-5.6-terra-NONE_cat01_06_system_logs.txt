### Grouping and transformation logic

Cases were defined primarily by the business artifact being handled:

- **DOC-Document1**: all work on `Document1.docx`, including the later return to the document after the budget update.
- **EMAIL-AnnualMeeting**: the identified email thread concerning the annual meeting.
- **PDF-ReportDraft**: review and annotation of `Report_Draft.pdf`.
- **XLS-Budget2024**: updates made to `Budget_2024.xlsx`.
- **DOC-QuarterlyReport**: editing and saving `Quarterly_Report.docx`.

Low-level actions were converted into analyst-friendly activities. For example:

- `FOCUS` and relevant `SWITCH` events became **Open/resume** activities when they established work on an artifact.
- `TYPING` events were translated based on the logged text context, such as **Update budget figures** or **Compose email reply**.
- `SCROLL` became navigation/review activities rather than retaining the technical interaction label.
- `SAVE`, `SEND`, `HIGHLIGHT`, and `CLOSE` were retained as meaningful business-level actions.

The initial `FOCUS` on `Quarterly_Report.docx` at 08:59:50 was omitted because it was immediately followed by work on another document and contains no substantive action on the quarterly report.
