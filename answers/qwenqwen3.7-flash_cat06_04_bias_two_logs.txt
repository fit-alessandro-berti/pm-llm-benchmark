Based on a comparative analysis of the event logs, **Group B (Unprotected Group)** exhibits bias in the form of **preferential treatment** based on community affiliation. The bias manifests through the **`ScoreAdjustment`** column, which applies a **+10 point boost** exclusively to applicants in Group B who belong to a specific community (`Highland Civic Darts Club`). Group A applicants receive **zero adjustment** regardless of their profile or preliminary scores.

*   **Group A:** Consistently processes applications with a `ScoreAdjustment` of `0`. Outcomes depend entirely on the raw `PreliminaryScore`.

*   **Implication:** The system treats applicants differently based on membership in a specific community, granting them a scoring advantage that Group A applicants cannot access.

*   **Conclusion:** The bias is tied to **community affiliation**, not residency status alone, though residency correlates with the unprotected group in this dataset.
