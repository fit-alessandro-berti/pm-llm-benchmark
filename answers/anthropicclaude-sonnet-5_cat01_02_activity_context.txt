# Analysis of Recurring Activities

Looking across the three cases, two activities appear **multiple times within the same case**, each time serving a **different functional role** in the workflow:

| Activity | Cases where it recurs | Pattern Observed |
|----------|----------------------|-------------------|
| **Verify Documents** | C001, C003 | First occurrence = initial identity check; Second occurrence = re-check triggered by new information (additional docs submitted or account adjusted) |
| **Call Customer** | C002 (twice); appears once with different intents in C001/C003 | Purpose varies: requesting missing info, clarifying a discrepancy, confirming a resolution, or recalling a customer physically |

Other activities (**Initiate Request**, **Submit Additional**, **Adjust Account**, **Approve Request**) appear only once per case, so they do not require disambiguation under the recurrence rule — though **Adjust Account** contextually differs (post-call correction in C002 vs. pre-verification correction in C003), which is noted but not renamed since it isn't a repeated activity within a single case.

---

## Renaming Logic

### 1. "Verify Documents"  Split by Position/Trigger
- **Verify Documents – Initial Check**: The first verification attempt, before any corrective action.
- **Verify Documents – Re-Verification**: A repeat check performed *after* new information was introduced (a document upload or an account correction).

### 2. "Call Customer"  Split by Intent
- **Call Customer – Request Missing Documents**: Used when the agent needs the customer to supply something (C001).
- **Call Customer – Clarify Discrepancy**: Used to resolve confusion about account details (C002, first call).
- **Call Customer – Confirm Adjustment**: Used to verify that a change was correctly applied (C002, second call).
- **Call Customer – Request Customer Return**: Used in a branch setting to physically recall the customer (C003).

## Justification Summary

| Renamed Activity | Contextual Clue Used |
|--------------------|------------------------|
| **Verify Documents – Initial Check** | Always the *first* verification step, occurring right after request initiation, before any corrective action. |
| **Verify Documents – Re-Verification** | Occurs *after* a triggering event (new document submission or internal account correction), indicating a validation loop rather than a fresh check. |
| **Call Customer – Request Missing Documents** | Explicit context: "agent calls to request missing docs." |
| **Call Customer – Clarify Discrepancy** | Explicit context: "call to clarify account discrepancy" — an investigative call. |
| **Call Customer – Confirm Adjustment** | Explicit context: "follow-up call to confirm adjustments made" — a verification/closure call. |
| **Call Customer – Request Customer Return** | Explicit context: "customer stepped out... calls to have them return" — a logistical/physical-presence call, distinct from information-related calls. |

This renaming clarifies the **functional intent** behind each recurrence, making the process log more suitable for accurate process mining, bottleneck analysis, and conformance checking — since analysts can now distinguish *why* an activity repeated rather than treating all repetitions as identical steps.
