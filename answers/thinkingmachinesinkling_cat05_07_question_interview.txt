Here is a set of targeted, open-ended questions organized by theme to help clarify the process flow, decision logic, roles, timing, and exception handling.

### 1. Initiation, Documentation, and Entry Criteria
- What specifically distinguishes a “complete” documentation package from one that is rejected or escalated, and who makes that final call?
- When documentation is outdated, is there a hard deadline or expiration rule that forces the process to stop, or can onboarding proceed in parallel with updates?
- At the point details are entered into the central database, who validates the accuracy of inputs like square footage, amenities, and price range, and what happens if those figures conflict with the inspector’s later findings?

### 2. Property Manager Assignment and Alignment
- How is “current workload” actually measured or balanced—by number of active units, geographic spread, or complexity score—and who has the authority to override the assignment if the best-fit manager is at capacity?
- To what extent does the landlord influence or approve the assigned manager, and what is the resolution path if their preferences conflict with workload or specialization rules?
- During the introductory meeting, what are the non-negotiable conditions versus flexible preferences, and how are disagreements about policies (e.g., pet rules, maintenance splits, payment channels) formally resolved?

### 3. Inspection, Repairs, and Marketing Activation
- If the certified inspector finds issues, is there a standardized severity scale that determines whether repairs are mandatory, recommended, or optional, and who approves the repair budget and timeline?
- How long is the repair/remediation window, and is there a point at which a property is withdrawn from onboarding if issues remain unresolved?
- Once the property passes inspection, how does the digital marketing team determine the “correct” pricing model and listing strategy—what market inputs are used, and who approves the final marketing package before activation?

### 4. Tenant Screening, Selection, and Lease Execution
- What are the explicit screening thresholds (credit, employment verification, reference checks), and who has the authority to approve an applicant who falls slightly outside those criteria?
- In multi-applicant scenarios, what is the formal decision hierarchy: is it strictly first-qualified-first-served, best financial profile, longest lease commitment, or a negotiated combination?
- How are short-term promotional discounts or customized lease terms authorized—do they require regional or financial approval, and how is profitability impact assessed in real time?
- Which lease clauses are standardized and non-negotiable, and what is the review process when a landlord or tenant requests a custom clause (such as special insurance or furnished-rental terms)?

### 5. Audits, Quality Checks, and Integration
- Are the compliance, financial, and IT audits conducted sequentially or in parallel, and what triggers a rollback or hold if one audit identifies a discrepancy?
- How does the compliance officer verify fair housing adherence without creating bottlenecks—are checklists used, and is the review done before or after lease signing?
- If the financial analyst finds that the agreed rental price conflicts with profitability targets, what is the escalation or renegotiation path with the landlord?
- How does the IT department confirm that listing data and internal records are correctly integrated, and who corrects mismatches if they are found post-activation?

### 6. Exceptions, Escalations, and Communication
- Can you describe a concrete example of an exception—such as luxury units, student housing, or properties requiring special insurance—and trace how it altered the standard assignment, marketing, or audit path?
- How are unique landlord requirements or custom lease terms flagged for ongoing management after onboarding is complete, so future property managers and support teams remain aware?
- Throughout the lifecycle, which interactions are formally captured in the CRM or project management tools versus handled informally via email, and is there a single “source of truth” for process status?

### 7. Timing, Boundaries, and Success Metrics
- What is the typical end-to-end timeline from initial landlord contact to tenant move-in, and where do the most common delays or bottlenecks occur?
- At what precise moment is a property considered fully “onboarded” versus merely “activated” in the portal—does it require a signed lease, a passed audit, a completed move-in, or all of the above?
- How do you measure the health or success of this onboarding process—time-to-first-lease, audit pass rate, landlord retention, or tenant satisfaction—and what indicates that a property should be de-prioritized or removed from the portfolio?
- Is there a formal handoff point where the onboarding property manager transfers accountability to an ongoing operations manager, or does the same manager retain responsibility indefinitely?

These questions should help surface the implicit rules, authority structures, and contingency pathways that keep the process running smoothly across regions and property types.
