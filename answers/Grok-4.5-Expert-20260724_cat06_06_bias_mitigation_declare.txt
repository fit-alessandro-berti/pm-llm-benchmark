**Rationale for the added constraints**

- **existence / responded_existence / coexistence involving `ManualReview`**: Guarantees that every loan case containing a `FinalDecision` also contains an independent human review. This removes the possibility that a decision is issued solely on the basis of an automated path that may have encoded demographic bias.

- **response / succession / precedence involving `BiasMitigationCheck`**: Whenever any sensitive-attribute activity (`CheckApplicantRace`, `CheckApplicantGender`, `CheckApplicantAge`) occurs, a dedicated mitigation step must eventually appear and must itself be followed by `ManualReview`. In addition, `BiasMitigationCheck` is required to precede `FinalDecision`. Together these force an explicit fairness intervention between the observation of a protected attribute and the final outcome.

- **nonsuccession / nonchainsuccession from sensitive checks to `FinalDecision`**: These two families of constraints forbid both an immediate next-step decision and any later decision that is not separated by the mitigation activities above. Consequently a reject/approve cannot be produced “on the spot” after the process has observed a sensitive attribute, eliminating the most direct route for demographic bias.

Collectively the new constraints raise the cost of a biased path to the point that it becomes impossible under the DECLARE model, while preserving the original control-flow skeleton of the loan-application process.
