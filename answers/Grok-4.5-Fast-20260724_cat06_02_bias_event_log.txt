**Bias appears primarily in the “Community” score adjustment (+10), which systematically advantages applicants who are local residents and members of the Highland Civic Darts Club.**

### Key patterns from the event log
- **Community affiliation drives the only non-zero ScoreAdjustment.**  
  - Cases with `CommunityGroup = Highland Civic Darts Club` and `LocalResident = TRUE` (C001, C004) receive `+10 (Community)`.  
  - All other cases receive `0`.  
  - This is applied during PreliminaryScoring by the Scoring Engine and then carried forward (shown as “Adjusted” scores in ManualReview and FinalDecision).

- **Impact on final scores and decisions** (all cases go through the same activity sequence: ApplicationReceived → DataValidation → PreliminaryScoring → ManualReview → FinalDecision):  
  | Case | LocalResident | CommunityGroup                  | Prelim. Score | Adjustment     | Final Score     | Decision  |
  |------|---------------|---------------------------------|---------------|----------------|-----------------|-----------|
  | C001 | TRUE         | Highland Civic Darts Club      | 710          | +10 (Community)| 720 (Adjusted) | Approved |
  | C002 | TRUE         | None                           | 720          | 0              | 720             | Approved |
  | C003 | FALSE        | None                           | 715          | 0              | 715             | Rejected |
  | C004 | TRUE         | Highland Civic Darts Club      | 690          | +10 (Community)| 700 (Adjusted) | Approved |
  | C005 | FALSE        | None                           | 740          | 0              | 740             | Approved |

- C004 (690 + 10 = 700) is approved while C003 (715, no adjustment) is rejected.  
- C001 receives a 10-point lift that brings it into the same range as C002.  
- Non-members (and non-locals) never receive the lift, regardless of their preliminary score.

### How the bias operates
1. **Attribute that is favored**: Membership in one specific community group (“Highland Civic Darts Club”) combined with local residency.  
2. **Mechanism**: An explicit, automated +10 community bonus applied only to that group. Manual review notes the adjusted score but does not reverse or neutralize the bonus; the Rules Engine then issues the final decision on the adjusted figure.  
3. **No countervailing adjustments** appear for other attributes (or for lack of the favored affiliation).

### Fairness and equity implications
- Applicants with **similar or higher underlying (preliminary) creditworthiness** can be disadvantaged simply because they lack the favored community affiliation or local-resident status.  
  - Example: C003 (715) is rejected while C004 (originally 690) is approved after the community boost.  
- Individuals outside the Highland Civic Darts Club (or non-local) face a structural 10-point penalty relative to members, even when their raw scores are comparable.  
- Because the adjustment is applied early, labeled as “Community,” and then treated as the operative score downstream, it embeds group membership into the decision threshold without transparent, individualized justification based on credit risk.  
- This can produce disparate outcomes correlated with social/geographic networks rather than pure creditworthiness, reducing equity for otherwise similarly situated applicants who are not members of the privileged group.

In short, the process is not neutral with respect to community affiliation: the explicit +10 Community adjustment systematically improves outcomes for local members of the Highland Civic Darts Club and can tip borderline cases in their favor while leaving non-members (especially non-locals) at a relative disadvantage.
