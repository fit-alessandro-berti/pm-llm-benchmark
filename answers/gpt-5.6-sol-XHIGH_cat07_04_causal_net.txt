- **a — register request** starts the process.
- The request then follows one of two examination paths:

  - **c — examine casually**.

- These activities converge at **e — decide**.
- The decision has three possible outcomes:
  - **g — pay compensation**, then **z — end**;
  - **h — reject request**, then **z — end**;
  - **f — reinitiate request**, which loops back to the examination and ticket-checking stage.
