The visualization is a workflow/process model shown as a directed graph, using labeled tasks and gateway symbols.

### Main process
- **a — “register request”** starts the workflow.
- From registration, the request can be examined in different ways:
  - **b — “examine thoroughly”**
  - **c — “examine casually”**
  - **d — “check ticket”**
- These paths feed into:
  - **e — “decide”**
- The decision leads to one of two outcomes:
  - **g — “pay compensation”**, then
  - **h — “reject request”**
- Both paths eventually reach:
  - **z — “end”**

There is also a loop involving:
- **f — “reinitiate request”**

This appears to send the process back to earlier examination/checking activities, representing a request being restarted or reconsidered.

### Visual notation
- Rounded rectangles represent activities/tasks.
- Black circular nodes and arrows represent routing/split/join points and directed flow.
- Multiple arrows entering or leaving the black nodes indicate different kinds of branching and synchronization.

### Legend
A gray inset at the lower right explains the routing symbols:
- **XOR-split / XOR-join:** exactly one outgoing or incoming path is selected.
- **AND-split / AND-join:** all branches are activated or must synchronize.
- **OR-split / OR-join:** one or more branches may be activated or joined.

Overall, it depicts a request-handling process with alternative examination routes, a decision point, possible compensation or rejection, and a reinitiation loop.
