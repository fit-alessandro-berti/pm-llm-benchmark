### Rationale for Added Constraints

To ensure fairness and reduce the risk of discriminatory decision-making within the loan application process, several unary and binary DECLARE constraints were introduced:

1. **Mandatory Fairness Activities (`existence`):**
   - Added `ManualReview` and `BiasMitigationCheck` to the `existence` constraints. This guarantees that every process execution includes the necessary checkpoints for human oversight and algorithmic fairness evaluation.

2. **Demographic Checking & Mitigation Response (`responded_existence` & `response`):**
   - Added a `responded_existence` and `response` constraint between `CheckApplicantDemographics` and `BiasMitigationCheck`. This ensures that whenever demographic or sensitive attributes are evaluated, a bias mitigation protocol is guaranteed to be triggered in response.

3. **Coexistence for Protected Attributes (`coexistence`):**
   - Linked `CheckApplicantDemographics` with `ManualReview` under `coexistence`. This ensures that if an applicant's sensitive demographic information is accessed or evaluated during the trace, a manual review step must also occur to safeguard against disparate treatment.

4. **Enforcing Review Before Final Decision (`precedence` & `succession`):**
   - Added a `precedence` constraint requiring `ManualReview` to occur before any `FinalDecision`. 
   - Added a `succession` constraint from `BiasMitigationCheck` to `ManualReview` to ensure that mitigation findings feed directly into the review process.

5. **Preventing Direct Biased Outcomes (`nonsuccession` & `nonchainsuccession`):**
   - Added a `nonsuccession` constraint between `CheckApplicantDemographics` and `Reject`. This strictly prevents a direct transition from evaluating sensitive demographic attributes to a rejection, thereby eliminating the possibility of immediate, un-reviewed biased rejections. 
   - Similarly, added a `nonchainsuccession` constraint between `CheckApplicantDemographics` and `FinalDecision` to prevent decisions from immediately chaining off demographic disclosures without intermediary checks.
