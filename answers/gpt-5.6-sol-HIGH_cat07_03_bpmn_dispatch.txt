The visualization is a BPMN swimlane process diagram for **dispatching goods at a computer hardware shop**. It has three responsibility lanes:

- **Logistics**
- **Secretary**
- **Warehouse**

### Process flow

1. The process begins with **“Ship goods.”**
2. A **parallel gateway** starts two concurrent workstreams:
   - The secretary **clarifies the shipment method**.
   - The warehouse **packages the goods**.

### Shipment-method branch

After clarifying the shipment method, an exclusive decision asks **“Special handling?”**

- **Yes:**  
  The secretary gets **three offers from logistics companies**, then **selects a logistics company and places the order**.

- **No:**  
  The secretary **checks whether insurance is required**, using customer information.
  - A package label is always written.
  - If insurance is necessary, Logistics also **insures the parcel**.
  - These activities are then merged.

Both special-handling alternatives eventually rejoin.

### Packaging branch

The warehouse performs **“Package goods.”** A **48-hour timer boundary event** can trigger **“Notify customer about delay.”** The normal packaging path and delay-notification path then converge.

### Completion

A parallel gateway synchronizes the shipment-arrangement work with the packaging work. The warehouse then:

1. **Fills out shipment paperwork**
2. **Moves the package to the pickup area**

The process ends at **“Shipment prepared.”**

The diagram also shows supporting data elements, including **Order details** and a **Customer information** data store.
