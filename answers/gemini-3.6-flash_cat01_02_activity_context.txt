### 1. Identification & Analysis of Recurring Activities

Within individual cases, two main activities recur with different contextual meanings:

*   **`Verify Documents`** (Recurs in **C001** and **C003**):
    *   *First Occurrence:* Represents the **initial check** of provided identification/documents right after request initiation.
    *   *Second Occurrence:* Represents a **re-evaluation/verification** step. In C001, it is done after the customer uploads missing document scans. In C003, it is done after internal back-office account adjustments are completed.
*   **`Call Customer`** (Recurs in **C002**):
    *   *First Occurrence:* An outbound **clarification call** to investigate an account discrepancy with the customer.
    *   *Second Occurrence:* A follow-up **confirmation call** to inform the customer that the account adjustments have been successfully processed.
