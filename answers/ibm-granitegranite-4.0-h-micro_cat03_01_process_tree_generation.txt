### Explanation:
1. **Root Parallel Execution**: The entire process starts with a parallel execution of all initial activities (`A`, `RG`, `IA`) at the root level. This reflects the simultaneous start of patient registration and assessment at the hospital.

2. **Triage and Diagnostic Branching**: After the intake assessment, the process diverges into two paths based on the triage classification. The choice between a cardiovascular ECG (`CE`) and a full blood panel (`FBP`) is exclusive (`XOR`), representing the exclusive decision-making process.

4. **Advanced Imaging and Lifestyle Support**: Once normal results are achieved, the patient proceeds to advanced imaging (`AI`) and nutritional counseling (`NC`) in parallel. This reflects the parallel execution of activities that are necessary for comprehensive patient care and recovery.

5. **Final Administrative and Financial Clearances**: After completing advanced imaging and nutritional counseling, the process concludes with a parallel block encompassing billing (`BL`), insurance claims processing (`ICP`), and medical records finalization (`MRF`). These activities are performed concurrently to ensure all administrative and financial aspects are finalized before the patient's discharge.
