## Transformation Logic

### Case identification

Cases were defined by the stable work object being handled:

- `DOC-QR-001`: editing `Quarterly_Report.docx`
- `DOC-DRAFT-001`: editing `Document1.docx`
- `MAIL-AM-001`: handling the Annual Meeting email
- `PDF-RD-001`: reviewing `Report_Draft.pdf`
- `XLS-BUDGET-001`: updating `Budget_2024.xlsx`

The mailbox-access event was assigned to the Annual Meeting case because it immediately preceded opening and replying to that email. Documents, the PDF, and the workbook remain separate cases because the log does not prove that they belong to one formal business transaction, even though their contents appear related.

### Activity abstraction

- `FOCUS` and destination-side `SWITCH` events became **Access** or **Resume** activities.
- Word and Excel `TYPING` became **Edit Document** or **Edit Workbook**.
- Email actions became **Open Email**, **Read Email**, **Initiate Reply**, **Compose Reply**, and **Send Reply**.
- PDF scrolling and highlighting became **Review PDF** and **Annotate PDF**.
- `SAVE` and `CLOSE` were retained as meaningful lifecycle activities.

All 26 source records are represented once. No artificial close events were added for the email, PDF, or workbook cases; those traces end at their last observed activity.
