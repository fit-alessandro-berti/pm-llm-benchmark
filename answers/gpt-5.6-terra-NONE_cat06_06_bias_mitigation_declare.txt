### Rationale for the bias-mitigating constraints

- **`coexistence` between demographic-specific decisions and `ManualReview`:**  
  Any approval or rejection explicitly associated with a protected demographic requires a manual review in the same process trace. This introduces human oversight and makes demographic-sensitive decisions auditable.

- **`response` from sensitive-attribute checks to `BiasMitigationCheck`:**  
  If race, gender, or age information is accessed, a fairness or bias check must subsequently occur. This ensures sensitive information cannot be used without review.

- **`precedence` from `BiasMitigationCheck` to decision activities:**  
  Approval and rejection activities require a prior mitigation check. This prevents decisions from being made before the fairness-control step.

- **`chainprecedence` between `ManualReview` and `BiasMitigationCheck`:**  
  A mitigation check must occur directly before the manual-review stage, tightly coupling review with an explicit fairness assessment.

- **`nonchainsuccession` from sensitive checks to decisions:**  
  This prevents an immediate decision directly after inspecting protected attributes. The process must include at least one intervening activity, such as `ManualReview` or `BiasMitigationCheck`, reducing the risk of direct discriminatory decisions.
