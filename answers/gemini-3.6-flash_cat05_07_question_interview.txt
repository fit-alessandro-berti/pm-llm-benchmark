Here is a structured list of targeted, open-ended questions grouped by process area to help you clarify and deepen your understanding of the property onboarding process:

---

### **1. Initial Intake & Documentation**
* **Rejection & Minimum Standards:** Are there specific criteria or red flags at the initial intake stage that would cause your company to decline a property or terminate the onboarding process immediately?
* **Document Exceptions:** How does the team handle situations where essential documents (e.g., compliance certificates or deeds) are delayed, missing, or under legal dispute? Is conditional onboarding ever permitted?
* **Multi-Unit Scope:** How does the initial intake process adapt when onboarding a large, multi-unit residential building versus a single-family home?

### **2. Property Manager Assignment & Alignment**
* **Assignment Logic:** How do you balance and prioritize the different assignment factors (workload, location, experience, track record)? Is this a manual decision made by leadership or a rule-based system?
* **Landlord Realignment:** If a landlord insists on custom conditions (e.g., unusual pet policies or tenant restrictions) during the initial alignment call that conflict with standard company policy or local regulations, how is that resolved?
* **Role Handoff:** What specific trigger signals the official transfer of responsibility from the Property Coordinator to the assigned Property Manager?

### **3. Inspections, Repairs & Marketing**
* **Parallel Processing:** Can the photography, inspection, and initial marketing setup happen concurrently, or must they occur in a strict sequence?
* **Inspection Failure Protocol:** If an inspector identifies significant safety or habitability issues and the landlord refuses—or cannot afford—to complete the necessary repairs, what is the protocol? Is there a timeout or exit strategy?
* **Pricing & Marketing Approvals:** Who has the final authority to set the listing price—the digital marketing team, the Property Manager, or the landlord—especially if their market assessments differ?

### **4. Tenant Screening & Lease Negotiation**
* **Selection Criteria:** When evaluating multiple competing tenant applications, what objective criteria or prioritization rules are used to make the final selection and avoid potential bias?
* **Negotiation Boundaries:** What level of discretion does the Property Manager or leasing agent have to offer short-term discounts or negotiate lease terms without needing secondary approval?
* **Custom Lease Clauses:** What is the legal review process when a landlord insists on incorporating non-standard, custom clauses into the lease agreement?

### **5. Timing, Sequencing & Quality Audits**
* **Audit Timing:** At what exact point in the workflow do the compliance, financial, and IT audits occur? Are they gatekeepers before a property is listed, before a lease is signed, or post-move-in quality checks?
* **Process Timelines & Bottlenecks:** What is the target end-to-end duration for onboarding a property from initial contact to portal activation, and which phase typically experiences the longest delays?
* **Audit Remediations:** If a compliance officer or financial analyst flags an issue *after* a lease has already been finalized with a tenant, how is that error remediated?

### **6. Governance, Systems & Communication**
* **Cross-Team Accountability:** Since the process spans multiple roles (Coordinators, PMs, Inspectors, Marketing, IT, Compliance), how do you ensure seamless handoffs and visibility across different platforms (CRM, email, project management tools)?
* **Escalation Paths:** What is the formal escalation process when a property gets stuck in the onboarding pipeline due to lack of response from a landlord or external contractor?
