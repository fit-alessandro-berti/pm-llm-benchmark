### Process scope and outcomes
1. What event formally starts the onboarding process, and what information must the landlord provide at that point?
2. What defines successful completion of onboarding: activation in the portal, execution of a lease, tenant move-in, or another milestone?
3. Are there different onboarding paths for different property types, regions, or landlord categories?
4. What are the major stages and formal status changes used to track a property through the process?

### Documentation and initial review

9. Are any risk, ownership, insurance, or conflict-of-interest checks performed at this stage?
10. Who approves the property to proceed after document verification?

### Property data

14. Which fields can be updated later, and which changes require review or approval?
15. How are multi-unit properties represented and processed compared with single-unit properties?

### Property-manager assignment
16. Who makes the property-manager assignment, and who can approve or override it?

### Landlord alignment and agreements
