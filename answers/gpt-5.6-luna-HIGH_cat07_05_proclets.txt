The visualization illustrates how a **lab visit** interacts with a **lab test** using two communication channels.

### (a) Process/protocol interaction

**Lab visit process, on the left:**
1. A doctor creates an **order entry**.
2. A lab assistant **takes a blood sample**.
3. The visit process waits to **receive the test result**.
4. The assistant **judges the results**.
5. If everything is fine, the system **sends the report**.
6. If the results are unsatisfactory, the process requests that the tests be performed again, receives an **updated result**, and then sends the report.

**Lab test process, on the right:**
1. The test request is received.
2. A chemical analyst **performs the test**.
3. The analyst **makes a report**.
4. The test process finishes and returns the result.
5. If a retest is requested, the analyst **performs the test again** and **provides an updated report**.

The two processes communicate through:

- **Order system** — used to send the initial test request.
- **HIS** — used to exchange test results and updated reports.

The arrows show the direction of communication, while the multiplicity labels indicate how many messages or participants may be associated with each connection.
