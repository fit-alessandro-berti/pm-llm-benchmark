### Explanation of the Loop Structure
The loop is modeled as `* ( tau, -> ( 'SC', X('TM', 'SPT'), X('FCE', 'FBA') ) )`. 
According to the definition, the loop executes the first part (`A`), then either exits or executes the second part (`B`) and repeats. By making the first part a silent step (`tau`), the patient initially just undergoes the preceding diagnostic test `X('CE', 'FBP')`. If the results are abnormal, they enter the loop's second part, executing the Specialist Consultation, exclusive treatment, and Re-Diagnostic Test. After the re-diagnostic test, the loop returns to `tau`, where it will either exit (if results are normal) or repeat the consultation and treatments (if still abnormal). This perfectly captures the requirement without incorrectly repeating the initial diagnostic tests.

root = ProcessTree(operator=Operator.SEQUENCE)

a = ProcessTree(label="A", parent=p1)
rg = ProcessTree(label="RG", parent=p1)
ia = ProcessTree(label="IA", parent=p1)
p1.children.extend([a, rg, ia])

p2 = ProcessTree(operator=Operator.SEQUENCE, parent=root)
ti = ProcessTree(label="TI", parent=p2)

p3 = ProcessTree(operator=Operator.SEQUENCE, parent=root)

# Exclusive choice for initial test
xor_initial_test = ProcessTree(operator=Operator.XOR, parent=p3)
ce = ProcessTree(label="CE", parent=xor_initial_test)
fbp = ProcessTree(label="FBP", parent=xor_initial_test)
xor_initial_test.children.extend([ce, fbp])

loop_node = ProcessTree(operator=Operator.LOOP, parent=p3)

seq_loop_body = ProcessTree(operator=Operator.SEQUENCE, parent=loop_node)

xor_treatment.children.extend([tm, spt])

xor_rdt = ProcessTree(operator=Operator.XOR, parent=seq_loop_body)

xor_rdt.children.extend([fce, fba])

seq_loop_body.children.extend([sc, xor_treatment, xor_rdt])
loop_node.children.extend([tau, seq_loop_body])

p3.children.extend([xor_initial_test, loop_node])
