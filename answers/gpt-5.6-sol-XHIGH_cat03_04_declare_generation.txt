The following model assumes every activity occurs exactly once. **TFC** and **CE** may occur in either order after **DD**, while both must precede **PC**. Similarly, **LT** and **UT** may occur in either order after **PC**, while both must precede **AG**.

activities = (

Using `succession` rather than `chainsuccession` permits unrelated activities between the two constrained activities. For example, `("DD", "TFC")` does not require TFC to occur immediately after DD. Empty negative-template dictionaries indicate that the scenario specifies no forbidden activity combinations or ordering relations.
