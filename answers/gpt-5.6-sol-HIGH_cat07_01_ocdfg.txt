The visualization is an object-centric process-flow map linking five business object types to procurement, fulfillment, invoicing, and returns activities.

- **Source objects:**  
  - VendorInvoiceItem — **1.2M objects** (green)  
  - ReturnOrderItem — **0.8M** (red)  
  - PurchaseRequisitionItem — **2.0M** (purple)  
  - SalesOrderItem — **3.5M** (orange)  
  - PurchaseOrderItem — **5.0M** (blue)

- **Major activities and overall volumes:**  
  - Create Requisition — **3.0M**  
  - Approve Requisition — **5.7M**  
  - Send Order — **5.2M**  
  - Check Stock — **1.0M**  
  - Create Order — **8.5M**  
  - Pack Goods — **2.0M**  
  - Ship Goods — **1.8M**  
  - Confirm Delivery — **1.7M**  
  - Close Order — **1.7M**  
  - Process Return — **0.8M**  
  - Approve Payment — **1.5M**  
  - Create Invoice — **2.0M**

The main sales-fulfillment path runs vertically on the right:

**Create Order  Pack Goods  Ship Goods  Confirm Delivery  Close Order  End**

The central procurement flow moves through **Create Requisition**, **Approve Requisition**, and branches into activities such as **Send Order**, **Check Stock**, **Approve Payment**, and **Create Invoice**. Return and vendor-invoice objects connect through **Approve Requisition**, **Process Return**, and invoicing/payment steps.

Colored lines correspond to the five object types, while labels such as **200ms**, **400ms**, and **800ms** show transition times between activities. Multiple crossing and backward arrows indicate shared steps, alternate routes, and rework loops. Several separate **End** nodes show that different object streams can complete through different paths.
