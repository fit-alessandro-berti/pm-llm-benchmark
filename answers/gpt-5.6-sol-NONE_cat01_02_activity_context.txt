## 1. Recurring activities requiring disambiguation

Two activities occur more than once within an individual case:

- **Verify Documents**
  - In **C001**, it represents an initial verification and then a re-verification after the customer supplies a missing ID scan.
  - In **C003**, it represents an initial physical verification and then a re-verification after an account correction.

- **Call Customer**
  - In **C002**, the first call seeks clarification about a discrepancy, while the second confirms that account adjustments were completed.

Activities that recur across different cases but only once per case—such as **Initiate Request** and **Approve Request**—do not create within-case ambiguity and are therefore left unchanged. The single occurrences of **Call Customer** in C001 and C003 are renamed as well because their specific purposes are clear from the context.

## 3. Naming rationale

- **Perform Initial Document Verification** identifies the first-pass verification occurring near the start of each case.
- **Perform Initial Physical Document Verification** additionally captures C003’s branch-based, physical inspection.
- **Re-verify Documents After Submission** distinguishes C001’s second verification, triggered by the customer’s additional ID upload.
- **Re-verify Documents After Account Correction** distinguishes C003’s second verification, triggered by an internal account adjustment.
- The customer calls are named by their immediate purpose:
  - requesting missing documents,
  - clarifying an account discrepancy,
  - confirming a completed adjustment, or
  - asking a customer to return to the branch.
- **Submit Additional** and **Adjust Account** were also made more specific where the context identifies the submitted item or reason for adjustment. This improves process readability even though those activities do not recur within the same case.
