### 3. Proposed Explanations and Mitigation Strategies

**Explanation of Performance Issues:**
The primary root cause of the long case durations is the **"Request Additional Documents" loop**, which is triggered by the **High Complexity** of the claims. Rather than requesting all necessary documents at once, the adjusters are requesting documents piecemeal. For example, in Case 2005, Adjuster_Lisa sent requests on three separate days. Each request requires waiting for the customer to respond, re-evaluating the claim, and then potentially finding another missing piece of information. This iterative back-and-forth delays the approval step significantly.

**Suggestions to Mitigate Root Causes:**
1.  **Implement "Complete Submission" Checklists:** For claims flagged as Medium or High complexity at the "Submit Claim" stage, provide the customer and CSR with a specific checklist of all potentially required documents upfront. This reduces the number of "Request Additional Documents" loops.
2.  **Batch Document Requests:** Train adjusters (like Adjuster_Lisa and Adjuster_Mike) to thoroughly evaluate the claim during the "Evaluate Claim" phase and batch their document requests. Instead of requesting one document at a time, they should request everything missing in a single communication.
3.  **Dedicated High-Complexity Workflow:** Create a specialized team or triage step for High Complexity claims. Instead of going through the standard Adjuster loop, high-complexity claims could be routed to a senior evaluator who can identify missing documents faster and coordinate directly with the claimant to gather everything in one go.
4.  **Automated Follow-ups:** Utilize automated reminders to customers for outstanding documents to reduce the idle time between the "Request Additional Documents" activity and the subsequent evaluation or approval.
