### Process scope and outcomes
1. What event formally starts the onboarding process, and what information must be captured at intake?
2. How do you define when onboarding is complete: portal activation, lease execution, tenant move-in, or another milestone?
3. What are the major stages and status labels used to track a property from initial contact through completion?
4. What outcomes could end the process early, such as rejecting a property or a landlord withdrawing?

### Documentation and initial review
5. Which documents are mandatory for every property, and which vary by city, property type, or ownership structure?
6. How does the coordinator determine whether a document is valid, current, and sufficient?
7. What happens when documents are missing, expired, inconsistent, or suspected to be inaccurate?
8. Who is responsible for following up with the landlord, and how long is the process held open while awaiting updates?
9. What approvals or sign-offs are required before property details can be entered or advanced to the next stage?
10. How are changes to property information handled after the initial verification has been completed?

### Property data and ownership
11. Which property details are considered essential, and who is accountable for verifying their accuracy?
12. How are complex properties handled, such as mixed-use buildings, multiple-unit portfolios, or properties spanning different regulatory jurisdictions?
13. Which system or record is treated as the authoritative source when information differs across email, CRM, and project management tools?
14. Who may update property records, and how are corrections or disputed data resolved?

### Property manager assignment
15. Who makes the final property manager assignment decision?
16. How are workload, geography, property-type experience, and landlord requirements weighed against one another?
17. What thresholds define whether a manager has sufficient capacity to take on another property?
18. How are ties or conflicts between otherwise suitable managers resolved?
19. Under what circumstances would a property be reassigned, and how is the handoff managed?
20. How are assignment decisions documented so that others can understand why a manager was selected?

### Landlord alignment
21. What topics must be resolved during the introductory meeting, and what deliverables come out of it?
22. Who has authority to accept or reject special landlord conditions, such as custom lease clauses or unusual maintenance responsibilities?
23. How are unresolved disagreements with the landlord escalated?
24. What happens when the landlord changes requirements after inspection, marketing, or activation has begun?

### Photography, inspection, and repairs
25. Which activities can occur in parallel, and which must wait for an earlier step to be completed?
26. Who schedules and coordinates the photographer, inspector, landlord, tenants, and property access?
27. What criteria determine whether an inspection issue is minor, requires remediation, or prevents the property from being onboarded?
28. Who decides which repairs are required, who selects contractors, and who approves the associated costs?
29. How are repair deadlines established, and what happens when the landlord delays or declines required work?
30. When is a reinspection required, and who confirms that the property has passed?
31. How are urgent safety issues or properties requiring special insurance handled differently?

### Marketing, pricing, and activation
32. What information and approvals does the marketing team need before creating listings and brochures?
33. Who approves photographs, listing content, amenities, and representations about the property?
34. How is the proposed rental price determined, and who can approve departures from the pricing recommendation?
35. What specific conditions must be satisfied before a property can be activated in the rental portal?
36. Who has the authority to pause or deactivate a listing if an issue is discovered after activation?
37. How are listing errors or differences between internal systems and public portals identified and corrected?

### Tenant screening and selection
38. At what point may applications be accepted, and who owns the screening process?
39. What criteria are used to evaluate applications, and how are those criteria applied consistently across applicants?
40. How are incomplete applications, marginal qualifications, guarantors, or requests for reasonable accommodation handled?
41. When several applicants qualify, what decision rules determine which applicant is selected?
42. Who may negotiate lease length, rental price, concessions, or promotional discounts, and within what boundaries?
43. How are applicant communications, selection decisions, and rejection reasons documented?
44. What controls ensure that screening and selection comply with fair housing and local tenant-protection requirements?

### Lease, payment, and move-in
45. Who prepares, reviews, approves, and signs the lease?
46. How are custom lease clauses assessed, especially when they conflict with company policy or local law?
47. What must happen before a security deposit can be accepted, and how are failed or delayed payments handled?
48. Which conditions must be met before the move-in date can be confirmed?
49. What happens if the tenant withdraws, fails screening late in the process, or does not complete the lease or deposit?

### Quality checks and audits
50. When do compliance, financial, and IT reviews occur relative to activation and lease signing?
51. Which checks are mandatory for every property, and which are triggered only by certain risk factors?
52. What issues are each reviewer expected to identify, and who owns remediation?
53. What happens if an audit finds a problem after the property has already been listed or leased?
54. Who has final authority to approve an exception or stop the onboarding process?

### Timing, handoffs, and performance
55. What is the expected duration of each stage and of the overall process?
56. Where do delays most commonly occur, and how are overdue tasks escalated?
57. What information must accompany each handoff between coordinators, managers, marketing, compliance, finance, and IT?
58. How are dependencies and pending decisions communicated to everyone involved?
59. Which metrics indicate that onboarding was successful—for example, cycle time, error rate, compliance findings, vacancy duration, or landlord satisfaction?
60. Which parts of the current process create the most rework, confusion, or risk, and what improvements have already been considered?
