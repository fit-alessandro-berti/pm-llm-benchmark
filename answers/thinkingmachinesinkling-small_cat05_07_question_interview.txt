Here is a structured set of open-ended clarifying questions you could ask, organized by process phase. Each is designed to expose decision logic, handoffs, and exception paths rather than technical implementation.

### 1. Intake & Documentation
- **Criteria:** When you review a landlord’s deeds, compliance certificates, and inspection reports, what specific signals make a document “accepted” versus “sent back for updates”? How do you handle cases where local regulations have recently changed and older certificates might no longer reflect current standards?
- **Timing:** Once documentation is verified, is there a standard turnaround time for entering property details into the central database, or does it vary by property type?

### 2. Manager Assignment & Alignment
- **Decision framework:** You mentioned workload, geographic specialization, experience with property types, and landlord uniqueness. How do you weight those factors when they conflict—for example, if the best-fit manager for luxury units is overloaded?
- **Meeting purpose:** During the introductory meeting between the manager and landlord, how are special conditions (pets, maintenance splits, payment channels, custom policies) captured? Are they translated directly into standardized lease templates, or do they create a separate “special conditions” file that flows to screening and legal review?
- **Roles:** Is the property manager solely responsible for scheduling the photographer and inspector, or does a coordinator or operations team handle logistics?

### 3. Inspection, Repair & Marketing Sequence
- **Sequencing:** What is the typical order and overlap between the intro meeting, the professional photography visit, and the certified inspection? Can they occur in parallel, and what is the critical path?
- **Repair decisions:** If the inspector finds habitability issues, how do you decide whether the landlord must complete repairs versus the manager engaging contractors? Who bears the cost, and how does that affect the marketing timeline?
- **Marketing activation:** When the digital team creates listings and updates pricing models, what market inputs do they use? And what does “activation” specifically entail—does it mean the portal listing goes live, or does it also include final integration of pricing and internal data?

### 4. Tenant Screening & Lease Finalization
- **Selection logic:** If multiple applicants are interested, what framework guides the decision—credit profile, length of lease commitment, negotiation flexibility, or reference quality? How are promotional discounts or short-term incentives approved?
- **Negotiation:** When a property manager negotiates lease terms, are there pre-approved boundaries, or does each negotiation require escalation?
- **Custom clauses:** How are landlords’ custom lease clauses reviewed for legal and fair-housing compliance? Is that a property-manager task, or does it go through a dedicated compliance or legal step?

### 5. Quality Checks, Audits & Exceptions
- **Gates vs. parallel tracks:** Are the compliance, financial, and IT checks sequential gates, or do they run in parallel? If one fails—say, IT finds a data integration error after the lease is signed—what is the remediation path?
- **Authority:** For exceptions like special insurance requirements or non-standard lease terms, who has authority to approve them? At what stage must they be flagged so they don’t delay activation?
- **Feedback loops:** How do quality-check findings feed back into the onboarding process? For instance, if a compliance officer repeatedly finds fair-housing gaps, does that trigger a change in manager training or lease templates?

### 6. Communication, Tracking & Lifecycle
- **Information flow:** How is communication tracked across regional offices, landlords, managers, and support teams? Do you rely on the CRM for all milestone updates, with email for urgent exceptions, or is there a different division?
- **Stakeholder handoffs:** At which points does primary ownership shift—e.g., from coordinator to manager, from manager to marketing, from manager to leasing agent? What ensures nothing is lost during those handoffs?
- **Success metrics:** How do you know the onboarding is successful for a given property? Is it time-to-activation, first-tenant quality, landlord satisfaction, or something else?

These should help you map out decision rights, sequencing dependencies, and exception paths without needing to ask about database schemas or query logic.
