**Normative HiretoRetire Backbone**

A standard hiring process is causally sequential:

`Post_Job_Ad  Screen_Candidates  Conduct_Interviews  Make_Hiring_Decision  Onboard_Employee  Add_to_Payroll  Close_Case`

The **critical logic** is:
1. **Screening** narrows the pool.
2. **Interviews** generate evidence for the decision.
3. **Decision** must come *after* interviews and *before* any commitment (onboarding/payroll).
4. **Payroll** and **close** follow onboarding; they should never precede evaluation or be skipped.

### 1. Analysis of **Model 1**

**Structure:** A mostly linear chain `Post  Screen  Decide  Onboard  Payroll  Close`, with `Screen` also feeding `Interview`.

**Anomalies identified:**

| Issue | Evidence in POWL | Impact on correctness |
|-------|------------------|------------------------|
| **Interview is not a prerequisite for the hiring decision** | Edge `Screen  Decide` exists, but no `Interview  Decide`. Because `Screen` is the only parent of both, `Decide` and `Interview` are concurrent siblings. | The process allows a hiring decision to be made **before or without any interview**. The evaluation step is structurally divorced from the commitment step. |
| **Interview is unconstrained downstream** | `Interview` has no outgoing edges to `Decide`, `Onboard`, `Payroll`, or `Close`. | Interviews can occur **after** onboarding, after payroll, or even **after case closure**. The process can be “complete” while evaluations remain pending. |
| **Screening feeds decision directly** | `Screen  Decide` bypasses interview synthesis. | The decision is based only on screening, not on interview results, which undermines selection integrity. |

**Severity:** **Fundamental / Structural**. The model breaks the causal heart of hiring: *interviews must inform the decision*. Because `Interview` is a “dangling” parallel branch, the process can legally execute `Make_Hiring_Decision  Onboard  Payroll  Close` while `Conduct_Interviews` is still unexecuted or even finished last.
