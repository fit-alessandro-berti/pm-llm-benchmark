## Reconstructed High-Level Process

**Idle/Setup  Cutting  Assembly/Material Handling  Welding  Cooling  Quality Inspection/Adjustment  Idle/Complete**

### Interpretation Notes

The labels are inferred from sensor behavior rather than explicit activity codes:

- **Cutting** is indicated by strong tool movement, high vibration, rising temperature, material flow, and increased energy.
- **Assembly or positioning** is associated with moderate, stable sensor values and limited tool movement.
- **Welding** is suggested by the isolated maximum in temperature and energy, together with high vibration and no material flow.
- **Inspection or adjustment** is inferred from low operating intensity, a fixed tool position, and a small amount of flow.
- The repeated low-energy, zero-motion states identify the beginning and end of the process cycle.
