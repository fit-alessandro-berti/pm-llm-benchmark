# 20 Thought-Provoking Questions on the Smartphone Supply Chain Process

1. How does the company balance the trade-offs between just-in-time replenishment for high-demand markets and the risk of supply disruptions from single-source international suppliers?

2. What contingency plans exist if a critical supplier (e.g., the Taiwan microprocessor fabricator) experiences a sudden shutdown due to geopolitical tensions, natural disasters, or pandemics?

3. How might over-reliance on a small number of countries for key components (Taiwan, Japan, South Korea) expose the company to systemic geopolitical or trade-policy risks?

4. In what ways could the company diversify its supplier base without significantly increasing costs or compromising quality standards?

5. How does the choice between air freight and cargo ship transportation get made, and what trade-offs (cost, speed, carbon footprint, reliability) are being optimized or sacrificed?

6. What mechanisms are in place to ensure quality consistency across components sourced from five different countries with potentially varying manufacturing standards?

7. How might currency fluctuations across multiple countries impact overall profitability, and what hedging strategies could mitigate this risk?

8. To what extent do machine learning models for demand forecasting account for black swan events (e.g., pandemics, sudden regulatory changes, or viral product trends)?

9. How does the company ensure ethical labor practices and environmental compliance across suppliers in different countries with varying regulatory rigor?

10. What are the hidden costs (financial, reputational, operational) of maintaining 24/7 multi-shift manufacturing, and how do they compare to potential alternatives like regional micro-factories?

11. How resilient is the supply chain to a simultaneous disruption in two or more regions (e.g., a natural disaster in Asia coinciding with a logistics strike in Europe)?

12. What strategies could reduce the carbon footprint of the global logistics network without significantly increasing lead times or costs?

13. How does the company balance centralization (main assembly plant in Malaysia) versus decentralization (regional assembly hubs) in terms of resilience, cost, and speed to market?

14. What is the real cost-benefit trade-off between aggregated monthly shipments for slower-moving markets versus adopting more frequent, smaller shipments to reduce inventory holding risk?

15. How prepared is the organization to manage a large-scale product recall across multiple countries with different regulatory and consumer protection laws?

16. What role does data security and cybersecurity play in protecting the digital systems that manage purchase orders, invoices, and customs declarations across multiple countries?

17. How might increasing automation on the assembly line affect workforce dynamics, labor relations, and long-term operational flexibility in Malaysia?

18. What are the potential unintended consequences of prioritizing speed and cost efficiency over supply chain transparency and traceability?

19. How does the company measure and improve resilience against "bullwhip effects" caused by demand forecasting inaccuracies rippling through multiple supply chain tiers?

20. In what ways could blockchain or distributed ledger technologies be integrated to improve transparency, traceability, and trust across the multinational supplier network?
