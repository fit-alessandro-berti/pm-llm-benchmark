# Clarifying Questions for Property Onboarding Process

## Documentation & Initial Verification
1. What specific criteria determine whether a document is "outdated"? Are there expiration timeframes for compliance certificates or inspection reports?
2. How long does the company typically wait for a landlord to provide updated documentation before the process stalls or is cancelled?
3. Is there a point at which incomplete documentation causes the process to be rejected entirely, rather than paused?
4. Who verifies the authenticity of the deeds and certificates—does the coordinator have final authority, or is legal/compliance involved at this stage?

## Property Manager Assignment
5. Is the assignment decision made by a single person (e.g., regional manager) or a committee/system? Is it manual or aided by software?
6. When workload, specialization, and experience factors conflict (e.g., the best-specialized manager is overloaded), how is the tie-breaking decision made?
7. What happens if no available property manager has experience with a unique property type (e.g., student housing)? Is training provided, or is the assignment delayed?
8. Can a landlord request a specific property manager or reject an assigned one?

## Landlord-Manager Introductory Meeting
9. What happens if the landlord and property manager disagree on key policies (e.g., pet policy, maintenance responsibilities) during this initial meeting? Is there an escalation path?
10. Is this meeting mandatory, or can it be skipped for repeat landlords who already have established relationships with the company?
11. Are the outcomes of this meeting documented in a formal agreement, or are they informal understandings captured in notes?

## Photography & Inspection
12. Who bears the cost of the photographer and inspector—the company or the landlord?
13. What is the typical timeframe between scheduling and completion of the photography/inspection visits?
14. What defines a "failing" inspection versus one with minor issues? Is there a severity scale?
15. If repairs are needed, who decides whether the landlord or the company arranges contractors, and how are costs allocated?
16. Is there a maximum number of inspection cycles allowed before a property is rejected from onboarding entirely?

## Marketing & Activation
17. Who approves the final pricing model before a property is activated—does the property manager have sole authority, or does a pricing/financial team need to sign off?
18. How are marketing materials reviewed and approved before going live? Is there a quality check by anyone other than the marketing team?
19. What happens if a property is activated but later found to have inaccurate details in the listing? Who corrects this and how quickly?

## Tenant Screening & Leasing
20. What are the specific criteria used to evaluate competing tenant applications (e.g., minimum credit score, income-to-rent ratio)?
21. Who has final authority to approve or reject a tenant—the property manager alone, or does the landlord retain veto power?
22. Are there standardized lease templates, or does each property manager draft leases individually? How are custom clauses (requested by landlords) reviewed for legal risk?
23. What is the process if a tenant's application is approved but they withdraw before signing the lease? Does the process revert to reviewing other applicants, or restart tenant screening?
24. How are promotional discounts approved—does the property manager have autonomy, or is there an approval threshold requiring managerial sign-off?

## Compliance, Audits & Exceptions
25. At what point in the process do compliance officers and financial analysts get involved—after the lease is signed, or do they have checkpoints earlier in the process?
26. What happens if the compliance officer finds a fair housing violation after the lease is already signed? Is there a remediation process?
27. If the financial analyst finds the agreed rental price doesn't match profitability targets, can the lease be renegotiated, or is this treated as a lesson learned for future pricing?
28. What triggers the need for special insurance on a property, and who determines this requirement?
29. How are custom lease clauses—especially unusual ones—escalated for legal review, and what's the typical turnaround time?

## Cross-Functional Coordination & Timing
30. What is the average end-to-end timeline for onboarding a property, from initial landlord contact to tenant move-in?
31. Which steps in this process, if any, can happen in parallel rather than sequentially (e.g., can marketing prep begin before inspection is fully complete)?
32. When multiple stakeholders (landlord, property manager, compliance, IT) are involved, who is ultimately accountable for keeping the process moving forward and resolving bottlenecks?
33. How are handoffs tracked between departments (e.g., from property coordinator to property manager, or marketing to leasing agent)? Is there a formal handoff checklist or notification system?
34. What are the most common bottlenecks or delays in this process, based on past experience?
35. How does the company handle a landlord who wants to withdraw from the onboarding process midway through? Are there any contractual or financial obligations at that point?

## Roles & Responsibilities
36. Is there a single "process owner" who has visibility into the entire onboarding journey, or does oversight get distributed across departments with no end-to-end owner?
37. What happens if the property manager assigned is unavailable (e.g., leaves the company, goes on leave) mid-process? How is continuity ensured?
38. Are leasing agents always distinct from property managers, or can one person hold both roles depending on the property/team size?
