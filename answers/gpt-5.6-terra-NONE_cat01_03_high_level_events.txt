## Proposed High-Level Process Model

For each `CaseID`, the low-level events can be aggregated into the following higher-level manufacturing stages:

1. **Material Preparation**
2. **Welding Assembly**
3. **Weld Quality Inspection**
4. **Protective Coating and Curing**
5. **Final Quality Inspection**

These stages reflect the product’s physical progression: material is identified and prepared, assembled through welding, checked, coated, cured, and finally inspected.

---

## Aggregation Rules and Rationale

| High-Level Activity | Included Low-Level Activities | Typical Resources | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Material Preparation** | Retrieve raw metal sheet; Scan barcode; Place metal sheet on table; Align metal sheet; Preheat metal sheet | Operator A, Robot Arm #2, Heating Unit #1 | These events prepare and validate the raw material before transformation. The barcode scan establishes traceability, placement and alignment position the material, and preheating conditions it for welding. |
| **Welding Assembly** | Pick up welding tool; Weld corner A; Weld corner B | Operator B | The tool pickup is setup for the welding operation, while the corner welds are the actual assembly actions. Together they form one coherent joining stage. |
| **Weld Quality Inspection** | Measure weld integrity | Quality Sensor #1 | This is a dedicated quality-control checkpoint immediately after welding. It evaluates whether the assembly operation met the required integrity threshold. |
| **Protective Coating and Curing** | Apply protective coating; Dry coating | Coating Unit #3, Heating Unit #2 | Applying coating and drying/curing it are inseparable parts of surface finishing. The coating is not complete until it has been cured. |
| **Final Quality Inspection** | Visual check | Operator C | The final visual check validates the completed, coated item before release to the next process, packaging, or shipment stage. |

## Structured Representation

### 1. Material Preparation

**Proposed aggregated activity name:** `Material Preparation`

**Low-level events included:**
- Retrieve raw metal sheet
- Scan barcode
- Place metal sheet on table
- Align metal sheet
- Preheat metal sheet

**Example for Case A1:**

| Start Time | End Time | High-Level Activity | Included Events |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 2024-03-01 08:00:05 | 2024-03-01 08:00:20 | Material Preparation | Retrieve raw metal sheet; Scan barcode; Place metal sheet on table; Align metal sheet; Preheat metal sheet |

**Rationale:**  
All actions concern obtaining, identifying, positioning, and conditioning the metal sheet. Even though several resources participate, they support the same business objective: ensuring that the correct material is ready for welding.

### 2. Welding Assembly

**Proposed aggregated activity name:** `Welding Assembly`

**Low-level events included:**
- Pick up welding tool
- Weld corner A
- Weld corner B

**Example for Case A1:**

| Start Time | End Time | High-Level Activity | Included Events |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 2024-03-01 08:01:00 | 2024-03-01 08:01:10 | Welding Assembly | Pick up welding tool; Weld corner A; Weld corner B |

**Rationale:**  
Picking up the welding tool is a setup action directly associated with the subsequent welds. The corner-specific weld activities are detailed substeps of the broader assembly operation.

### 3. Weld Quality Inspection

**Proposed aggregated activity name:** `Weld Quality Inspection`

**Low-level events included:**
- Measure weld integrity

**Example for Case A1:**

| Start Time | End Time | High-Level Activity | Included Events |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 2024-03-01 08:01:20 | 2024-03-01 08:01:20 | Weld Quality Inspection | Measure weld integrity |

**Rationale:**  
Although it is represented by one event in the sample, it is a semantically distinct inspection stage. It uses a quality sensor and produces a measurement (`IntegrityScore`) used to determine whether welding succeeded.

**Potential rule:**  
- If `IntegrityScore >= acceptance threshold`, continue to coating.
- If below threshold, route the case to rework, repair, or rejection.

### 4. Protective Coating and Curing

**Proposed aggregated activity name:** `Protective Coating and Curing`

**Low-level events included:**
- Apply protective coating

**Rationale:**  
The coating application and drying operation belong to one finishing stage. Applying polyurethane alone does not provide the required protective result; curing/drying finalizes the protective layer.

### 5. Final Quality Inspection

**Proposed aggregated activity name:** `Final Quality Inspection`

**Low-level events included:**
- Visual check

**Example for Case A1:**

| Start Time | End Time | High-Level Activity | Included Events |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 2024-03-01 08:02:00 | 2024-03-01 08:02:00 | Final Quality Inspection | Visual check |

**Rationale:**  
This inspection occurs after all transformation and finishing work has completed. It serves as the final acceptance check, confirming the product’s visible condition and overall process outcome.

## General Grouping Logic for the Full Log

A practical aggregation procedure for the larger event log would be:

1. **Sort events by `CaseID` and `Timestamp`.**
2. **Map activity names to process phases** using an activity-to-stage dictionary.
3. **Combine consecutive events with the same assigned phase** into one high-level event.
4. **Preserve important information** from low-level events as attributes of the aggregated event:
   - material identifier and barcode;
   - temperatures;
   - welding tool and weld type;
   - inspection scores;
   - coating type;
   - pass/fail results.
5. **Split groups when a clear process boundary occurs**, such as:
   - changing from preparation to assembly;
   - changing from production to inspection;
   - an inspection failure causing rework;
   - a large time gap beyond an operational threshold;
   - movement to a different production station or process route.

An example activity-to-stage mapping is:

```text
Material Preparation:
  Retrieve raw metal sheet
  Scan barcode
  Place metal sheet on table
  Align metal sheet
  Preheat metal sheet

Welding Assembly:
  Pick up welding tool
  Weld corner A
  Weld corner B
  Weld corner <any>

Weld Quality Inspection:
  Measure weld integrity
  Inspect weld
  Scan weld quality result

Protective Coating and Curing:
  Apply protective coating
  Dry coating
  Cure coating
  Verify coating temperature

Final Quality Inspection:
  Visual check
  Final measurement
  Final approval
  Release product
```
