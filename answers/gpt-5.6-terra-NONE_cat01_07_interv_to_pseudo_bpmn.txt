## BPMN-like Textual Process Model: Invoice Approval and Payment Scheduling

### Participants / Swimlanes
1. **Supplier**
2. **Accounts Payable (AP) Clerk — Mary**
3. **Purchasing Department / Purchasing Officer — Dan**
4. **Department Manager — Carlos**
5. **AP Supervisor — Rita**
6. **Supplier Relationship Manager — Karen**
7. **Accounting / Invoice Management System**

### Key Artifacts / Data Objects
- Supplier invoice
- Purchase order (PO)
- Goods/services receipt confirmation
- PO/invoice matching record
- Supplier contract or agreed pricing terms
- Corrected/revised invoice
- Retrospective PO
- Manager approval/rejection decision
- Payment schedule / payment-run entry
- Exception/escalation record

---

## Main Process Flow

```text
START EVENT
  Trigger: Supplier submits invoice, typically by email.

1. Receive Invoice
  Performer: AP Clerk
  Input: Supplier invoice received by email.
  Output: Invoice available for validation.

2. Validate Purchase Order Reference
  Performer: AP Clerk
  Action: Check whether the invoice contains a valid PO number.

  EXCLUSIVE GATEWAY: "Valid PO number present?"
  
  [Yes]
    -> Continue to Step 3: Log Invoice in Accounting System.

  [No]
    -> Step 2A: Request Missing PO Information / Return Invoice
       Performer: AP Clerk
       Action: Send invoice back to supplier and request PO number or additional details.
       Output: Request for corrected/revised invoice.

    -> Optional Step 2B: Supplier Relationship Assistance
       Performer: Supplier Relationship Manager
       Condition: AP requires help obtaining PO information from supplier, especially for key suppliers.
       Action: Contact supplier and request PO number or revised invoice.

    -> EXCLUSIVE GATEWAY: "Supplier provides valid PO or corrected invoice?"
       
       [Yes]
         -> Return to Step 2: Validate Purchase Order Reference.

       [No / Cannot obtain PO]
         -> Step 2C: Escalate Missing-PO Invoice
            Performer: AP Clerk
            Action: Escalate invoice to AP Supervisor.
            -> Continue to Exception Handling Flow A.
```

## Standard Validation and Approval Flow

```text
3. Log Invoice in Accounting System
  Performer: AP Clerk
  System: Accounting / invoice management system
  Action: Record invoice details, supplier details, amount, and PO reference.
  Output: Logged invoice record.

4. Request Goods/Services Receipt Confirmation
  Performer: AP Clerk
  Action: Forward invoice or validation request to Purchasing.
  Input: Logged invoice and associated PO.
  Output: Request for confirmation that ordered goods/services were received.

5. Verify PO, Receipt, and Invoice Details
  Performer: Purchasing Officer
  Action:
    - Open relevant PO.
    - Compare invoice quantities, prices, delivery charges, and other line-item details with the PO.
    - Confirm that goods or services were received, where applicable.
  Output: Purchasing confirmation or discrepancy notification.

  EXCLUSIVE GATEWAY: "Goods/services received and invoice details consistent with PO?"
  
  [Yes]
    -> Step 6: Send Purchasing Confirmation to AP
       Performer: Purchasing Officer
       Action: Email or otherwise send confirmation to AP.
       Output: Receipt/PO verification confirmation.

    -> Step 7: Match Invoice Against PO in System
       Performer: AP Clerk
       System: Accounting / invoice management system
       Action: Complete invoice-to-PO matching in the system.
       Input: Invoice, PO, and Purchasing confirmation.
       Output: Matched invoice record.

    -> Step 8: Request Department Manager Approval
       Performer: Accounting / Invoice Management System
       Trigger: Invoice is matched to the PO and goods/services receipt has been confirmed.
       Action: Automatically notify department manager and request sign-off.
       Output: Approval task/notification for Department Manager.

    -> Step 9: Review Invoice for Approval
       Performer: Department Manager
       System: Accounting / invoice management system
       Action: Review invoice details, PO, Purchasing confirmation, quantities, pricing, budget impact, and special notes.

    -> EXCLUSIVE GATEWAY: "Manager approves invoice?"

       [Approved]
         -> Step 10: Record Manager Approval
            Performer: Department Manager / System
            Output: Approved invoice.

         -> Step 11: Schedule Invoice for Payment
            Performer: AP Clerk
            System: Accounting / invoice management system
            Action: Add approved invoice to the next payment cycle/payment run.
            Output: Payment-scheduled invoice.

         -> END EVENT
            Result: Invoice approved and scheduled for payment.

       [Rejected or Put on Hold]
         -> Step 9A: Notify AP of Rejection/Hold and Investigation Request
            Performer: Department Manager
            Action: Reject invoice or instruct AP to hold it pending investigation.
            Reason examples:
              - Unexpected items
              - Suspicious invoice details
              - Significant variance from budget
              - Unexpected total amount

         -> Continue to Exception Handling Flow C.

  [No]
    -> Step 5A: Notify AP of Discrepancy / Request Invoice Hold
       Performer: Purchasing Officer
       Action:
         - Inform AP that invoice does not match PO, receipt, quantities, price, or delivery charges; and/or
         - Ask AP to place invoice on hold pending supplier correction.
       Output: Discrepancy notification and held invoice.

    -> Continue to Exception Handling Flow B.
```

## Exception Handling Flow A: Missing PO / No Identifiable PO

```text
A1. Review Missing-PO Exception
  Performer: AP Supervisor
  Trigger: Invoice lacks a PO and AP cannot obtain a valid PO reference.

A2. Investigate Invoice Origin and Authorization
  Performer: AP Supervisor
  Participants as needed:
    - Department Manager / Department Head
    - Supplier Relationship Manager
    - Supplier
  Action:
    - Determine whether the invoice relates to an authorized purchase.
    - Determine whether services were contracted informally.
    - Seek supporting documentation or supplier clarification.

  EXCLUSIVE GATEWAY: "Can a valid PO be obtained or created?"

  [Existing PO identified / Supplier supplies corrected invoice]
    -> Return to Step 2: Validate Purchase Order Reference.

  [Retrospective PO required and approved]
    -> A3. Create Retrospective PO
       Performer: Relevant department / department head, coordinated by AP Supervisor.
       Output: Retrospective PO.
       -> Return to Step 3: Log Invoice in Accounting System,
          or, if already logged, proceed to Step 4: Request Goods/Services Receipt Confirmation.

  [Invoice is unauthorized or cannot be supported]
    -> A4. Reject Invoice
       Performer: AP Supervisor, potentially with department head involvement.
       Action: Notify supplier and/or AP that invoice will not proceed.
       -> END EVENT
          Result: Invoice rejected; no payment scheduled.
```

## Exception Handling Flow B: PO/Invoice/Receipt Discrepancy

```text
B1. Place Invoice on Hold
  Performer: AP Clerk
  System: Accounting / invoice management system
  Trigger: Purchasing identifies a mismatch or AP identifies a discrepancy during matching.
  Examples:
    - Incorrect pricing
    - Extra billed units
    - Missing invoice items
    - Incorrect delivery charges
    - Receipt not confirmed
    - Invoice does not align with PO terms

B2. Clarify Discrepancy
  Performer: AP Clerk and/or Purchasing Officer
  Action:
    - Contact supplier directly; or
    - Ask supplier to submit a corrected invoice; or
    - Review PO and contract details.

B3. Optional Supplier Relationship Escalation
  Performer: Supplier Relationship Manager
  Condition: Supplier is a key supplier, discrepancy concerns agreed terms, or AP/Purchasing needs assistance.
  Action:
    - Contact supplier.
    - Review supplier contract.
    - Confirm correct pricing or terms.
    - Request a revised invoice or missing information.
  Output: Clarification, contract-based pricing guidance, or corrected invoice.

  EXCLUSIVE GATEWAY: "Discrepancy resolved?"

  [Yes — corrected invoice/clarification confirms valid charges]
    -> If invoice was revised:
         Return to Step 2 or Step 3 as appropriate to validate and log the revised invoice.
    -> If existing invoice can be corrected/validated:
         Return to Step 4: Request Goods/Services Receipt Confirmation
         or Step 5: Verify PO, Receipt, and Invoice Details,
         depending on the point at which the discrepancy was resolved.

  [No — cannot be resolved by AP/Purchasing]
    -> B4. Escalate to AP Supervisor
       Performer: AP Clerk
       -> Continue with B5.

B5. Supervisor-Led Resolution
  Performer: AP Supervisor
  Action:
    - Coordinate between AP, Purchasing, department heads, Supplier Relationship Manager, and supplier.
    - Determine whether the invoice should be corrected, supported with a retrospective PO, or rejected.

  EXCLUSIVE GATEWAY: "Exception resolution outcome?"

  [Resolved]
    -> Return to Step 4 or Step 5 for confirmation and matching before manager approval.

  [Rejected]
    -> Notify supplier/AP and close invoice.
    -> END EVENT
       Result: Invoice rejected; no payment scheduled.
```

## Exception Handling Flow C: Manager Rejection or Hold

```text
C1. Hold Invoice for Investigation
  Performer: AP Clerk
  Trigger: Department Manager rejects invoice or requests investigation.
  Input: Manager comments/rejection reason.

C2. Investigate Manager Concern
  Performer: AP Clerk
  Participants as needed:
    - Purchasing Officer
    - AP Supervisor
    - Supplier Relationship Manager
    - Supplier
    - Department Manager
  Examples of investigation triggers:
    - Large variance from departmental budget
    - Unexpected item
    - Suspicious amount
    - Pricing or quantity concern
    - Incomplete supporting information

  EXCLUSIVE GATEWAY: "Concern resolved and invoice remains payable?"

  [Yes]
    -> Revalidate invoice, PO, receipt, and any corrected details.
    -> Return to Step 5: Verify PO, Receipt, and Invoice Details,
       then proceed through matching and manager approval again.

  [No]
    -> C3. Reject Invoice / Do Not Pay
       Performer: Department Manager and/or AP Supervisor, depending on organizational authority.
       Action: Mark invoice as rejected and notify relevant parties.
       -> END EVENT
          Result: Invoice rejected; no payment scheduled.
```

## Consolidated End States

```text
END EVENT 1: Invoice approved and scheduled for payment
  Condition:
    - Valid PO available
    - Goods/services receipt confirmed
    - Invoice matches PO or discrepancies resolved
    - Department Manager approves
  Output:
    - Invoice included in next payment cycle.

END EVENT 2: Invoice rejected
  Condition examples:
    - No valid or retrospective PO can be established
    - Supplier cannot resolve missing information or discrepancy
    - Invoice is unauthorized
    - Manager rejects invoice after investigation
  Output:
    - Invoice closed without payment.

END EVENT 3: Invoice remains on hold
  Condition:
    - Awaiting supplier response, corrected invoice, PO creation, receipt confirmation, or internal investigation.
  Note:
    - This is an intermediate operational state rather than a desired final outcome. AP Supervisor intervention is intended to prevent invoices from remaining here indefinitely.
```

## Modeling Notes and Observed Ambiguities

1. **Order of receipt confirmation versus detailed PO matching:**  
   Mary describes first obtaining confirmation that goods/services were received and then matching invoice line items against the PO. Dan describes his Purchasing review as covering quantities, prices, delivery charges, and potentially receipt status in one activity. The model treats these as a combined Purchasing verification step followed by AP’s system-based matching activity.

2. **System versus email-based communication:**  
   Most handoffs appear to occur through email, except manager approval, which is explicitly system-based and triggered automatically after PO match and receipt confirmation. The exact system workflow for Purchasing confirmation is not fully specified.

3. **Missing-PO invoices:**  
   The normal process requires a confirmed PO. However, Rita identifies a special path for informally contracted services, where a retrospective PO may be created. Such invoices must not proceed to approval without either an existing PO or a retrospective PO.

4. **Authority to reject invoices:**  
   The department manager can reject or hold an invoice. The AP Supervisor may determine that an exception invoice should be rejected after investigation, although the transcripts do not fully specify formal approval authority for that rejection.

5. **Payment execution is out of scope:**  
   The described process ends when AP schedules the approved invoice for the next payment run. Actual payment-run execution, bank processing, and supplier remittance are not described in the interviews.
