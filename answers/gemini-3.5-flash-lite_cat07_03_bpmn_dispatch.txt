---

### **Overview**
* **Title:** Dispatch of goods – Computer Hardware Shop
* **Type:** BPMN Collaborative Process / Swimlane Diagram
* **Actors (Swimlanes):**
  1. **Logistics** (Top lane)
  2. **Secretary** (Middle lane)
  3. **Warehouse** (Bottom lane)

---

### **Process Flow Breakdown**

#### **1. Initiation (Secretary Lane)**
* **Start Event:** The process begins with the event **"Ship goods"** (represented by a thin circular icon).
* **Parallel Split (AND-Gateway):** Immediately after starting, the process splits into two parallel paths:
  * **Upper Path (Secretary / Logistics):** Handles shipment method, insurance, and labeling.
  * **Lower Path (Warehouse):** Handles packaging goods and potential delays.

---

#### **2. Upper Path: Shipment Method & Documentation (Secretary & Logistics)**

* **Clarify Shipment Method:** The Secretary clarifies the method of shipment, referencing an **"Order details"** document.
* **Exclusive Gateway (XOR):** **"Special handling?"**
  * **Branch A ("no"):**
    * Moves to **"Check if insurance is required"** (references a **"Customer information"** database).
    * **Inclusive/Exclusive Gateway:** **"If insurance necessary"**
      * **"If insurance necessary" branch:** Goes up to the **Logistics** lane to **"Insure parcel"**, then merges back.
      * **"always" branch:** Proceeds directly to **"Write package label"** (Secretary lane).
    * Both sub-branches converge at a gateway.
  * **Branch B ("yes"):**
    * Proceeds sequentially:
      1. **"Get 3 offers from logistic companies"**
      2. **"Select logistic company and place order"**
  * Both major branches (Special handling Yes/No) converge at an **Exclusive Gateway (XOR)** in the Secretary lane.

---

#### **3. Lower Path: Packaging & Delays (Warehouse)**

* **Package goods:** The warehouse begins packaging the items.
* **Timer Intermediate Event:** Attached to "Package goods" is a timer set to **"48 hours"**.
  * If packaging takes longer than 48 hours, a trigger fires: **"Notify customer about delay"**.
* **Merge Gateway:** The normal packaging path and the delay notification path merge.

---

#### **4. Convergence and Finalization (Warehouse Lane)**

* **Parallel Gateway (AND):** The upper path (completed paperwork/labeling from Secretary/Logistics) and the lower path (packaged goods from the Warehouse) converge.
* **Final Steps (Warehouse Lane):**
  1. **"Fill out Shipment paperwork"**
  2. **"Move package to pickup area"**
* **End Event:** The process terminates with the event **"Shipment prepared"** (represented by a bold circular icon).
