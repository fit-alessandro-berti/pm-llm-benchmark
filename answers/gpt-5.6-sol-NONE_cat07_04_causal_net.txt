The visualization is a workflow/process graph for handling a request.

### Main process
- **a — register request** starts the workflow.
- The request can proceed to one or more review activities:
  - **b — examine thoroughly**
  - **c — examine casually**
  - **d — check ticket**
- These paths converge at **e — decide**.
- From the decision, the request branches to either:
  - **g — pay compensation**, or
  - **h — reject request**
- Both outcomes lead to **z — end**.
- **f — reinitiate request** forms a feedback loop, allowing the case to return to earlier examination/checking steps and then be reconsidered.

Rounded rectangles represent activities, arrows show process flow, and black dots indicate split/join routing points.

### Legend
The gray panel at the bottom right illustrates six routing patterns:
- **XOR-split:** choose exactly one outgoing path.
- **AND-split:** activate all outgoing paths.
- **OR-split:** activate one or more outgoing paths.
- **XOR-join:** merge alternatives without synchronization.
- **AND-join:** wait for all incoming paths.
- **OR-join:** synchronize the incoming paths that are active.
