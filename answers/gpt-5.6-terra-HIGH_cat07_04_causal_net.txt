The visualization is a workflow/process diagram for handling a request, using rounded rectangular activity boxes, directed arrows, and black-dot routing nodes.

### Main workflow
- **a — register request** begins the process.
- The request can be routed to one of several examination/checking activities:
  - **b — examine thoroughly**
  - **c — examine casually**
  - **d — check ticket**
- These review paths feed into:
  - **e — decide**
- From the decision, the process branches toward either:
  - **g — pay compensation**, then
  - **h — reject request**
- Both of those outcomes lead to:
  - **z — end**

There is also a feedback/rework activity:
- **f — reinitiate request**
- Curved arrows show that, under certain conditions, the process can loop back from later stages to reinitiate the request and send it again through examination/checking activities.

### Routing notation
The black circles and connecting lines represent control-flow split and join patterns. A legend in the lower-right gray panel illustrates six possible routing mechanisms:

- **XOR-split:** chooses exactly one outgoing path.
- **AND-split:** starts all outgoing paths in parallel.
- **OR-split:** starts one or more outgoing paths.
- **XOR-join:** merges alternative paths.
- **AND-join:** waits for all incoming paths.
- **OR-join:** synchronizes one or more incoming paths depending on which were activated.

Overall, the graphic demonstrates a request-handling process with alternative review steps, a decision point, compensation or rejection outcomes, and a possible reinitiation loop.
