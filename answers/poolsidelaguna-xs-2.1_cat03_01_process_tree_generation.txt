### Step-by-Step Explanation:

1. **Initial Sequence (A  RG  IA  TI  TC):**
   - The patient starts with Arrival (A), followed by Registration (RG), Intake Assessment (IA), Triage Interview (TI), and Triage Classification (TC), all executed in sequence.

2. **Exclusive Choice After Triage (CE or FBP):**
   - Based on the triage classification, the patient undergoes either a Comprehensive ECG (CE) or a Full Blood Panel (FBP). This is modeled as an XOR operator between CE and FBP.

3. **Loop for Abnormal Results:**
   - If the initial diagnostic test results are abnormal, a loop initiates. The loop includes Specialist Consultation (SC), an exclusive treatment choice (Targeted Medication (TM) or Specialized Physical Therapy (SPT)), and a Re-Diagnostic Test (RDT) with an exclusive choice between Follow-up ECG (FCE) or Follow-up Blood Analysis (FBA). The loop continues until results are normal. This is modeled using the loop operator (`*`):
     - **Loop Body (SC  (TM or SPT)  (FCE or FBA))**: The sequence of SC, treatment choice, and RDT.
     - **Exit (tau)**: After each iteration, the loop can be exited (via tau) if results are normal.

4. **Parallel Advanced Imaging and Nutritional Counseling:**
   - After exiting the loop, the patient undergoes Advanced Imaging (AI) and Nutritional Counseling (NC) in parallel.

5. **Follow-up Steps (FAS  DDI):**
   - Post-advanced diagnostics, the patient completes Follow-up Appointment Scheduling (FAS) and Detailed Discharge Instructions (DDI) in sequence.

6. **Final Parallel Administrative Clearance (BL, ICP, MRF):**
