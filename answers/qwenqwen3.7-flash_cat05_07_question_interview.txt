Do these checks happen simultaneously upon activation, or are they staggered? If a check fails after activation, what is the immediate remediation workflow, and does the listing go offline during this period?

### 2. Decision Criteria and Stakeholder Authority
*   **Property Manager Assignment:** You listed several factors for assigning a property manager (workload, geography, experience, track record). How are these factors weighted? Is there a formal scoring rubric used to determine the best fit, or is this largely a judgment call by a supervisor?
*   **Pricing Strategy:** The digital marketing team updates pricing models based on market trends, but the coordinator also records an "expected rental price range." How do these inputs reconcile? If the market trend suggests a price outside the landlord's expected range, what is the negotiation process, and who has the final authority to approve the listing price?
*   **Tenant Selection Boundaries:** In the event of multiple qualified applicants, you mentioned the manager may negotiate terms or offer discounts. What are the specific boundaries of a property manager's authority regarding lease concessions? At what threshold (e.g., discount percentage or rent reduction) does approval from a senior stakeholder become required?
*   **Tie-Breakers:** When evaluating tenants, if two candidates appear equally suitable based on credit and employment, what secondary criteria does the process use to prioritize one over the other?

### 3. Exception Handling and Escalation
*   **Documentation Delays:** If a landlord is unable to provide critical documentation (like deeds or compliance certificates) within a reasonable timeframe, does the process halt entirely? Or is there a provisional status that allows certain downstream activities to proceed conditionally?
*   **Repair Disputes:** When the inspector identifies safety or habitability issues, you noted the manager works with the landlord to address them. How is the decision made regarding who bears the cost of repairs? What is the escalation path if the landlord refuses to make mandatory changes?
*   **Custom Clauses and Insurance:** For properties requiring special insurance or custom lease clauses, how are these exceptions routed? Is there a centralized legal review process, or is this handled regionally? How do we ensure these customizations don't introduce risk or compliance gaps?
*   **Failed Inspections:** If a property fails inspection repeatedly, at what point does the company consider terminating the onboarding relationship? Is there a defined limit on re-inspections or repair attempts?

### 4. Roles, Responsibilities, and Handoffs
*   **Coordinator vs. Manager Transition:** You described a clear shift from the Property Coordinator to the Property Manager. Is there a formal handoff protocol or checklist to ensure no information is lost when responsibility transfers? Does the coordinator remain involved in any capacity after the manager takes over?

*   **IT Integration Validation:** The IT department checks that data entries are correctly integrated. How is "correct integration" defined? Is there an automated validation suite that runs, or is this a manual spot-check? How are discrepancies between the central database and the rental portal resolved?

### 5. Definition of Completion and Metrics
*   **Process Closure:** Could you clarify the definition of "onboarding complete"? Is the process considered finished once the lease is signed, once the security deposit is collected, or only after the move-in date has passed and the quality audits are cleared?
