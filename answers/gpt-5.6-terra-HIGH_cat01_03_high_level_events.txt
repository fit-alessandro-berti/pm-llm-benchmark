## Proposed High-Level Manufacturing Process

The low-level events can be aggregated into five coherent manufacturing stages:

1. **Material Preparation**
2. **Welding Assembly**
3. **In-Process Weld Quality Inspection**
4. **Surface Coating and Curing**
5. **Final Quality Inspection**

### 1. Aggregation Rules

| High-Level Activity | Included Low-Level Activities | Grouping Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Material Preparation** | Retrieve raw metal sheet; Scan barcode; Place metal sheet on table; Align metal sheet; Preheat metal sheet | These activities prepare, identify, position, and condition the raw material before fabrication begins. They occur consecutively and transform the sheet from inventory material into a production-ready workpiece. |
| **Welding Assembly** | Pick up welding tool; Weld corner A; Weld corner B | The tool-selection event is setup for the welding operation, while both corner welds are execution steps of the same assembly/fabrication task. |
| **In-Process Weld Quality Inspection** | Measure weld integrity | This is a quality-control checkpoint performed immediately after welding. It evaluates the output of the welding stage before the product proceeds to finishing. |
| **Surface Coating and Curing** | Apply protective coating; Dry coating | Both activities form a single finishing operation. The coating is applied first and then cured/dried to produce the final protective surface. |
| **Final Quality Inspection** | Visual check | This is the final acceptance check after all production and finishing work is completed. It determines whether the finished item passes release criteria. |

## Detailed Event-to-Stage Mapping

### Material Preparation

| Order | Low-Level Event | Resource | Purpose Within Stage |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Retrieve raw metal sheet | Operator A | Obtains the required input material. |
| 2 | Scan barcode | Operator A | Identifies and records material traceability information. |
| 3 | Place metal sheet on table | Operator A | Moves the material to the production workstation. |
| 4 | Align metal sheet | Robot Arm #2 | Positions the material accurately for processing. |
| 5 | Preheat metal sheet | Heating Unit #1 | Conditions the material for welding or subsequent fabrication. |

**Proposed high-level name:** `Material Preparation`

**Rationale:**  
All events relate to bringing raw material into the work area, verifying its identity, positioning it, and preparing it thermally. Although multiple resources are involved, they contribute to one shared outcome: a correctly identified and prepared metal sheet ready for welding.

---

### Welding Assembly

| Order | Low-Level Event | Resource | Purpose Within Stage |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pick up welding tool | Operator B | Sets up the tool required for fabrication. |
| 2 | Weld corner A | Operator B | Performs the first required joining operation. |
| 3 | Weld corner B | Operator B | Performs the second required joining operation. |

**Proposed high-level name:** `Welding Assembly`

**Rationale:**  
Picking up the welding tool is operational setup directly tied to the subsequent welding actions. Welding corners A and B are repeated or component-level actions within the same larger assembly operation. They should not generally appear as separate high-level process steps unless analysis specifically needs corner-level detail.

---

### In-Process Weld Quality Inspection

| Order | Low-Level Event | Resource | Purpose Within Stage |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Measure weld integrity | Quality Sensor #1 | Evaluates whether the completed welds meet integrity requirements. |

**Proposed high-level name:** `In-Process Weld Quality Inspection`

**Rationale:**  
This event is logically distinct from welding because it does not create or modify the product; it verifies the result of the welding operation. It is also performed by a quality-specific resource and occurs before finishing, making it an in-process quality gate.

---

### Surface Coating and Curing

| Order | Low-Level Event | Resource | Purpose Within Stage |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Apply protective coating | Coating Unit #3 | Adds a protective surface layer. |
| 2 | Dry coating | Heating Unit #2 | Cures or dries the coating so that it becomes durable and complete. |

**Proposed high-level name:** `Surface Coating and Curing`

**Rationale:**  
Coating application and drying are inseparable parts of the same finishing process. The coating is not functionally complete until it has been dried or cured. Grouping them avoids treating an intermediate, unfinished coating state as a full process stage.

---

### Final Quality Inspection

| Order | Low-Level Event | Resource | Purpose Within Stage |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Visual check | Operator C | Performs final visual acceptance verification. |

**Proposed high-level name:** `Final Quality Inspection`

**Rationale:**  
The visual check occurs after welding, weld validation, coating, and drying. It therefore serves as the final release or acceptance control for the finished product.

---

## Resulting High-Level Workflow

```text
Material Preparation
        
Welding Assembly
        
In-Process Weld Quality Inspection
        
Surface Coating and Curing
        
Final Quality Inspection
```

This aggregation preserves important process boundaries: material readiness, physical fabrication, quality verification, finishing, and final release. It also makes the event log more suitable for process mining, bottleneck analysis, cycle-time analysis, and quality-performance reporting.
