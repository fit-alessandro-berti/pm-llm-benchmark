## 1. Recurring activities and their contextual meanings

### Verify Documents
This activity occurs more than once in cases C001 and C003, but the checks have different purposes:

- **First occurrence:** initial identity or document verification.
- **Later occurrence:** a follow-up verification after new information or an account correction has been provided.

The follow-up also differs by case:

- **C001:** re-check after the customer submitted a missing ID scan.
- **C003:** re-check after an internal account adjustment.

### Call Customer
This activity occurs twice in C002:

- **First call:** clarify an account discrepancy.
- **Second call:** confirm that the account adjustment was completed.

The single calls in C001 and C003 also have distinct purposes:

- **C001:** request missing documents.
- **C003:** ask the customer to return to the branch.

`Initiate Request` also has different channel contexts—online versus in-branch—so it is made more specific in the revised log.

## 3. Naming rationale

- **“Initial … Verification”** identifies the first document check in a case.
- **“Re-verify … After …”** identifies a later verification and states what triggered it.
- **“Contact Customer to …”** replaces the generic `Call Customer` label with the actual business purpose of each call.
- **“Online”** and **“In-Branch”** distinguish the two request-initiation channels.
- **“Final Request Approval”** makes clear that the approval is the concluding supervisory step rather than an intermediate approval.
