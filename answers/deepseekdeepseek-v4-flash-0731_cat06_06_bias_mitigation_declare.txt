### Brief rationale for added bias-mitigating constraints

- **`response(CheckApplicant*, ManualReview)`**  
  Whenever a sensitive attribute is checked, a manual review must eventually occur. This prevents the process from relying directly on demographic data without an additional human/fairness check.

- **`precedence(ManualReview, FinalDecision/Approve/Reject)`**  
  No decision can be made before the manual review happens. This blocks the harmful pattern where an applicant is rejected or approved immediately after sensitive data is seen.

- **`nonchainsuccession(CheckApplicant*, FinalDecision/Approve/Reject)`**  
  A sensitive-attribute check cannot be directly followed by a decision. This explicitly forbids the most direct biased pathway, such as `CheckApplicantRace -> Reject`.

- **`coexistence(ManualReview, FinalDecision)`**  
  A final decision and a manual review must appear together in the same trace. This ensures that the manual review safeguard is not skipped at the end of the process.

All added constraints use `support: 1.0` and `confidence: 1.0`, so they are mandatory in the updated process model.
