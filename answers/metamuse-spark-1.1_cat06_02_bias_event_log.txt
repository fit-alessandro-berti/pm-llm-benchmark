Bias manifests in two places in this process log, compounding to favor a specific in-group.

### 1. Where the bias enters the process

**a) PreliminaryScoring -> `ScoreAdjustment`**
Only one value ever appears: `+10 (Community)` for members of `Highland Civic Darts Club`. 
`None` = 0. This is not based on creditworthiness - membership in a darts club has no predictive relationship to loan repayment.

* C001: 710 -> 720 [Adjusted] because Highland Civic Darts Club
* C004: 690 -> 700 [Adjusted] because Highland Civic Darts Club
* C002, C003, C005: No adjustment despite similar or higher preliminary scores

**b) FinalDecision -> Decision rule using `LocalResident`**

The Rules Engine is applying a different approval threshold based on `LocalResident`:

* If LocalResident = TRUE: Approved at **700** Adjusted - C004 is approved at 700
* If LocalResident = FALSE: Rejected at **715** - C003 is rejected at 715, Approved only at **740** - C005

> Two cases prove this is disparate treatment:

> **C003 vs C004:** C003 is FALSE, None, 715, **Rejected**. C004 is TRUE, Highland Club, 690 raw / 700 adjusted, **Approved**. C003 is 15-25 points *more* creditworthy on underlying score but rejected.

> **C001 vs C002 vs C003:** C001 raw 710 +10 =720 TRUE Approved. C002 raw 720 TRUE Approved. C003 raw 715 FALSE Rejected. Same 710-720 band, different outcome based solely on locality.

ManualReview by the Underwriter does not correct it - it carries the adjusted score forward.

### 2. Which groups are favored

**Favored:** `LocalResident = TRUE` AND `CommunityGroup = Highland Civic Darts Club`
They get both advantages: a score bump in scoring, and a lower decision threshold.

**Secondarily favored:** `LocalResident = TRUE` alone. Even without a community group, 720 is enough to be approved - C002.

**Disfavored:** `LocalResident = FALSE` and `CommunityGroup = None`. They must demonstrate significantly higher creditworthiness to get the same outcome.

This is a classic proxy bias / in-group favoritism problem:
* `LocalResident` is geographic discrimination - similar to redlining. It penalizes non-locals regardless of risk.
* `Highland Civic Darts Club` is social capital / affiliation bias. It rewards membership in a specific, unrelated social group. It is a proxy for nepotism, network, and likely race / class / gender if club membership is not diverse.

### 3. Implications for fairness and equity

This violates the principle that similar creditworthiness should lead to similar decisions.

For an individual who lacks these attributes, even with identical PreliminaryScore:

1.  **They are scored lower** for a non-financial reason.
2.  **They are judged by a stricter standard** at decision time.
3.  The system creates **disparate impact**: Non-locals and non-members must be *more* creditworthy - 25-40 points higher in this log - to access the same resource.

Over time, this compounds inequality: locals/club members get credit, build credit history, get more credit. Non-locals are denied, cannot build local history. The attributes have nothing to do with ability to repay, so the process is not just unfair, it is also legally risky under fair lending principles like ECOA - you cannot use geography or social affiliation as a credit factor.
