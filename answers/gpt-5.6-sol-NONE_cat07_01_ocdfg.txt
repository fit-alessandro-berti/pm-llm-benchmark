The clearest linear path is the sales/order-fulfillment sequence:

**Create Order  Pack Goods  Ship Goods  Confirm Delivery  Close Order  End**

Other paths converge around **Approve Requisition**, then branch toward order sending, stock checks, returns, payment approval, and invoice creation. Several feedback or cross-process links show that the workflow is not purely linear.

Colors distinguish the originating process/object stream—for example, purple for requisitions, blue for purchase/order-related paths, orange for sales fulfillment, green for invoicing, and red for returns. Multiple **End** nodes indicate different possible completion points.
