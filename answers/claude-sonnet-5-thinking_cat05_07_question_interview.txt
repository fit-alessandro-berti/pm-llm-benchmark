# Clarifying Questions for Property Onboarding Process

## Documentation & Verification Phase
1. What specific criteria determine whether a document is "outdated" versus acceptable? Are there defined validity periods for each document type?
2. How long does the company typically wait for a landlord to provide updated documentation before the process stalls or is escalated?
3. Is there a point at which incomplete documentation causes the process to be terminated or paused indefinitely?
4. Who verifies the authenticity of the documents (deeds, certificates), and is there a formal approval sign-off, or is this a subjective judgment call by the coordinator?

## Property Manager Assignment
5. Is there a formal scoring or weighting system for balancing workload, geographic specialization, and experience, or is this assignment more discretionary?
6. What happens if no property manager is a strong match (e.g., no one has experience with a specific property type like student housing)? Is training provided, or is the property assigned anyway?
7. Can a landlord request a change of property manager after the initial assignment, and if so, what triggers that change?

## Introductory Meeting
8. What happens if the landlord and property manager disagree on policies (e.g., landlord wants to allow pets against company policy)? Who has final authority in these disputes?
9. Is this meeting mandatory, or can it be skipped/expedited for repeat landlords who already have properties in the portfolio?

## Inspection & Photography
10. How are the photographer and inspector scheduled—are they in-house staff, or third-party vendors? Does this affect timing or cost responsibility?
11. What is the typical timeline between scheduling and completion of the inspection/photography visit?
12. When the inspector finds issues, who decides whether the landlord or the company is financially responsible for repairs?
13. Is there a maximum number of re-inspection cycles allowed before a property is rejected from onboarding entirely?
14. Are photography and inspection always sequential, or can they happen simultaneously or in either order?

## Marketing & Activation
15. Who has final approval authority on pricing before a listing goes live—the property manager, financial analyst, or someone else?
16. How often are internal pricing models updated, and what market data sources feed into them?
17. Once "activated," can a property be temporarily deactivated (e.g., landlord changes mind), and what process governs that?

## Tenant Screening & Leasing
18. What specific criteria disqualify a tenant application (e.g., credit score thresholds, income-to-rent ratios)?
19. Who has decision-making authority when multiple qualified tenants apply—does the landlord have veto power, or is this solely the property manager's call?
20. Are promotional discounts pre-approved within certain limits, or does each discount require separate authorization?
21. What is the process if a chosen tenant fails to finalize the lease (e.g., backs out) — does the process revert to the next candidate, or restart screening?

## Compliance, Audits & Exceptions
22. At what stage does the compliance officer review the lease—before or after the tenant has signed?
23. What happens if the compliance officer or financial analyst identifies a problem after the lease is signed? Is the lease amended, voided, or handled some other way?
24. How are exceptions like special insurance requirements or custom lease clauses flagged early in the process, rather than discovered late?
25. Is there a standard escalation path when a landlord insists on non-standard terms that conflict with company policy?

## Cross-Cutting / Process-Level Questions
26. What is the average end-to-end timeline for onboarding a property, and which stages tend to cause the most delays?
27. Are there different onboarding pathways for different property types (e.g., luxury units vs. student housing), or is this always the same linear process?
28. How is communication and status tracking handled between the CRM, project management tools, and email—do these systems sync automatically, or require manual updates?
29. If a landlord withdraws mid-process (e.g., after documentation but before tenant screening), what happens to the property record and any work already completed?
30. Who "owns" the overall process end-to-end—is there a single accountable role, or is responsibility distributed across coordinators, managers, and support teams?
