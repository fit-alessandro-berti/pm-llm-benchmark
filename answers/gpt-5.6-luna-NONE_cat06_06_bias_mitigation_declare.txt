### Rationale

- **Existence of `BiasMitigationCheck`**: Ensures that every modeled process contains a fairness review before completion.
- **Coexistence with `ManualReview`**: Applications involving sensitive-attribute checks or demographic-specific decisions must also contain a manual review.
- **Response constraints**: After an attribute-related activity such as `CheckApplicantRace`, a `BiasMitigationCheck` must eventually occur.
- **Precedence constraints**: Decisions such as `Approve` or `Reject` cannot occur unless a bias-mitigation check has already taken place.
- **Succession constraint**: The mitigation check must be followed by the final decision, preserving the intended review order.
- **Non-succession constraints**: Prevent direct transitions from sensitive-attribute checks to approval or rejection.
- **Non-chain-succession constraints**: Specifically prevent an immediate sensitive-attribute-to-rejection transition, reducing the risk of automatic adverse decisions based solely on protected information.

These constraints enforce procedural safeguards. They do not, by themselves, prove statistical fairness; that would additionally require monitoring decision outcomes across demographic groups.
