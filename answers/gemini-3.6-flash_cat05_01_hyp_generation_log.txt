# Process Anomaly & Conformance Analysis Report

## 1. Identified Anomalies and Process Deviations

### **Case 1001: Baseline / Happy Path**
* **Status:** **Fully Compliant**
* **Sequence:** `Register Order` $\rightarrow$ `Perform Credit Check` $\rightarrow$ `Validate Stock` $\rightarrow$ `Confirm Shipment` $\rightarrow$ `Ship Goods` $\rightarrow$ `Issue Invoice` $\rightarrow$ `Receive Payment`
* **Observation:** Follows the expected process flow perfectly with logical time gaps and appropriate role separation.

---

### **Case 1002: Out-of-Order Execution & Post-Shipment Controls**
* **Anomalies Identified:**
  1. **Premature Shipping:** Goods were confirmed (`08:25`) and shipped (`08:40`) **before** mandatory credit and stock checks were conducted.
  2. **Retroactive Risk & Control Checks:** `Perform Credit Check` (`09:10`) and `Validate Stock` (`09:15`) occurred **after** the physical goods had already left the warehouse.
* **Impact:** Exposure to unapproved credit risk (shipping to insolvent customers) and inventory discrepancy risks (shipping without prior allocation).

---

### **Case 1003: Skipped Controls & Post-Hoc Confirmation**
* **Anomalies Identified:**
  1. **Skipped Step:** `Validate Stock` was completely bypassed.
  2. **Inverted Confirmation Flow:** `Ship Goods` (`09:10`) occurred before `Confirm Shipment` (`09:45`), explicitly flagged as `late_confirmation=Y`.
* **Impact:** High risk of partial/failed fulfillment due to unverified inventory, alongside compliance breaches in logistical workflow authorization.

---

### **Case 1004: Payment-Before-Billing & Unscheduled Dispatch**
* **Anomalies Identified:**
  1. **Inverted Financial Flow:** `Receive Payment` (`09:05`) occurred 15 minutes **before** `Issue Invoice` (`09:20`).
  2. **Skipped Steps:** Both `Perform Credit Check` and `Validate Stock` were bypassed.
  3. **Disregarded Logistics Gate:** `Confirm Shipment` was explicitly logged with `shipment_scheduled=N` at `09:25`, yet `Ship Goods` still executed at `09:50`.
* **Impact:** Revenue recognition errors, potential unallocated cash, and failure of warehouse dispatch controls.

## 2. Process Anomaly Hypotheses

| Anomaly Category | Root Cause Hypotheses |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Premature Shipping (Cases 1002, 1003)** | • **Bypass of ERP Hard Gates:** The ERP/WMS system lacks system-level blocking rules preventing warehouse staff from creating picking/shipping tasks prior to order approval.<br>• **Priority Overrides:** High-priority orders (`order_type = 'priority'` in Case 1002) are being rushed manually via direct communication between sales and warehouse staff. |
| **Skipped Credit / Stock Checks (Cases 1003, 1004)** | • **UI/UX Workarounds:** Warehouse staff bypassing mandatory checks when processing urgent orders.<br>• **System Outages:** Downstream integration failures between Credit/Inventory services and the core Order Management System. |
| **Payment Before Invoice (Case 1004)** | • **Pre-payment / Pro-Forma Process:** Customer paid upfront via cash/credit card, but the process lacks a dedicated `Receive Prepayment` event type.<br>• **Manual Entry Error:** Accounting team back-dated or mistimed event logging. |
| **Shipment Despite Unscheduled Status (Case 1004)** | • **Physical Process Decoupling:** Warehouse personnel rely on physical paperwork or direct verbal instruction rather than system authorization flags (`shipment_scheduled=N`). |

## 3. Investigative SQL Queries (PostgreSQL)

### **Query 1: Detect Cases Where Goods Were Shipped Before Credit Approval**
*Identifies cases where physical dispatch occurred before financial credit authorization.*

```sql
WITH credit_checks AS (
    SELECT case_id, MIN(timestamp) AS credit_check_time
    FROM order_event_log
    WHERE activity = 'Perform Credit Check'
    GROUP BY case_id
),
shipments AS (
    SELECT case_id, MIN(timestamp) AS ship_time
    FROM order_event_log
    WHERE activity = 'Ship Goods'
    GROUP BY case_id
)
SELECT 
    s.case_id,
    o.order_type,
    o.order_value,
    c.credit_check_time,
    s.ship_time,
    CASE 
        WHEN c.credit_check_time IS NULL THEN 'Credit Check Skipped'
        WHEN s.ship_time < c.credit_check_time THEN 'Shipped Before Credit Check'
    END AS anomaly_type
FROM shipments s
LEFT JOIN credit_checks c ON s.case_id = c.case_id
JOIN orders o ON s.case_id = o.case_id
WHERE c.credit_check_time IS NULL 
   OR s.ship_time < c.credit_check_time;
```

### **Query 2: Identify Completely Bypassed Process Steps**
*Evaluates process completeness against mandatory process steps for all completed orders.*

```sql
SELECT 
    o.case_id,
    o.order_type,
    BOOL_OR(l.activity = 'Perform Credit Check') AS has_credit_check,
    BOOL_OR(l.activity = 'Validate Stock')       AS has_stock_validation,
    BOOL_OR(l.activity = 'Confirm Shipment')     AS has_shipment_confirmation,
    BOOL_OR(l.activity = 'Issue Invoice')        AS has_invoice
FROM orders o
JOIN order_event_log l ON o.case_id = l.case_id
GROUP BY o.case_id, o.order_type
HAVING NOT (
    BOOL_OR(l.activity = 'Perform Credit Check') 
    AND BOOL_OR(l.activity = 'Validate Stock')
    AND BOOL_OR(l.activity = 'Confirm Shipment')
);
```

### **Query 3: Detect Out-of-Sequence Events Using Lead/Lag Window Functions**
*Tracks any case where dependent events were logged out of chronological order.*

```sql
WITH ordered_events AS (
    SELECT 
        case_id,
        event_id,
        activity,
        timestamp,
        LAG(activity) OVER (PARTITION BY case_id ORDER BY timestamp, event_id) AS prev_activity,
        LAG(timestamp) OVER (PARTITION BY case_id ORDER BY timestamp, event_id) AS prev_timestamp
    FROM order_event_log
)
SELECT 
    case_id,
    prev_activity,
    prev_timestamp,
    activity AS current_activity,
    timestamp AS current_timestamp
FROM ordered_events
WHERE 
    -- Payment received before Invoice
    (activity = 'Issue Invoice' AND prev_activity = 'Receive Payment')
    -- Confirm Shipment after Ship Goods
    OR (activity = 'Confirm Shipment' AND prev_activity = 'Ship Goods')
    -- Shipping before Stock Validation
    OR (activity = 'Validate Stock' AND prev_activity = 'Ship Goods');
```

### **Query 4: Identify Unscheduled Dispatches (`shipment_scheduled = 'N'`)**
*Finds orders that were physically shipped despite failing logistics approval.*

```sql
SELECT 
    l_confirm.case_id,
    l_confirm.timestamp AS confirmed_time,
    l_confirm.resource  AS confirmed_by,
    l_ship.timestamp    AS shipped_time,
    l_ship.resource     AS shipped_by,
    r.department        AS shipped_by_dept
FROM order_event_log l_confirm
JOIN order_event_log l_ship 
    ON l_confirm.case_id = l_ship.case_id 
   AND l_ship.activity = 'Ship Goods'
LEFT JOIN resources r 
    ON l_ship.resource = r.resource_id
WHERE l_confirm.activity = 'Confirm Shipment'
  AND l_confirm.additional_info LIKE '%shipment_scheduled=N%';
```
