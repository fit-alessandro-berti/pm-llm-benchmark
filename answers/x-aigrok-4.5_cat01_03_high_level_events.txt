### Notes on Grouping Rules Applied
- **Temporal + phase boundaries**: Gaps between groups (e.g., after preheat before tool pickup; after weld check before coating) mark transitions.
- **Resource/role coherence**: Operators and machines change roles/types across groups (prep operators/machines  weld operator  sensors  coaters  final inspector).
- **Logical process stages**: Aligns with standard discrete manufacturing (prep  join/assemble  mid-process QA  finish  final QA). Measurement values and AdditionalInfo (e.g., scores, temperatures, part IDs) stay as attributes of the high-level steps rather than splitting further.
- **No over-fragmentation**: Single-event groups (inspections) are kept only when they represent distinct process gates; they could later be merged into a combined “Quality Assurance” if coarser analysis is needed.
- **Extensibility**: In a full log, these names can be used for process mining (e.g., as activity labels), bottleneck analysis (durations of high-level steps), or conformance checking against an ideal process model.
