## Discovered process sequence

**Idle/Setup  Cutting  Assembly/Positioning  Welding  Quality Inspection  Packaging/Final Handling  Idle/Reset**

The strongest classifications are cutting and welding because they have distinctive high-vibration, high-temperature, and high-energy signatures. The inspection and packaging labels are less certain: event 10 could also represent post-weld cooling, while events 11–12 could represent unloading or transfer rather than literal packaging. Additional sensor context or known machine states would resolve those ambiguities.
