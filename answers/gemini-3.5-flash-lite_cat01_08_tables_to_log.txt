Based on the provided ERP tables, here is the integrated, chronological event log suitable for process mining. 

### Assumptions & Notes:
1. **Case ID:** `order_id` is used as the unique case identifier.
2. **Activity Granularity:** 
   - `Order Created` is logged from the `Orders` table.
   - `Item Picked` is logged individually for each item line in the `Order Lines` table, mapped with the corresponding warehouse staff (`picker_id`).
   - `Order Dispatched` and `Order Arrived` are extracted from the `Delivery` table, utilizing `carrier_id` as the resource.
   - `Invoice Issued` is derived from the `Invoice` table, using `billing_person_id` as the resource.
   - `Payment Received` is derived from the `Payments` table, linked via `invoice_id`. (Since payments are tied to invoices, their timestamps naturally follow invoice generation).
3. **Order 1004 Exception:** Order 1004 (`CUST-BBB`) has an `Order Created` and an `Item Picked` event, followed by an `Invoice Issued` and `Payment Received` (via INV-1004), but **does not have a delivery record** in the provided tables. It has been included up to its available process steps.
4. **Resources:** Names and roles from the `Resources Table` have been resolved into the `Resource/Actor` and `Role` columns for better contextual clarity.
