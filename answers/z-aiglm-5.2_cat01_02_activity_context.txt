### 4. Justification of Naming Choices

- **Initial Document Verification vs. Document Re-verification:** In cases C001 and C003, the "Verify Documents" activity happens twice. The first instance represents the standard first-pass check of customer ID, making "Initial Document Verification" highly accurate. The second instance is triggered by a specific intervening event (uploading missing scans in C001, or correcting account details in C003), so renaming it to "Document Re-verification" clearly communicates that it is a secondary validation step rather than a redundant or mistaken repeat of the first.

- **Call for Missing Documents / Call for Discrepancy Clarification / Call for Adjustment Confirmation / Call to Recall Customer:** The generic "Call Customer" obscures the actual business purpose of the interaction. 
  - In **C001**, the context explicitly notes that the verification was incomplete, leading to the name *Call for Missing Documents*.
  - In **C002**, the first call mentions a discrepancy, and the second is a follow-up to confirm internal adjustments, leading to *Call for Discrepancy Clarification* and *Call for Adjustment Confirmation*.
  - In **C003**, the context notes the customer physically stepped out of the branch, so *Call to Recall Customer* precisely captures the physical-spatial context of the interaction.
