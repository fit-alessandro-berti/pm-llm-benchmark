# BPMN-Like Textual Process Model: Invoice Approval

## 1. Process Scope

**Start:** An invoice is received by Accounts Payable, usually by email.  
**Successful end:** The approved invoice is scheduled for a payment cycle.  
**Alternative end:** The invoice is rejected because required information or discrepancies cannot be resolved.

## 2. Participants / Lanes

### Internal pool: Organization
- **Accounts Payable Clerk (AP Clerk)**
- **Purchasing Officer / Purchasing Department**
- **Department Manager**
- **Accounts Payable Supervisor**
- **Supplier Relationship Manager**
- **Department Head** — involved when a retrospective PO may be needed
- **Accounting / Workflow System**

### External pool
- **Supplier**

## 3. Main Process Flow

All gateways below are **exclusive (XOR)** unless otherwise stated.

### A. Invoice intake and PO validation

1. **[Message Start Event] Invoice received**
   - **Performer:** AP Clerk
   - **Input:** Supplier invoice, usually received by email

2. **[Task A1] Check invoice for a valid purchase order reference**
   - **Performer:** AP Clerk
   - The AP Clerk verifies that a PO number is present and appears valid.

3. **[Gateway G1] Valid PO reference available?**
   - **Yes:** Continue to Task A2.
   - **No:** Follow the missing-PO exception flow in Section 4.1.

4. **[Task A2] Log invoice in accounting system**
   - **Performer:** AP Clerk
   - **Output:** Invoice record in the accounting system

5. **[Task A3] Forward invoice and PO information to Purchasing**
   - **Performer:** AP Clerk
   - **Recipient:** Purchasing Officer
   - **Inputs:** Invoice, PO reference, accounting-system record

---

### B. Purchasing verification

6. **[Subprocess B1] Verify invoice and receipt against purchase order**
   - **Performer:** Purchasing Officer
   - The subprocess includes:
     - Confirm that the goods or services were received.
     - Compare quantities ordered against quantities invoiced.
     - Compare PO and invoice prices.
     - Review delivery charges and other applicable terms.
   - Both receipt confirmation and order-detail validation must be completed before the invoice can proceed.

7. **[Gateway G2] Receipt confirmed and invoice details consistent with PO?**
   - **Yes:** Continue to Task B2.
   - **No:** Follow the discrepancy-resolution flow in Section 4.2.
   - **PO missing or invalid despite initial AP check:** Return to Gateway G1 and follow the missing-PO path.

8. **[Task B2] Send purchasing confirmation to Accounts Payable**
   - **Performer:** Purchasing Officer
   - **Output:** Confirmation email or equivalent confirmation record

---

### C. Formal AP matching and approval request

9. **[Task C1] Match invoice line items against PO in the accounting system**
   - **Performer:** AP Clerk
   - **Inputs:** Invoice, PO, receipt confirmation, Purchasing confirmation

10. **[Gateway G3] Formal invoice-to-PO match successful?**
    - **Yes:** Continue to Task C2.
    - **No:** AP Clerk requests clarification from Purchasing and enters the discrepancy-resolution flow in Section 4.2.

11. **[Task C2] Generate manager approval request**
    - **Performer:** Accounting / Workflow System
    - **Trigger condition:** Receipt has been confirmed and the invoice has successfully matched the PO.
    - **Action:** Send an automatic approval notification to the Department Manager.
    - **Attached/displayed information:** Invoice, PO, Purchasing confirmation, and relevant notes.

---

### D. Department manager approval

12. **[Task D1] Review invoice for final approval**
    - **Performer:** Department Manager
    - Review includes:
      - Quantities and pricing
      - Total amount
      - Comparison with budget expectations
      - Unexpected items
      - Special notes or other suspicious details

13. **[Gateway G4] Manager decision**
    - **Approve:** Continue to Task D2.
    - **Hold for investigation:** Follow the manager-review exception in Section 4.3.
    - **Reject:** Continue to End Event E2.

14. **[Task D2] Approve invoice in the system**
    - **Performer:** Department Manager
    - **Output:** Recorded approval decision

15. **[Task D3] Schedule invoice for payment**
    - **Performer:** AP Clerk / Accounts Payable
    - The invoice is added to the next applicable payment cycle.

16. **[End Event E1] Invoice scheduled for payment**

> Actual payment execution is outside the scope described in the interviews.

---

## 4. Exception and Variation Flows

### 4.1 Missing or invalid PO

1. From **Gateway G1**, if no valid PO is available:

2. **[Task X1] Place invoice on hold and request PO information or a revised invoice**
   - **Performer:** AP Clerk
   - **Recipient:** Supplier
   - The Supplier Relationship Manager may assist, especially for key suppliers.
   - **Possible output:** PO number, additional details, or revised invoice

3. **[Intermediate Message Event] Supplier information or revised invoice received**

4. **[Gateway XG1] Can the invoice now be associated with a valid PO?**
   - **Yes:** Return to Task A1 for PO validation and then log or update the invoice.
   - **No, because the invoice relates to an informal service or no PO exists:** Escalate to Task X2.
   - **No, and no acceptable basis for payment can be established:** Proceed to End Event E2.

5. **[Task X2] Investigate non-PO invoice**
   - **Performer:** AP Supervisor
   - **Supporting participant:** Relevant Department Head
   - The Supervisor coordinates with the supplier and internal departments.

6. **[Gateway XG2] Disposition of non-PO invoice**
   - **Retrospective PO authorized:** Department Head/Purchasing creates or arranges the PO; return to Task A1.
   - **Supplier provides corrected information or revised invoice:** Return to Task A1.
   - **Invoice should not be accepted:** Proceed to End Event E2.

Purchasing may also discover a missing PO after AP intake. In that case, Purchasing returns the invoice to AP, and this same exception flow is used.

---

### 4.2 Quantity, pricing, delivery, receipt, or other discrepancy

1. A discrepancy may be detected during:
   - Purchasing verification at Gateway G2, or
   - AP formal matching at Gateway G3.

2. **[Task X3] Place invoice on hold and document discrepancy**
   - **Performer:** Purchasing Officer or AP Clerk

3. **[Subprocess X4] Resolve discrepancy**
   - One or more of the following variations may occur:
     - Purchasing contacts the supplier directly.
     - Purchasing asks AP to hold the invoice until a corrected invoice is received.
     - AP requests clarification from Purchasing.
     - Supplier Relationship Manager contacts the supplier.
     - Supplier Relationship Manager checks the supplier contract to determine the correct price or terms.
     - Supplier provides a corrected or revised invoice.
     - AP Supervisor coordinates between AP, Purchasing, the supplier, and relevant departments.

4. **[Gateway XG3] Has the discrepancy been resolved?**
   - **Yes, with clarification only:** Return to Subprocess B1 for Purchasing re-verification.
   - **Yes, with a revised invoice or new PO information:** Return to Task A1 so the updated documents are revalidated.
   - **No, but further escalation is possible:** Continue to Task X5.
   - **No, and invoice must be rejected:** Proceed to End Event E2.

5. **[Task X5] Escalate unresolved discrepancy**
   - **Performer:** AP Clerk
   - **Recipient:** AP Supervisor

6. **[Task X6] Coordinate final exception resolution**
   - **Performer:** AP Supervisor
   - The Supervisor may contact suppliers, involve the Supplier Relationship Manager, or coordinate across departments.

7. **[Gateway XG4] Supervisor resolution**
   - **Issue resolved:** Return to Subprocess B1. The invoice must re-enter the normal flow through Purchasing confirmation, AP matching, and manager approval.
   - **Retrospective PO required:** Follow the non-PO path beginning at Task X2.
   - **Issue cannot be resolved:** Proceed to End Event E2.

---

### 4.3 Manager hold or rejection

1. From **Gateway G4**, if the manager identifies an unusual total, unexpected item, budget concern, or other suspicious condition:

2. **Hold path:**
   - **[Task X7] Place invoice on hold and request investigation**
     - **Performer:** Department Manager
     - **Recipient:** AP Clerk
   - **[Task X8] Investigate manager concern**
     - **Primary performer:** AP Clerk
     - Purchasing, the AP Supervisor, or Supplier Relationship Manager may assist.
   - If the concern is resolved, return to **Subprocess B1** to revalidate the invoice before generating a new manager approval request.
   - If the concern cannot be resolved, proceed to End Event E2.

3. **Reject path:**
   - **[Task X9] Record manager rejection**
     - **Performer:** Department Manager / Workflow System
   - Proceed to End Event E2.

4. **[End Event E2] Invoice rejected or returned without payment scheduling**

---

## 5. Main Artifacts and Data Objects

| Artifact / Data Object | Created or Used By | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Supplier invoice | Supplier, AP, Purchasing, Manager | Primary payment request |
| Revised/corrected invoice | Supplier | Resolves missing or incorrect information |
| Purchase order | Purchasing/AP/System | Establishes authorized purchase details |
| Retrospective PO | Department Head/Purchasing | Regularizes an otherwise valid non-PO invoice |
| Receipt confirmation | Purchasing | Confirms goods or services were received |
| Purchasing confirmation email/record | Purchasing | Confirms validation to AP |
| Supplier contract | Supplier Relationship Manager | Resolves pricing or terms disputes |
| Discrepancy/hold information | AP/Purchasing/System | Records why processing was paused |
| Accounting-system invoice record | AP/System | Tracks the invoice through the process |
| Manager approval notification | Workflow System | Requests final approval |
| Manager approval/rejection record | Manager/System | Records the final decision |
| Payment-cycle schedule | AP/System | Places the invoice into an upcoming payment run |

## 6. Noted Ambiguities and Consolidation Decisions

- Mary describes Purchasing primarily as confirming receipt, while Dan describes Purchasing as also comparing quantities, prices, and delivery charges. These activities are consolidated into one Purchasing verification subprocess.
- Mary performs a formal system line-item match after Purchasing confirmation. This is modeled as a separate AP control rather than as a duplicate of Purchasing’s operational verification.
- Responsibility for contacting the supplier varies by case. Purchasing, AP, the AP Supervisor, or the Supplier Relationship Manager may perform this work; therefore, it is represented as a flexible exception-resolution subprocess.
- Manager rejection may be final or may lead to investigation. The model separates an explicit rejection path from a temporary hold-and-investigate path.
