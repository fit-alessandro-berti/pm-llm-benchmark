### Assumptions

- `order_id` is the case identifier.
- Each order-line picking record becomes a separate **Item Picked** event.
- Each delivery produces separate **Order Dispatched** and **Order Arrived** events.
- Each invoice and payment produces a separate event.
- Payments are linked to orders through `invoice_id  order_id`.
- No delivery, invoice, or payment events are fabricated where source data is absent.
- Events are sorted chronologically within each case. The table includes identifiers and relevant attributes to distinguish repeated activities.
