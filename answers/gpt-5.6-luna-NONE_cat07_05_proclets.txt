The visualization is a multi-part UML-style workflow diagram for a medical laboratory testing process.

- **Left panel (a): Two “proctel”/process classes connected through channels**
  - The workflow begins with a **doctor** entering an order.
  - A **lab assistant** takes a blood sample.
  - The sample is processed through steps such as **Receive result**, **Judge results**, and **Do tests again** if necessary.
  - The process ends with **Send report**.
  - On the right, the laboratory side performs the test, **makes a report**, and may **perform the test again** and provide an updated report.
  - Dashed boxes labeled **Order system** and **HIS** represent communication channels between the two processes.
  - Circles and black square markers indicate message/event points or process termination points.
  - Roles such as doctor, lab assistant, and chemical analyst are shown beside the activities they perform.

- **Right panel (b): Class diagram**
  - It shows two classes: **Lab visit** and **Lab test**.
  - A “Lab visit” **requires** one or more “Lab test” instances:
    - One lab visit can require multiple lab tests (`1..*`).
    - Each lab test is associated with one lab visit (`1..1`).

- **Bottom panel (c): Example of a performative/message**
  - At **11:00**, the **lab visit – John** sends a **Create** action through the **Order system** to the **lab test – HGB test for John**.
  - The message content is: **“Can you perform a HGB test for John?”**
  - The message is marked **Private** and has an **OUT** direction.

Overall, the figure illustrates how a laboratory visit initiates and coordinates one or more lab tests through workflow activities, system channels, and explicit messages.
