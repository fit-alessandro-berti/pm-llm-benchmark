**BPMN-like Textual Process Model: Invoice Approval Process**

**Process Name:** Invoice Approval (from receipt to payment scheduling)  
**Primary Goal:** Validate, match, approve, and schedule supplier invoices for payment while handling mismatches and missing information.

**Participants / Lanes (Roles):**
- **Accounts Payable (AP) Clerk** (Mary) – primary handler of intake, logging, matching, escalation, and payment scheduling.
- **Purchasing Officer** (Dan) – verifies receipt of goods/services and alignment with Purchase Order (PO).
- **Department Manager** (Carlos) – final operational approval.
- **AP Supervisor** (Rita) – exception handling and escalation resolution.
- **Supplier Relationship Manager** (Karen) – optional support for supplier communication and contract clarification (invoked on mismatches).
- **External: Supplier** – provides invoices and responds to requests for corrections/PO numbers.
- **System** (Accounting System) – logging, matching support, automatic notifications/emails, and approval workflow.

**Key Artifacts / Data Objects:**
- Invoice (incoming, usually email; later system record).
- Purchase Order (PO) – must be valid/referenced.
- Receipt Confirmation (from Purchasing).
- Match result / discrepancy notes.
- Manager Approval (system action).
- Payment schedule entry.

**Start Event:** Invoice received (typically by email to AP).

### Main Sequence Flow (Happy Path)

1. **Receive Invoice** (AP Clerk)  
   Trigger: Email arrival (or equivalent intake).

2. **Check for Valid PO Number** (AP Clerk)  
   Exclusive Gateway (XOR): “Valid PO present?”  
   - **No** → **Request Missing Details / Revised Invoice from Supplier** (AP Clerk; may involve Karen).  
     - Loop: Wait for corrected invoice → return to Receive Invoice / Check for Valid PO.  
     - (Note: In some cases involving informal/service contracts with no PO, escalate immediately to AP Supervisor – see Exceptions.)  
   - **Yes** → continue.

3. **Log Invoice into Accounting System** (AP Clerk)  
   Creates system record linked to PO.

4. **Forward Invoice to Purchasing for Receipt Confirmation** (AP Clerk)  
   Hand-off: Invoice (and PO reference) sent to Purchasing Officer.

5. **Verify Receipt of Goods/Services and Compare to PO** (Purchasing Officer)  
   Activities: Open PO; compare quantities, prices, delivery charges, and line items against invoice.  
   Exclusive Gateway (XOR): “Details match and goods/services confirmed received?”  
   - **No (Discrepancy)** → **Resolve Discrepancy** path (see Exceptions / Alternate Paths).  
   - **Yes** → **Send Confirmation Email/Notification to AP** (Purchasing Officer).

6. **Match Invoice Line Items to PO in System** (AP Clerk)  
   Performed after Purchasing confirmation is received. (Interviewees describe slight overlap: Purchasing does the detailed comparison; AP finalizes system match.)

7. **System Triggers Manager Approval Request** (System)  
   Automatic email/notification to Department Manager once match + confirmation exist.  
   (Mary: “triggered after we’ve confirmed both the receipt of goods and that the invoice matches the PO.”)

8. **Review and Approve Invoice** (Department Manager)  
   System shows invoice, PO, and confirmation.  
   Exclusive Gateway (XOR): “Approve?”  
   - **Yes** → continue.  
   - **No / Suspicious** → **Reject or Request Hold + Investigation** (Manager asks AP Clerk to investigate; rare – ~once every couple of months). Returns to AP for rework (loop back toward matching or escalation).

9. **Schedule for Payment** (AP Clerk)  
   Add approved invoice to next payment cycle/run.

**End Event:** Invoice scheduled for payment (ready for payment run).

### Gateways Summary
- **XOR – Valid PO?** (after intake)  
- **XOR – Match & Receipt Confirmed?** (Purchasing verification)  
- **XOR – Manager Approves?** (final operational gate)

### Exceptions, Variations & Alternate Paths (Consolidated from all interviews)
These are invoked when the happy path fails. Multiple interviewees describe overlapping resolution mechanisms; the model consolidates them while noting role involvement.

**A. Missing or Invalid PO**  
- AP Clerk returns invoice to Supplier (or asks for PO number).  
- If Supplier Relationship Manager is looped in (common for key suppliers): Karen contacts Supplier for PO or revised invoice.  
- Special case (informal/service contracts with no original PO): Escalate to AP Supervisor. Supervisor works with Department Heads to create a retrospective PO or decide to reject the invoice. Once resolved, re-enter main flow at Logging or Purchasing verification.

**B. Discrepancy (pricing, quantities, missing items, unexpected charges)**  
- Purchasing Officer may contact Supplier directly **or** ask AP Clerk to hold the invoice pending corrected version.  
- AP Clerk emails Purchasing for clarification.  
- If not easily resolved: Escalate to AP Supervisor (Rita). Supervisor coordinates between departments, contacts Suppliers, and/or involves Karen (who checks contracts and clarifies correct pricing/terms).  
- Karen does **not** perform system approvals; she only facilitates resolution so the invoice can re-enter the normal flow.  
- After resolution: Return to Purchasing confirmation → system match → Manager approval → Schedule Payment.  
  (Rita explicitly states that cleared exceptions rejoin the normal path.)

**C. Escalation / Stuck Invoices**  
- AP Supervisor intervenes primarily on unresolved mismatches or missing information to prevent indefinite delays.  
- Does **not** perform final approvals.

**D. Manager Rejection / Investigation**  
- Manager may reject or instruct AP to hold and investigate (budget variance, unexpected items).  
- Returns control to AP Clerk (possible further escalation to Supervisor or Karen).

**Uncertainty / Conflicting Accounts Noted:**
- Exact ownership of “matching” is described with mild overlap (Mary performs final system match after confirmation; Dan performs detailed comparison upon receipt of the invoice from AP). Model treats Purchasing as the verification step and AP as the system-recording step.
- Timing of Karen involvement is reactive (on mismatch or missing PO) rather than a fixed step.
- “Hold until corrected invoice” vs. “contact Supplier directly” are both valid resolution tactics; either may be used depending on the case.
- Retrospective PO creation is mentioned only by the Supervisor and applies mainly to informal contracts.

**Overall Process Characteristics:**
- Linear happy path with three main decision points.  
- Strong emphasis on PO existence and three-way match (Invoice ↔ PO ↔ Receipt).  
- Exceptions form rework loops that eventually rejoin the main path after resolution.  
- System automation is limited mainly to notifications and approval workflow; most investigation remains human-driven.  
- No parallel gateways are described; all branching is exclusive (XOR) with eventual rejoining or rejection.

This textual model is sufficiently detailed and unambiguous for direct translation into a standard BPMN 2.0 diagram (pools/lanes for roles, tasks, exclusive gateways, sequence flows, message flows for Supplier interactions, and annotation for exception handlers).
