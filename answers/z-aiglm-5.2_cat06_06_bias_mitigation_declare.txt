### Explanation of Added Constraints and Rationale

1. **`existence` of `BiasMitigationCheck`**: 
   - **Rationale**: By enforcing that a `BiasMitigationCheck` activity must exist in every valid trace, we ensure that no loan application can be processed to completion without passing through a step explicitly designed to evaluate and mitigate algorithmic or human bias.

2. **`response` from `CheckApplicantRace` to `BiasMitigationCheck`**: 
   - **Rationale**: This constraint ensures that whenever an applicant's sensitive attribute (e.g., race) is checked during the process, a `BiasMitigationCheck` must eventually follow. This prevents the process from simply observing a sensitive attribute and proceeding directly to a decision without evaluating the fairness implications of that observation.

3. **`precedence` from `BiasMitigationCheck` to `FinalDecision`**: 
   - **Rationale**: This dictates that a `BiasMitigationCheck` must occur *before* a `FinalDecision` is made. It guarantees that the final outcome (whether an approval, rejection, or request for more info) is informed by the results of the bias mitigation step, rather than bypassing it.

4. **`coexistence` of `ManualReview` and `FinalDecision`**: 
   - **Rationale**: This constraint ensures that human oversight (`ManualReview`) is inherently tied to the `FinalDecision` step. If a final decision is made, a manual review must also be present in the trace, and vice versa. This adds a layer of human accountability to the final outcome, reducing the risk of automated biased decisions.

5. **`nonchainsuccession` from `CheckApplicantRace` to `Reject`**: 
   - **Rationale**: This explicitly forbids a `Reject` activity from immediately following a `CheckApplicantRace` activity. It breaks any direct causal chain where the observation of a minority status could instantly trigger a rejection, forcing the process to route through intermediate steps (like the `BiasMitigationCheck` or `ManualReview`) before a rejection can be finalized.
