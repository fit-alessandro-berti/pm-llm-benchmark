### Rationale for the Added Constraints

| Constraint | What it enforces | How it reduces bias |
|---|---|---|
| **`existence`** (`ManualReview`, `BiasMitigationCheck`) | These two fairness activities must appear in every trace. | Guarantees that no applicant’s file can be processed without an explicit oversight and mitigation step being present in the workflow. |
| **`coexistence`** (`Reject_Minority`  `ManualReview`, `Approve_Minority`  `ManualReview`) | A minority-targeted decision and `ManualReview` must always occur together. | Prevents a biased decision (approve/reject for a minority applicant) from being made in isolation; it forces a paired manual review, adding accountability. |
| **`response`** (`CheckApplicantRace/Gender/Age`  `BiasMitigationCheck`) | Whenever a sensitive attribute is checked, `BiasMitigationCheck` must eventually occur. | Stops the process from disclosing demographic data and then skipping the fairness check before reaching a decision. |
| **`succession`** (`CheckApplicantRace/Gender/Age`  `BiasMitigationCheck`) | The sensitive disclosure and the mitigation check must occur in correct sequential order (both directions of dependency). | Strengthens `response` by ensuring the mitigation step is structurally tied to the disclosure event, not merely present elsewhere in the trace. |
| **`precedence`** (`BiasMitigationCheck`  `Reject`/`Approve` and minority variants) | Every decision can only happen after `BiasMitigationCheck` has already occurred. | Blocks any direct or indirect path to a decision (including biased minority-specific outcomes) unless the bias-mitigation gate has been satisfied first. |
| **`nonsuccession`** (`CheckApplicantRace/Gender/Age`  decision activities) | A decision can **not** directly follow a sensitive-attribute disclosure. | Eliminates the risk of an immediate biased outcome (e.g., seeing race and instantly rejecting) by forcing at least one intermediate event. |
| **`nonchainsuccession`** (`CheckApplicantRace/Gender/Age`  decision activities) | A decision can **not** be the *very next* event after a sensitive disclosure (no adjacency). | Closes the “adjacent bias” loophole, ensuring that even if other activities exist, the decision is never immediately adjacent to the sensitive check, reinforcing separation of disclosure and judgment. |

Together, these rules restructure the loan-review flow so that sensitive demographic checks are always followed by an explicit mitigation review, decisions are always preceded by that review, and minority-specific outcomes are never rendered without a co-occurring manual review—substantially reducing the structural opportunity for discriminatory processing.
