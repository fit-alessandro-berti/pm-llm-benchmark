**The DECLARE model defines a set of declarative constraints on a loan application process rather than a single fixed sequence of steps.** It uses unary constraints (existence, absence, exactly-one, init) and binary templates (response, precedence, succession, alternate variants, chain variants, coexistence, non-coexistence, etc.) to restrict which activities may, must, or must not occur and in what relative order. All support/confidence values are 1.0, so the constraints are treated as hard rules.

Below is a practical description of the process that the model aims to enforce, an explanation of how the individual constraints contribute to order and compliance, the real-world motivations behind them, and the consequences of violations or omissions. Because the model as written contains internal inconsistencies, those are called out explicitly.

### Practical Step-by-Step Description of the Intended Process

From the customer’s initial application through to (attempted) final disbursement and notification, a process that tries to respect the coherent positive constraints would look roughly like this:

1. **Receive_Application** (mandatory start)  
   The customer submits a loan application. This is required to be the first activity (init) and must occur at least once (existence). The response constraint then requires that a Preliminary_Credit_Check eventually follows.

2. **Preliminary_Credit_Check** (exactly once)  
   An initial automated or manual assessment of the applicant’s creditworthiness is performed. The succession constraint with Gather_Additional_Documents requires that document collection follows this step, and the exactly-one constraint prevents repeated or missing checks.

3. **Gather_Additional_Documents** (mandatory)  
   Pay stubs, identification, bank statements, or other supporting material are collected. Because of coexistence with Authorize_Contract_Terms (and the existence of Gather_Additional_Documents), authorization of terms must also occur somewhere in the case. The alternate-succession constraint with Quality_Assurance_Review further requires that these two activities alternate if either occurs more than once.

4. **Quality_Assurance_Review**  
   The prepared materials or draft offer are checked against internal quality and compliance standards. The precedence constraint ensures this review occurs before any Authorize_Contract_Terms. The chain-succession constraint with Assemble_Loan_Offer_Package would require the two activities to occur as an immediate pair (Quality_Assurance_Review immediately followed by Assemble_Loan_Offer_Package).

5. **Assemble_Loan_Offer_Package**  
   Final terms, interest rates, conditions, and documents are prepared. The responded-existence constraint requires that a Quality_Assurance_Review exists somewhere in the trace if this activity occurs. The chain-response constraint would then demand that Transfer_Funds occurs immediately afterward.

6. **Authorize_Contract_Terms**  
   Official approval or finalization of the loan terms. This must occur (coexistence + existence of Gather_Additional_Documents) and must be preceded by Quality_Assurance_Review.

7. **Transfer_Funds** and **Notify_Customer**  
   The approved amount is disbursed and the customer is informed of the outcome. Alternate-response and alternate-precedence constraints would govern their mutual sequencing and prevent multiple transfers without intervening notifications (and vice versa). The non-succession constraint prevents any further Preliminary_Credit_Check after notification, and the non-chain-succession constraint forbids Authorize_Contract_Terms being immediately followed by Notify_Customer.

Throughout the entire process, **Proceed_Without_Compliance** is forbidden (absence).

**Important caveat on model consistency.** Taken literally, two constraints make a complete end-to-end process that includes fund disbursement impossible:

- Non-coexistence of Transfer_Funds and Receive_Application, combined with the mandatory existence and init of Receive_Application, implies Transfer_Funds can never occur.
- Because chain-response requires Assemble_Loan_Offer_Package to be immediately followed by Transfer_Funds, Assemble_Loan_Offer_Package is also effectively forbidden (otherwise the non-coexistence constraint would be violated).
- Independently, chain-precedence of Authorize_Contract_Terms over Preliminary_Credit_Check requires that the (exactly one) Preliminary_Credit_Check be immediately preceded by Authorize_Contract_Terms. This places authorization of terms before the early-stage credit check, which conflicts with the logical ordering established by response, succession, precedence of Quality_Assurance_Review before Authorize_Contract_Terms, and the later positioning of authorization.

Consequently, any fully conforming trace under the given model can never reach fund transfer or even offer-package assembly. A compliant process is limited to the assessment, document-gathering, quality-review, and authorization phases (or to cases that stop short of disbursement). The constraints involving Transfer_Funds become vacuously true. This is most likely an artifact of a synthetic or imperfectly constructed example rather than an intentional design for a working loan process.

### How the Constraints Enforce Compliant, Ordered Behavior

- **Init + existence of Receive_Application + response to Preliminary_Credit_Check** guarantee that every case begins with a formal application and that a credit check will eventually follow.
- **Exactly-one Preliminary_Credit_Check + succession with Gather_Additional_Documents** ensure a single early risk assessment precedes document collection.
- **Existence of Gather_Additional_Documents + coexistence with Authorize_Contract_Terms** force both activities to appear together, preventing incomplete files or unauthorized decisions.
- **Precedence of Quality_Assurance_Review before Authorize_Contract_Terms**, **responded-existence of Quality_Assurance_Review for Assemble_Loan_Offer_Package**, **alternate-succession between Gather_Additional_Documents and Quality_Assurance_Review**, and **chain-succession between Quality_Assurance_Review and Assemble_Loan_Offer_Package** tightly couple quality control with assembly and authorization, ensuring reviews happen before finalization and that critical hand-offs are adjacent or alternating.
- **Alternate-response / alternate-precedence between Transfer_Funds and Notify_Customer**, **non-succession of Notify_Customer before any later Preliminary_Credit_Check**, and **non-chain-succession of Authorize_Contract_Terms immediately followed by Notify_Customer** control the sequencing of disbursement and customer communication and prevent illogical late re-checks or immediate post-authorization notifications.
- **Absence of Proceed_Without_Compliance** is an absolute ban on any bypass of mandatory rules.
- The negative constraints (non-coexistence, non-succession, non-chain-succession) act as safety interlocks that forbid dangerous or circular combinations.

Collectively these rules turn an otherwise free-form set of activities into a controlled, auditable workflow that cannot skip critical risk or quality gates.

### Real-World Motivations

These constraints mirror well-established principles in regulated consumer lending:

- Formal intake and early, single credit assessment protect against wasted effort on ineligible applicants, support fair-lending and ability-to-repay requirements, and reduce fraud exposure.
- Mandatory document collection linked to authorization ensures complete credit files for audits, risk decisions, and regulatory examination.
- Tight quality-assurance and assembly constraints before authorization and disbursement guard against erroneous terms, disclosure violations (e.g., Truth in Lending / Regulation Z), and subsequent customer disputes or rework.
- Controlled sequencing of funds transfer and customer notification supports transparency, operational coordination, and timely communication expectations.
- Absolute prohibition on proceeding without compliance reflects the high regulatory, legal, and reputational cost of control failures in banking (AML/KYC, consumer-protection rules, internal-control frameworks such as SOX or Basel expectations).
- Negative constraints act as additional safety nets that separate stages or prevent premature or out-of-order actions that could create security, audit, or customer-experience problems.

Even the conflicting constraints can be read as extreme expressions of separation-of-duties or system-isolation ideas, though in their present form they overshoot and render a complete successful loan impossible.

### Consequences of Out-of-Order Execution or Omitted Mandatory Steps

- Missing or delayed Receive_Application destroys the audit trail, creates fair-lending and timing disputes, and leaves the institution unable to prove when or how an application was received.
- Skipping, repeating, or mis-ordering the Preliminary_Credit_Check produces incomplete risk assessment, higher default rates, regulatory criticism for inadequate underwriting, and wasted document-collection effort.
- Gathering documents without a prior credit check or without subsequent authorization wastes resources, creates unnecessary privacy exposure, and yields incomplete decision files.
- Assembling an offer package or authorizing terms without the required quality-assurance review (or breaking the required adjacency/alternation) risks incorrect rates, fees, or disclosures, exposing the lender to customer harm, legal claims, and supervisory action.
- Any occurrence of Transfer_Funds despite the non-coexistence rule (or without the upstream quality, assembly, and authorization steps) constitutes unauthorized disbursement—direct financial loss, fraud opportunity, severe regulatory sanctions, potential criminal liability, and reputational damage. Conversely, because the model forbids Transfer_Funds entirely, a strictly conforming process never actually disburses money; customers never receive funds, applications stall, and the institution cannot fulfill its core product.
- Notification out of sequence generates customer confusion, complaints, mismatched expectations about funds, and possible regulatory issues around timely disclosure.
- Any instance of Proceed_Without_Compliance is an immediate, serious compliance breach that can trigger fines, consent orders, heightened supervisory scrutiny, personal accountability, and temporary suspension of lending activity.

In short, systematic violations erode the control environment, elevate credit, operational, and compliance risk, damage customer relationships, and can ultimately threaten the institution’s ability to continue operating as a lender. The value of a DECLARE model (and of process mining more generally) lies precisely in making such rule violations and model inconsistencies visible so they can be repaired before they cause real-world harm.

The provided model therefore serves as a useful illustration of both the power of declarative constraints and the necessity of validating that a discovered or hand-crafted set of rules is itself consistent and aligned with the intended business outcome.
