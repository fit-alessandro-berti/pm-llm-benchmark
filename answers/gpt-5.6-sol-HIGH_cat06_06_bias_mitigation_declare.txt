### Rationale

- **`exactly_one(FinalDecision)`** ensures each application receives one authoritative outcome, preventing repeated or inconsistent decisions after sensitive information is disclosed.
- **`coexistence(FinalDecision, ManualReview)`** applies manual review to every completed application. This universal safeguard avoids imposing additional scrutiny only on protected groups.
- **`succession(BiasMitigationCheck, ManualReview)`** requires every bias-mitigation check to be followed by manual review and every manual review to have a preceding mitigation check.
- **`succession(ManualReview, FinalDecision)`** ensures the review occurs before the final outcome and is eventually followed by that outcome.
- **`chainresponse(CheckApplicant*, BiasMitigationCheck)`** requires an immediate mitigation check whenever age, gender, or race information is accessed.
- **`nonchainsuccession(CheckApplicant*, FinalDecision)`** explicitly prohibits a final decision from occurring immediately after a sensitive-attribute check.

`nonchainsuccession` is used rather than `nonsuccession` because the goal is to prohibit a **direct** transition. A full non-succession constraint would prevent any later final decision after checking a sensitive attribute, making valid completion impossible. These are process-level safeguards; outcome monitoring and statistical fairness audits are still needed to detect disparities in actual approvals and rejections.
