**Key design choices reflected in the model:**
- **`init`**: Every trace starts with `IG`.
- **`exactly_one`**: One `IG` and one `FL` per case.
- **`response` / `precedence` / `succession`**: Enforce the main flow: design  feasibility/cost  prototype  lab/user testing  approval  marketing  launch.
- **`coexistence`**: `TFC` and `CE` occur together; `LT` and `UT` occur together.
- **`alt*` / `chain*`**: Capture stricter ordering (e.g., no intermediate approvals between `AG` and `MP`).
- **`non*`**: Prevent backward jumps (e.g., no design draft after prototype, no launch before idea).
