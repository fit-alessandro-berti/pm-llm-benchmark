**Justification of renaming choices**

- **Verify Documents** appears twice in C001 and twice in C003 (and once in C002).  
  - The first occurrence in each case is always the primary identity/document check performed immediately after initiation → renamed **Initial Document Verification**.  
  - The second occurrence is always a subsequent check that depends on intervening work (additional documents uploaded in C001, or an account adjustment in C003) → renamed **Re-Verify Documents After Submission** / **Re-Verify Documents After Adjustment** to capture the causal context.

- **Call Customer** appears twice in C002 (and once each in C001 and C003).  
  - In C001 the call is triggered by incomplete verification and explicitly requests missing material → **Call Customer for Missing Documents**.  
  - In C002 the first call investigates an account discrepancy; the second call confirms the resulting internal change → **Call Customer to Clarify Discrepancy** and **Follow-up Call to Confirm Adjustments**.  
  - In C003 the call is made solely to bring a walk-in customer back into the branch → **Call Customer to Return to Branch**.

These renames preserve the original activity semantics while making the distinct process roles (initial vs. corrective, investigative vs. confirmatory, request-driven vs. presence-driven) explicit from the activity name alone.
