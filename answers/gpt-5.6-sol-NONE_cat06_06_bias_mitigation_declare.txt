### Rationale

- **Sensitive-attribute responses:** Each race, gender, or age check requires a later `BiasMitigationCheck`, introducing an explicit fairness control.
- **Decision precedence:** `Approve`, `Reject`, and `FinalDecision` may occur only after a `BiasMitigationCheck`.
- **No immediate sensitive-attribute decisions:** `nonchainsuccession` prohibits a sensitive-attribute check from being immediately followed by approval, rejection, or another final decision.
- **Manual review:** Decisions explicitly associated with sensitive groups must coexist with, and be preceded by, `ManualReview`. Coexistence ensures the review occurs in the same case, while precedence ensures it happens before the decision.

These constraints provide procedural safeguards. They do not by themselves establish statistical fairness, which should additionally be evaluated through outcome, error-rate, and disparate-impact monitoring.
