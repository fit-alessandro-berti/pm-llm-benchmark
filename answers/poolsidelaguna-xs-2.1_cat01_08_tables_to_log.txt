### **Assumptions and Notes**
1. **Resource Mapping**: Resource names (e.g., Alice Brown, GlobalTransport) are derived from the `Resources` table.
2. **Item Details**: `item_description` and `item_category` are joined from the `Products` table.
3. **Payment Events**: No resource is assigned since the `Payments` table does not include a resource/actor field.
4. **Order 1004**: Only includes events up to "Items Picked" as there are no subsequent events (no delivery, invoice, or payment records).
5. **Delivery Events**: Both "Order Dispatched" and "Order Arrived" are included for each delivery record.
6. **Invoice/Payment Linking**: Each invoice/payment event is tied to its respective `invoice_id` or `order_id` for traceability.
