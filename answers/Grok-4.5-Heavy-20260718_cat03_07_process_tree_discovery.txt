`-> ( 'RA', 'VF', 'AE', X( ->( *('RMI', tau), 'VF' ), tau ), 'AD', 'SC', 'AC' )`

This model was agreed by the full team (Grok, Harper, Benjamin, Lucas) as the best balance of fitness, precision and simplicity:
