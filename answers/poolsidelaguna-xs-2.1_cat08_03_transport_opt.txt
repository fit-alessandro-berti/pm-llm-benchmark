### 2. Performance Analysis and Bottleneck Identification

#### **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**

1. **On-Time Delivery Rate (OTDR)**:  
   - **Calculation**:  
     - Compare "Delivery Success" timestamps with customer-requested time windows.  
     - Formula: `(Delivered On-Time / Total Delivered) × 100`.  

2. **Average Time per Delivery Stop (ATDS)**:  
   - **Calculation**:  
     - Duration between "Arrive Customer" and "Depart Customer" events.  
     - Formula: `(Stop Duration) / Total Stops`.  

3. **Travel Time vs. Service Time Ratio**:  
   - **Calculation**:  
     - **Travel Time**: Total vehicle movement time (from GPS speed data).  
     - **Service Time**: Cumulative "Arrive Customer" to "Depart Customer" durations.  
     - Formula: `Travel Time / (Travel Time + Service Time)`.  

4. **Fuel Consumption per km/Package**:  
   - **Calculation**:  
     - Use GPS speed data and vehicle fuel efficiency (if available) or maintenance logs (e.g., fuel top-ups).  
     - Formula: `Total Fuel Used / (Total Distance + Total Packages Delivered)`.  

5. **Vehicle Utilization Rate**:  
   - **Calculation**:  
     - Time vehicle is actively moving or delivering vs. total shift duration.  
     - Formula: `(Active Time / Total Shift Duration) × 100`.  

6. **Traffic Delay Frequency/Duration**:  
   - **Calculation**:  
     - GPS shows speed <10 km/h for 15 minutes.  
     - Formula: `Number of Traffic Delays / Total Driving Hours`.  

7. **Failed Delivery Rate (FDR)**:  
   - **Calculation**:  
     - Packages marked "Delivery Failed" vs. total attempted deliveries.  
     - Formula: `(Failed Deliveries / Total Delivery Attempts) × 100`.
