**Key design choices:**
- **Direct flow:** The nine `directly_follows` pairs encode the exact sequence from receiving the order to recording payment.
- **Precedence:** `always_after` and `always_before` contain the full transitive closure of that sequence.
- **Occurrence bounds:** Every activity is mandatory and happens exactly once (`{1}`).
- **No conflicts:** `never_together` is empty since all activities belong to the same successful case.
- **Equivalence:** Left empty because no special “same-occurrence” pairing beyond the universal single-occurrence pattern is required.
