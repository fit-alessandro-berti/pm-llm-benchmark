**Group B (Unprotected Group) exhibits the bias.**

### Key Differences Between the Logs
- **Group A (Protected)**: All cases have `LocalResident = FALSE` and `CommunityGroup = None`. `ScoreAdjustment` is uniformly 0 (or N/A). Final decisions follow the raw `PreliminaryScore` directly (720 → Approved, 710 → Rejected, 740 → Approved). No external attribute influences the score.
- **Group B (Unprotected)**: All cases have `LocalResident = TRUE`. When `CommunityGroup = "Highland Civic Darts Club"`, a `+10 (Community Boost)` is applied during PreliminaryScoring (and carried forward). This raises the effective score used for the FinalDecision. The non-member case (U002) receives no boost.

### How the Bias Manifests
The bias is visible in the **ScoreAdjustment** column and its downstream effect on decisions:

| Case | Group | PreliminaryScore | CommunityGroup              | ScoreAdjustment          | Effective Score | Decision  |
|------|-------|------------------|-----------------------------|--------------------------|-----------------|-----------|
| P001 | A     | 720              | None                        | 0                        | 720             | Approved  |
| U001 | B     | 720              | Highland Civic Darts Club   | +10 (Community Boost)    | 730             | Approved  |
| P002 | A     | 710              | None                        | 0                        | 710             | Rejected  |
| U002 | B     | 710              | None                        | 0                        | 710             | Rejected  |
| P003 | A     | 740              | None                        | 0                        | 740             | Approved  |
| U003 | B     | 695              | Highland Civic Darts Club   | +10 (Community Boost)    | 705             | Approved  |

- Membership in the “Highland Civic Darts Club” triggers a systematic +10 boost that is absent for non-members and for every case in Group A.
- This boost changes outcomes for borderline or lower-scoring applications (U003: 695 becomes 705 and is approved). Without the boost, a comparable score would likely be rejected, as seen with the unboosted 710 cases.
- `LocalResident = TRUE` is perfectly correlated with the presence of the CommunityGroup attribute in these logs, so the preferential treatment is available only to the local/unprotected cohort.

### Systematic Effect on Final Decisions
The process treats two otherwise similar applications differently solely because of the `CommunityGroup` (and associated `LocalResident`) attributes. Applicants who belong to the named club receive an artificial score inflation that improves their chance of approval; applicants outside that club (and the entire protected group) do not. This creates a measurable disparity in approval rates that is driven by the protected/unprotected group distinction rather than by the raw preliminary score alone.

In short, the Group B event log encodes a preferential scoring rule based on community affiliation that is never applied in Group A, producing systematically more favorable outcomes for the boosted cases.
