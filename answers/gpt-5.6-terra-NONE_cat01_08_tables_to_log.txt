## Assumptions

- Each `order_id` is one process **Case ID**.
- One **Items Picked** event is created per order-line record because each line has its own picking timestamp and picker.
- One **Order Dispatched** and one **Order Arrived** event are created per delivery record; split deliveries therefore produce multiple events.
- Payments are linked to orders through `Payments.invoice_id  Invoice.invoice_id  Invoice.order_id`.
- `Order Created` is attributed to the sales person assigned to the order.
- No delivery, invoice, or payment exists for order `1004`; this is retained as an incomplete case rather than inventing missing events.
- All timestamps are UTC (`Z`).
