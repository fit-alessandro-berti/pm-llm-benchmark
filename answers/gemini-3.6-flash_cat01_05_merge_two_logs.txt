### **Reconciliation & Matching Logic**

#### 1. **Primary Timestamp Selection Rule**
* **Rule**: Log A explicitly records the time events are *received* centrally, whereas Log B records actual event *execution* at the operational level (e.g., warehouse, payment gateway). Therefore, for merged events, **Log B's timestamp** is used as the primary execution timestamp (or Log A's if Log B is missing).
* Both original timestamps are preserved in `Log A Timestamp` and `Log B Timestamp` columns for auditing purposes.

#### 2. **Event Alignment Breakdown**

1. **Order Received** (`09:59:58Z` / `10:00:00Z`)
   * **Matching**: Merged. Delta is **2 seconds**.
   * **Reasoning**: `Order Received` (Log A) aligns semantically with `OrderReceived` (Log B). System A logged receipt 2 seconds after System B generated the event.

2. **Order Validated** (`10:01:29Z` / `10:01:30Z`)
   * **Matching**: Merged. Delta is **1 second**.
   * **Reasoning**: `Order Validated` (Log A) matches `OrderValidation` (Log B).

3. **Payment Processed** (`10:02:00Z` / `10:02:05Z`)
   * **Matching**: Merged. Delta is **5 seconds**.
   * **Reasoning**: `Payment Processed` (Log A) and `PaymentCheck` (Log B) represent the same business milestone. Although the time difference exceeds a strict 2-second threshold by 3 seconds, System B's note explicitly mentions `"Payment gateway delay"`, which justifies the minor timing discrepancy.

4. **Quality Check** (`10:03:00Z`)
   * **Matching**: Standalone (Log B only).
   * **Reasoning**: Log A has no matching event within this timeframe. System A only tracks high-level lifecycle events and omitted internal warehouse steps like internal quality inspections.

5. **Item Shipped** (`10:05:00Z` / `10:05:02Z`)
   * **Matching**: Merged. Delta is **2 seconds**.
   * **Reasoning**: `Item Shipped` (Log A) aligns directly with `Shipping` (Log B). 

6. **Item Delivered** (`10:20:00Z`)
   * **Matching**: Standalone (Log A only).
   * **Reasoning**: Log B ends at the shipping stage when the package left the warehouse. Delivery tracking occurred externally and was only reported back to System A.

### **Attributes Schema Strategy**
* **Unified Event Name**: Standardized into standard human-readable Title Case (e.g., `OrderReceived` $\rightarrow$ `Order Received`).
* **Source Systems**: Explicitly flags if an event was confirmed by both systems or sourced from a single log.
* **Metadata Enrichment**: Attributes unique to System B (`user_id`, `resource_id`, `notes`) were merged into the final schema to provide visibility into who performed the action and on what resource.
