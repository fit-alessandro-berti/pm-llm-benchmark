# BPMN-like Process Model: Invoice Approval Process

## Pools & Lanes (Participants)

**Pool: Invoice Approval Process**
- Lane 1: Supplier (External)
- Lane 2: Accounts Payable Clerk (Mary)
- Lane 3: Purchasing Officer (Dan)
- Lane 4: Department Manager (Carlos)
- Lane 5: Accounts Payable Supervisor (Rita)
- Lane 6: Supplier Relationship Manager (Karen)
- Lane 7 (System Lane): Accounting System (automated notifications/logging)

---

## Artifacts / Data Objects
- **Invoice** (received via email)
- **Purchase Order (PO)**
- **PO Confirmation** (goods/services receipt confirmation)
- **Match Result** (line-item match status)
- **Approval Notification** (system-generated)
- **Corrected Invoice** (from supplier)
- **Retrospective PO** (created for informal/contracted services)
- **Payment Schedule Entry**

---

## Process Flow (Textual BPMN Description)

### Start Event
- **Start:** Invoice received by Accounts Payable (via email)  *Invoice* artifact created

---

### Task 1 (Lane: AP Clerk – Mary)
**Activity:** Check invoice for valid PO number

**Gateway 1 (Exclusive/XOR): "PO Number Present?"**
- **[No]**  **Task 1a:** Send invoice back to supplier requesting PO/details
   Flow returns to Supplier lane  *Sub-process: Supplier resubmits invoice with PO*  loops back to Gateway 1
- **[Yes]**  Proceed to Task 2

---

### Task 2 (Lane: AP Clerk – Mary)
**Activity:** Log invoice into accounting system
- Output: *Invoice record created in system*

 **Sequence Flow to Purchasing Officer**

---

### Task 3 (Lane: Purchasing Officer – Dan)
**Activity:** Confirm receipt of goods/services
- Output: *PO Confirmation*

**Gateway 2 (Exclusive/XOR): "PO Number Confirmed/Valid?"**
*(Dan reiterates: invoice cannot proceed without confirmed PO reference — reinforces Gateway 1 logic; noted as redundant check per Dan's account)*
- **[No / Missing]**  Loop back to AP Clerk to obtain PO info (aligns with Task 1a)
- **[Yes]**  Proceed to Task 4

---

### Task 4 (Lane: Purchasing Officer – Dan)
**Activity:** Compare invoice details (quantities, prices, delivery charges) against PO

**Gateway 3 (Exclusive/XOR): "Invoice Matches PO?"**

- **[Match OK]**  Dan sends confirmation email to AP  Proceed to Task 5 (AP Clerk)

- **[Discrepancy Found]**  **Task 4a:** Contact supplier directly OR notify AP Clerk to hold invoice
  - **Sub-decision:** 
    - If supplier issue  **Task 4b:** Supplier sends corrected invoice (Lane: Supplier)  loop back to Task 4
    - If pricing/contract ambiguity  **Task 4c:** Loop in Supplier Relationship Manager (Karen)
      - Karen checks contract terms, informs correct pricing to AP/Purchasing
      -  loop back to Task 4 (re-match)

---

### Task 5 (Lane: AP Clerk – Mary)
**Activity:** Match invoice line items against PO in system (redundant/confirmatory check)

**Gateway 4 (Exclusive/XOR): "Match Confirmed by AP?"**
- **[Yes]**  Proceed to Manager Approval (system auto-triggers)
- **[No – Discrepancy Persists]**  **Task 5a:** Email Purchasing team for clarification (loop to Task 4)
  - **Gateway 5 (Exclusive/XOR): "Discrepancy Resolved Easily?"**
    - **[Yes]**  Return to Task 5 (re-match)
    - **[No]**  **Escalation Path:** Escalate to AP Supervisor (Rita)

---

### Exception Sub-Process (Lane: AP Supervisor – Rita)
**Trigger:** Escalation from Mary (unresolved discrepancy, missing PO, or informal/no-PO invoice)

**Task E1:** Rita investigates — contacts suppliers, coordinates between departments

**Gateway E1 (Exclusive/XOR): "Invoice Matches Any Existing PO?"**
- **[No — Informal Service Agreement]**  **Task E2:** Work with department heads to create **Retrospective PO**
  - **Gateway E2:** "Retrospective PO Approved?"
    - **[Yes]**  Return to normal flow (Task 4: Purchasing confirmation)
    - **[No]**  **End Event (Alternate):** Invoice Rejected
- **[Yes]**  Return to normal flow at appropriate matching step (Task 4/5)

*(Note: Karen may also be looped in here for supplier contract verification, per her account — parallel consultation possible)*

---

### Task 6 (System-Triggered, Lane: Accounting System)
**Activity:** Auto-send approval request email to Department Manager
- **Trigger Condition:** PO confirmed by Purchasing AND invoice match confirmed by AP Clerk

 Sequence Flow to Department Manager

---

### Task 7 (Lane: Department Manager – Carlos)
**Activity:** Review invoice, PO, and confirmation details in system

**Gateway 6 (Exclusive/XOR): "Manager Approves?"**
- **[Yes]**  Click "Approve" in system  Proceed to Task 8
- **[No / Suspicious — e.g., budget variance, unexpected items]** 
  - **(Rare exception — ~once every two months per Carlos)**
  - **Task 7a:** Reject invoice OR send back to Mary to "hold and investigate"
     Loop back to AP Clerk (Task 5) or Escalation Path (Rita) depending on issue severity

---

### Task 8 (Lane: AP Clerk – Mary)
**Activity:** Schedule invoice for payment
- Output: *Payment Schedule Entry*

### End Event
- **End:** Invoice added to next payment cycle / Payment scheduled

---

## Summary of Key Gateways

| Gateway | Type | Condition | Paths |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | XOR | PO number present? | Yes  Log invoice / No  Return to supplier |
| G2 | XOR | PO confirmed valid (Purchasing)? | Yes  Continue / No  Loop to AP |
| G3 | XOR | Invoice matches PO (Purchasing check)? | Match  Confirm to AP / Discrepancy  Contact supplier or Karen |
| G4 | XOR | Match confirmed (AP re-check)? | Yes  Manager approval / No  Clarify with Purchasing |
| G5 | XOR | Discrepancy easily resolved? | Yes  Re-match / No  Escalate to Rita |
| G6 | XOR | Manager approves? | Yes  Schedule payment / No  Reject or hold for investigation |
| GE1 | XOR (Rita's sub-process) | Invoice matches any PO? | Yes  Return to flow / No  Create retrospective PO |
| GE2 | XOR (Rita's sub-process) | Retrospective PO approved? | Yes  Resume flow / No  Reject invoice |

## Noted Ambiguities & Conflicting Accounts

1. **Redundant PO validation:** Both Mary (Task 1) and Dan (Task 3/Gateway 2) independently check PO validity. It's unclear if this is a true redundant check or if Dan's account conflates the initial AP check with his own confirmation step. **Modeled as sequential reinforcement, but may be process redundancy worth consolidating.**

2. **Order of Manager Approval trigger:** Mary states the system automatically emails the manager after receipt confirmation + match; Dan simply "assumes" Mary's team moves it forward; Carlos confirms he's notified after Dan's confirmation. **Consolidated as system-triggered handoff**, but exact automation vs. manual triggering is unclear.

3. **Karen's involvement:** Not clearly sequenced — she states she's "looped in" by either AP or Purchasing during discrepancies, but no interviewee specifies exactly when/how she's engaged relative to Rita's escalation process. **Modeled as a parallel consultative task**, potentially triggered from either Task 4a (Purchasing) or Rita's exception handling (Task E1) — actual trigger point should be validated with additional data.

4. **Post-Manager-Approval handoff:** Carlos "believes" it goes back to AP; Dan also "assumes" this. No definitive confirmation of system automation at this step — **modeled as direct handoff**, but may involve additional undocumented validation.

5. **Escalation threshold:** It's unclear precisely when Mary escalates to Rita vs. handling discrepancies herself via Purchasing — the interviews suggest a judgment call ("if it can't be resolved easily"). **Gateway 5 criteria are subjective/non-formalized.**

- Use **message flows** (dashed arrows) between the Supplier lane and internal lanes, since supplier interactions cross pool boundaries (external participant).
- Annotate **Gateway 5 and Manager rejection path (G6)** with a text annotation: "Threshold criteria not formally defined — based on subjective judgment."
