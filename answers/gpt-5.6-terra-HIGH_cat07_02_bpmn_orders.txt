The visualization is a **BPMN-style cross-functional process diagram** for fulfilling a customer order. It is organized into four horizontal swimlanes:

1. **Sales**
2. **Production**
3. **Procurement**
4. **Warehouse**

### Sales process
- The process begins each **morning** with a timer event.
- Sales checks whether the order is complete.
- It then checks whether the customer is already in the database.
  - If the customer is not present, Sales adds the customer to the database.
  - The paths merge after the customer check.
- Sales sends the order details onward.
- After receiving confirmation that the product has been sent, Sales drafts an invoice and ends the process.

### Production process
- Production receives the **order details** from Sales.
- A parallel gateway starts planning and coordinates production-related work.
- Production plans the work and then manufactures the product.
- The manufacturing flow depends on receiving a message that the materials are acceptable/not faulty.
- When manufacturing is complete, Production sends a **product complete** message to the Warehouse.

### Procurement process
- Procurement also receives the order details.
- It contains a **Material Compensation Subprocess**:
  - Materials are ordered.
  - If necessary, a compensation path allows materials to be returned.
- Procurement checks the delivered materials.
  - If materials are suitable, it sends a **material not faulty** message to Production.
  - If there is an issue, an **order cancellation** message/event ends the procurement path.

### Warehouse process
- The Warehouse receives the **product complete** notification from Production.
- It sends the finished product.
- The resulting **product sent** message is passed back to Sales, allowing Sales to draft the invoice.

### Notation used
- **Solid arrows** represent the sequence of activities within a department.
- **Dashed arrows** represent message flows or communications between departments.
- **Diamonds** are gateways/decision or synchronization points.
- **Envelope symbols** represent message events.
- The cylinder near the customer check represents the customer **database**.
- The embedded compensation subprocess in Procurement handles returning materials if the procurement operation must be compensated or reversed.
