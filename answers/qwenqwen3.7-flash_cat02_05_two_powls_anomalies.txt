### Normative Process Baseline
To evaluate the models, we establish the standard "Hire-to-Retire" workflow:
1.  **Pre-Hiring:** Post Job $\rightarrow$ Screen Candidates $\rightarrow$ Conduct Interviews $\rightarrow$ Make Decision.
2.  **Post-Hiring:** Onboard Employee $\rightarrow$ Add to Payroll $\rightarrow$ Close Case.
3.  **Constraints:** Interviews must precede decisions; payroll must follow onboarding; and all paths must terminate at "Close."

---

### Analysis of POWL Model 1

**1. Pre-Hiring Ordering Violation**
In Model 1, `Screen` is the parent of both `Decide` and `Interview`. Because there is no precedence edge between `Interview` and `Decide`, the model allows the hiring decision to be made **before** or **during** the interview phase. This violates the core logic of vetting candidates before selection.

**2. Structural Deadlock (Interview Branch)**
The `Interview` activity is a "sink node" with no outgoing edges. If the process follows the path `Post  Screen  Interview`, the workflow halts immediately. The case is never closed, resulting in an incomplete trace.

**3. Positive Constraint Enforcement**
Despite its flaws, Model 1 correctly enforces the mandatory sequence `Onboard  Payroll  Close`. The use of a strict partial order here ensures that payroll is never skipped and is always completed before the case is finalized.

---

### Analysis of POWL Model 2

**1. Structural Deadlock (Screening Branch)**
Similar to Model 1, `Screen` is a sink node. Any process instance reaching `Screen` will deadlock and fail to reach the `Close` state.

**2. Compliance Failure (Payroll Skip)**
The `xor_payroll` operator (`XOR(Payroll, skip)`) introduces a critical anomaly. It allows the process to reach `Close` by taking the `skip` path, meaning an employee can be onboarded and the case closed without ever being added to the payroll system. This represents a severe legal and financial compliance violation.

**3. Control Flow Bypass**
The model includes a direct edge from `Post` to `Interview`. This allows the process to bypass the `Screen` activity entirely, creating a scenario where unvetted candidates are interviewed.

---

### Comparative Verdict

**Model 1 more closely aligns with a normative version of the process.**

While both models contain significant anomalies, Model 1 is superior for three reasons:
1.  **Financial Integrity:** Model 1 guarantees that payroll is processed. Model 2’s use of an XOR operator to allow skipping payroll is a fundamental breach of business rules.
2.  **Process Completion:** Model 1 provides a clear, complete path (`Post  Screen  Decide  Onboard  Payroll  Close`). Model 2 contains a hard deadlock in its primary screening branch.
3.  **Severity of Deviation:** While Model 1 allows for "blind hiring," it still ensures the administrative and financial lifecycle of the hire is completed. Model 2 fails to ensure the employee is paid and fails to guarantee the process reaches its intended end state.
