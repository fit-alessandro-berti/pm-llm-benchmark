### Rationale for Bias-Mitigating Constraints

1. **Existence (`BiasMitigationCheck`)**:
   * **Purpose:** Requires that the `BiasMitigationCheck` activity occurs at least once in every execution trace of the loan process, guaranteeing algorithmic or procedural fairness checks are universally applied.

2. **Coexistence (`CheckApplicantRace` $\leftrightarrow$ `ManualReview`)**:
   * **Purpose:** Ensures that if sensitive demographic information (e.g., `CheckApplicantRace`) is checked during the review, a `ManualReview` step must also take place in the same trace. This prevents fully automated, unmonitored rejections based on demographic attributes.

3. **Response (`CheckApplicantRace` $\rightarrow$ `BiasMitigationCheck`)**:
   * **Purpose:** Dictates that accessing sensitive demographic information must always trigger a subsequent `BiasMitigationCheck` before the case can proceed.

4. **Precedence (`BiasMitigationCheck` $\rightarrow$ `FinalDecision`)**:
   * **Purpose:** Ensures that a `FinalDecision` (Approve/Reject) cannot occur unless a `BiasMitigationCheck` has already been completed earlier in the process trace.

5. **Nonchainsuccession (`CheckApplicantRace` $\nrightarrow$ `FinalDecision`)**:
   * **Purpose:** Prevents a `FinalDecision` from immediately following `CheckApplicantRace`. This eliminates direct "fast-path" decisions (such as immediate automated rejections) based on sensitive attributes without intermediate review steps.
