**Rationale for added constraints (bias mitigation):**

- **existence** of `ManualReview` and `BiasMitigationCheck`: Guarantees these fairness safeguards always occur in every valid trace, preventing unchecked decisions.
- **responded_existence / coexistence** linking minority-specific decisions (`Reject_Minority`, `Approve_Minority`) to `ManualReview`, and sensitive-attribute checks to `BiasMitigationCheck`: Ensures any outcome involving protected groups is always accompanied by an independent review, reducing disparate treatment.
- **response** from sensitive-attribute checks (`CheckApplicantRace/Gender/Age`) to `BiasMitigationCheck`: Forces a mitigation step after any sensitive attribute is observed, before proceeding further.
- **precedence** of `BiasMitigationCheck` (and `ManualReview`) before final/reject decisions: Blocks decisions from occurring unless fairness checks have already taken place.
- **succession** of `BiasMitigationCheck` to `FinalDecision`: Strengthens the ordering so mitigation is a required predecessor of the outcome.
- **nonsuccession / nonchainsuccession** forbidding sensitive-attribute checks from being followed (directly or at all) by `Reject`/`Approve`: Prevents immediate biased decisions that could be driven solely by the sensitive attribute without intervening fairness controls.
