### Step 1: Review the Event Log

First, let's review the key sensor readings for each event:

- **Temperature (°C)**
- **Pressure (bar)**
- **Vibration Level (Hz)**
- **Material Flow (units/s)**
- **Tool Position (mm)**
- **Energy Consumption (kWh)**

The gradual increase in temperature and vibration could indicate heating elements or preparatory actions, while low material flow and tool position suggest no actual cutting or assembly.

2. **Active Processing Phase (Events 7-13):**

The significant increase in temperature, pressure, vibration, material flow, tool position, and energy consumption are typical indicators of these operations. The pattern suggests that the machine is actively cutting or welding metal, based on the high energy and material flow, along with increased tool position.

3. **Cool Down/Shutdown Phase (Events 14-15):**
   - **Temperature** drops back to 20°C, returning to the initial low value.

### Step 3: Assign Intuitive Labels

Based on the analysis above, we can assign intuitive labels to each phase:

1. **Phase 1: Setup/Calibration**
   - **Label:** "Setup/Calibration"
   - **Explanation:** This phase involves machine preparation, calibration, and initial adjustments. The gradual increase in temperature and vibration, along with no material flow or tool movement, suggests a preparatory stage before actual operations.

2. **Phase 2: Cutting/Welding**
   - **Label:** "Cutting/Welding"
   - **Explanation:** The significant increase in temperature, pressure, vibration, material flow, tool position, and energy consumption during this phase strongly suggests a cutting or welding operation. The machine is actively processing materials, as indicated by the rising and peak values of these sensors.

3. **Phase 3: Shutdown/Idle**
   - **Label:** "Shutdown/Idle"
   - **Explanation:** After the active processing phase, the machine returns to a state of inactivity. Temperatures and pressures normalize, vibration returns to zero, and there is no material flow or tool movement. This phase indicates the machine is idle or undergoing shutdown procedures.
