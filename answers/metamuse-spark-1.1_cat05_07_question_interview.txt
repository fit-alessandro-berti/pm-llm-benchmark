When modeling a process this complex, I need to clarify the exact flow, decisions, roles, and exceptions. Here are the targeted questions I would ask:

**1. Intake & Documentation Verification**
* What specific documents are *mandatory* for every property vs. optional depending on property type or city?
* When documentation is missing or outdated, what is the process for follow-up? Is there a deadline or a limit to how many times you request updates before the onboarding is paused or rejected?
* Who has the final authority to say documentation is "verified" - is it always the property coordinator, or does it require approval from legal/compliance?

**2. Roles, Responsibilities & Handoffs**
* Can you walk me through the distinct responsibilities of the Property Coordinator vs. the Property Manager vs. the Leasing Agent? At what exact point does ownership transfer from Coordinator to Manager?
* How do the regional offices interact with the central database team, marketing team, IT, compliance, and finance? Are those support teams centralized?
* Who is responsible for hiring external stakeholders like the photographer, inspector, and contractors?

**3. Property Manager Assignment & Landlord Alignment**
* How do you weigh the assignment criteria for a property manager - workload, geography, property type experience, and track record with unique landlord requirements? Which factor is most important?
* What happens if no suitable property manager is available, or if all managers in that geography are at full capacity?
* What happens if expectations are *not* aligned in that introductory call with the landlord? Is there a negotiation process, or can either party decide not to proceed?

**4. Inspection, Repair & Marketing Preparation - Sequencing and Timing**
* What is the typical timeline for the onboarding process from initial contact to activation? Which steps are the biggest bottlenecks?
* Are the photographer and inspector scheduled in parallel or does one depend on the other? Could marketing work start before the inspection has passed?
* If the inspector finds issues that require repairs, what is the process? Who pays for and approves contractors? What if the landlord refuses to make the required repairs? Does that terminate the onboarding?

**5. Activation, Tenant Screening & Leasing Decisions**
* What are the specific criteria that must be met for a property to be officially "activated" in the portal? Who gives final sign-off to activate it?
* Walk me through your decision process when you have multiple qualified tenant applicants at the same time. How do you decide between lease length, promotional discounts, move-in readiness, etc.?
* Who makes the final decision on choosing a tenant - the property manager, the landlord, or both? Does the landlord have veto power?

**6. Quality Assurance, Compliance & Exception Handling**
* Are compliance, financial, and IT checks done for *every* property sequentially at the end, or are they done in parallel and only for a sample of properties?
* What are the most common exceptions or special cases you encounter - e.g., properties requiring special insurance, furnished/luxury/student housing, or landlords insisting on custom lease clauses? How does the process change for those?
* What happens if a compliance check fails after the lease has been drafted, or if a tenant is found to be non-compliant with fair housing after selection? Is there a formal rework loop?
* When is the onboarding process officially considered complete and successful?

**7. Systems, Communication & Visibility**
* How is the status of a property tracked as it moves through these stages? If I wanted to know where a property is in the onboarding journey, where would I look?
* Where do most communication breakdowns or delays happen between regional offices, landlords, managers, and support teams?
* Are there any steps in this process that are frequently skipped, repeated, or done out of order in practice?
