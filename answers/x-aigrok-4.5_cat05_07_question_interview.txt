**Targeted questions for clarifying the property onboarding process:**

### Initiation and Documentation
1. What triggers the start of the formal onboarding process after a landlord first contacts a regional office, and how is that handoff (or ownership) managed if the inquiry comes through multiple channels?
2. Beyond deeds, certificates of compliance, and inspection reports, are there any other documents or data points that are required or strongly preferred before a property can proceed, and how do you handle partial or jurisdiction-specific documentation differences across cities?
3. What exactly constitutes “verified” documentation, what are the most common reasons a coordinator requests updates, and what is the decision process (and timeline expectations) if a landlord is slow to provide them or refuses certain items?

### Property Recording and Manager Assignment
4. Once documentation is complete, who has final authority to accept a property into the portfolio versus decline it, and what criteria or red flags typically lead to rejection at this stage?
5. Can you walk through how the property manager assignment decision is typically made in practice—how the factors (workload, geography, property-type experience, track record with unique requirements) are weighed against one another, and who resolves conflicts when multiple managers appear equally suitable or when no ideal match exists?
6. After assignment, what specific outcomes or agreements are expected from the introductory call/meeting, how are they documented, and what happens if the landlord and manager cannot align on key policies (pets, maintenance responsibilities, payment channels, etc.)?

### Inspection, Photography, and Remediation
7. How are the professional photographer and certified inspector scheduled and coordinated—sequentially, in parallel, or opportunistically—and what drives prioritization when there is a backlog?
8. If the inspector identifies issues, what are the typical thresholds for “must-fix before activation” versus “can be deferred,” how is responsibility and cost-sharing negotiated with the landlord, and what escalation path exists if agreement cannot be reached or repairs run long?
9. Who decides whether contractors are hired through the company versus by the landlord, and how is quality or completion of those repairs re-verified before the process continues?

### Marketing, Pricing, and Activation
10. How does the digital marketing team determine listing content, photography selection, and promotional approach, and who has approval authority over the final materials and the initial rental price (or range) before the property goes live?
11. What specifically constitutes “activation” in the rental portal and internal systems—what checklist or gate criteria must be met, who performs/owns the activation, and can a property be partially activated (e.g., visible but not accepting applications)?

### Tenant Screening, Selection, and Lease Finalization
12. Can you describe the end-to-end tenant screening process in more detail: which checks are mandatory versus discretionary, what thresholds or scoring typically lead to approval/rejection, and who makes the final offer decision when results are borderline?
13. When multiple qualified applicants compete for a unit, what process and criteria guide prioritization, negotiation of terms, or the decision to offer promotional discounts or other concessions, and how much latitude does the property manager or leasing agent have?
14. How are custom lease clauses requested by landlords evaluated and approved or rejected (especially those that might conflict with standard company terms or fair-housing rules), and who must sign off?

### Quality Assurance, Audits, Exceptions, and Cross-Functional Flow
15. What specifically do the compliance, financial, and IT quality checks examine, what are the most common issues discovered, and what happens (including rework loops and ownership) if a discrepancy is found after marketing or even after a tenant has been selected?
16. How are exceptions such as properties needing special insurance, unique landlord requirements, furnished/luxury/student housing nuances, or multi-unit variations handled differently from the baseline process—who decides on exceptions, what additional approvals are required, and how is the altered path tracked?
17. Can you describe the typical communication cadence and points of friction that occur among regional offices, property coordinators, assigned managers, landlords, marketing, leasing agents, compliance, finance, and IT—where do handoffs most often break down or create rework?
18. Roughly how long does a straightforward onboarding take from initial landlord contact to a signed lease and move-in, where are the longest waits or most frequent bottlenecks, and how do you currently measure process health or success (for the company, landlord, and eventual tenant)?
19. Are there any other decision points, parallel workstreams, or informal practices that significantly affect throughput or quality that were not covered in the description?

These questions focus on decision criteria, roles/ownership, sequencing and timing, missing steps or variations, exception handling, and cross-team coordination without presupposing tools, data models, or technical implementations.
