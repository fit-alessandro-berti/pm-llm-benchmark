**Rationale for the added constraints (how they reduce bias):**

- **Coexistence (`Approve_Minority`/`Reject_Minority` ↔ `ManualReview`)**: Any decision specifically involving minority applicants must coexist with a `ManualReview` activity. This prevents automated or unchecked decisions on sensitive demographic cases and forces human oversight for fairness and consistency.

- **Response (`CheckApplicantRace`/`Gender`/`Age` → `BiasMitigationCheck`)**: Whenever a sensitive attribute is inspected, a dedicated `BiasMitigationCheck` must eventually occur in the trace. This inserts an explicit fairness intervention after disclosure of potentially biasing information and before outcomes are finalized.

- **Nonsuccession (`CheckApplicantRace`/`Gender`/`Age` ↛ `Reject_Minority`)**: Directly blocks any path in which checking a sensitive attribute is (eventually) followed by a minority rejection. This eliminates the most direct discriminatory sequences that could arise from attribute-driven bias.

- **Precedence (`BiasMitigationCheck` ≺ `FinalDecision`)**: `BiasMitigationCheck` must occur before `FinalDecision`. Combined with the original existence constraint on `FinalDecision`, this effectively requires the bias-mitigation step in every process instance, providing a strong, process-wide safeguard against discriminatory outcomes.

These additions preserve the original process skeleton while systematically inserting fairness checkpoints, reviews, and prohibitions on biased activity sequences.
