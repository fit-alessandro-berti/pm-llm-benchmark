**Rationale for Added Constraints**

- **Response: `CheckSensitiveAttribute`  `BiasMitigationCheck`**  
  When the system inspects a sensitive attribute (e.g., race, age, gender), a bias mitigation check must follow. This guarantees that an algorithmic fairness step is never skipped after accessing sensitive information.

- **Nonsuccession: `CheckSensitiveAttribute`  `FinalDecision`**  
  An immediate transition from checking a sensitive attribute to the final decision is forbidden. This prevents snap, potentially biased decisions right after a sensitive attribute is revealed.

- **Succession: `BiasMitigationCheck`  `FinalDecision`**  
  If a bias mitigation check occurs, the final decision can only be made after it finishes (when both appear in a trace, the bias check must precede the decision). Together with the response constraint, this enforces a mandatory “fairness gate” before any decision.

- **Coexistence: `SensitiveApplicant`  `ManualReview`**  
  Whenever the trace records that the applicant belongs to a sensitive group (`SensitiveApplicant`), a `ManualReview` must also occur, and viceversa. This ensures that all sensitive applications receive human oversight, removing full automation from potentially discriminatory paths.

- **Nonchainsuccession: `CheckSensitiveAttribute`  `Reject` / `Approve`**  
  A direct succession from a sensitive attribute check to either an approval or a rejection is explicitly blocked. This eliminates a common pattern where race or gender directly and instantaneously triggers the loan outcome.

Collectively, these constraints insert mandatory fairness checkpoints and human review, severing the direct link between sensitive attributes and the final decision, and thereby reducing the risk of discrimination in the loan application process.
