**`Subprocess: Predictive Classification Engine`**

**`Gateway (Complex / XOR with Data Conditions): "Predictive Routing & Confidence Check"`**
*   **[Score > 85% Standard]**  Route directly to automated standard path (skip manual B1 review).
*   **[Score > 85% Custom]**  Pre-allocate resources and route to the enhanced custom feasibility track.
*   **[Ambiguous / Hybrid (40–60%) OR Low Confidence]**  Trigger **`Subprocess: Ambiguity Resolution & Parallel Light-Check`**. Here, *lightweight* versions of standard validation (B1’) and custom pre-feasibility (B2’) run in parallel via an **Inclusive Gateway (OR)**.
