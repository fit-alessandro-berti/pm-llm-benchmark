### Rationale

- **Mandatory bias check:** `existence(BiasMitigationCheck)` ensures every application receives at least one fairness check.
- **Check after sensitive information:** The `chainresponse` rules require a `BiasMitigationCheck` immediately after race, gender, or age is inspected.
- **No decision before mitigation:** The `precedence` rules require the mitigation check before approval, rejection, or the final decision.
- **No immediate sensitive-attribute decision:** `nonchainsuccession` explicitly prevents a decision from immediately following a sensitive-attribute check. `nonsuccession` is not used because it would prohibit any later decision, rather than only a direct transition.
- **Manual review for demographic-specific decisions:** `Approve_Minority` and `Reject_Minority` require a `ManualReview`, and the review must occur before the decision.

`responded_existence` is intentionally used instead of declaring both demographic-specific outcomes as coexistent with the same `ManualReview`. DECLARE coexistence is symmetric, so two such constraints could inadvertently force both approval and rejection whenever a manual review occurs. These control-flow constraints reduce opportunities for immediate or unchecked biased decisions, although outcome-level fairness should also be verified through statistical and compliance audits.
