2. How can R&D incorporate supplier capabilities, component availability, regulatory constraints, and sustainability objectives earlier in the design process?

3. What criteria are used to select suppliers in Taiwan, Japan, South Korea, Germany, and the United States, and how are cost, quality, innovation, lead time, and geopolitical risk balanced?

4. How should the process respond when one component supplier is delayed, given that assembly cannot begin until all required components are secured?

5. What sourcing alternatives, safety-stock policies, or design substitutions could reduce dependence on single countries or specialized suppliers?

6. How are demand forecasts translated into component orders, production capacity, and transportation plans, especially when smartphone demand is highly volatile?

7. What communication and data-sharing mechanisms should connect suppliers, the Malaysian assembly plant, logistics providers, customs agents, distribution centers, retailers, and e-commerce channels?

8. How could shipment consolidation, supplier proximity, or postponement strategies improve the flow of components to the assembly plant?

9. What operational, workforce, maintenance, cybersecurity, and safety risks arise from running assembly operations continuously, and how should they be mitigated?

10. Which quality standards and diagnostic metrics determine whether a smartphone passes inspection, requires rework, or must be scrapped?

11. How should information from quality failures be fed back to assembly teams, R&D, and component suppliers to prevent recurring defects?

12. What factors should determine whether finished smartphones are transported by air freight or cargo ship, and does the current parallel split accurately represent that decision?

13. How can the company balance transportation speed, cost, product-launch deadlines, carbon emissions, and disruption risk when selecting distribution routes?

14. How should customs clearance and regulatory compliance be organized when different shipments arrive in multiple markets under different legal and documentation requirements?

15. What contingency plans are needed for events such as port closures, trade restrictions, natural disasters, supplier insolvency, cyberattacks, or sudden geopolitical tensions?

16. How should inventory be allocated among regional distribution centers when supply is constrained and regional demand, profitability, and service commitments differ?

17. Are local warehousing, retailer distribution, e-commerce fulfillment, and VIP shipments truly parallel activities that must all finish before the process continues, or would conditional routing better reflect actual order flows?

18. What performance indicators should be used to measure supplier reliability, manufacturing efficiency, first-pass yield, rework rates, logistics performance, inventory health, order fulfillment, and customer satisfaction?

19. How can real-time sales, inventory, production, shipment, and supplier data be integrated to improve forecasting and accelerate decisions when demand changes?

20. How should lessons from each sales cycle be captured and used to revise product design, sourcing strategy, production plans, distribution policies, and future process models?
