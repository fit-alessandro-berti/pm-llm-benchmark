The visualization is a process-flow / object-centric workflow diagram showing how several business document types move through procurement, sales-order fulfillment, returns, payment, and invoicing activities.

### Inputs / source objects
Five crosshatched source boxes at the top feed into the process, each with an object count:

- **VendorInvoiceItem** — **1.2M objects**
- **ReturnOrderItem** — **0.8M objects**
- **PurchaseRequisitionItem** — **2.0M objects**
- **SalesOrderItem** — **3.5M objects**
- **PurchaseOrderItem** — **5.0M objects**

Each begins at an oval **Start** node. Colored paths then connect the object types to activities.

### Main process areas

#### Requisition and approval
- **PurchaseRequisitionItem** flows through **Create Requisition** (Overall: **3.0M**) and into **Approve Requisition** (Overall: **5.7M**).
- Vendor invoices, returns, and part of the order flow also converge at **Approve Requisition**.
- From approval, the process branches toward:
  - **Send Order** (Overall: **5.2M**)
  - **Check Stock** (Overall: **1.0M**)
  - **Approve Payment** (Overall: **1.5M**)
  - **Process Return** (Overall: **0.8M**)
  - **Create Invoice** (Overall: **2.0M**)

#### Sales-order and fulfillment
- **SalesOrderItem** and **PurchaseOrderItem** both feed into **Create Order** (Overall: **8.5M**).
- The primary fulfillment sequence runs vertically:
  1. **Create Order**
  2. **Pack Goods** (Overall: **2.0M**)
  3. **Ship Goods** (Overall: **1.8M**)
  4. **Confirm Delivery** (Overall: **1.7M**)
  5. **Close Order** (Overall: **1.7M**)
  6. **End**

#### Returns, payment, and invoicing
- Return-related flows move through **Process Return**.
- Payment-related paths reach **Approve Payment**.
- Several paths converge at **Create Invoice**, which has separate colored routes to an **End** node.

### Timing annotations
Edges are labeled with elapsed times, generally in milliseconds, such as:

- **300 ms** from requisition start to Create Requisition
- **400 ms** from sales-order start to Create Order
- **350 ms** from purchase-order start into Create Order
- **300 ms** from Create Order to Pack Goods
- **200 ms** from Pack Goods to Ship Goods
- **220 ms** from Ship Goods to Confirm Delivery
- **180 ms** from Confirm Delivery to Close Order
- Other cross-process paths range from roughly **100 ms to 800 ms**

### Visual encoding
- **Rounded rectangles** represent activities.
- **Ovals** represent start and end events.
- **Crosshatched boxes** represent source object types and their volumes.
- **Colored lines and dots** distinguish different object streams or process paths:
  - purple is associated with requisition-related flow,
  - blue with purchase-order/payment-related flow,
  - orange with sales-order/fulfillment flow,
  - teal/green with invoice-related flow,
  - red with return-related flow.
- The colored dots on an activity indicate which object/process streams participate in that activity.

Overall, the diagram emphasizes that the business process is not a single linear path: requisitions, orders, invoices, returns, payments, and delivery activities intersect at shared activities such as **Approve Requisition**, **Create Order**, and **Create Invoice**.
