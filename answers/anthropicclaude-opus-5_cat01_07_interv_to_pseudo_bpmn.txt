# BPMNLike Process Model: **Supplier Invoice Approval & Payment Scheduling**

*Derived from interviews with Mary (AP Clerk), Dan (Purchasing Officer), Carlos (Dept. Manager), Rita (AP Supervisor), Karen (Supplier Relationship Manager).*

---

## 1. Participants (Pools / Lanes)

| Pool | Lane | Role / Actor | Responsibility in process |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Supplier** (external pool – black box) | – | Supplier | Sends invoice; provides PO reference, corrected invoices, price clarifications |
| **Company – Invoice Processing** | Lane A | **Accounts Payable Clerk** (Mary) | Intake, PO check, logging, matching, routing, payment scheduling |
| | Lane B | **AP Supervisor** (Rita) | Exception handling / escalation owner; ensures no invoice stalls |
| | Lane C | **Purchasing Officer** (Dan) | Confirms receipt of goods/services; verifies invoice vs. PO |
| | Lane D | **Department Manager** (Carlos) | Final approval / rejection / hold |
| | Lane E | **Supplier Relationship Manager** (Karen) | Supplier outreach, contract/price verification (no system access) |
| | Lane F (system lane) | **Accounting / ERP System** | Invoice register, PO matching support, automatic approval notification, payment run |

---

## 2. Data Objects / Artifacts

| ID | Artifact | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Invoice** (usually PDF via email) | Trigger of the process |
| D2 | **Purchase Order (PO)** | Retrieved from ERP; must exist and be valid |
| D3 | **Invoice record in accounting system** | Created at logging step |
| D4 | **Goods/Services Receipt Confirmation** (email from Purchasing) | Required precondition for approval |
| D5 | **Discrepancy / clarification email** | AP  Purchasing  Karen  Supplier |
| D6 | **Corrected / revised invoice** | From supplier; restarts verification |
| D7 | **Supplier contract / price list** | Used by Karen to determine correct price |
| D8 | **Retrospective PO** | Created by Rita with department heads for informally contracted services |
| D9 | **Approval record / manager signoff** | System-recorded |
| D10 | **Payment schedule / payment run entry** | Output of the process |

## 3. Process Flow (normative "happy path" + branches)

### Start
- **SE1 – Start Event (Message):** "Invoice received from supplier" (channel: email; occasionally other channels — not specified).
  - Input: **D1 Invoice**

### Main sequence

**T1 – Receive and review incoming invoice** — *AP Clerk*
- Input: D1  Output: D1 (registered in inbox, not yet in ERP)
- Flow: SE1  T1  **G1**

**G1 – XOR Gateway: "Does the invoice reference a valid PO number?"** — *AP Clerk decision*
- **Branch G1a [No PO / invalid PO]**  **G2**
- **Branch G1b [Valid PO present]**  **T5**

**G2 – XOR Gateway: "Is the missing PO a supplier data problem or an internal (noPO purchase) problem?"**
*(This distinction is implied by combining Mary's, Dan's and Rita's accounts; interviewees did not state an explicit rule  see Ambiguity A1.)*
- **Branch G2a [Supplier omitted / mistyped PO reference]**  **T2**
- **Branch G2b [No PO exists — e.g. informally contracted service]**  **T4 (Escalation Subprocess)**

**T2 – Return invoice to supplier requesting PO / missing details** — *AP Clerk*
- Output: D5  Message flow to **Supplier pool**
- Flow: T2  **IE1**

**T3 – Contact supplier to obtain PO number or revised invoice** — *Supplier Relationship Manager (Karen)*
- Optional parallel/alternative to T2, invoked when AP or Purchasing "loops Karen in"
- Output: D5  Message flow to Supplier
- Flow: T3  **IE1**

**IE1 – Intermediate Event (Message, with attached Timer/Escalation):** "Corrected invoice or PO reference received from supplier"
- Input: **D6 / PO reference**
- Flow: IE1  **T1** (rereview) *(rework loop)*
- **Boundary Timer Event on IE1 [no supplier response within tolerance]**  **T4 (Escalation Subprocess)** — Rita's stated mandate: "problem invoices don't get stuck indefinitely." (Concrete SLA not given  Ambiguity A2.)

**T5 – Log invoice into accounting system** — *AP Clerk* (system: ERP)
- Input: D1, D2  Output: **D3**
- Flow: T5  **T6**

**T6 – Forward invoice to Purchasing for receipt confirmation and PO verification** — *AP Clerk*
- Output: D1 (+ link to D2/D3)  to Lane C
- Flow: T6  **T7**

**T7 – Verify goods/services receipt and compare invoice against PO** — *Purchasing Officer*
- Checks: quantities, unit prices, delivery/freight charges, special notes
- Input: D1, D2  Output: verification result
- Flow: T7  **G3**

**G3 – XOR Gateway: "Do invoice details match the PO and confirmed receipt?"**
- **Branch G3a [Match]**  **T8**
- **Branch G3b [Discrepancy: overbilling, price difference, missing/extra items]**  **G4 (Discrepancy resolution)**

**T8 – Send receipt & match confirmation to Accounts Payable** — *Purchasing Officer*
- Output: **D4 Confirmation email**
- Flow: T8  **T9**

**T9 – Match invoice line items against PO in the system** — *AP Clerk*
- Input: D1, D2, D3, D4  Output: matched invoice record
-  **Overlap / possible duplicate control:** Mary describes this as her own step *after* Purchasing's confirmation; Dan describes the same comparison as *his* step. Model it as a distinct AP verification task, but flag it (**Ambiguity A3**) — in some organisations/cases this is skipped and the flow goes T8  G6 directly.
- Flow: T9  **G5**

**G5 – XOR Gateway: "Does the AP match succeed?"**
- **Branch G5a [Match OK]**  **T10**
- **Branch G5b [Discrepancy found in AP]**  **G4 (Discrepancy resolution)**
- **Branch G5c [Cannot match at all / no matching PO]**  **T4 (Escalation Subprocess)**

**T10 – System sends automatic approval request to Department Manager** — *Accounting/ERP System (Send Task / automated)*
- Trigger condition stated by Mary: receipt confirmed **AND** invoice matched to PO
- Output: notification + view of D1, D2, D4
- Flow: T10  **T11**

**T11 – Review invoice, PO and confirmation** — *Department Manager*
- Checks: quantities, pricing, budget/total amount plausibility, unexpected items, special notes
- Flow: T11  **G6**

**G6 – XOR Gateway: "Manager decision"**
- **Branch G6a [Approve]**  **T12** (Output: **D9 Approval record**)
- **Branch G6b [Hold & investigate]**  **T13**
- **Branch G6c [Reject]**  **T14**
*(Carlos: G6b/c occur roughly once every two months — lowfrequency paths.)*

**T12 – Schedule invoice for payment / add to next payment run** — *AP Clerk* (system: ERP)
- Input: D3, D9  Output: **D10 Payment schedule entry**
- Flow: T12  **EE1**

**EE1 – End Event:** "Invoice approved and scheduled for payment"
*(Actual payment execution / payment run is downstream and out of scope of the interviews — Ambiguity A4.)*

**T13 – Hold invoice and investigate manager's query** — *AP Clerk (supported by AP Supervisor)*
- Flow: T13  **G7**

**G7 – XOR Gateway: "Investigation outcome"**
- **[Clarified – invoice valid]**  **T10** (resubmit for approval)
- **[Correction needed from supplier]**  **G4 (Discrepancy resolution)**
- **[Unresolvable]**  **T4 (Escalation Subprocess)**

**T14 – Reject invoice and notify supplier** — *AP Clerk / AP Supervisor*
- Output: rejection notice  message flow to Supplier pool
- Flow: T14  **EE2**

**EE2 – End Event:** "Invoice rejected"

## 4. Discrepancy Resolution (Subprocess, collapsed as **G4/SP1**)

**Entry from:** G3b (Purchasing finds mismatch), G5b (AP finds mismatch), G7 (manager query needs supplier correction).

**G4 – XOR Gateway: "Resolution route"** *(routing rule not formalised by interviewees  Ambiguity A5)*
- **G4a  T15 – Contact supplier directly about the discrepancy** — *Purchasing Officer*
- **G4b  T16 – Ask AP to hold the invoice pending a corrected version** — *Purchasing Officer  AP Clerk*
- **G4c  T17 – Email Purchasing to clarify discrepancy** — *AP Clerk* (APinitiated variant)
- **G4d  T18 – Verify contracted price/terms against supplier contract and inform AP/Purchasing** — *Supplier Relationship Manager* (Input: **D7**)
- **G4e  T19 – Contact supplier to request revised invoice / correct price** — *Supplier Relationship Manager*

*T15–T19 may occur in sequence or in combination; treat as an adhoc / loosely ordered set inside SP1.*

**G8 – XOR Gateway: "Is the discrepancy resolved?"**
- **[Resolved, corrected invoice received]**  **T1** or **T7** (reverification) — *interviews suggest reverification by Purchasing; if a brandnew invoice document arrives, the flow restarts at T1* (**Ambiguity A6**)
- **[Resolved without new document — e.g. price confirmed correct]**  **T8 / T9** (continue normal flow)
- **[Not resolvable easily]**  **T4 (Escalation Subprocess)**

---

## 5. Exception Escalation (Subprocess **T4 / SP2**) — *AP Supervisor lane*

**Entry from:** G2b (no PO at all), G5c (cannot match), G8 (unresolved discrepancy), IE1 timer (supplier unresponsive), G7 (unresolvable manager query).

**T20 – Escalate exception to AP Supervisor** — *AP Clerk*
**T21 – Analyse exception and coordinate between departments** — *AP Supervisor*
**T22 – Contact supplier (directly or via Supplier Relationship Manager)** — *AP Supervisor / Karen*
**G9 – XOR Gateway: "Is there a legitimate underlying purchase without a PO?"**
- **[Yes – informal/retrospective purchase]**  **T23 – Coordinate with department head to create retrospective PO** — *AP Supervisor + Department Head* (Output: **D8**)  reenter main flow at **T5/T6** (log & send to Purchasing for confirmation)
- **[No / invoice not legitimate]**  **T14 – Reject invoice and notify supplier**  **EE2**
- **[Discrepancy clarified, PO exists]**  reenter main flow at **T7** (Purchasing confirmation)  T8  T9  T10  T11 …

*Rita explicitly states the reentry pattern: "the invoice goes back into the normal flow: confirmation from Purchasing, approval by the Manager, then scheduling for payment."*

**Noninterrupting Timer / Monitoring:** AP Supervisor monitors aged/stuck exceptions ("I ensure problem invoices don't get stuck indefinitely") — model as a **noninterrupting timer boundary event on SP2** looping to T21.

## 7. Business Rules Extracted

- **R1:** No invoice may proceed past intake without a confirmed, valid PO reference ("We never approve anything without a confirmed PO reference" – Dan). Exception: a **retrospective PO** may be created by the AP Supervisor with the department head (Rita).
- **R2:** Manager approval is triggered **only** when (a) receipt of goods/services is confirmed **AND** (b) invoice matches the PO. The trigger is an automatic system email.
- **R3:** Payment scheduling occurs **only after** manager approval, and is executed by Accounts Payable.
- **R4:** Karen (SRM) has **no system authority** — advisory/communication role only.
- **R5:** Rita (AP Supervisor) performs **no final approvals** — exception coordination only.

## 8. Ambiguities / Conflicting Accounts (to validate with stakeholders)

| ID | Issue | Nature of conflict |
|---|---|---|
| **A1** | Routing when PO is missing | Mary: return to supplier. Dan: tell Mary to obtain PO. Rita: escalate for retrospective PO. No stated rule for choosing. Modelled as G2 with an inferred criterion. |
| **A2** | Timer for unresponsive suppliers | Rita implies aging control exists, but no SLA/threshold was given. |
| **A3** | Duplicate invoice–PO matching | Mary and Dan each claim to perform the invoicevsPO comparison. Modelled as two tasks (T7, T9); may in reality be one task, or T9 may be a light system check. |
| **A4** | End of process scope | All interviewees stop at "scheduled for payment"/"next payment cycle"; actual payment execution, remittance and supplier notification are out of scope. |
| **A5** | Choice of discrepancy resolution route | Dan: "we either contact the supplier directly or ask AP to hold." Criterion not stated; Karen's involvement is described as adhoc ("they loop me in"). |
| **A6** | Reentry point after a corrected invoice | Unclear whether a revised invoice restarts at intake (T1, new document, new logging) or resumes at Purchasing verification (T7). |
| **A7** | Manager rejection handling | Carlos says he "might reject it or ask Mary to hold it"; no interviewee describes what happens *after* a formal rejection (supplier notification? credit note? resubmission?). |
| **A8** | Goods receipt vs. PO verification | Mary treats Purchasing's step as "confirm goods received"; Dan describes it as "compare invoice to PO." Likely one combined task (T7) but could be two separate controls (receipt confirmation + price/quantity check). |
| **A9** | Invoice intake channel | "Usually by email" — other channels (post, portal) not described. |

## 9. Pain Points Reported (annotations for the diagram)

- **PP1 (at G1/T2):** Invoices arriving without PO numbers cause loops back to the supplier — main source of delay.
- **PP2 (at SP1):** Discrepancies (pricing, extra units, delivery charges) require multiparty email coordination (AP  Purchasing  SRM  Supplier) with no single owner.
- **PP3 (at SP2):** Invoices for informally contracted services have no PO at all and require retrospective PO creation.
- **PP4 (at T7/T9):** Redundant / unclear ownership of the invoice–PO matching control.
- **PP5 (at SP2):** Risk of invoices stalling indefinitely in exception handling; mitigated only by supervisor's manual monitoring.
