## 1. Recurring activities and contextual meanings

Two activities recur within the same case:

- **Verify Documents**
  - **C001:** An initial check identifies missing documentation; the later occurrence is a re-verification after the customer uploads the missing ID scan.
  - **C003:** The first occurrence is an in-person physical ID check; the later occurrence is a re-verification after an account correction.

- **Call Customer**
  - **C002:** The first call clarifies an account discrepancy before an adjustment; the second confirms the adjustment afterward.

For consistency, all occurrences of these overloaded activity labels are renamed according to their purpose, including their single occurrences in other cases.

## 3. Naming rationale

- **Initial Document Verification** indicates the first-pass review before any corrective action or additional submission.
- **Supplemental-Document Re-verification** is used because the second C001 verification follows the customer’s upload of a missing ID scan.
- **Initial In-Person Document Verification** reflects both the initial stage and the branch-based physical inspection in C003.
- **Post-Adjustment Document Re-verification** shows that the later C003 verification validates documents after account data was corrected.
- **Missing-Document Request Call** distinguishes C001’s request for additional evidence.
- **Account-Discrepancy Clarification Call** describes the purpose of the first C002 call and its position before account adjustment.
- **Post-Adjustment Confirmation Call** identifies the second C002 call as confirmation after the adjustment.
- **Customer Return Request Call** captures the distinct operational purpose of the C003 call.

Activities such as **Initiate Request** and **Approve Request** remain unchanged because their process roles are consistent and they do not recur within an individual case.
