The visualization is a **BPMN-style process diagram** for dispatching goods from a computer hardware shop. It is divided into three swimlanes:

- **Logistics**
- **Secretary**
- **Warehouse**

The process begins with **“Ship goods”** and immediately splits into parallel activities:

1. **Clarify shipment method** in the Secretary lane.
2. **Package goods** in the Warehouse lane.

From **Clarify shipment method**, the process checks whether the shipment involves **special handling**:

- If **yes**, the secretary gets three offers from logistics companies, selects a logistics company, and places the order.
- If **no**, the process checks customer information to determine whether **insurance is required**.

The insurance decision branches as follows:

- If insurance is **necessary**, the logistics department performs **“Insure parcel.”**
- If insurance is **not required**, the process proceeds directly.
- Both paths converge before **“Write package label.”**

The shipping and labeling paths then merge and continue toward the warehouse.

In the Warehouse lane:

- The goods are packaged.
- A **48-hour timer** may trigger **“Notify customer about delay.”**
- The normal packaging path and delay-notification path merge.
- The process then completes the **shipment paperwork**, moves the package to the **pickup area**, and ends at **“Shipment prepared.”**

Overall, the diagram depicts parallel preparation and logistics coordination, conditional handling for special shipments and insurance, and an exception path for packaging delays.
