### **(a) Two proclet classes connected through two channels**
This section illustrates two parallel Petri-net-like process flows—**Lab visit** (left) and **Lab test** (right)—interacting asynchronously via two channels in the center (**Order system** and **HIS**).

#### **1. Lab Visit Process (Left Side):**
* **Start:** Initiated by a doctor performing **Order entry**.
* **Take blood sample:** Handled by a *lab assistant*. This step sends a message through the **Order system** channel to create a new instance of a **Lab test**.
* **Receive result:** A grey task block that waits for test results sent back from the **HIS** channel (`1, *` $\leftrightarrow$ `1, 1`).
* **Judge results:** Handled by a *lab assistant*. A decision gateway splits into two paths:
  * **Everything fine:** Sends a signal through the **HIS** channel (`*, 1` $\rightarrow$ `1, ?`) to the **Finish lab test** activity on the right.
  * **Do tests again:** Sends a signal through the **HIS** channel (`*, 1` $\rightarrow$ `1, ?`) to the **Perform test again** activity on the right.
* **Receive updated result:** Receives updated findings from the HIS channel after re-testing.
* **Send report:** Handled by a *lab assistant*, leading to process completion (End node).

#### **2. Lab Test Process (Right Side):**
* **Start:** Triggered by the **Order system** channel message.
* **Perform test:** Executed by a *chemical analyst*.
* **Make report:** Executed by a *chemical analyst*. Sends initial results back to the **Lab visit** process via the **HIS** channel.
* **Decision Split:**
  * If the result was acceptable: Proceed to **Finish lab test** (grey block), ending the test process.
  * If re-testing was requested: Proceed to **Perform test again** (chemical analyst) $\rightarrow$ **Provide updated report** (chemical analyst), which sends updated data back across the **HIS** channel (`1, ?` $\rightarrow$ `1, *`).
* **End:** Both paths terminate at the process end node.

#### **3. Channels (Middle):**
* **Order system:** Connects the initial blood sample task to the start of the Lab test process with cardinality/multiplicity constraints (`*, 1` to `1, 1`).
* **HIS (Hospital Information System):** Handles multiple bidirectional message exchanges regarding test results, requests for re-testing, confirmation, and updated reports between the two processes.
